




dhātupāṭha  vilāsiniyā

||namau tassa bhagavatau arahatau sammāsambuddhassa||

1.

 sammāsambuddha sūriyau yau sambaudhau dayau ditau|

jagu paṅkaja saṅghātai baudhayī paṇamāmi taṃ||

2.

 saddhammabhānu yau laukā laukaṃ katvāna dhī tamaṃ|

dhaṃsayī muninā sammā pātubhūtau namāmi taṃ||

3.

 silagandhasamākiṇṇau buddhau saddhammahāya yau|

saṅghatauyaruhau pāṇa lī tausaisi namāmi taṃ-

4.

 natvā mama garuṃvāsi padumārāma nāmakaṃ|

pāḷiṃ nissāya kassā haṃ dhātupāṭhavilāsini-

5.

 imañhi ganthakaraṇaṃ satthāgamanayai ratau|

mūkalaṃgamu saṅkhātai gāmai saj‍janakāritai-

6.

 suvisuddhārāmanāma vihāramhi nivāsakau|

tasmiṃ padhāna thairau si katañ‍ñū santavutti yau-

7.

 guṇālaṅkāranāmau sau thairau thairanvayai ratau|

yāci maṃ abhigantvāna mittau mai vaṅkamānasau||

8.

 buddhau haissaṃ yadā laukai niddaisau haṃ tadā iti|

pāpuṇissa mahaṅkāraṃ kau vādau pani hantarai-

appac‍cayau  parau hauti bhūvādi gaṇatau sati|

suddhakattu kirayākhyānai sabbadhātuka nissitaṃ-

payuttau  kattunā yaugai ṭhitau yaivā ppadhāniyai|

kirayaṃ sādhaiti aitassa dīpakaṃ sāsanai padaṃ-

karaṇa vacanaṃyaiva yaibhuyyaina padissati|

ākhyātai kāritaṭṭhānaṃ sandhāya kathitaṃ idaṃ|

na nāmai kārataṭṭhānaṃ baudhaitā itiādikaṃ-

sunakhaihipi khādāpainti ic‍cādini padānitu|

āharitvāna dīpaiyya payauga kusalau budhau-yī||

kathitau  sac‍ca saṅkhaipai pac‍canta vacanaina vai|

bhuyyatai iti saddassa sambandhau bhāvadīpanau-

niddaisapāḷiyaṃ rūpaṃ vihauti vihavīyati|

iti dassanatauvāpi pac‍cattavacanaṃ thiraṃ-

tathā dhajaggasuttantai muninā hac‍ca bhāsitai|

sau pahīyissati iti pāḷidassanataupica-

pāramitānu bhāvaina mahaisīnaṃva daihatau|

san‍ni nipphādanā naiva sak‍kaṭādi vacau viya-

pac‍catta dassanainaiva purisattaya yaujanaṃ|

aikavacanikañ‍cāpi bahuvacanikampica|

kātabba miti nau khantī parassapadaādikai-

bhāvai kirayāpadaṃ nāma pāḷiyā atiduddasaṃ|

tasmā taggahaṇūpāyau vuttau aittāvatā mayā-yī||

yaṃ  tikālaṃ tipurisaṃ kirayāvāci tikārakaṃ|

attiliṅgaṃ dvivacanaṃ ta dākhyātanti vuc‍cati-yī||

ākhyāta  sāgara matha j‍jatani taraṅgaṃ,

dhātuj‍jalaṃ vikaraṇa gama kālamīnaṃ|

laupā nubandha raya mattha vibhāgatīraṃ,

dhīrā taranti kavinau puthu buddhi nāvā-yī||

cakkhakkhī  nayanaṃ naittaṃ laucanaṃ diṭṭhi dassanaṃ|

paikkhanaṃ acchi pamhantu pakhumanti pavuc‍cati-yi||

‘‘pabbājitau  sakā raṭṭhā, añ‍ñaṃ janapadaṃ gatau,

mahantaṃ kauṭṭhaṃ kayirātha, duruttānaṃ nivaitavai’’-

paurāṇa  maitaṃ atula naitaṃ aj‍jatanāmiva,

nindanti tuṇhi māsīnaṃ nindanti bahubhāṇinaṃ|

mitabhāṇinampi nindanti natthi laukai aninditau-

nagaraṃ  yathā pac‍cantaṃ ‘‘guttaṃ’’ santarabāhiyaṃ,

aivaṃ ‘gaupaitha’ attānaṃ, khaṇai vai mā upac‍cagā-

dhiratthu  taṃ visavantaṃ, yamahaṃ jīvita kāraṇā,

vantaṃ pacchā vamissāmi, mataṃ mai jivitaṃ varaṃ-

viluppataiva  purisau, yāvassa upakappati,

yadā cañ‍ñai vilumpantī, sau viluttau vilumpatī-

‘‘appamādau  amatapadaṃ, pamādau mac‍cunau padaṃ,

appamattā na mīyanti, yai pamattā yathāmatā’’||

1. 

 ñāṇavimala tissākhyau, yau mahāsaṅgha nāyakau,

marammavaṃsaṃ ādauca, dīpai saṇṭhāpayī idha-

2.

 tassa padhāna sissausi, pāḷi yaṭṭhakathā vidū,

dhammadhāra samañ‍ñātau, yau mahā saṅghasāmikau-

3.

 yau tassa mukhyasiṃssā si, dhammai satthaiva kauvidau,

ñāṇānanda mahāthairau, khai mā viya supākaṭau-

4.

 vimalasāra tissākhyau, mahāsaṃsādhipau kavi,

sissausi dutiyau tassa, pariyatti visāradau-

5.

 padumārāma nāmau mai, ācairau thairapuṅgavau,

tatiyau tassa sissau si sikkhāgārava sañ‍ñutau-

6.

 saṅghādhipauca vimala, sārākhyau thairakuñ‍jarau,

padumārāma vikhyāta, mahāthairau cimai duvai-

7.

 dhammādhāra mahāsaṅgha, sāminauca upantikai,

ñāṇānanda mahāthaira, ssantikaiva samuggahuṃ-

8. 

 taisu khau padumārāma mahāthairau avaṃ mamaṃ,

sikkhayi sadda satthaica, pāḷiyaṭṭhakathāsu ca-

9.

 tasmiṃ divaṅgatai pacchā, chandau vyākaraṇādikaṃ,

vimalasāra mahāthaira, ssantikaica samuggahiṃ-

10.

 tassa khau padumārāma mahāthairassa dhīmatau,

sissaina ñāṇatilaka thairaina saṃsasāminā-

11.

 buddhassa parinibbāṇa vīsahassai catussatai,

sa sattatyādhikai vassai jaiṭṭhamāsai manauramai-

12.

 aṭṭhamiyaṃ kāḷapakkhai, katāyaṃ matisūdanī,

dhātupāṭhattha baudhāya dhātupāṭha vilāsinī-

13.

 ādi muddāpanaṃ assā, guṇālaṅkāra nāminau,

aunaujitaṃ, mamāyattaṃ tataupari tapassinau-

14.

 sissau mayhaṃ gunānandau unākuruva gāmajau,

mamu patthamhitau āsi, gaṇṭhiṭṭhānaisanāditau|

15.

 bastyaṃ samañ‍ñakau rājā, mac‍cau mama pitā ahu,

auntīnyā vī sanāmā mai mātā saināpatānyanu–

16.

 ācairā caiva pācairā, janakau jananīva mai,

daivā caityaṅginau sabbai, nainapappauntu nibbutinti-

dhātupāṭhavilāsiniyā samāptayi|
