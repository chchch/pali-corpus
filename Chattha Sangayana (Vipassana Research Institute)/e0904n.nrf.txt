




|| namau tassa bhagavatau arahatau sammāsambuddhassa||

suttantapiṭaka

aṅguttaranikāyai

saṃgāyanassa pucchā vissaj‍janā

pucchā – paṭhamamahāsaṃgītikālai  āvusau dhammasaṃgāhakā mahākassapādayau mahāthairavarā paurāṇasaṃgītikārā paṭhamaṃ vinayapiṭakaṃ saṃgāyitvā suttantapiṭakai ca dīghamajjhimasaṃyuttasaṅkhātai tayau nikāyai saṃgāyitvā tadanantaraṃ kiṃ nāma pāvacanaṃ saṃgāyiṃsu|

vissaj‍janā – paṭhamamahāsaṃgītikālai bhantai dhammasaṃgāhakā mahākassapādayau mahāthairavarā paurāṇasaṃgītikārā paṭhamaṃ vinayaṃ saṃgāyitvā suttantapiṭakai ca dīghamajjhimasaṃyuttasaṅkhātai tayau nikāyai saṃgāyitvā tadanantaraṃ navahi ca suttasahassaihi pañ‍cahi ca suttasattaihi sattapañ‍ñāsāya ca suttaihi paṭimaṇḍitaṃ vīsatibhāṇavārasataparimāṇaṃ aṅguttaranikāyaṃ nāma pāvacanaṃ saṃgāyiṃsu|

pucchā – aṅguttaranikāyaipi  āvusau aikakanipātau dukanipātau tikanipātautiādinā nipātapakaraṇaparicchaidavasaina aikādasavidhā| tattha kataraṃ nipātapakaraṇaṃ saṃgāyiṃsu|

vissaj‍janā – aṅguttaranikāyai bhantai aikādasasu nipātapakaraṇaparicchaidaisu aikakanipātaṃ paṭhamaṃ saṃgāyiṃsu|

1. rūpādivagga

pucchā – aikakanipātaipi  āvusau rūpādivaggau nīvaraṇappahānavaggau akammaniyavaggautiādinā vaggabhaidavasaina bahuvidhā| tattha kataraṃ vaggaṃ paṭhamaṃ saṃgāyiṃsu|

vissaj‍janā – aikakanipātai bhantai rūpādivaggau nīvaraṇappahānavaggautiādinā vīsatiyā vaggaisu rūpādivaggaṃ paṭhamaṃ saṃgāyiṃsu|

pucchā – sādhu sādhu āvusau, mayampi dāni āvusau tatauyaiva paṭṭhāya saṃgāhanatthāya saṃgītipubbaṅgamāni dhammapucchanavissaj‍janakic‍cāni āvahituṃ samārabhāma| taināvusau bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhaina aṅguttaranikāyai aikakanipātai rūpādivaggai purimāni pañ‍ca suttāni kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitāni|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ  bhantai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha rūpādigarukānaṃ pañ‍can‍naṃ purisānaṃ ajjhāsayavasaina ‘‘nāhaṃ bhikkhavai añ‍ñaṃ aikarūpampi samanupassāmi, yaṃ aivaṃ purisassa cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati, yathayidaṃ bhikkhavai itthirūpaṃ, itthirūpaṃ bhikkhavai purisassa cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhatī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitāni|

nāhaṃ bhikkhavai añ‍ñaṃ aikarūpampi samanupassāmi, yaṃ aivaṃ purisassa cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati, yathayidaṃ bhikkhavai itthirūpaṃ, itthirūpaṃ bhikkhavai purisassa cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati|

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau pacchimāni pañ‍ca suttāni bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitāni|

vissaj‍janā – tasmiṃyaiva bhantai sāvatthiyaṃ sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha rūpādigarukānaṃ pañ‍can‍naṃ itthīnaṃ ajjhāsayavasaina ‘‘nāhaṃ bhikkhavai añ‍ñaṃ aikarūpampi samanupassāmi, yaṃ aivaṃ itthiyā cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati, yathayidaṃ bhikkhavai purisarūpaṃ, purisarūpaṃ bhikkhavai itthiyā cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhatī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitāni|

nāhaṃ bhikkhavai añ‍ñaṃ aikarūpampi samanupassāmi, yaṃ aivaṃ itthiyā cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati, yathayidaṃ bhikkhavai purisarūpaṃ, purisarūpaṃ bhikkhavai itthiyā cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati|

nītatthavasaina, naiyyatthavasaina

pucchā – imasmiṃ  āvusau rūpādivaggai bhagavatā nītatthavasaina kiṃ kathitaṃ, naiyyatthavasaina pana kiṃ ñāpitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – imasmiṃ bhantai rūpādivaggai bhagavatā nītatthavasaina vaṭṭaṃ kathitaṃ| naiyyatthavasaina pana vivaṭṭampi ñāpitaṃ|

2. nīvaraṇappahānavagga

pucchā – dutiyau  pana āvusau nīvaraṇappahānavaggau bhagavatā kattha kathañ‍ca bhāsitau|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai ‘‘nāhaṃ bhikkhavai añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi, yaina anuppan‍nau vā kāmacchandau uppaj‍jati, uppan‍nau vā kāmacchandau bhiyyaubhāvāya vaipul‍lāya saṃvattati, yathayidaṃ bhikkhavai subhanimittaṃ, subhanimittaṃ bhikkhavai ayaunisau manasikarautau anuppan‍naucaiva kāmacchandau uppaj‍jati, uppan‍nau ca kāmacchandau bhiyyaubhāvāya vaipul‍lāya saṃvattatī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitau|

pucchā – imasmiṃ  pana āvusau dutiyai nīvaraṇappahānavaggai bhagavatā kiṃ kathitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – imasmiṃ pana bhantai dutiyai nīvaraṇappahānavaggai bhagavatā vaṭṭampi vivaṭṭampi kathitaṃ|

3. akammaniyava

pucchā – taināvusau…pai॰…  sammāsambuddhaina aṅguttaranikāyai aikakanipātai tatiyai akammaniyavaggai kīdisī dhammadaisanāyau daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – tatiyai bhantai akammaniyavaggai ‘‘nāhaṃ bhikkhavai añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi, yaṃ aivaṃ abhāvitaṃ akammaniyaṃ hauti, yathayidaṃ bhikkhavai cittaṃ, cittaṃ bhikkhavai abhāvitaṃ akammaniyaṃ hauti| nāhaṃ bhikkhavai añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi, yaṃ aivaṃ bhāvitaṃ kammaniyaṃ hauti, yathayidaṃ bhikkhavai cittaṃ| cittaṃ bhikkhavai bhāvitaṃ kammaniyaṃ hautī’’ti aivamādikā bhagavatā dhammadaisanāyau daisitā|

4. adantavagga

pucchā – catutthai  panāvusau adantavaggai bhagavatā kīdisī dhammadaisanāyau daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – catutthai bhantai adantavaggai ‘‘nāhaṃ bhikkhavai añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi, yaṃ aivaṃ adantaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattati, yathayidaṃ bhikkhavai cittaṃ, cittaṃ bhikkhavai adantaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattati| nāhaṃ bhikkhavai añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi, yaṃ aivaṃ dantaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattati, yathayidaṃ bhikkhavai cittaṃ, cittaṃ bhikkhavai dantaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattatī’’ti aivamādikā bhagavatā dhammadaisanāyau daisitā|

5. paṇihitaacchavagga

pucchā – pañ‍camai  āvusau paṇihitaacchavaggai bhagavatā kīdisī dhammadaisanāyau daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – pañ‍camai bhantai paṇihitaacchavaggai ‘‘saiyyathāpi bhikkhavai sālisūkaṃ vā yavasūkaṃ vā micchāpaṇihitaṃ hatthaina vā pādaina vā ak‍kantaṃ hatthaṃ vā pādaṃ vā bhaicchati, lauhitaṃ vā uppādaissatīti naitaṃ ṭhāna vij‍jati| taṃ kissahaitu, micchāpaṇihitattā bhikkhavai sūkassa| aivamaiva khau bhikkhavai sau vata bhikkhu micchāpaṇihitaina cittaina avij‍jaṃ  bhaicchati, vij‍jaṃ uppādaissati, nibbānaṃ sacchikarissatīti naitaṃ ṭhānaṃ vij‍jati, taṃ kissa haitu, micchāpaṇihitattā bhikkhavai cittassā’’ti aivamādikā bhagavatā dhammadaisanāyau daisitā|

6. accharāsaṅghātavagga

pucchā – accharāsaṅghātavaggai  āvusau paṭhama dutiyasuttāni bhagavatā kathaṃ bhāsitāni|

vissaj‍janā – accharāsaṅghātavaggai bhantai bhagavatā ‘‘pabhassaramidaṃ bhikkhavai cittaṃ, tañ‍ca khau āgantukaihi upak‍kilaisaihi upak‍kiliṭṭhaṃ, taṃ assutavā puthuj‍janau yathābhūtaṃ nappajānāti, tasmā ‘assutavatau puthuj‍janassa cittabhāvanā natthī’ti vadāmi| pabhassaramidaṃ bhikkhavai cittaṃ, tañ‍ca khau āgantukaihi upak‍kilaisaihi vippamuttaṃ, taṃ subhavā ariyasāvakau yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti| tasmā ‘sutapatau  ariyasāvakassa cittabhāvanā atthī’ti vadāmī’’ti| aivaṃ khau bhagavatā bhāsitāni|

tatiyasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau tatiyasuttādīni bhagavatā kathaṃ bhāsitāni|

vissaj‍janā – tatthaiva bhantai tatiyasuttādīni pañ‍casuttāni ‘‘accharāsaṅghātamattampi cai bhikkhavai bhikkhu maittācittaṃ āsaivati, bhāvaiti, manasikarauti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati bhikkhavai bhikkhu arittajjhānau viharati satthusāsanakarau auvādapatikarau amaughaṃ raṭṭhapiṇḍaṃ bhuñ‍jati, kau pana vādau yai naṃ bahulīkaraunti| yaikaici bhikkhavai dhammā akusalā akusalabhāgiyā akusalapakkhikā, sabbaitai manaupubbaṅgamā, manau taisaṃ dhammānaṃ paṭhamaṃ uppaj‍jati, anvadaiva akusalādhammā, yaikaici bhikkhavai dhammā kusalā kusalabhāgiyā kusalapakkhikā, sabbaitai manaupubbaṅgamā, manau taisaṃ dhammānaṃ paṭhamaṃ uppaj‍jati, anvadaiva kusalā dhammā’’ti aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitāni|

aṭṭhamasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau aṭṭhama navama dasamasuttāni ca vīriyārambhādivaggai dasasuttāni ca kalyāṇamittādivaggai paṭhamasuttañ‍ca bhagavatā kathaṃ bhāsitāni|

vissaj‍janā – tatthaiva bhantai aṭṭhama navama dasamasuttāni ca vīriyārambhādivaggai dasasuttāni ca kalyāṇamittādivaggai paṭhamasuttañ‍ca ‘‘nāhaṃ bhikkhavai añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi, yaina anuppan‍nā vā akusalā dhammā uppaj‍janti, uppan‍nā vā kusalā dhammā parihāyanti, yathayidaṃ bhikkhavai pamādau, pamattassa bhikkhavai anuppan‍nā caiva akusalā dhammā uppaj‍janti, uppan‍nā ca kusalā dhammā parihāyantī’’ti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitāni|

dutiyasutta

pucchā – kalyāṇamittādivaggai  āvusau dutiya tatiyasuttāni bhagavatā kathaṃ bhāsitāni|

vissaj‍janā – kalyāṇamittādivaggai bhantai dutiya tatiyasuttāni ‘‘nāhaṃ bhikkhavai añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi, yaina anuppan‍nā vā akusalā dhammā uppaj‍janti, uppan‍nā vā kusalā dhammā parihāyanti, yathayidaṃ bhikkhavai anuyaugau akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ ananuyaugau kusalānaṃ dhammāna’’nti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitāni|

chaṭṭhasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau chaṭṭhasuttādīni ca pamādādivaggai paṭhamasuttañ‍ca bhagavatā kathaṃ bhāsitāni|

vissaj‍janā – tatthaiva bhantai chaṭṭhasuttādīni ca pamādādivaggai paṭhamasuttañ‍ca ‘‘appamattikā aisā bhikkhavai parihāni yadidaṃ ñātiparihāni| aitaṃ patikiṭṭhaṃ bhikkhavai parihānīnaṃ yadidaṃ pañ‍ñāparihānīti| appamattikā aisā bhikkhavai vuddhi yadidaṃ ñātivuddhi| aitadaggaṃ bhikkhavai vuddhīnaṃ yadidaṃ pañ‍ñāvuddhi| tasmātiha bhikkhavai aivaṃ sikkhitabbaṃ ‘pañ‍ñāvuddhiyā vaddhissāmā’ti| aivañhi vau bhikkhavai sikkhitabba’’nti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitāni|

pamādādivagga

pucchā – taināvusau…pai॰…  sammāsambuddhaina pamādādivaggai dutiyasuttādīni kathaṃ bhāsitāni|

vissaj‍janā – nāhaṃ bhikkhavai añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi, yau aivaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattati, yathayidaṃ bhikkhavai pamādau| pamādau bhikkhavai mahatau anatthāya saṃvattati| nāhaṃ bhikkhavai añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi, yau aivaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattati, yathayidaṃ bhikkhavai appamādau, appamādau bhikkhavai mahatau anatthāya saṃvattatīti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitāni|

dutiya pamādādivagga

pucchā – dutiyai  panāvusau pamādādivaggai paṭhamasuttādīni bhagavatā kathaṃ bhāsitāni|

vissaj‍janā – dutiyai bhantai pamādādivaggai ajjhattikaṃ bhikkhavai aṅganti karitvā nāñ‍ñaṃ aikaṅgampi samanupassāmi, yaṃ aivaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattati, yathayidaṃ bhikkhavai pamādau| pamādau bhikkhavai mahatau anatthāya saṃvattati| ajjhattikaṃ bhikkhavai aṅganti karitvā nāñ‍ñaṃ aikaṅgampi samanupassāmi, yaṃ aivaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattati, yathayidaṃ bhikkhavai appamādau| appamādau bhikkhavai mahatau atthāya saṃvattatīti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitāni|

dasasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau taittiṃsamādīnica dasasuttāni adhammavaggai ca dasasuttāni bhagavatā kathaṃ bhāsitāni|

vissaj‍janā – tatthaiva bhantai taittiṃsamādīni dasasuttāni ca adhammavaggai dasasuttāni ca yai tai bhikkhavai bhikkhū adhammaṃ ‘‘dhammau’’ti dīpainti, tai bhikkhavai bhikkhū bahujanaahitāya paṭipan‍nā bahujanaasukhāya bahunau janassa anatthāya ahitāya dukkhāya daivamanussānaṃ| bahuñ‍ca tai bhikkhavai bhikkhū apuñ‍ñaṃ pasavanti, tai cimaṃ saddhammaṃ antaradhāpaintīti aivamādinā ca, yai tai bhikkhavai bhikkhū adhammaṃ ‘‘adhammau’’ti dīpainti, tai bhikkhavai bhikkhū bahujanahitāya paṭipan‍nā bahujanasukhāya bahujanau janassa atthāya hitāya sukhāya daivamanussānaṃ| bahuñ‍ca tai bhikkhavai bhikkhū puñ‍ñaṃ pasavanti, tai cimaṃ saddhammaṃ ṭhapaintīti aivamādinā ca bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitāni|

aikapuggalavagga

pucchā – aikapuggalavaggai  āvusau bhagavatā kīdisī dhammadaisanāyau daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – aikapuggalavaggai bhantai bhagavatā aikapuggalau bhikkhavai laukai uppaj‍jamānau uppaj‍jati bahujanahitāya bahujanasukhāya laukānukampāya atthāya hitāya sukhāya daivamanussānaṃ| katamau aikapuggalau, tathāgatau arahaṃ sammāsambuddhau, ayaṃ khau bhikkhavai aikapuggalau laukai uppaj‍jamānau uppaj‍jati bahujanahitāya bahujanasukhāya laukānukampāya atthāya hitāya sukhāya daivamanussānanti aivamādikā bhagavatā dhammadaisanāyau daisitā|

aitadaggavagga

añ‍ñāsikauṇḍañ‍ña vatthu

pucchā – aitadavaggai  āvusau āgataisu aikacattālīsāya thairaisu āyasmā añ‍ñāsikauṇḍañ‍ñatthairau kathaṃ bhagavatā aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitau|

vissaj‍janā – aitadaggavaggai bhantai āgataisu aikacattālīsāya mahāthairaisu ‘‘aitadaggaṃ bhikkhavai mama sāvakānaṃ bhikkhūnaṃ rattañ‍ñūnaṃ yadidaṃ añ‍ñāsikauṇḍañ‍ñau’’ti, aivaṃ khau bhantai āyasmā añ‍ñāsikauṇḍañ‍ñatthairau bhagavatā aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitau|

aggasāvakavatthu

pucchā – taināvusau  bhagavatā…pai॰… sammāsambuddhaina aṅguttaranikāyai aikakanipātai aitadaggavaggai āgataisu aikacattālīsāya mahāthairaisu āyasmā ca sāriputtatthairau dhammasaināpati paṭhamau aggasāvakau āyasmā ca mahāmauggal‍lānatthairau dutiyau aggasāvakau kathaṃ aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitau|

vissaj‍janā – āyasmā bhantai sāriputtatthairau dhammasaināpati ‘‘aitadaggaṃ bhikkhavai mama sāvakānaṃ bhikkhūnaṃ mahāpañ‍ñānaṃ yadidaṃ sāriputtau’’ti| āyasmā pana mahāmauggal‍lānatthairau ‘‘aitadaggaṃ bhikkhavai mama sāvakānaṃ bhikkhūnaṃ iddhimantānaṃ yadidaṃ mahāmauggal‍lānau’’ti| aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitau|

idaṃ  tai āsanaṃ vīra, pañ‍ñattaṃ tavanucchaviṃ|

mama cittaṃ pasādaintau, nisīda pupphamāsanai||

yaṃnūnāhaṃ imaṃ bhikkhuṃ upasaṅkamitvā pañhaṃ pucchaiyyaṃ|

yai dhammā haituppabhavā,

aitadaggaṃ bhikkhavai mama sāvakānaṃ bhikkhūnaṃ iddhimantānaṃ yadidaṃ mahāmauggal‍lānau|

mahākassapavatthu

pucchā – āyasmā  pana āvusau mahākassapatthairau paṭhamamahāsaṃgītikālai pāmaukkhasaṅghanāyakabhūtau bhagavatā kathaṃ aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitau|

vissaj‍janā – āyasmā bhantai mahākassapatthairau paṭhamamahāsaṃgītikālai saṅghapāmaukkhabhūtau ‘‘aitadaggaṃ bhikkhavai mama sāvakānaṃ bhikkhūnaṃ dhutavādānaṃ yadidaṃ mahākassapau’’ti| aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitau|

anuruddhatthairavatthu

pucchā – taināvusau…pai॰…  sammāsambuddhaina aṅguttaranikāyai aikakanipātai aitadaggavaggai āgataisu aikacattālīsāya mahāthairaisu āyasmā anuruddhatthairau kathaṃ aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitau|

vissaj‍janā – āyasmā  bhantai anuruddhatthairau ‘‘aitadaggaṃ bhikkhavai mama sāvakānaṃ bhikkhūnaṃ dibbacakkhukānaṃ yadidaṃ anuruddhau’’ti aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitau|

sīvalivatthu

pucchā – āyasmā  pana āvusau sīvalitthairau bhagavatā kathaṃ aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitau|

vissaj‍janā – āyasmā bhantai sīvalitthairau ‘‘aitadaggaṃ bhikkhavai mama sāvakānaṃ bhikkhūnaṃ lābhīnaṃ yadidaṃ sīvalī’’ti aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitau|

sammāsambuddhau  vata sau bhagavā, yau imassa aivarūpassa dukkhassa pahānāya dhammaṃ daisaiti|

‘‘aitadaggaṃ  bhikkhavai mama sāvikānaṃ bhikkhunīnaṃ rattañ‍ñūnaṃ yadidaṃ mahāpajāpatigautamī’’ –

vissaj‍janā – khaimāthairī bhantai paṭhamā aggasāvikā ‘‘aitadaggaṃ bhikkhavai mama sāvikānaṃ bhikkhunīnaṃ mahāpañ‍ñānaṃ yadidaṃ khaimā’’ti aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitā|

‘‘yai rāgarattānupatanti sautaṃ,

sayaṃkataṃ mak‍kaṭauva jālaṃ,

aitampi chaitvāna vajanti dhīrā|

anapaikkhinau sabbadukkhaṃ pahāya’’ –

paṭācārāthairībhikkhunīmavatthu

pucchā – taināvusau bhagavatā…pai॰… sammāsambuddhaina aṅguttaranikāyai aikakanipātai aitadaggavaggai āgatāsu tairasasu thairīsu paṭācārānāma thairī kathaṃ aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitā|

vissaj‍janā – aitadaggavaggai bhantai āgatāsu tairasasu thairikāsu paṭācārā thairī ‘‘aitadaggaṃ bhikkhavai mama sāvikānaṃ bhikkhunīnaṃ vinayadharānaṃ yadidaṃ paṭācārā’’ti aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitā|

‘‘ubhau puttā kālaṅkatā,

panthai mayhaṃ patī matau|

mātāpitā ca bhātā ca,

aikacitakasmiṃ ḍayharai’’ –

‘‘catūsu samuddaisu jalaṃ parittakaṃ,

tatau bahuṃ assujalaṃ anappakaṃ|

dukkhaina phuṭṭhassa narassa saucanā,

kiṃ kāraṇā amma tuvaṃ pamaj‍jasi’’ –

‘‘na  santi puttā tāṇāya,

na pitā nāpi bandhavā|

antakainādhipan‍nassa,

natthi ñātīsu tāṇatā|

aitamatthavasaṃ ñatvā, paṇḍitau sīlasaṃvutau|

nibbānagamanaṃ maggaṃ, khippamaiva visaudhayai’’ –

‘‘yau  ca vassasataṃ jīvai,

apassaṃ udayabbayaṃ|

aikāhaṃ jīvitaṃ saiyyau,

passatau udayabbayaṃ’’ –

dhammadin‍nāthairībhikkhunīmavatthu

vissaj‍janā – dhammadin‍nā bhantai thairī ‘‘aitadaggaṃ bhikkhavai mama sāvikānaṃ bhikkhunīnaṃ dhammakathikānaṃ yadidaṃ dhammadin‍nā’’ti aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitā|

akiñ‍canaṃ anādānaṃ,

tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ’’ –

yasaudharāthairībhikkhunīmavatthu

tapussabhal‍likavatthu

‘‘aitai  bhantai mayaṃ bhagavantaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāma dhammañ‍ca, upāsakai nau bhagavā dhāraitu aj‍jataggai pāṇupaitai saraṇaṃ gatai’’ hu –

anāthapiṇḍakavatthu

pucchā – taināvusau  bhagavatā arahatā…pai॰… sammāsambuddhaina aṅguttaranikāyai aikakanipātai aitadaggavaggai āgataisu aikādasasu upāsakaisu sudattau gahapati anāthapiṇḍikau kathaṃ aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitau|

aitadaggaṃ  bhikkhavai mama sāvakānaṃ upāsakānaṃ dāyakānaṃ yadidaṃ sudattau gahapati anāthapiṇḍikau–

sujātā upāsikāvatthu

‘‘aitā  mayaṃ bhantai bhagavantaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāma dhammañ‍ca bhikkhusaṅghañ‍ca, upāsikāyau nau bhagavā dhāraitu aj‍jataggai pāṇupaitā saraṇaṃ gatā’’ hu –

‘‘aitadaggaṃ bhikkhavai mama sāvikānaṃ upāsikānaṃ paṭhamaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchantīnaṃ yadidaṃ sujātā sainiyadhītā’’ –

‘‘aitadaggaṃ  bhikkhavai mama sāvikānaṃ upāsikānaṃ dāyikānaṃ yadidaṃ visākhā migāramātā’’ hu –

khuj‍juttarā upāsikāvatthu

vissaj‍janā – aitadaggavaggai bhantai āgatāsu dasasu upāsikāsu khuj‍juttarā upāsikā ‘‘aitadaggaṃ bhikkhavai mama sāvikānaṃ upāsikānaṃ bahussutānaṃ yadidaṃ khuj‍juttarā’’ti aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitā|

vissaj‍janā – kāḷī bhantai upāsikā kuraragharikā ‘‘aitadaggaṃ bhikkhavai mama sāvikānaṃ anussavappasan‍nānaṃ yadidaṃ kāḷī upāsikā kuraragharikā’’ti aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā aitadaggaṭṭhānai ṭhapitā|

‘‘aitadaggaṃ  bhikkhavai mama sāvikānaṃ upāsikānaṃ anussavappasan‍nānaṃ yadidaṃ kāḷī upāsikā kuraragharikā’’ –

aṭṭhānapāḷi

pucchā – sakalāpi  āvusau aṭṭhānapāḷi bhagavatā kathaṃ bhāsitā|

vissaj‍janā – aṭṭhānamaitaṃ bhikkhavai anavakāsau, yaṃ diṭṭhisampan‍nau puggalau kañ‍cisaṅkhāraṃ nic‍catau upagacchaiyya, naitaṃ ṭhānaṃ vij‍jati| ṭhānañ‍ca khau aitaṃ bhikkhavai vij‍jati, yaṃ puthuj‍janau kañ‍cisaṅkhāraṃ nic‍catau upagacchaiyya, ṭhānamaitaṃ vij‍jatīti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā sakalāpi aṭṭhānapāḷi bhāsitā|

aikadhammapāḷi paṭhamavagga

pucchā – aikadhammapāḷiyaṃ  āvusau paṭhamavaggai kīdisī dhammadaisanā bhagavatā daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – aikadhammapāḷiyaṃ bhantai paṭhamavaggai ‘‘aikadhammau bhikkhavai bhāvitau bahulīkatau aikantanibbidāya virāgāya niraudhāya upasamāya abhiñ‍ñāya sambaudhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati| katamau aikadhammau, buddhānussati, ayaṃ khau bhikkhavai aikadhammau bhāvitau bahulīkatau aikantanibbidāya virāgāya niraudhāya upasamāya abhiñ‍ñāya sambaudhāya nibbānāya saṃvattatī’’ti aivamādikā bhagavatā dhammadaisanā daisitā|

pucchā – dutiyavaggai  pana āvusau bhagavatā kīdisī dhammadaisanāyau daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – dutiyavaggai  bhantai bhagavatā ‘‘nāhaṃ bhikkhavai añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi, yaina anuppan‍nā vā akusalā dhammā uppaj‍janti, uppan‍nā vā akusalā dhammā bhiyyaubhāvāya vaipul‍lāya saṃvattanti, yathayidaṃ bhikkhavai micchādiṭṭhi| micchādiṭṭhikassa bhikkhavai anuppan‍nā caiva akusalā dhammā uppaj‍janti, uppan‍nā ca akusalā dhammā bhiyyaubhāvāya vaipul‍lāya saṃvattantī’’ti| aivamādikā bhagavatā dhammadaisanāyau daisitā|

tatiyavagga

pucchā – tatiyavaggai  pana āvusau pañ‍camādīni aṭṭha suttāni bhagavatā kathaṃ bhāsitāni|

vissaj‍janā – tatiyavaggai bhantai pañ‍camādīni aṭṭhasuttāni ‘‘durakkhātai bhikkhavai dhammavinayai yau ca samādapaiti, yañ‍ca samādapaiti, yau ca samādapitau tathattāya paṭipaj‍jati| sabbaitai bahuṃ apuñ‍ñaṃ pasavanti| taṃkissahaitu, durakkhābhattā bhikkhavai dhammassa| svākkhātai bhikkhavai dhammavinayai yau ca samādapaiti, yañ‍ca samādapaiti, yau ca samādapitau, tathattāya paṭipaj‍jati, sabbaitai bahuṃ puñ‍ñaṃ pasavanti| taṃkissahaitu svākkhātattā bhikkhavai dhammassā’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitāni|

apara accharāsaṅghātavagga

pucchā – aparaaccharāsaṅghātavaggai  pana āvusau bhagavatā kīdisī dhammadaisanāyau daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – aparaaccharāsaṅghātavaggai bhantai ‘‘accharāsaṅghātamattampi cai bhikkhavai bhikkhu paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ bhāvaiti, ayaṃ vuc‍cati bhikkhavai bhikkhu arittajjhānau viharati satthusāsanakarau auvādapatikarau amaughaṃ raṭṭhapiṇḍaṃ bhuñ‍jati, kau pana vādau yai naṃ bahulīkarauntī’’ti aivamādikā bhantai bhagavatā dhammadaisanāyau daisitā|

kāyagatāsativagga

pucchā – sakalaupi  āvusau kāyagatāsativaggau bhagavatā kathaṃ bhāsitau|

vissaj‍janā – yassa kassaci bhikkhavai mahāsamuddau caitasā phuṭau, antaugadhā tassa kun‍nadiyau yākāci samuddaṅgamā, aivamaiva bhikkhavai yassa kassaci kāyagatāsati bhāvitā bahulīkatā, antaugadhā tassa kusalā dhammā yaikaici vij‍jābhāgiyā’’ti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā sakalaupi kāyagatāsativaggau bhāsitau|

amatavagga

pucchā – sakalaupi  āvusau amatavaggau bhagavatā kathaṃ bhāsatau|

vissaj‍janā – amataṃ tai bhikkhavai na paribhuñ‍janti, yai kāyagatāsatiṃ na paribhuñ‍janti| amataṃ tai bhikkhavai paribhuñ‍janti, yai kāyagatāsatiṃ paribhuñ‍janti| amataṃ tai bhikkhavai aparibhuttaṃ, yaisaṃ kāyagatāsati aparibhuttā| amataṃ tai bhikkhavai paribhuttaṃ, yaisaṃ kāyagatāsati paribhuttāti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā sakalaupi amatavaggau bhāsitau|

kammakaraṇavagga, vaj‍jasutta

pucchā – taināvusau  bhagavatā…pai॰… sammāsambuddhaina aṅguttaranikāyai dukanipātai kammakaraṇavaggai paṭhamaṃ vaj‍jasuttaṃ kattha kassa kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai sambahulānaṃ bhikkhūnaṃ ‘‘dvaimāni bhikkhavai vaj‍jāni| katamāni dvai diṭṭhadhammikañ‍ca vaj‍jaṃ samparāyikañ‍ca vaj‍ja’’nti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

tasmātiha  bhikkhavai aivaṃ sikkhitabbaṃ ‘‘diṭṭhadhammikassa vaj‍jassa bhāyissāma, samparāyikassa vaj‍jassa bhāyissāma, vaj‍jabhīrunau bhavissāma vaj‍jabhayadassāvinau’’ti| aivañhi khau bhikkhavai sikkhitabbaṃ|

padhānasutta

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃyaiva bhantai sambahulānaṃ bhikkhūnaṃ ‘‘dvaimāni bhikkhavai padhānāni durabhisambhavāni laukasmiṃ| katamāni dvai, yañ‍ca gihīnaṃ agāraṃ ajjhāvasataṃ cīvarapiṇḍapātasaināsanagilānapac‍cayabhaisaj‍japarikkhārānuppādanatthaṃ padhānaṃ, yañ‍ca agārasmā anagāriyaṃ pabbajitānaṃ sabbūpadhipaṭinissaggattaṃ padhānaṃ| imāni khau bhikkhavai dvai padhānāni durabhisambhavāni laukasmi’’nti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

tapanīyasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau tapanīyasuttaṃ bhagavatā kathaṃ bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – dvaimai bhikkhavai dhammā tapanīyā| katamai dvai, idha bhikkhavai aikac‍cassa kāyaduc‍caritaṃ kataṃ hauti, akataṃ hauti kāya sucaritaṃ, vacīduc‍caritaṃ kataṃ hauti, akataṃ hauti vacīsucaritaṃ, manauduc‍caritaṃ kataṃ hauti, akataṃ hauti manausucaritaṃ| sau ‘‘kāyaduc‍caritaṃ mai kata’’nti tappati, ‘‘akataṃ mai kāyasucarita’’nti tappati| vacīduc‍caritaṃ (pa) ‘‘manauduc‍caritaṃ mai kata’’nti tappati, ‘‘akataṃ mai manausucarita’’nti tappati| imai khau bhikkhavai dvai dhammā tapanīyāti| aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

adhikaraṇavagga, sattamasutta

pucchā – adhikaraṇavaggai  pana āvusau sattamasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai jāṇussauṇiṃ brāhmaṇaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| jāṇussauṇi bhantai brāhmaṇau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca ‘‘kau nu khau bhau gautama haitu kau pac‍cayau, yaina midhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃmaraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapaj‍jantī’’ti| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘katattā ca brāhmaṇa akatattā ca aivamidhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃmaraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapaj‍jantī’’ti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

kau  nu khau bhau gautama haitu kau pac‍cayau, yaina midhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapaj‍janti|

‘‘katattāca brāhmaṇa akatattāca aivamidhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapaj‍janti’’ –

kau  pana bhau gautama haitu kau pac‍cayau, yaina midhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā sugatiṃ saggaṃ laukaṃ upapaj‍janti –

katattā ca brāhmaṇa akatattā ca aivamidhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā sugatiṃ saggaṃ laukaṃ upapaj‍janti|

na khau ahaṃ imassa bhautau gautamassa saṃkhittaina bhāsitassa vitthāraina atthaṃ avibhattassa vitthāraina atthaṃ –

sādhu mai bhavaṃ gautamau tathā dhammaṃ daisaitu|
