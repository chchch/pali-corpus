1. aikakapuggalapañ‍ñatti

1. katamau  ca puggalau samayavimuttau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau kālaina kālaṃ samayaina samayaṃ aṭṭha vimaukkhai kāyaina phusitvā phassitvā (sī॰ pī॰) viharati, pañ‍ñāya cassa disvā aikac‍cai āsavā parikkhīṇā haunti – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘samayavimuttau’’|

2. katamau ca puggalau asamayavimuttau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau na  haiva khau kālaina kālaṃ samayaina samayaṃ aṭṭha vimaukkhai kāyaina phusitvā viharati, pañ‍ñāya cassa disvā āsavā parikkhīṇā haunti – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘asamayavimuttau’’| sabbaipi ariyapuggalā ariyai vimaukkhai asamayavimuttā|

3. katamau ca puggalau kuppadhammau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau lābhī hauti rūpasahagatānaṃ vā arūpasahagatānaṃ vā samāpattīnaṃ| sau ca khau na nikāmalābhī hauti na akicchalābhī na akasiralābhī; na yatthicchakaṃ yadicchakaṃ yāvaticchakaṃ samāpaj‍jatipi vuṭṭhātipi| ṭhānaṃ khau panaitaṃ vij‍jati, yaṃ tassa puggalassa pamādamāgamma tā samāpattiyau kuppaiyyuṃ – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘kuppadhammau’’|

4. katamau ca puggalau akuppadhammau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau lābhī hauti rūpasahagatānaṃ vā arūpasahagatānaṃ vā samāpattīnaṃ| sau ca khau nikāmalābhī hauti akicchalābhī akasiralābhī; yatthicchakaṃ yadicchakaṃ yāvaticchakaṃ samāpaj‍jatipi vuṭṭhātipi| aṭṭhānamaitaṃ anavakāsau  yaṃ tassa puggalassa pamādamāgamma tā samāpattiyau kuppaiyyuṃ – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘akuppadhammau’’| sabbaipi ariyapuggalā ariyai vimaukkhai akuppadhammā|

5. katamau ca puggalau parihānadhammau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau lābhī hauti rūpasahagatānaṃ vā arūpasahagatānaṃ  vā samāpattīnaṃ| sau ca khau na nikāmalābhī hauti na akicchalābhī na akasiralābhī; na  yatthicchakaṃ  yadicchakaṃ yāvaticchakaṃ samāpaj‍jatipi vuṭṭhātipi| ṭhānaṃ khau panaitaṃ vij‍jati, yaṃ sau puggalau pamādamāgamma tāhi samāpattīhi parihāyaiyya – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘parihānadhammau’’|

6. katamau ca puggalau aparihānadhammau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau lābhī hauti rūpasahagatānaṃ vā arūpasahagatānaṃ vā samāpattīnaṃ| sau ca khau nikāmalābhī hauti akicchalābhī akasiralābhī; yatthicchakaṃ yadicchakaṃ yāvaticchakaṃ samāpaj‍jatipi vuṭṭhātipi| aṭṭhānamaitaṃ anavakāsau yaṃ sau puggalau pamādamāgamma tāhi samāpattīhi parihāyaiyya – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘aparihānadhammau’’| sabbaipi ariyapuggalā ariyai vimaukkhai aparihānadhammā|

7. katamau ca puggalau caitanābhabbau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau lābhī hauti rūpasahagatānaṃ vā arūpasahagatānaṃ vā samāpattīnaṃ| sau ca khau na nikāmalābhī hauti na akicchalābhī na akasiralābhī; na yatthicchakaṃ yadicchakaṃ yāvaticchakaṃ samāpaj‍jatipi vuṭṭhātipi| sacai anusañ‍caitaiti, na parihāyati tāhi samāpattīhi| sacai na anusañ‍caitaiti, parihāyati tāhi samāpattīhi – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘caitanābhabbau’’|

8. katamau ca puggalau anurakkhaṇābhabbau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau lābhī hauti rūpasahagatānaṃ vā arūpasahagatānaṃ vā samāpattīnaṃ| sau ca khau na nikāmalābhī hauti na akicchalābhī na akasiralābhī; na yatthicchakaṃ yadicchakaṃ yāvaticchakaṃ samāpaj‍jatipi vuṭṭhātipi| sacai anurakkhati, na  parihāyati tāhi samāpattīhi| sacai na anurakkhati, parihāyati tāhi samāpattīhi – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘anurakkhaṇābhabbau’’|

9. katamau  ca puggalau puthuj‍janau? yassa puggalassa tīṇi saṃyaujanāni appahīnāni; na ca taisaṃ dhammānaṃ pahānāya paṭipan‍nau – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘puthuj‍janau’’|

10. katamau ca puggalau gautrabhū? yaisaṃ  dhammānaṃ samanantarā ariyadhammassa avak‍kanti hauti taihi dhammaihi saman‍nāgatau – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘gautrabhū’’|

11. katamau  ca puggalau bhayūparatau? satta saikkhā bhayūparatā, yai ca puthuj‍janā sīlavantau| arahā abhayūparatau|

12. katamau ca puggalau abhabbāgamanau? yai tai puggalā kammāvaraṇaina saman‍nāgatā, kilaisāvaraṇaina saman‍nāgatā, vipākāvaraṇaina saman‍nāgatā, assaddhā acchandikā duppañ‍ñā aiḷā, abhabbā niyāmaṃ auk‍kamituṃ kusalaisu dhammaisu sammattaṃ – imai vuc‍canti puggalā ‘‘abhabbāgamanā’’|

13. katamau ca puggalau bhabbāgamanau? yai tai puggalā na kammāvaraṇaina saman‍nāgatā, na kilaisāvaraṇaina saman‍nāgatā, na vipākāvaraṇaina saman‍nāgatā, saddhā chandikā pañ‍ñavantau pañ‍ñavantā (sī॰) anaiḷā, bhabbā niyāmaṃ auk‍kamituṃ kusalaisu dhammaisu sammattaṃ – imai vuc‍canti puggalā ‘‘bhabbāgamanā’’|

14. katamau  ca puggalau niyatau? pañ‍ca puggalā ānantarikā, yai ca micchādiṭṭhikā niyatā, aṭṭha ca ariyapuggalā niyatā| avasaisā puggalā aniyatā|

15. katamau ca puggalau paṭipan‍nakau? cattārau maggasamaṅginau puggalā paṭipan‍nakā, cattārau phalasamaṅginau puggalā phalai ṭhitā|

16. katamau ca puggalau samasīsī? yassa puggalassa apubbaṃ acarimaṃ āsavapariyādānañ‍ca hauti jīvitapariyādānañ‍ca – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘samasīsī’’|

17. katamau  ca puggalau ṭhitakappī? ayañ‍ca puggalau sautāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipan‍nau assa, kappassa ca uḍḍayhanavailā assa, naiva tāva kappau uḍḍayhaiyya yāvāyaṃ puggalau na sautāpattiphalaṃ sacchikarauti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘ṭhitakappī’’| sabbaipi  maggasamaṅginau puggalā ṭhitakappinau|

18. katamau ca puggalau ariyau? aṭṭha ariyapuggalā ariyā| avasaisā puggalā anariyā|

19. katamau  ca puggalau saikkhau? cattārau maggasamaṅginau tayau phalasamaṅginau puggalā ‘‘saikkhā’’| arahā asaikkhau| avasaisā puggalā naivasaikkhanāsaikkhā|

20. katamau ca puggalau taivij‍jau? tīhi vij‍jāhi saman‍nāgatau puggalau  ‘‘taivij‍jau’’|

21. katamau ca puggalau chaḷabhiñ‍ñau? chahi abhiñ‍ñāhi saman‍nāgatau puggalau ‘‘chaḷabhiñ‍ñau’’|

22. katamau ca puggalau sammāsambuddhau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau pubbai ananussutaisu dhammaisu sāmaṃ sac‍cāni abhisambujjhati; tattha ca sabbañ‍ñutaṃ pāpuṇāti, balaisu ca vasībhāvaṃ – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘sammāsambuddhau’’|

23. katamau ca puggalau pac‍caikasambuddhau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau pubbai ananussutaisu dhammaisu sāmaṃ sac‍cāni abhisambujjhati; na ca tattha sabbañ‍ñutaṃ pāpuṇāti, na ca balaisu vasībhāvaṃ – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘pac‍caikasambuddhau’’|

24. katamau ca puggalau ubhataubhāgavimuttau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau aṭṭha vimaukkhai kāyaina phusitvā viharati; pañ‍ñāya cassa disvā āsavā parikkhīṇā haunti – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘ubhataubhāgavimuttau’’|

25. katamau  ca puggalau pañ‍ñāvimuttau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau na haiva khau aṭṭha vimaukkhai kāyaina phusitvā viharati; pañ‍ñāya cassa disvā āsavā parikkhīṇā haunti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘pañ‍ñāvimuttau’’|

26. katamau ca puggalau kāyasakkhī? idhaikac‍cau puggalau aṭṭha vimaukkhai kāyaina phusitvā viharati; pañ‍ñāya cassa disvā aikac‍cai  āsavā parikkhīṇā haunti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘kāyasakkhī’’|

27. katamau ca puggalau diṭṭhippattau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau ‘‘idaṃ dukkha’’nti yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti, ‘‘ayaṃ dukkhasamudayau’’ti yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti, ‘‘ayaṃ dukkhaniraudhau’’ti yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti, ‘‘ayaṃ dukkhaniraudhagāminī paṭipadā’’ti yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti| tathāgatappavaiditā cassa  dhammā pañ‍ñāya vaudiṭṭhā haunti vaucaritā| pañ‍ñāya cassa disvā aikac‍cai āsavā parikkhīṇā haunti – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘diṭṭhippattau’’|

28. katamau ca puggalau saddhāvimuttau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau ‘‘idaṃ dukkha’’nti yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti, ‘‘ayaṃ dukkhasamudayau’’ti yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti, ‘‘ayaṃ dukkhaniraudhau’’ti yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti, ‘‘ayaṃ dukkhaniraudhagāminī paṭipadā’’ti yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti| tathāgatappavaiditā cassa dhammā pañ‍ñāya vaudiṭṭhā haunti vaucaritā| pañ‍ñāya cassa disvā aikac‍cai āsavā parikkhīṇā haunti, nau ca khau yathā diṭṭhippattassa – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘saddhāvimuttau’’|

29. katamau ca puggalau dhammānusārī? yassa puggalassa sautāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipan‍nassa pañ‍ñindriyaṃ adhimattaṃ hauti, pañ‍ñāvāhiṃ pañ‍ñāpubbaṅgamaṃ ariyamaggaṃ bhāvaiti – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘dhammānusārī’’| sautāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipan‍nau puggalau dhammānusārī phalai ṭhitau diṭṭhippattau|

30. katamau  ca puggalau saddhānusārī? yassa puggalassa sautāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipan‍nassa saddhindriyaṃ adhimattaṃ hauti, saddhāvāhiṃ saddhāpubbaṅgamaṃ ariyamaggaṃ bhāvaiti – ayaṃ vuc‍cati  puggalau ‘‘saddhānusārī’’| sautāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipan‍nau puggalau saddhānusārī phalai ṭhitau saddhāvimuttau|

31. katamau ca puggalau sattakkhattuparamau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau tiṇṇaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā sautāpan‍nau  hauti avinipātadhammau niyatau sambaudhiparāyanau sambaudhiparāyaṇau (sī॰ ka॰)| sau sattakkhattuṃ daivai ca mānusai ca sandhāvitvā saṃsaritvā dukkhassantaṃ karauti – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘sattakkhattuparamau’’|

32. katamau ca puggalau kaulaṅkaulau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau tiṇṇaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā sautāpan‍nau hauti avinipātadhammau niyatau  sambaudhiparāyanau| sau dvai vā tīṇi vā kulāni sandhāvitvā saṃsaritvā dukkhassantaṃ karauti – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘kaulaṅkaulau’’|

33. katamau ca puggalau aikabījī? idhaikac‍cau puggalau tiṇṇaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā sautāpan‍nau hauti avinipātadhammau niyatau sambaudhiparāyanau| sau aikaṃyaiva mānusakaṃ bhavaṃ nibbattaitvā dukkhassantaṃ karauti – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘aikabījī’’|

34. katamau ca puggalau sakadāgāmī? idhaikac‍cau puggalau tiṇṇaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā rāgadausamauhānaṃ tanuttā sakadāgāmī hauti, sakidaiva imaṃ laukaṃ āgantvā dukkhassantaṃ karauti – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘sakadāgāmī’’|

35. katamau ca puggalau anāgāmī? idhaikac‍cau puggalau pañ‍can‍naṃ aurambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā aupapātikau hauti, tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammau tasmā laukā – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘anāgāmī’’|

36. katamau ca puggalau antarāparinibbāyī? idhaikac‍cau puggalau pañ‍can‍naṃ aurambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā aupapātikau hauti, tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammau tasmā laukā| sau upapan‍naṃ vā samanantarā appattaṃ vā vaimajjhaṃ āyuppamāṇaṃ ariyamaggaṃ sañ‍janaiti upariṭṭhimānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ pahānāya – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘antarāparinibbāyī’’|

37. katamau  ca puggalau upahac‍caparinibbāyī? idhaikac‍cau puggalau pañ‍can‍naṃ aurambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ  parikkhayā aupapātikau hauti, tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammau tasmā laukā| sau atik‍kamitvā vaimajjhaṃ āyuppamāṇaṃ upahac‍ca vā kālakiriyaṃ kālaṃ kiriyaṃ (ka॰) ariyamaggaṃ sañ‍janaiti upariṭṭhimānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ pahānāya – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘upahac‍caparinibbāyī’’|

38. katamau ca puggalau asaṅkhāraparinibbāyī? idhaikac‍cau puggalau pañ‍can‍naṃ aurambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā aupapātikau hauti, tattha  parinibbāyī  anāvattidhammau tasmā laukā| sau asaṅkhāraina ariyamaggaṃ sañ‍janaiti upariṭṭhimānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ pahānāya – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘asaṅkhāraparinibbāyī’’|

39. katamau ca puggalau sasaṅkhāraparinibbāyī? idhaikac‍cau puggalau pañ‍can‍naṃ aurambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā aupapātikau hauti, tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammau tasmā laukā| sau sasaṅkhāraina ariyamaggaṃ sañ‍janaiti upariṭṭhimānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ pahānāya – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘sasaṅkhāraparinibbāyī’’|

40. katamau ca puggalau uddhaṃsautau akaniṭṭhagāmī? idhaikac‍cau puggalau pañ‍can‍naṃ aurambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā aupapātikau hauti, tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammau tasmā laukā| sau avihā cutau atappaṃ gacchati, atappā cutau sudassaṃ gacchati, sudassā cutau sudassiṃ gacchati, sudassiyā cutau akaniṭṭhaṃ gacchati; akaniṭṭhai ariyamaggaṃ sañ‍janaiti upariṭṭhimānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ pahānāya – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘uddhaṃsautau akaniṭṭhagāmī’’|

41. katamau ca puggalau sautāpan‍nau sautāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipan‍nau? tiṇṇaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ pahānāya paṭipan‍nau puggalau sautāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipan‍nau| yassa puggalassa tīṇi saṃyaujanāni pahīnāni – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘sautāpan‍nau’’|

42. kāmarāgabyāpādānaṃ tanubhāvāya paṭipan‍nau puggalau sakadāgāmiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipan‍nau| yassa  puggalassa kāmarāgabyāpādā tanubhūtā – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘sakadāgāmī’’|

43. kāmarāgabyāpādānaṃ anavasaisappahānāya paṭipan‍nau puggalau  anāgāmiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipan‍nau| yassa puggalassa kāmarāgabyāpādā anavasaisā pahīnā – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘anāgāmī’’|

44. rūparāgaarūparāgamānauddhac‍caavij‍jāya anavasaisappahānāya paṭipan‍nau puggalau arahattaphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipan‍nau| yassa puggalassa rūparāgau  arūparāgau mānau uddhac‍caṃ avij‍jā anavasaisā pahīnā – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘arahā’’|

2. dukapuggalapañ‍ñatti

45. katamau ca puggalau kaudhanau? tattha katamau kaudhau? yau kaudhau kujjhanā kujjhitattaṃ dausau dussanā dussitattaṃ dūsanā dūsitattaṃ (syā॰) byāpatti byāpaj‍janā byāpaj‍jitattaṃ viraudhau paṭiviraudhau caṇḍik‍kaṃ asuraupau anattamanatā cittassa – ayaṃ vuc‍cati kaudhau| yassa puggalassa ayaṃ kaudhau appahīnau – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘kaudhanau’’|

46. katamau ca puggalau upanāhī? tattha katamau upanāhau? pubbakālaṃ kaudhau aparakālaṃ upanāhau| yau aivarūpau upanāhau upanayhanā upanayhitattaṃ aṭṭhapanā āṭhapanā (ka॰) vibha॰ 891 ṭhapanā saṇṭhapanā anusaṃsandanā anuppabandhanā daḷhīkammaṃ kaudhassa – ayaṃ vuc‍cati upanāhau| yassa puggalassa ayaṃ upanāhau appahīnau – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘upanāhī’’|

47. katamau ca puggalau makkhī? tattha katamau makkhau? yau makkhau makkhāyanā makkhāyitattaṃ makkhīyanā makkhīyitattaṃ (sī॰), makkhiyanā makkhiyitattaṃ (ka॰)  niṭṭhuriyaṃ niṭṭhuriyakammaṃ – ayaṃ vuc‍cati makkhau| yassa puggalassa ayaṃ makkhau appahīnau – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘makkhī’’|

48. katamau ca puggalau paḷāsī? tattha katamau paḷāsau? yau paḷāsau paḷāsāyanā paḷāsāyitattaṃ  paḷāsāhārau vivādaṭṭhānaṃ yugaggāhau appaṭinissaggau – ayaṃ vuc‍cati paḷāsau| yassa puggalassa ayaṃ paḷāsau appahīnau – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘paḷāsī’’|

49. katamau  ca puggalau issukī? tattha katamā issā? yā paralābhasak‍kāragarukāramānanavandanapūjanāsu issā issāyanā issāyitattaṃ usūyā usūyanā ussuyā ussuyanā (ka॰) vibha॰ 893 usūyitattaṃ – ayaṃ vuc‍cati issā| yassa puggalassa ayaṃ issā appahīnā – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘issukī’’|

50. katamau ca puggalau maccharī? tattha katamaṃ macchariyaṃ? pañ‍ca macchariyāni – āvāsamacchariyaṃ, kulamacchariyaṃ, lābhamacchariyaṃ, vaṇṇamacchariyaṃ, dhammamacchariyaṃ| yaṃ aivarūpaṃ macchairaṃ maccharāyanā maccharāyitattaṃ vaivicchaṃ kadariyaṃ kaṭukañ‍cukatā aggahitattaṃ cittassa – idaṃ vuc‍cati macchariyaṃ| yassa puggalassa idaṃ macchariyaṃ appahīnaṃ – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘maccharī’’|

51. katamau ca puggalau saṭhau? tattha katamaṃ sāṭhaiyyaṃ? idhaikac‍cau saṭhau hauti parisaṭhau| yaṃ tattha saṭhaṃ saṭhatā sāṭhaiyyaṃ  kak‍karatā kak‍kariyaṃ kakkhaḷatā kakkhaḷiyaṃ (syā॰) aivaṃ khuddakavibhaṅgadukaniddaisaipi parikkhattattā pārikkhattiyaṃ – idaṃ vuc‍cati sāṭhaiyyaṃ| yassa puggalassa idaṃ sāṭhaiyyaṃ appahīnaṃ – ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ‘‘saṭhau’’|

194. aivamaivaṃ pañ‍cimai yaudhājīvūpamā puggalā santau saṃvij‍jamānā bhikkhūsu| katamai pañ‍ca? idhaikac‍cau bhikkhu rajaggañ‍ñaiva disvā saṃsīdati visīdati na santhambhati na sak‍kauti brahmacariyaṃ sandhāraituṃ santānaituṃ (sī॰ syā॰) a॰ ni॰ 5.75, sikkhādubbalyaṃ āvikatvā āvīkatvā (sī॰) sikkhaṃ pac‍cakkhāya hīnāyāvattati| kimassa rajaggasmiṃ? idha bhikkhu suṇāti – ‘‘asukasmiṃ nāma gāmai vā nigamai vā itthī vā kumārī vā abhirūpā dassanīyā pāsādikā paramāya vaṇṇapaukkharatāya saman‍nāgatā’’ti| sau taṃ sutvā saṃsīdati visīdati na santhambhati na sak‍kauti brahmacariyaṃ sandhāraituṃ, sikkhādubbalyaṃ āvikatvā sikkhaṃ pac‍cakkhāya hīnāyāvattati| idamassa rajaggasmiṃ|

6. chak‍kapuggalapañ‍ñatti

tatra yvāyaṃ puggalau pubbai ananussutaisu dhammaisu sāmaṃ sac‍cāni anabhisambujjhati, na ca diṭṭhaiva dhammai dukkhassantakarau hauti, anāgāmī hauti anāgantā itthattaṃ, anāgāmī  taina daṭṭhabbau|

7. sattakapuggalapañ‍ñatti

203. kathañ‍ca puggalau sakiṃ nimuggau nimuggauva hauti? idhaikac‍cau puggalau saman‍nāgatau hauti aikantakāḷakaihi akusalaihi dhammaihi| aivaṃ puggalau sakiṃ nimuggau nimuggauva hauti|

kathañ‍ca puggalau ummuj‍jitvā nimuj‍jati? idhaikac‍cau puggalau ummuj‍jati ‘‘sāhu saddhā kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu sāhu (sī॰ syā॰) aivaṃ tīsu ṭhānaisupi hirī kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu auttappaṃ kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu vīriyaṃ viriyaṃ (sī॰ syā॰) kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu pañ‍ñā kusalaisu dhammaisū’’ti| tassa sā saddhā naiva tiṭṭhati nau vaḍḍhati hāyatiyaiva, tassa sā hirī naiva tiṭṭhati nau vaḍḍhati hāyatiyaiva, tassa  taṃ auttappaṃ naiva tiṭṭhati nau vaḍḍhati hāyatiyaiva, tassa taṃ vīriyaṃ naiva tiṭṭhati nau vaḍḍhati hāyatiyaiva, tassa sā pañ‍ñā naiva tiṭṭhati nau vaḍḍhati hāyatiyaiva| aivaṃ puggalau ummuj‍jitvā nimuj‍jati|

kathañ‍ca  puggalau ummuj‍jitvā ṭhitau hauti? idhaikac‍cau puggalau ummuj‍jati ‘‘sāhu saddhā kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu hirī kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu auttappaṃ kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu vīriyaṃ kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu pañ‍ñā kusalaisu dhammaisū’’ti| tassa sā saddhā naiva hāyati nau vaḍḍhati ṭhitā hauti, tassa sā hirī naiva hāyati nau vaḍḍhati ṭhitā hauti, tassa taṃ auttappaṃ naiva hāyati nau vaḍḍhati ṭhitaṃ hauti, tassa taṃ vīriyaṃ naiva hāyati nau vaḍḍhati ṭhitaṃ hauti, tassa sā pañ‍ñā naiva hāyati nau vaḍḍhati ṭhitā hauti| aivaṃ puggalau ummuj‍jitvā ṭhitau hauti|

kathañ‍ca puggalau ummuj‍jitvā vipassati vilaukaiti? idhaikac‍cau puggalau ummuj‍jati ‘‘sāhu saddhā kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu hirī kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu auttappaṃ kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu vīriyaṃ kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu pañ‍ñā  kusalaisu dhammaisū’’ti| sau tiṇṇaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ  parikkhayā sautāpan‍nau hauti avinipātadhammau niyatau sambaudhiparāyanau| aivaṃ puggalau ummuj‍jitvā vipassati vilaukaiti|

kathañ‍ca puggalau ummuj‍jitvā patarati? idhaikac‍cau puggalau ummuj‍jati ‘‘sāhu saddhā kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu hirī kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu auttappaṃ kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu vīriyaṃ kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu pañ‍ñā kusalaisu dhammaisū’’ti| sau tiṇṇaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā rāgadausamauhānaṃ tanuttā sakadāgāmī hauti sakidaiva imaṃ laukaṃ āgantvā dukkhassantakarau hauti| aivaṃ puggalau ummuj‍jitvā patarati|

kathañ‍ca puggalau ummuj‍jitvā patigādhappattau hauti? idhaikac‍cau puggalau ummuj‍jati ‘‘sāhu saddhā kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu hirī kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu auttappaṃ kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu vīriyaṃ kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu pañ‍ñā kusalaisu dhammaisū’’ti| sau pañ‍can‍naṃ aurambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ  parikkhayā aupapātikau hauti tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammau tasmā laukā| aivaṃ puggalau ummuj‍jitvā patigādhappattau hauti|

kathañ‍ca puggalau ummuj‍jitvā tiṇṇau hauti pāraṅgatau thalai tiṭṭhati brāhmaṇau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau ummuj‍jati ‘‘sāhu saddhā kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu hirī kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu auttappaṃ kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu vīriyaṃ kusalaisu dhammaisu, sādhu pañ‍ñā kusalaisu dhammaisū’’ti| sau āsavānaṃ khayā anāsavaṃ caitauvimuttiṃ pañ‍ñāvimuttiṃ diṭṭhaiva dhammai sayaṃ abhiñ‍ñā sacchikatvā upasampaj‍ja viharati| aivaṃ puggalau ummuj‍jitvā tiṇṇau hauti pāraṅgatau thalai tiṭṭhati brāhmaṇau|

9. navakapuggalapañ‍ñatti

208. katamau  ca puggalau sammāsambuddhau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau pubbai ananussutaisu dhammaisu sāmaṃ sac‍cāni abhisambujjhati, tattha ca sabbañ‍ñutaṃ  pāpuṇāti balaisu ca vasībhāvaṃ| ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau sammāsambuddhau|

katamau ca puggalau pac‍caikasambuddhau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau pubbai ananussutaisu dhammaisu sāmaṃ sac‍cāni abhisambujjhati, na ca tattha sabbañ‍ñutaṃ pāpuṇāti na ca balaisu vasībhāvaṃ| ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau pac‍caikasambuddhau|

katamau ca puggalau ubhataubhāgavimuttau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau aṭṭha vimaukkhai kāyaina phusitvā viharati pañ‍ñāya cassa disvā āsavā parikkhīṇā haunti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau ubhataubhāgavimuttau|

katamau ca puggalau pañ‍ñāvimuttau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau na haiva khau aṭṭha vimaukkhai kāyaina phusitvā viharati pañ‍ñāya cassa disvā āsavā parikkhīṇā haunti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau pañ‍ñāvimuttau|

katamau  ca puggalau kāyasakkhī? idhaikac‍cau puggalau aṭṭha vimaukkhai kāyaina phusitvā viharati pañ‍ñāya cassa disvā aikac‍cai āsavā parikkhīṇā haunti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau kāyasakkhī|

katamau  ca puggalau diṭṭhippattau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau ‘‘idaṃ dukkha’’nti yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti…pai॰… ‘‘ayaṃ dukkhaniraudhagāminī paṭipadā’’ti yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti, tathāgatappavaiditā cassa dhammā pañ‍ñāya vaudiṭṭhā haunti vaucaritā, pañ‍ñāya cassa disvā aikac‍cai āsavā parikkhīṇā haunti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau diṭṭhippattau|

katamau ca puggalau saddhāvimuttau? idhaikac‍cau puggalau ‘‘idaṃ dukkha’’nti yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti…pai॰… ‘‘ayaṃ dukkhaniraudhagāminī paṭipadā’’ti yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti, tathāgatappavaiditā cassa  dhammā pañ‍ñāya vaudiṭṭhā  haunti vaucaritā, pañ‍ñāya cassa disvā aikac‍cai āsavā parikkhīṇā haunti, nau ca khau yathā diṭṭhippattassa| ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau saddhāvimuttau|

katamau ca puggalau dhammānusārī? yassa puggalassa sautāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipan‍nassa pañ‍ñindriyaṃ adhimattaṃ hauti, pañ‍ñāvāhiṃ pañ‍ñāpubbaṅgamaṃ ariyamaggaṃ bhāvaiti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau dhammānusārī| sautāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipan‍nau puggalau dhammānusārī, phalai ṭhitau diṭṭhippattau|

katamau ca puggalau saddhānusārī? yassa puggalassa sautāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipan‍nassa saddhindriyaṃ adhimattaṃ hauti, saddhāvāhiṃ saddhāpubbaṅgamaṃ ariyamaggaṃ bhāvaiti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati puggalau saddhānusārī| sautāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipan‍nau puggalau saddhānusārī, phalai ṭhitau saddhāvimuttauti|
