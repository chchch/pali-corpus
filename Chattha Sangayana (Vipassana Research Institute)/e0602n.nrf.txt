mahāpaṇāmapāṭha

(buddhavandanā)

1.

 sau, kau| nai, saṃ|

2.

 satthā, naisaṃ| khaimaṃ, dātā|

3.

 haiḷāta-k‍kārau sau| jantūnaṃ, nau mā nu|

4.

 sau laukandhaṃ, hantvā nak‍kau|

sattambaujaṃ, baudhaitā nu||

5.

 jinasūriyau, tibhavanabhai|

muhatimiraṃ, abhitapayai||

6.

 jainau jinamūlai, disvā janakāyaṃ|

saṃsāranimuggaṃ, sabbañ‍ñuta micchaṃ||

7.

 hitvā karupaitaṃ, maukkhaṃ paṇidhānaṃ|

vattaisi dayāṇā, yau taṃ muni vandai||

8.

 sutāyubhariyā mai, dhanaṅgamapi caj‍jaṃ||

cinaṃ budhinidānai, agā muniyaṃ maitaṃ||

9.

 sau sasajātiya daihaṃ, caj‍jiya dānavarañ‍ca|

nāgusabhau varasīlaṃ, pūrayi samparicāgau||

10.

 naikkhammaggaṃ rājā hutvā, sainabbidvā pañ‍ñāsaiṭṭhaṃ|

vaidaihindau vīruk‍kaṃsaṃ, khantīvādī khantīsaiṭṭhaṃ||

11.

 sau sutasaumau tathagaṃ, taimiya dhiṭṭhānavaraṃ|

aikabhugau maittavaraṃ, laumahasaupaikkhataraṃ||

12.

 pāramī  tiduk‍karāva, pūriyāna saiṭṭhabaudhi|

pāpuṇittha yau ananta-dhammasāradaṃ namāmi||

13.

 pahāya cak‍kavattikaṃ, karaupanīta maddiyaṃ|

bhusidharai sasainakaṃ, ajaini pāpimaṃ namai||

14.

 athuttaraṃ jayākaraṃ, bahūpakārataṃ jinau|

paṭic‍ca nimmisakkhibhi, udikkhatai namāmi taṃ||

15.

 caṅkamai maruvimati yau, chindayaṃ kami rataniyai|

iddhipāṭihiraṇakarau, cakkhumaṃ tamabhipaṇamai||

16.

 atha ratanagharai buddhau, chadidhiti janayaṃ saṅkhyaṃ|

sīmīsā tamabhidhammaṃ yau, suranaramahitaṃ vandai||

17.

 nigraudhantika nubbhavī sukhaṃ, dhammaṃ yau vicinaṃ nadhīvarau|

kicchāladdha manañ‍ñabaudhiyaṃ, vandai taṃ sugataṃ nigraucaraṃ||

18.

 sabbhaugaihi tha mucalindaina,

nāgindaina rahasi pāvuttau|

saukhyaṃ yau nubhavi vimuttiṃtaṃ,

vandai mārāji atulappañ‍ñau||

19.

 kāruṇikau rājāyatanai yau,

bhauja muḷāraṃ bhauj‍jarasaikaṃ|

vāsa makā nantagguṇadhārau,

aikagatau vandāmi mahaisī||

20.

 kaiyyaṃ kaiyyaṃ abhijayakaituṃ,

jaiyyaṃ jaiyyaṃ varajayapānaṃ|

paiyyaṃ paiyyaṃ suvici namai taṃ,

naiyyaṃ naiyyaṃ samatamupaikkhiṃ||

21.

 satta ca sattāhai vijitāvī,

khaipiya nigraudhaṃ puna gantvā|

dhammasududdasyaṃ pati jātau,

dhammakathā ppausuk‍ka vitak‍kau||

22.

 marugaṇaihi  brahmunā samaṃ,

ratanadāmamairuṇā dadā|

samabhiyācitau paṭissavaṃ,

dasabalau namāmi daisituṃ||

23.

 gantvā isippātak‍kānanañ‍ca,

satthā migaddāyaṃ daisayittha|

sac‍cappakāraṃ yau dhammacak‍ka-

suttaṃ namai chabbīdhaṃsu tāva||

24.

 rañ‍ñau magadhassa katappaṭiñ‍ñau,

gantvānatha rājagahaṃ vinaiti|

sattai malaraugayutai gadaggaṃ,

pāyaituna taṃ paṇamai bhisak‍kaṃ||

25.

 sakyādhivāsai kapilai sañ‍ñatiṃ,

mānaddhajaṃ bhindiya middhivātā|

dhammāmataṃ pāyi pituppabhuti,

vandāmi sakyinda manaumadassiṃ||

26.

 anāthapiṇḍauti vhayaina nanta-

ddhanaṃ cajitvā sukatai vihārai|

vihāsi bhiyyau janatā hitatthaṃ,

sudhammabhairiṃ vadayaṃ bhivandai||

27.

 upaitapuñ‍ñaṃ varalakkhaṇaukaṃ-

ṇaukaṃ visuddhaṃ jalitappadīmaṃ|

dīpaṃ pajānaṃ anighaṃ navaj‍jaṃ-

navaj‍javācaṃ sugataṃ namāmi||

28.

 munibhānu dhammapabhāhi jana-

mbuja muddhatandhatamau sutapau|

karuṇāruṇau samabaudhayi yau,

bhavisītalattapanau paṇamai||

29.

 indu viyambaramajjhatalai yau,

rājati tāragaṇaina munindau|

ariyagaṇapparitau tibhavai taṃ,

sādara muggatasaubha mavandi||

30.

 mūlāmūlā  tiṃsatipāramiyau,

sākhāsākhā majjhimajjhānakāyā|

pattāpattā buddhacakkhuni yassa,

pupphāpupphā kāruṇā maggakhandhau||

31.

 sārāsārā yamakā phaiggubhiñ‍ñā,

sabbālambaṅkurakaṃ sīlavaṇṭaṃ|

saisādhammā madhupak‍kapphalāni,

raṃsīchal‍lī sutacā lakkhaṇāni||

32.

 chāyupagā suṃ puthunaradaivā,

mārajidīpaṅkara mahijātaṃ|

patthatanantañ‍ca kusalabījaṃ,

aikaruhaṃ pādapamuni vandai||

33.

 māratitthiyaripuṃ tamañ‍ca yau,

dhaṃsayaṃ nagaradhamma māpayaṃ|

sīlanīvaraṇa dvārakauṭṭhakaṃ,

aisikāparamasaddha muttamaṃ||

34.

 dvārapālasuyataṃ satiṭālaṃ,

ñāṇacac‍cara sighāṭapadiddhiṃ|

dhammamaggakuṭisāla sahittaṃ,

dhammasainapatisārijabhūtaṃ||

35.

 dutiyasutaparauhitaṃ mahā-

dhutaguṇadharamaggha dassikaṃ|

vinayacinaka dhammarakkhakaṃ,

tibhavapatika dhammarājiyaṃ||

36.

 sutaṃ sutaṃ sabbadhi saj‍janaṃjanaṃ,

mitaṃ mitaṃ nantaguṇālayaṃ layaṃ|

hutaṃ hutaṃ jantunamānamaṃ namaṃ,

bhavai bhavai taibhavamaṅgalaṃ galaṃ||

37.

 laukābhirāmaṃ ramaṇaiyyavaiduraṃ,

laukantaguṃ antubhayānupāgataṃ|

laukaddhajaṃ mānadhajappahārakaṃ,

laukaggaruṃ aggaru pac‍cayai namai||

38.

 makuṭaggaphalaṃ  nayanañ‍canikaṃ,

savanabbhusanaṃ vadanabbhusanaṃ|

galamaṇḍanamiddhi manañ‍ñurasaṃ,

kiriyañ‍ñudaraṃ dvikhaṇañ‍ñukaraṃ||

39.

 kaṭivibhūsana majjhimañ‍ñaṇakaṃ,

dayavilaipana dhāraṇamaṇḍanaṃ|

paṭakhilañ‍ñunivattha manuttaraṃ,

munimaruttama maika mahaṃ namai||

40.

 sativara sucakaṃ ibbhaggapañ‍ñaṃ,

suvīriyavasuvāhaṃ pītisailaṃ|

gahapatisamathaggaṃ thīpasaddhiṃ,

asaṇiyasunupaikkhaṃ sattabhaugaṃ||

41.

 catudisikiddhippada masahāyaṃ,

suparivutaṃ khattiyavirajānaṃ|

suciparisaṃ daivanara manāpaṃ,

dasabalacak‍kādhipati bhivandai||

42.

 samādhibbahiddhaṃ saticchaṭṭhasailaṃ,

virappañ‍camā jīvamajjhaṃ sukamma-

ttatiyaṃ suvācādutiyaṃ bhisaiṭṭhaṃ,

samātak‍kapubbañ‍ca sattappavāraṃ||

43.

 vividhañāṇapabhutibbasākaraṃ,

bahumahiddhika marūna māsayaṃ|

dhuṃvapabhājalitarāmaṇaiyyakaṃ,

sugatanairunagarāja mānamai||

44.

 sīlaj‍jalaṃ ratanadhamma mākaraṃ,

bhiñ‍ñāviciṃ satikulaṭṭhi gambhiraṃ|

vitthiṇṇañāṇa mariyaudajāsayaṃ,

aṭṭhambīdhacchariyakaṃ asandanaṃ||

45.

 bahunaj‍jamausaraṇa maikarasaṃ,

durupaggamaṃ acitasambharinaṃ|

puthupautapuñ‍ñavatamaiva namai,

dvīpaduttamaṇṇava nadindavaraṃ||

46.

 abhiniharadhanuṃ  guṇapāramiṃ,

naḷamatimanabājarahammukhā|

dhitisamitasaraina sudhārayaṃ,

malama gagaṇa maikapavaidanā||

47.

 yau pāvaidhaisiṃ aggamaggañ‍ca tūṇiṃ,

dhammālaṅkārabbamma maikabbalaṃ taṃ|

naittiṃsānantañāṇa gīrail‍limantaṃ,

sabbañ‍ñussāsaṃ iddhidaṇḍaṃ namāmi||

48.

 varatapavuṭṭhi subījakasaddhā,

matiyuganaṅgala hīrutapīsā|

susamatharaj‍ju satidvijatuttaṃ,

vīriyadurāvaha sac‍canidānaṃ||

49.

 vatisaṃvaraṃ sauratamaucanañ‍caṃ,

saraṇā nivattabbahanatthaṃ yassa|

amatapphalaṃ naikarasaihupaitaṃ,

sugataṃ mahākassaka mābhivandai||

50.

 daivaggau tidasapurai varāsanai yau,

daivānaṃ janikapabhutinaṃ bhidhammaṃ|

bālak‍kauva laḷayamācalai timāsaṃ,

daisaisyā paravisayaṃ namai ajaiyyau||

51.

 mahāyasau vividhasubhappakāsakaṃ,

kurūsu yau amitaguṇau tamaunudau|

parappavādahari subhānuyauginaṃ,

namāmi taṃ kathayi satippaṭhānakaṃ||

52.

 samathakapalikau sasnaihasammāsati,

paramamatiginī savaṭṭiparak‍kamau|

sakalajutikarau sudhammapadīpakau,

ima mupajalitau jinaina namāmahaṃ||

53.

 vigatagatamalaṃ malagatavigataṃ,

mahita hitamanaṃ manahitamahitaṃ|

vibhavabhavakaraṃ karabhavavibhavaṃ,

sujana janaguṇaṃ guṇajanasujanaṃ||

54.

 sīlaggadaṇḍavicitaṃ  susamādhipattaṃ,

saubhāsamuj‍jala manantaga ñāṇasīkhaṃ|

saddhammasaiṭṭharatanañ‍ca tilaukakaituṃ,

vandāmi laucanabhisaika susaubhayuttaṃ||

55.

 vinayanaya manayavinaya manamitaṃ,

vijayajaya majayavijaya matulitaṃ|

vibhajabhaja mabhajavibhaja mananakaṃ,

visamasama masamavisama mabhinamai||

56.

 paramarama maramaparama matiguṇaṃ,

pagahagati magatipagata mamamakaṃ|

pacayacaya macayapapaya manaṇakaṃ,

pakatakata makatapakata macalakaṃ||

57.

 ujuka mayanamaggai maukkhadaisaṃ niyāsi,

vararathakujaraina cammacak‍kaina satthā|

hiritapadukapālambaina dhammassutaina,

satinivarayutaina ppāṭihiraddhajaina||

58.

 avihananakkhinā suyama naimupakkharā,

udariyamabbhinā paripuraṅgasac‍cinā|

kusalavibhūsinā nimadakupparaina yau,

akharanataisinā gupitasilanandanā||

59.

 anusanughātinā matipuraijavaina kāla-

ñ‍ñutamatisārikai na ca visāradatthidaṇḍā|

satituda dhītirasmi manadammasindhavaina,

vinayagaṇai namāmi ta matulyasatthavāhaṃ||

60.

 yamak‍kaggijālaṃ paravisaya macchairasahitaṃ,

dudiṭṭhandhubbāhaṃ yugagahaṇatitthīna makarī|

bahūnaṃ majjhai yau ratanakamanai pāṭihariyaṃ,

jayak‍kaitussāpi ta mabhinami kaṇṭambasamipai||

61.

 nakhajutirajaṃ cak‍kaṅgaupaita pādavarambujaṃ,

subhasirimatau raṃsijālaṅgulidasasaṃsubhiṃ|

pavarasirasā daivādaivā sadā na pilandhayuṃ,

ta mativa manaurammaṃ tittīkarā nami yassa kai||

62.

 buddhaupyaikau  nidhanaguṇinau vaṇṇayai yāvajīvaṃ,

kāmaṃ añ‍ñaṃ kathamabhaṇa māsuṃ khiyaithā yukappau|

na tvaivā yaṃ khaya mupavaj‍jai yassa vaṇṇau anantau,

taṃ sabbañ‍ñuṃ sakalarirī naikanāthaṃ namāmi||

63.

 pādidīpādaṃ dvinayanadijaṃ dhammakāyaṃ dhisauṇḍaṃ,

bhāṇīsauṇḍaggaṃ saraṇasirasiṃ maggāvālaṃ subhaṅgaṃ|

sīlālaṅkāraṃ vimalibhavu taṃ sattatiṭṭhidhidabbaṃ,

nāthaibhindaggaṃ phalakariṇukaṃ maukkhabhaujaṃ namāmi||

64.

 malālauḷul‍laulaṃ atibhayajanaṃ duggasaṃsārasinduṃ,

phiyabbhāniggāhau sivataṭamukhau nāvi kaj‍jaiṭṭhanāthau|

padappārak‍kāmaṃ bahujanagaṇaṃ aikamaggattaramhi,

samāraupaitvā mattari ta matulasādarañ‍cā bhivandai||

65.

 byāmaṃsugghanadhāra mak‍kuḷusatabbhāṇuj‍jalantattanaṃ,

uk‍kaṃsaj‍juti kaitumālavicitaṃ saddhammajautindharaṃ|

bundin‍niggatapaj‍jalanta didhitiṃ aj‍jatthanā yāva ca,

vandaitaṃ muni sakyapuṅgava mahaṃ puṇṇinduvattampi ca||

66.

 sattamaṃtamaṃ vināsakaṃsakaṃ dadaṃ vinaiyyakānamaiva,

bhāvanaṃvanaṃ dhulīkaraṃ karaṃ tiduk‍karaṃ pajābhibhuñ‍ja|

gāravaṃravaṃ manauharaṃharaṃ narānarānayaṃ namāmi,

sādaraṃdaraṃ vinaudakaṃdakaṃ pavassakaṃ pajānamiva||

67.

 buddhau nigraudhabimbau mudukaracaraṇau brahmaghausaiṇijaṅghau,

kausacchādaṅgajātau punarapi sugatau suppatiṭṭhitapādau|

mudaudātuṇṇalaumau athamapi sugatau brahmujuggattabhāvau,

nīlakkhī dīghapaṇhī sukhumamalachavī thaumyarassaggasaggī||

68.

 cattālīsaggadantau samakalapanajau antaraṃsappapīṇau,

cak‍kainaṅkītapādau aviraḷadasanau mārajussaṅkhapādau|

tiṭṭhantaunaumaintau bhayakaramudunā jaṇṇukānā masantau,

vaṭṭakkhandhau jinau gautaruṇapakhumakau sīhapubbaḍḍhukāyau||

69.

 sattappīṇau ca dīghaṅgulaṃ matha sugatau laumakūpaikalaumau,

sampan‍naudātadāṭhau kanakasamatacau nīlamuddhaggalaumau|

sambuddhau thūlajivhau atha sīhahanukau jālikappādahatthau,

nāthau uṇhīsasīsau iti guṇasahitaṃ taṃ mahaisiṃ namāmi||

70.

 vaṭṭacitānupubbakasubhaṅgulī  ruhiramaṭṭhatuṅganakhakau,

nigguḷagaupphakau samapadau sīhausabhibha haṃsasan‍nibhakamau|

dakkhiṇatāvatak‍kami samantamaṇḍala nigaṇṭhi jāṇusubhakau,

byañ‍janapuṇṇapausatanu nābhigambhīra achiddadakkhiṇavaṭau||

71.

 dviradakarappakāsurubhujau suvibbhajanupubbamaṭṭhaanunā-

nunaalinānupubbarucira ttilādirahitabbisuddhatanukau|

dasasatakauṭi hatthibaladhāraṇau kanakatuṅganāsikasubhau,

suruhiradantamaṃsatha sucisiniddhadasanau tha laukasaraṇau||

72.

 suddhapasādindri ca vaṭṭataradāṭhau ruhirauṭṭha ca suranaranāthau,

āyatasaubhabbadanau tha muni gambhīrujukāyatasuruciralaikhau|

byāmapabhāmaṇḍalabundi supuraggaṇṇi ca āyatavisaṭasubhakkhī,

pañ‍capasādakkhi ca kuñ‍cikasubhaggapakhumau mudutanuruṇajivhau||

73.

 saummasiniddha tyuj‍jalakaumala varuṇavimalatanu ca amitaguṇau,

kaumala dakkhiṇāvaṭa añ‍janabhidasarisanilaka mudutanuruhau|

dakkhiṇavaṭṭakaumala saṇusamasunila alulita siraruhi jinau,

saubhaṇasaṇṭhānau tha siniddhasiraruhi ca supacitasatakusala jau||

74.

 nigguḷaunigganticchattassarisaatisubhagasira cāyatāruci kaṇṇakau,

sausaṇṭhānassaṇhāhārānukamapahutabhamutha suāyatabbhamukau ca sau|

suggandhaggattau muddhicātha vadani ca puthulakanalāṭa āyatasaubhaṇau,

assāsappassāsātissaṇu dharamasamasama nami kaitumālavicittakaṃ||

75.

 buddhuppādau kimaṅgaṃ atidulabhataraṃ ghausamattampi laukai,

tasmā nānappakāraṃ saparahitasukhaṃ viddhasū patthayantā|

yātiṭṭhatthāva haṃ taṃ suranarasaraṇaṃ antarāyappahānaṃ,

puñ‍ñakkhaittaikabhūtaṃ sugatamavirataṃ sādhu vandantu santau||

76.

 khaittavaraṅgatatthutipurai javapaṇama taijasā idha bhavai,

raugabhayādyupaddavahatau anunasukha bhaugapuñ‍ñamatikau|

daivamanussabhaugapavaraṃ paratthanubhāvañ‍ca antimabhavai,

añ‍ñatarau tibaudhipavarai bhavissati yathāsayaṃ katanatau||

77.

 puñ‍ñainānaina  sauhaṃ nipuṇajavamati paimavācau sakhīlau,

saddhau kalyāṇamittautisaraṇagamanau sīlavā cāgayaugau|

hirauttappī sudakkhau avitasucaritau dhitimā sac‍cabhāṇī,

bāhussac‍ci vibhāgi saparahitakarau vaggurāvau bhirupau||

78.

 dīghaj‍jīvi niraugau sucikulapassutau dhammarattau virattau,

nic‍cāpalyau katañ‍ñu atimudujumanau sādhubhāvādiviñ‍ñū|

dhammājīvau bhavaiyyaṃ bahukusalaratau appakaudhau aluddhau,

aivañ‍cañ‍ñaṃ karaiyyaṃ paṇidhi carimakai maukkhanibbānabhāgī||

79.

 (1) mahākathaṃ buddhaghausau, tanumaiva karaṃapi|

cajaṃ haiyyā diyā daiyyaṃ, akarittha yathā tathā||

80.

 (2) mahāpaṇāmapaurāṇaṃ, kiñ‍ci aiva punappunaṃ|

kāmauk‍kamaṃ dudhārañ‍ca, cajaṃ daiyyā diyañ‍ñattha||

81.

 (3) sutaj‍jaya anubhava-ṭṭhapanatthaina lañchinā|

sutaina garunānaina, katauyaṃ paṇāmau navau||

82.

 (4) aikakkharāya gāthāyau, yāva chabbisatakkharā|

jātiya paj‍jasattatyā, saṅkhatau caturādhikā||

83.

 (5) aṭṭhādhikā sahaivuyyau- janādīhi midaṃ nataṃ|

yathāvuttatthakā kāmā, yai nic‍caṃ dhārayantu tai||

84.

 rājātirājātimanauharau yau,

daivātidaivātiguṇaughadhārī|

brahmātibrahmātibhavantagū taṃ,

saṅghātisaṅghātivirāva vandai||

85.

 anaṅganaṅgaṃ naradaivadaivaṃ,

aniñ‍janiñ‍jaṃ bhayatāṇatāṇaṃ|

anaṇḍanaṇḍañ‍ca anāthanāthaṃ,

khayantayantaṃ paṇamāmi māmi||

86.

 tambasiniddhatuṅganakhakau nuṃvaṭṭasucituraṅguli ca munisau,

sīhusabhaubhahaṃsasamagau nigūḷasama gaupphakāyatamukhau|

kaumaladakkhāṇāvaṭatanuruhau sucimaluj‍jalābhasarirau,

pañ‍capasādayuttanayanau sugandhamukhatuṅganāsikayutau||

87.

 kauṭisahassanāgabalikau  surattamadharau suvaṭṭadasanau,

āyatasaṇulaumabhamūkau muduttanukaratta jivhasahitau|

chattasamānasaubhaṇasirau sukaisavara kaitumālavicitau,

ic‍canubyañ‍janaibhi sahitaṃ munindapavaraṃ namāmi sirasā||

88.

 sakalamalaihi sau muni sudūratāya ca malāriniṃ hatatayā,

tibhavarathai samānitamanārakāni ca namālayau naravarau|

malakaraṇai rahāraha manantañaiyya mabhijānanā muni tathā,

caraṇayutau tivij‍ji ca suvācatā sugamanā janaisu sugatau||

89.

 laukavidū sau nitalaukatayatā sākalatau asamanaradamā sau,

sārathi jinau anusāsanakarau satthavahau dupathataraṇasattā|

bujjhati sāmaṃ catusac‍camakhilaṃ baudhayi jantugaṇamiti ca buddhau,

 udisaka  caityakaipi vikiriya nāsati dha sau thirau sagaṇatau,

vijahiya puratau ttaravanaika kumbhakaragāmakaṃ nivasayai|

satagaṇakaihi tattha janakaupi ‘‘bhauta idha vāsa sabbayatinaṃ,

upaṭṭhahamī’’ti tamhi katipāhanaṃ vasati khau vimaṃsiya sukhaṃ||

(lalitagāthā)

ga.

 tattha arañ‍ñai ramitau sucari saddhamma-timāni suyatīhi tipīkau,

dhūrasuyuttau parigāhiya sujātau supaṭṭhāti kunadita-muyānaṃ|

sau satamac‍caihi kataṃ nagari daṃ tassa ca pācina raha dhikakausai,

pac‍cayanāyāsu da chāyabahukau suddhayatipi idha vasanakālai||

(tanugāthā)

gha.

 phagguṇamāsai chadinai raciya niṭṭhaṃva gatau paramari iminā-yaṃ,

sijjhatu paimaṃ vata rakkhatu sudaivau uda vaḍḍhatu jinavacanai taṃ|

vāsamālinī niṭṭhitā|
