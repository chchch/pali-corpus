




dāṭhāvaṃsau 

|| namau tassa bhagavatau arahatau sammāsambuddhassa||

1.

 visāradaṃ vādapathāti vattinaṃ,

tilaukapaj‍jautamasayhasāhinaṃ|

asaisa ñaiyyāvaraṇappahāyinaṃ,

namāmi satthāramanantagaucaraṃ||

2. 

 tilauka nāthappabhavaṃ bhayāpahaṃ,

visuddhavij‍jācaraṇaihi saivitaṃ|

papañ‍ca saṃyaujanabandhanacchidaṃ,

namāmi dhammaṃ nipuṇaṃ sududdasaṃ||

3.

 pasādamattaina’pi yattha pāṇinau,

phūsanti dukkhakkhayamac‍cutaṃ padaṃ|

tamāhuṇaiyyaṃ susamāhitindriyaṃ,

namāmi saṅghaṃ munirājasāvakaṃ||

4.

 vibhusayaṃ kāḷakanāgaranva yaṃ,

parak‍kamau kāruṇikau camūpati|

gavaisamānau jinasāsanassa yau,

virūḷhimatthañ‍ca janassa patthayaṃ||

5. 

 sudhāmayūkhāmala paṇḍuvaṃsajaṃ,

virūḷhasaddhaṃ munirājasāsanai|

piyaṃ vadaṃ nītipathānuvattiniṃ,

sadā pajānaṃ janikaṃ’va mātaraṃ||

6.

 piyaṃ parak‍kantibhujassa rājinau,

mahaisi mac‍cun‍natakhuddhisampadaṃ|

vidhāya līlāvatimicchitatthadaṃ,

asaisa laṅkātalaraj‍jalakkhiyaṃ||

7.

 kumāramārādhitasādhumantinaṃ,

mahādayaṃ paṇḍunarinda vaṃsajaṃ|

vidhāya saddhaṃ madhurindanāmakaṃ,

susikkhītaṃ pāvacanai kalāsu ca||

8. 

 narindasuñ‍ñaṃ suciraṃ tisīhaḷaṃ,

itippatītaṃ ayasaṃ apānudi|

ciraṃ paṇītaina ca civarādinā,

susañ‍ñatai saṃyaminau atappayi||

9.

 ciraṭṭhitiṃ pāvacanassa icchatā,

katañ‍ñunā vik‍kama buddisālinā|

satīmatā candima bandhukittinā,

sagāravaṃ taina’bhīyācitau ahaṃ||

10.

 sadaisa bhāsāya kavīhi sīhaḷai,

katampi vaṃsaṃ jinadantadhātuyā|

niruttiyā māgadhikāya vuddhiyā,

karaumi dīpantaravāsinaṃ api||

11. 

 jīnau’yamiddhai amaravhayai purai,

kadāci hutvāna sumaidhanāmakau|

savaidavaidaṅgavibhāgakauvidau,

mahaddhanai vippakulamhi māṇavau||

12.

 ahañhi jātibyasanaina pīḷitau,

jarābhibhutau maraṇaina autthaṭau|

sivaṃ padaṃ jātijarādinissaṭaṃ,

gavaisayissaṃ’ti rahau vicintiya||

13.

 anaikasaṅkhaṃ dhanadhañ‍ñasampadaṃ,

patiṭṭhapaitvā kapaṇaisi duc‍cajaṃ|

anappakai paimabharānubandhinau,

vihāya mittai ca sutaica bandhavai||

14. 

 pahāya kāmai nikhilai manauramai,

gharābhinikkhamma himācalantikai|

mahīdharaṃ dhammikanāma vissutaṃ,

upaic‍ca nānātarurājibhusitaṃ||

15.

 manaunukūlai surarājanimmītai,

asammigānaṃ agatimhi assamai|

nivattacīrau ajinakkhipaṃ vahaṃ,

jaṭādharau tāpasa vaisamaggahī||

16.

 susañ‍ñatattauparivāri tindriyau,

phalāphalādīhi pavattayaṃ tanuṃ|

gatau abhiñgñāsu ca pāramiṃ vasī,

tahiṃ samāpatti sukhaṃ avindi sau||

17. 

 susaj‍jitai rammapurādhivāsinā,

mahājanaina’ttamanaina añ‍jasai|

pathappadaisai abhiyantamattanau,

aniṭṭhitaiyaiva sumaidha tāpasau||

18.

 agādhañaiyyaudadhīpāradassinaṃ,

bhavantaguṃ nibbanathaṃ vināyakaṃ|

anaikakhīṇāsavalakkhasaivitaṃ,

kadā ci dīpaṅkarabuddhamaddasa||

19.

 tatau sasaṅghassa tilaukabhattunau,

paric‍cajitvāna tanumpi jīvitaṃ|

pasārayitvāna jaṭājinādikaṃ,

vidhāya saituṃ tanumaiva pal‍lalai||

20. 

 anak‍kamitvā kalalaṃ mahādayai,

sabhikkhukau gac‍catu piṭṭhiyā iti|

adhiṭṭhahitvāna nipan‍nakau tahiṃ,

anātha maitaṃ tibhavaṃ samaikkhiya||

21.

 dayāya sañ‍cauditamānasau janai,

bhavaṇṇavā uddharituṃ dukhadditai|

akāsi sambaudhipadassa pattiyā,

mahābhinīhāramudaggavik‍kamau||

22. 

 athau viditvā vasinau tamāsayaṃ,

adāsi sau vyākaraṇaṃ mahāmuni|

tatau puraṃ tamhi tathāgatai gatai,

sayaṃvasī sammasi pāramiguṇai||

23.

 tatau ca kappānamalīnavik‍kamau,

asaṃkhiyai sau caturau salakkhakai|

tahiṃ tahiṃ jātisu baudhipācanai,

visuddhasambhāraguṇai apūrayi|

athābhijātau tusitai mahāyasau,

visuddhasambaudhipadaupaladdhiyā|

udikkhamānau samayaṃ dayādhanau,

ciraṃ vibhutiṃ anubhausi sabbasau||

25. 

 sahassasaṅkhā dasacak‍kavālatau,

samāgatānaikasurādhipādihi|

udaggudaggaihi jinattapattiyā,

sagāravaṃ sau abhigamma yācitau||

26.

 tatau cavitvā kapiḷavhayai purai,

sadā satau sakyakulaikakaitunau|

ahausi suddhaudanabhumibhattunau,

mahādimāyāya mahaisiyā sutau||

27. 

 vijātamattau’va vasundharāya sau,

patiṭṭhahitvāna disā vilaukayī|

tadā ahaisuṃ vivaṭaṅganā disā,

apūjayuṃ tattha ca daivamānusā||

28.

 ādhārayuṃ ātapavāraṇādikaṃ,

adissamānāva nabhambhi daivatā|

padāni sau satta ca uttarāmukhau,

upaic‍ca nicchārayi vācamāsabhiṃ||

29.

 yathatthasiddhattakumāranāmakau,

mahabbalau yaubbanahāriviggahau|

ututtayānucchavikaisu tīsu sau,

nubhausi pāsādavaraisu sampadaṃ||

30. 

 kadāci uyyānapathai jarāhataṃ,

tathāturaṃ kālakatañ‍ca saṃyamiṃ|

kamaina disvāna virattamānasau,

bhavaisu sau pabbajituṃ akāmayi||

31.

 sapupphadīpādīkaraihi rattiyaṃ,

purakkhatau sau tidivādhivāsihi|

sachan‍nakau kantakavājiyānatau,

tatau mahākāruṇikau’bhinikkhami||

32.

 kamaina patvāna anaumamāpagaṃ,

sudhautamuttāphalahārisaikatai|

patiṭṭhahitvā varamauḷibandhanaṃ,

sitāsilūnaṃ gaganai samukkhipī||

33. 

 paṭiggahaitvā tidasānamissarau,

suvaṇnacaṅgauṭavaraina taṃ tadā|

tiyaujanaṃ nīlamaṇīhi caitiyaṃ,

akāsi cūḷāmaṇimattanau purai||

34.

 tatau ghaṭīkārasaraujayauninā,

samāhaṭaṃ dhārayī cīvarādikaṃ|

athau sakaṃ vatthayugaṃ nabhatthalai,

pasatthavaisaggahaṇau samukkhipī||

35.

 paṭiggahaitvāna tamambujāsanau,

mahiddhikau bhattibharaina cauditau|

sakai bhavai dvādasayaujanaṃ akā,

maṇīhi nīlādihi dussacaitiyaṃ||

36. 

 susañ‍ñatattau satimā jitindriyau,

vinītavaisau rasagaidhavaj‍jitau|

chahāyanānaiva anaumavik‍kamau,

mahāpadhānaṃ padahittha duk‍karaṃ||

37.

 visākhamāsassatha puṇṇamāsiyaṃ,

upaic‍ca mūlaṃ sahajāya baudhiyā|

tiṇāsanai cuddasahatthasammitai,

adhiṭṭhahitvā vīriyaṃ nisaj‍ji sau||

38.

 avattharantiṃ vasudhaṃca ambaraṃ,

virūpavaisaggahaṇaina bhiṃsanaṃ|

pakampayantau sadharādharaṃ mahiṃ,

jinau padausai’jini māravāhiṇiṃ||

39. 

 surāsurabrahmagaṇaihi saj‍jitai,

jagattayai pupphamayagaghikādinā|

pavattamānai suradundubhissarai,

abujjhibaudhiṃ rajanīparikkhayai||

40.

 tadā pakampiṃsu sasailakānanā,

sahassasaṃkhā dasalaukadhātuyau|

agañ‍ji sau lauṇapayaudhi sādhutaṃ,

mahāvabhāsau bhuvanaisu pattharī||

41.

 labhiṃsu andhā vimalai vilaucanai,

suṇiṃsu saddai badhirāpi jātiyā|

lapiṃsu mūgā vacanaina vaggunā,

cariṃsu khailaṃ padasā’va paṅgulā||

42.

 bhaviṃsu khuj‍jā ujusaummaviggahā,

sikhī’pi nibbāyi avīciādisu|

apāgamuṃ bandhanatau’pi jantavau,

khudādikaṃ paitabhavā apak‍kamī||

43. 

 samiṃsu raugavyasanāni pāṇinaṃ,

bhayaṃ tirac‍cānagatai na pīḷayī|

janā ahaisuṃ sakhilā piyaṃvadā,

pavattayuṃ kauñ‍canadaṃ mataṅgajā||

44.

 hayā ca haisiṃsu pahaṭṭhamānasā,

nadiṃsu sabbā sayamaiva dundubhī|

raviṃsu daihābharaṇāni pāṇinaṃ,

disā pasīdiṃsu samā samantatau||

45.

 pavāyi mandau sukhasītamārutau,

pavassi maighau’pi akālasambavau|

jahiṃsu ākāsagatiṃ vihaṅgamā,

mahiṃ samubhij‍jajalaṃ samuṭṭhahī||

46.

 asandamānā’va ṭhitā savantiyau,

nabhai virauciṃsu asaisajautiyau|

bhavā ahaisuṃ vivaṭā samantatau,

janassa nāsuṃ vacanūpapattiyau||

47. 

 samaikkhataṃ nāvaraṇā nagādayau,

pavāyi gandhau api dibbasammatau|

dumā ahaisuṃ phalapuppha dhārinau,

ahausi chan‍nau kamalaihi aṇṇavau||

48.

 thalaisu tauyaisu ca pupphamānakā,

vicittapupphā vikasiṃsu sabbathā|

nirantaraṃ pupphasugandhavuṭṭhiyā,

ahausi sabbaṃ vasudhambaran‍naraṃ||

49.

 nisaj‍ja pal‍laṅkavarai tahiṃ jinau,

sukhaṃ samāpattivihārasambhavaṃ|

tatau’nubhauntau sucirābhipatthitaṃ,

dināni sattaiva atik‍kamāpayī||

50. 

 samuppatitvā gagaṇaṅganaṃ tatau,

padassayitvā yamakaṃ mahāmuni|

sapāṭihīraṃ tidivādhivāsinaṃ,

jinattanai saṃsayitaṃ nirākari||

51.

 athautaritvāna jayāsanassa sau,

ṭhitau’va pubbuttarakaṇṇanissitau|

dināni sattānimisaina cakkhunā,

tamāsanaṃ baudhitaruṃca’pūjayi||

52.

 atha’ntarāḷai maṇicaṅkamai jinau,

ṭhitappadaisassa ca āsanassa ca|

mahārahai daivavarābhinimmitai,

dināni sattaiva akāsi vaṅkamaṃ||

53. 

 tatau disāyaṃ aparāya baudhiyā,

upāvisitvā ratanālayai jinau|

samantapaṭṭhānanayaṃ vicintayaṃ,

dināni sattaiva savītināmayi||

54.

 viniggatau satthusarīratau tadā,

jutippabandhau paṭibandhavaj‍jitau|

pamāṇasuñ‍ñāsu ca laukadhātusu,

samantatau uddhamadhau ca pattharī||

55.

 vaṭassa mūlai ajapālasañ‍ñinau,

sukhaṃ phusantau pavivaikasambhavaṃ|

vināyakau satta vihāsi vāsarai,

anantadassī surarājapūjitau||

56. 

 vihāsi mūlai mucalindasākhinau,

nisaj‍ja bhaugāvalimandiraudarai|

vikiṇṇapupphai mucalindabhauginau,

samādhinā vāsarasattakaṃ jinau||

57.

 dumai’pi rājāyatanai samādhinā,

vihāsi rattindiva sattakaṃ muni|

sahassanaittau atha dantapauṇakaṃ,

mukhaudakañ‍cāpi adāsi satthunau||

58.

 tatau mahārājavaraihi ābhataṃ,

silāmayaṃ patta catuk‍kamaikakaṃ|

vidhāya matthaṃ madhupiṇḍikaṃ tahiṃ,

paṭiggahaitvāna savāṇijāhaṭā||

59. 

 katan‍nakic‍cau saraṇaisu tai ubhau,

patiṭṭhapaitvāna tapassubhal‍likai|

adāsi taisaṃ abhipūjituṃ sakaṃ,

parāmasitvāna siraṃ sirauruhai||

60.

 vaṭassamulai ajapālasañ‍ñinau,

sahampatībrahmavaraina yācitau|

janassa kātuṃ varadhammasaṅgahaṃ,

agañchi bārāṇasimaikakau muni||

61. 

 gantvā sau dhammarājā vanamisipatanaṃ sañ‍ñatānaṃ nikaitaṃ,

pal‍laṅkasmiṃ nisin‍nau tahimavicalitaṭṭhānasampāditamhi|

āsāḷhai puṇṇamāyaṃ sitaruciruciyā jautitai cak‍kavāḷai,

daivabrahmādikānaṃ duritamalaharaṃ vattayi dhammacak‍kaṃ||

62.

 sutvā saddhammamaggaṃ tibhūvanakuharābhaugavitthārikaṃ taṃ,

añ‍ñākauṇḍañ‍ñanāmadvijamunipamukhāṭṭhārasabrahmakauṭī|

añ‍ñāsuṃ maggadhammaṃ parimitarahitai cak‍kavāḷai uḷārau,

aubhāsau pātubhutau sapadi bahuvidhaṃ āsi acchairakañ‍ca||

paṭhamau paricchaidau|

63. 

 tatau paṭṭhāya sau satthā vinaintau daivamānusai,

baudhitau phussamāsamhi navamai puṇṇamāsiyaṃ||

64.

 laṅkamāgamma gaṅgāya tīrai yaujanavitthatai,

mahānāgavanuyyānai āyāmaina tiyaujanai||

65.

 yakkhānaṃ samitiṃ gantvā ṭhatvāna gaganai tahiṃ|

vātandhakāravuṭṭhihi katvā yakkhai bhayadditai||

66.

 laddhābhayaihi yakkhaihi taihi din‍nāya bhumiyā|

cammakhaṇḍaṃ pasāraitvā nisīditvāna taṅkhaṇai||

67.

 cammakhaṇḍaṃ padittaggi jālāmālāsamākulaṃ|

iddhiyā vaḍḍhayitvāna yāva sindhuṃ samantatau||

68. 

 javaina sindhuvailāya rāsibhutai nisācarai|

giridīpamidhānaitvā patiṭṭhāpaisi tai tahiṃ||

69.

 daisayitvā jinau dhammaṃ tadā daivasamāgamai|

bahun‍naṃ pāṇakauṭīnaṃ dhammābhisamayaṃ akā||

70.

 mahāsumanadaivassa sailai sumanakūṭakai|

datvā namassituṃ kaisai agā jaitavanaṃ jinau||

71.

 patiṭṭhapaitvā tai satthu nisin‍nāsanabhumiyaṃ|

indanīlamayaṃ thūpaṃ karitvā sau apūjayi||

72.

 nissāya maṇipal‍laṅkaṃ pabbataṇṇavavāsinau|

disvā yuddhatthikai nāgai cūḷaudara mahaudarai||

73.

 baudhitau pañ‍camai vassai cittamāsai mahāmuni|

upausathai kālapakkhai nāgadīpamupāgamī||

74. 

 tadā samiddhisumanau daivau jaitavanai ṭhitaṃ|

attanau bhavanaṃyaiva rājāyatanapādapaṃ||

75.

 indanīladdikūṭaṃ’va gahaitvātuṭṭhamānasau|

dhārayitvā sahāgañchi chattaṃ katvāna satthunau||

76.

 ubhin‍naṃ nāgarājūnaṃ vattamānai mahāhavai|

nisin‍nau gaganai nāthau māpayittha mahātamaṃ||

77.

 ālaukaṃ dassayitvātha assāsaitvāna bhauginau|

sāmaggikaraṇaṃ dhammaṃ abhāsi purisāsabhau||

78.

 asītikauṭiyau nāgā acalambudhivāsinau|

patiṭṭhahiṃsu muditā sīlaisu saraṇaisu ca||

79. 

 datvāna maṇipal‍laṅkaṃ satthunau bhujagādhipā|

tatthāsīnaṃ mahāvīraṃ an‍napānaihi tappayuṃ||

80.

 patiṭṭhapaitvā sau tattha rājāyatanapādapaṃ|

pal‍laṅkaṃ tañ‍ca nāgānaṃ adāsi abhipūjituṃ||

81.

 baudhitau aṭṭhamai vassai vaisākhai puṇṇamāsiyaṃ|

maṇiakkhikanāmaina nāgindaina nimantitau||

82.

 nāgarājassa tassaiva bhavanaṃ sādhu saj‍jītaṃ|

kalyāṇiyaṃ pañ‍cabhikkhū sataihi saha āgami||

83.

 kalyāṇicaitiyaṭṭhānai katai ratana maṇḍapai|

mahārahamhi pal‍laṅkai upāvisi narāsabhau||

84.

 dibbaihi khaj‍jabhauj‍jaihi sasaṅghaṃ laukanāyakaṃ|

santappaisi phaṇindau sau bhujaṅgaihi purakkhatau||

85. 

 daisayitvāna saddhammaṃ saggamaukkhasukhāvahaṃ|

sau satthā sumanai kūṭai dassaisi padalañchanaṃ||

86.

 tatau pabbatapādamhi sasaṅghau sau vināyakau|

divāvihāraṃ katvāna dīghavāpiṃ upāgami||

87.

 thūpaṭṭhānai tahiṃ buddhau sasaṅghau’bhinisīdiya|

samāpatti samubabhutaṃ avindi asamaṃ sukhaṃ||

88.

 mahābaudhitaruṭṭhānai samādhiṃ appayi jinau|

mahāthūpappadaisai ca viharittha samādhinā||

89.

 thūpārāmamhi thūpassa ṭhānai jhānasukhaina sau|

sabhikkhusaṅghau sambuddhau muhuttaṃ vītināmayī||

90. 

 silāthupappadaisamhi ṭhatvākālāvidū muni|

daivai samanusāsitvā tatau jaitavanaṃ agā||

91.

 agiddhau lābhasak‍kārai asayhamavamānanaṃ|

sahantau kaivalaṃ sabba laukanittharaṇatthikau||

92.

 saṃvaccharāni ṭhatvāna cattāḷīsañ‍ca pañ‍ca ca|

daisayitvāna suttādi navaṅgaṃ satthusāsanaṃ||

93.

 tāraitvā bhavakantārā janai saṅkhyātivattinau|

buddhakic‍cāni sabbāni niṭṭhāpaitvāna cakkhumā||

94. 

 kusinārāpurai rañ‍ñaṃ mal‍lānamupavattanai|

sālavanamhi yamakasālarukkhānamantarai||

95.

 mahārahai supañ‍ñattai mañ‍cai uttarasīsakaṃ|

nipan‍nau sīhasaiyyāya vaisākhai puṇṇamāsiyaṃ||

96.

 daisaitvā paṭhamai yāmai mal‍lānaṃ dhammamuttamaṃ|

subhaddaṃ majjhimai yāmai pāpaitvā amataṃ padaṃ||

97.

 bhikkhu pacchimayāmamhi dhammakkhandhai asaisakai|

saṅgayha auvaditvāna appamāda padaina ca||

98.

 pac‍cusasamayai jhānasamāpattivihāratau|

uṭṭhāya parinibbāyi saisaupādivivaj‍jitau||

99. 

 mahīkampādayau āsuṃ tadā acchariyāvahā|

pūjāvisaisā vattiṃsu daivamānusakā bahū||

100.

 parinibbāṇasuttantai vuttānuk‍kamatau pana|

pūjāvisaisau viñ‍ñaiyyau icchantaihi asaisatau||

101.

 ahataihi ca vatthaihi vaiṭhaitvā paṭhamaṃ jinaṃ|

vaiṭhayitvāna kappāsapicunā vihataina ca||

102.

 aivaṃ pañ‍casatakkhattuṃ vaiṭhayitvāna sādhukaṃ|

pakkhipitvā suvaṇṇāya tailapuṇṇāya dauṇiyā||

103.

 vīsaṃhatthasatubbaidhaṃ gandhadārūhi saṅkhataṃ|

āraupayiṃsu citakaṃ mal‍lānaṃ pamukhā tadā||

104.

 mahākassapathairaina dhammarājai avanditai|

citakaṃ mā jalitthāti daivadhiṭṭhānatau pana||

105. 

 pāmaukkhā mal‍larājūnaṃ vāyamantaupa’naikadhā|

citakaṃ taṃ na sakkhiṃsu gāhāpaituṃ hutāsanaṃ||

106.

 mahākassapathairaina adhiṭṭhānaina attanau|

vatthādīni mahādauṇiṃ citakañ‍ca mahārahaṃ||

107.

 dvidhā katvāna nikkhamma sakasīsai patiṭṭhitā|

vanditā satthunau pādā yathāṭṭhānai patiṭṭhitā||

108.

 tatau daivānubhāvaina paj‍jalittha citānalau|

na masi satthudaihassa daḍḍhassāsi na chārikā||

109.

saddhādayādhikaguṇābharaṇābhirāmā,

taṃ sak‍kariṃsu bahudhā jinadantadhātuṃ||

408.

 satthārā sambhatatthaṃ purimatarabhavai sampajānaṃ pajānaṃ,

sambaudhiṃ tassa sabbāsavavigamakariṃ saddahantau’dahantau|

sautaṃ tassa’ggadhammai nipuṇamati sataṃ saṅgamaisaṅgamaisaṃ,

nibbāṇaṃ santamicchai tibhavabhayaparic‍cāgahaituṃ gahaituṃ||

pañ‍camau paricchaidau|

409. 

 yau candagaumi racitaivarasaddasatthai,

ṭīkaṃ pasatthamakarittha ca pañ‍cikāya|

buddhappabhāvajana niṃca akā samanta,

pāsādikāya vinayaṭṭhakathāya ṭīkaṃ||

410.

 aṅguttarāgamavaraṭṭhakathāya ṭīkaṃ,

sammaubhavibbhama vighātakariṃ akāsi|

atthāya saṃyamigaṇassa padhānikassa,

ganthaṃ akā vinayasaṅgahanāmadhaiyyaṃ||

411.

 santindriyassapaṭipatti parāyaṇassa,

sal‍laikha vutti niratassa samāhitassa|

appicchatādi guṇayauga vibhusanassa,

sambuddhasāsanamahaudayakāraṇassa||

412. 

 sabbaisuācariyataṃ paramaṃ gatassa,

satthaisu sabbasamayantara kauvidassa|

sissainasāritanujassa mahādisāmi||

413.

 suddhanvayaina karuṇādiguṇaudayaina,

tak‍kāgamādi kusalaina visāradaina|

sabbattha patthaṭa sudhākararaṃsijāla,

saṅkāsakittivisaraina parikkhakaina||

414. 

 saddhādhanaina sakhilaina ca dhammakitti,

nāmaina rājagarunā cariyaina aisau|

sautuppasādajananau jinadantadhātu,

vaṃsau katau nikhiladassipabhāvadīpau||

415.

 dhammau pavattatu cirāya munissarassa,

dhammai ṭhitā vasumatīpatayau bhavantu|

kālai pavassatu ghanau nikhilā pajā’pi,

añ‍ñauñ‍ñamaittipaṭilābhasukhaṃ labhantu||
