




|| namau tassa bhagavatau arahatau sammāsambuddhassa||

nītimañ‍jarī

1.

 kulakkhayai  vinassanti,

kuladhammā sanantanā|

dhammai naṭṭhai kulaṃ sabbaṃ,

adhammau abhibhū khalaṃ||

2.

 adhammābhibhavā dantā,

padussanti kulitthiyau|

thīsu duṭṭhā sva dhammaina,

jāyatai vaṇṇasaṅkarau||

3.

 piyaṃ  bhāsai guṇaggāhau,

sūrau siyā vikantanau|

dātā candasamā nārī,

diṭṭhaṃ diṭṭhaṃ nahāsayai||

4.

 kutautthi  kumittai sac‍caṃ,

kudārai rativaḍḍhanaṃ|

kudaisamhi manau rammaṃ,

kurājai bhaugasampadaṃ||

saṅkaitaiva  amittasmiṃ,

mittasmiṃ pi navissasai|

abhayā bhaya muppan‍naṃ,

api mūlāni kantati||

adiṭṭhauva  parau saiyyau,

dummittau nau vissāsikau||

aggihaumaphalaṃ  vaidau,

satthaṃsīlaphalaṃ mataṃ|

ratiputtaphalaṃ nārī,

dānabhuttiphalaṃ dhanaṃ||

asac‍caṃ  sāhasaṃ māyā,

mūḷhatta ma tilaubhatā|

asaucaṃ niddayattañ‍ca,

thīnaṃ dausā sabhāvajā||

jāyāya  bhattunau bhārau,

sissaina gurunau katau|

amac‍cakaihi rājassa,

pitarānaṃ nijainaca||

5.

 uyyamaina  hi sijjhanti,

kammāni na manaurathā|

na hi suttassa sīhassa,

pavīsanti mukhai migā||

atisītaṃ  atiuṇhaṃ,

atisāyamidaṃ ahu|

iti visaṭṭhakammantai,

khaṇā ac‍cainti māṇavai||

ādānassa padānassa,

kattabbassa ca kammunau|

khippaṃ akayyamānassa,

kālai pivati sampadaṃ||

nādabbai  nihitā kāci,

kriyā phalavatī bhavai|

nabyāpārasataināpi,

sukauva pāṭhatai bakau||

yau  dandhakālai tarati,

taraṇīyai ca dandhayai|

sukkhapaṇṇaṃ va ak‍kamma,

atthaṃ bhañ‍jati attanau||

6.

 yaṃ  dadāti yaṃ bhuñ‍jati,

tadaiva dhaninau dhanaṃ|

añ‍ñai matassa kīḷanti,

dāraihipi dhanaihipi||

dānaupabhaugahīnaina,

dhanaina dhaninau sukhaṃ|

kau visaisau daliddassa,

adhikaṃ dhanarakkhaṇaṃ||

nijasaukhyaṃ  nirundhantau,

nīcabhaugau mitampacau|

dhanaṃ sañ‍cayatai yau sau,

parabhāravahau pasu||

yaṃ  ussukā saṅkharaunti,

alakkhikā bahuṃ dhanaṃ|

sippavantau asippāvā,

lakkhi vā tāni bhuñ‍jati||

7.

 sampatyaṃ  mahataṃ cittaṃ,

bhavai uppalai kaumalaṃ|

vipatyaṃca mahāsaila,

silāsaṅghātakak‍kasaṃ||

8.

 asambhabyaguṇaṃ  thutvā,

khaidau mudhāva jāyatai|

avhāyaṃ canda mu l‍laukya,

nacandauta mu pāgamī||

9.

 sac‍caṃ  mukhamhi dhāraiyya,

kaṇṇai sutaṃ bhujai jayaṃ|

hadayamhi khamaṃ vīraṃ,

laukādāsaṃca laucanai||

saddamattaṃ  naphandaiyya,

añ‍ñatvā saddakāraṇaṃ|

saddahaituṃ pariñ‍ñāya,

pamaudau vā bhayau tathā||

sabbasuta  ma dhīyaiyya,

hīnamuk‍kaṭṭhamajjhimaṃ||

10.

 dun‍nāriyā  kulaṃ suddhaṃ,

puttau nassati lālanā|

samiddhi anayā bandhu,

pavāsā madanā hirī||

lālayai  pañ‍cavassāni,

dasavassāni tālayai|

pattaitu sauḷasaivassai,

puttaṃ mittaṃva ācarai||

lālanai bahavau dausā,

lālanai bahavau guṇā||

pāpā  nivārayati yaujayatai hitāya,

guyhāni gūhati guṇaṃ pakaṭīkarauti|

āpattikañ‍ca najahāti dadāti kālai,

sammitta lakkhaṇamidaṃ pavadanti santau||

11.

 duj‍janau  jīyatai yutyā,

niggahaina nadhīmatā|

nipātyatai mahārukkhau,

tassamīpa khatikkhayā||

vanai  migāca luddhānaṃ,

duj‍janānañ‍ca saj‍janā|

akāraṇavairī haunti,

tiṇabhakkhā supaisalā||

pādalaggaṃ  karaṭṭhaina,

kaṇḍakainaiva kaṇḍakaṃ||

bālaṃ  napassai nasuṇai,

nacabālaina saṃvasai|

bālaināl‍lāpasal‍lāpaṃ,

nakarai nacaraucayai||

12.

 upa kattuṃ yathā khuddau,

samatthau natathāmahā|

kūpau hi hanti pipāsaṃ,

natu pāyau mahambudhi||

13.

 ādānassa  padānassa,

kattabbassaca kammunau|

khippaṃ akaramānassa,

kālau bhakkhati taṃ rasaṃ||

nakkhattaṃ  paṭimānaintaṃ,

atthau bālaṃ upajjhagā|

atthau atthassa nakkhattaṃ,

kiṃ karissanti tārakā||

ajarāmarauva  pañ‍ñau,

vij‍jamatthañ‍ca cintayai|

gahitauviya kaisaisu,

mac‍cunā dhammamācarai||

14.

 vaj‍jā gurūca mantīca,

tayau raṭṭhābhisaṅkhatā|

jīvīta dakkha kausānaṃ,

vaḍḍhanā nāsanāca tai||

15.

 thiraina  kammaṃ vaḍḍhati,

athiraina turaina nau|

phalanti samayai rukkhā,

sittāpi bahuvārinā||

vāyāmaithaiva  purisau,

nanibbindaiyya paṇḍitau||

payatanau  tādisau naiva,

kayyau yaina phalaṃ nahi|

sailaggai kūpakhaṇanā,

kathaṃ tauyasamāgamau||

ñāṇaṅkusaina  sammaggaṃ,

niyyatyussāhakuñ‍jarau||

asamaikkhitakammantaṃ,

turitābhi nipātinaṃ|

tānikammāni tappainti,

uṇhaṃ va jjhauhitaṃ mukhai||

16.

 chaddausā  purisainaiha,

hātabbā bhūtimicchantā|

niddā maj‍jaṃ bhayaṃ kaudhau,

ālasyaṃ dīghasuttatā||

na  divā suppasīlaina,

rattimuṭṭhānadaissinā|

nic‍casauṇḍaina mattaina,

sak‍kā āvasituṃ gharaṃ||

abhaitabbamhi  bhāyanti,

bhāyitabbai nabhāyarai|

bhayābhaya vimuḷhā tai,

jimhānugā ujuñ‍jahā||

yassa  manussabhūtassa,

natthi bhaugāca sippakaṃ|

kiṃ phalaṃ tassa mānussaṃ,

dvipādaṭṭhau hi sau migau||

17.

 nānaupāyauva  kattabbau,

sacai bhavaiyya attanau|

atthasiddhi yathākāmaṃ,

upāyau hi hitañ‍jasau||

lañ‍jadānabālisaina,

kūṭaḍḍakāradhīvarā|

vinicchayamahāmacchaṃ,

auṭṭainti laubhasāgarai||

yassaitai caturau dhammā,

vānarinda yathātava|

sac‍caṃ dhammau dhīti cāgau,

diṭṭhaṃ sau ativattati||

18.

 vidvāca  ratanaṃ nārī,

vīṇā sātthaṃ giraṃmahī|

guṇavisaisa māgamma,

guṇāni aguṇānica||

dhanavā  balavā laukai,

dhanā bhavati paṇḍitau||

sumanai  nissitau kīṭau,

nigguṇau hīnakau sayaṃ|

taṃ pupphaihi maṇḍaintānaṃ,

rañ‍ñaṃ siraupi rauhati||

alakkhikaihi  sañ‍cītā,

dhanabhaugāca cintitā|

lakkhikassa bhavantaitai,

lakkhivā suṭṭhubhuñ‍jati||

khattiyau  saiṭṭhau janai tasmiṃ,

yau gauttapaṭisārinau|

vij‍jācaraṇasampan‍nau,

sau saiṭṭhau daivamānusai||

visāpi  amataṃ gaṇhai,

gūthatau maṇimuttamaṃ|

kaṇṭakapādapā pupphaṃ,

thirataṃ duk‍kulā varaṃ||

dhanissarādiguṇaummi -

vaigaina vāhitā pajā||

19.

 yassa tthi satataṃ maittā,

sabbalaukasuval‍labhā|

kūpāyatai samuddaupi,

aggi tassa jalāyatai||

20.

 sakkharāyati mairūpi,

visabhakkhau sudhāyatai|

sasāyatai migarāja,

byālau mālāguṇāyatai|

daulāyatai chamācālau,

nānāvudhā tiṇāyarai||

21.

 samaiva  sati ussāhai,

sukhavāhau hitaṅkarau|

ūnai-dhikai tathā nauhi,

majjhagau sādhu sabbadā||

sādhu  khau paṇḍitaunāma,

natvaiva atipaṇḍitau||
