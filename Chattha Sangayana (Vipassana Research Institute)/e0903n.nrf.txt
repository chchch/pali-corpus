saṃyuttanikāyai

sagāthāvaggapāḷi

saṃgāyanassa pucchā vissaj‍janā

pucchā – paṭhamamahāsaṃgītikālai  āvusau dhammasaṃgāhakā mahākassapādayau mahāthairavarā paurāṇasaṃgītikārā paṭhamaṃ vinayapiṭakaṃ saṃgāyitvā tadanantaraṃ suttantapiṭakaṃ saṃgāyantā dīghanikāyañ‍ca majjhimanikāyañ‍ca saṃgāyitvā tadanantaraṃ kiṃnāma pāvacanaṃ saṃgāyiṃsu|

vissaj‍janā – paṭhamamahāsaṃgītikālai bhantai dhammasaṃgāhakā mahākassapādayau paurāṇathairavarā paṭhamaṃ vinayapiṭakaṃ saṃgāyitvā tadanantaraṃ suttantapiṭakaṃ saṃgāyantā dīghanikāyañ‍ca majjhimanikāyañ‍ca saṃgāyitvā tadanantaraṃ sattahi ca suttasahassaihi sattahi ca suttasataihi dvāsaṭṭhiyā ca suttaihi paṭimaṇḍitaṃ bhāṇavārasataparimāṇaṃ saṃyuttanikāyaṃ nāma pāvacanaṃ saṃgāyiṃsu|

pucchā – saṃyuttanikāyai  ca āvusau sagāthāvaggau nidānavaggau khandhavaggau saḷāyatanavaggau mahāvaggauti pañ‍casaṃyuttappakaraṇāni, taisu paṭhamaṃ kataraṃ saṃyuttappakaraṇaṃ tai saṃgāyiṃsu|

vissaj‍janā – pañ‍casu bhantai saṃyuttappakaraṇaisu paṭhamaṃ sagāthāvaggasaṃyuttappakaraṇaṃ saṃgāyiṃsu|

pucchā – sagāthāvaggaipi  āvusau daivatāsaṃyuttādivasaina aikādasasaṃyuttāni, taisu paṭhamaṃ kataraṃ saṃyuttaṃ tai saṃgāyiṃsu|

vissaj‍janā – aikādasasu bhantai saṃyuttaisu paṭhamaṃ daivatāsaṃyuttaṃ saṃgāyiṃsu|

pucchā – daivatāsaṃyuttaipi āvusau naḷavaggādayau aṭṭha vaggā, aughataraṇasuttādīni ca aikāsīti suttāni, taisu paṭhamaṃ kataraṃ vaggaṃ katarañ‍ca suttaṃ sagāyiṃsu|

vissaj‍janā – aṭṭhasu bhantai vaggaisu paṭhamaṃ naḷavaggaṃ aikāsītiyā ca suttaisu paṭhamaṃ aughataraṇasuttaṃ saṃgāyiṃsu|

sādhu  sādhu āvusau, mayampi dāni tatauyaiva paṭṭhāya saṃgītipubbaṅgamāni pucchanavissaj‍janakic‍cāni āvahituṃ samārabhāra|

daivatāsaṃyutta

aughataraṇasutta

pucchā – taināvusau bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhaina aughataraṇasuttaṃ kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai añ‍ñataraṃ daivataṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ, añ‍ñatarā bhantai daivatā abhik‍kantāya rattiyā abhik‍kantavaṇṇā kaivalakappaṃ jaitavanaṃ aubhāsaitvā yaina bhagavā tainupasaṅkami, upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ aṭṭhāsi, aikamantaṃ ṭhitā khau sā daivatā bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca ‘‘kathaṃ nu tvaṃ mārisa aughamatarī’’ti,

tasmiṃ  bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘apatiṭṭhaṃ khvāhaṃ āvusau anāyūhaṃ aughamatariṃ’’ti, aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai añ‍ñataraṃ daivataṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| añ‍ñatarā bhantai daivatā bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca–

‘‘ac‍cainti kālā tarayanti rattiyau,

vayauguṇā anupubbaṃ jahanti,

aitaṃ bhayaṃ maraṇai paikkhamānau,

puñ‍ñāni kayirātha sukhāvahānī’’ti–

tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ –

‘‘ac‍cainti kālā tarayanti rattiyau,

vayauguṇā anupubbaṃ jahanti,

aitaṃ bhayaṃ maraṇai paikkhamānau,

laukāmisaṃ pajahai santipaikkhau’’ti –

aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

accharāsutta

pucchā – accharāsuttaṃ panāvusau bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai añ‍ñataraṃ daivaputtaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| añ‍ñatarau bhantai daivaputtau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca–

‘‘accharāgaṇasaṅghaṭṭhaṃ, pisāca gaṇasaivitaṃ|

vanantaṃ mauhanaṃ nāma, kathaṃ yātrā bhavissatī’’ti||

tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ–

‘‘ujukau nāma sau maggau, abhayā nāma sā disā,

rathau akūjanau nāma, dhammacak‍kaihi saṃyutau||

hirī  tassa apālambau, satyassa parivāraṇaṃ|

dhammāhaṃ sārathiṃ brūmi, sammādiṭṭhi puraijavaṃ||

yassa aitādisaṃ yānaṃ, itthiyā purisassa vā|

sa vai aitaina yānaina, nibbānassaiva santikai’’ti||

aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

accharāgaṇasaṅghuṭṭhaṃ, pisācagaṇasaivitaṃ|

vanantaṃ mauhanaṃ nāma, kathaṃ yātrā bhavissati–

pucchā – taināvusau  bhagavatā…pai॰… sammāsambuddhaina yakkhasaṃyuttai āḷavakasuttaṃ kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – āḷaviyaṃ bhantai āḷavakassa yakkhassa bhavanai āḷavakaṃ yakkhaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| āḷavakau bhantai yakkhau bhagavantaṃ gāthāya ajjhabhāsi–

kiṃ sūdhavittaṃ purisassa saiṭṭhaṃ,

kiṃ su suciṇṇaṃ sukhamāvahāti|

kiṃ su havai sādutaraṃ rasānaṃ,

kathaṃ jīviṃ jīvitāmāhu saiṭṭhanti||

tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ–

saddhīca vittaṃ purisassa saiṭṭhaṃ,

dhammau suciṇṇau sukhamāvahāti|

sac‍caṃ havai sādutaraṃ rasānaṃ,

pañ‍ñājīviṃ jīvitamāhu saiṭṭhanti||

aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

na  khvāhaṃ taṃ āvusau nikkhamissāmi, yaṃ tai karaṇīyaṃ, taṃ karauhi,

pañhaṃ taṃ samaṇa pucchissāmi, sacai mai na byākarissasi, cittaṃ vā tai khipissāmi, hadayaṃ vā tai phālaissāmi, pādaisu vā gahaitvā pāragaṅgāya khipissāmi–

kiṃ  sūkha vittaṃ purisassa saiṭṭhaṃ, kiṃ su suciṇṇaṃ sukhamāvahāti|

kiṃ su havai sādubharaṃ rasānaṃ, kathaṃ jīviṃ jīvitamāhu saiṭṭhaṃ–

saddhīdha vittaṃ purisassa saiṭṭhaṃ, dhammau suciṇṇau sukhamāvahāti|

sac‍caṃ havai sādutaraṃ rasānaṃ, pañ‍ñājīviṃ jīvitamāhu saiṭṭhaṃ|

kathaṃsu  tarati aughaṃ, kathaṃsu tarati aṇṇavaṃ|

kathaṃsu dukkha mac‍caiti, kathaṃsu parisujjhati||

saddhāya tarati aughaṃ, appamādaina aṇṇavaṃ|

vīriyaina dukkha mac‍caiti, pañ‍ñāya parisujjhati–

kathaṃsu  labhatai pañ‍ñaṃ, kathaṃsu vindatai dhanaṃ|

kathaṃsu kittiṃ pappauti, kathaṃ mittāni ganthati|

asmā laukā paraṃ laukaṃ, kathaṃ paic‍ca na saucati–

saddahānau arahataṃ, dhammaṃ nibbānapattiyā|

sussūsaṃ labhatai pañ‍ñaṃ, appamattau vicakkhaṇau|

patirūpakārī dhuravā, uṭṭhātā vindatai dhanaṃ|

sac‍caina  kittiṃ pappauti, dadaṃ mittāni ganthati|

asmā laukā paraṃ laukaṃ, aivaṃ paic‍ca na saucati|

yassaitai caturau dhammā, saddhassa gharamaisinau|

sac‍caṃ dhammau dhiti cāgau, sa vai paic‍cana saucati–

sak‍kasaṃyutta

vatapadasutta

pucchā – sak‍kasaṃyuttai  panāvusau vatapadasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha sak‍kassa bhikkhavai daivānamindassa pubbai manussabhūtassa satta vatapadāni samattāni samādin‍nāni ahaisuṃ, yaisaṃ samādin‍nattā sak‍kau sak‍kattaṃ ajjhagāti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

sak‍kassa  bhikkhavai daivānamindassa pubbai manussabhūtassa imāni satta vatapadāni samattāni samādin‍nāni ahaisuṃ|

mātāpaittibharaṃ jantuṃ, kulai jaiṭṭhāpacāyinaṃ|

saṇhaṃ sakhilasambhāsaṃ, paisuṇaiyyappahāyinaṃ|

macchairavinayai yuttaṃ, sac‍caṃ kaudhābhibhuṃ naraṃ|

taṃ vai daivā tāvatiṃsā, āhu ‘‘sappurisau’’iti||

daliddasutta

pucchā – daliddasuttaṃ panāvusau kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ| vissaj‍janā| rājagahai bhantai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha ‘‘bhūtapubbaṃ bhikkhavai añ‍ñatarau purisau imasmiṃyaiva rājagahai manussadaliddau  ahausi manussakapaṇau manussavarākau, sau tathāgatappavaiditai dhammavinayai saddhaṃ samādiyi, sīlaṃ, sutaṃ, cāgaṃ, pañ‍ñaṃ samādiyī’’ti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

yassa  saddhā tathāgatai, acalā suppatiṭṭhitā|

sīlañ‍ca yassa kalyāṇaṃ, ariyakantaṃ pasaṃsitaṃ||

yajamānasutta

pucchā – yajamānasuttaṃ panāvusau bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – rājagahai  bhantai sak‍kaṃ daivānamindaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| sak‍kau bhantai daivānamindau bhagavantaṃ gāthāya ajjhabhāsi–

‘‘yajamānānaṃ manussānaṃ, puñ‍ñapaikkhāna pāṇinaṃ|

karautaṃ aupadhikaṃ puñ‍ñaṃ, kattha din‍naṃ mahapphala’’nti||

tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ–

‘‘cattārau ca paṭipan‍nā, cattārauca phalai ṭhitā|

aisa saṅghau ujubhūtau, pañ‍ñāsīlasamāhitau|

yajamānānaṃ manussānaṃ, puñ‍ñapaikkhāna pāṇinaṃ|

karautaṃ aupadhikaṃ puñ‍ñaṃ, saṅghai din‍naṃ mahapphalaṃ’’nti||

aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

yajamānānaṃ  manussānaṃ, puñ‍ñapaikkhāna pāṇinaṃ,

karautaṃ aupadhitaṃ puñ‍ñaṃ, kattha din‍naṃ mahapphalaṃ–

cattārau ca paṭipan‍nā, cattārau ca phalai ṭhitā|

aisa saṅghau ujubhūtau, pañ‍ñāsīlasamāhitau|

yajamānānaṃ manussānaṃ, puñ‍ñapaikkhāna pāṇinaṃ|

karautaṃ aupadhikaṃ puñ‍ñaṃ, saṅghai din‍naṃ mahapphalaṃ–

gahaṭṭhavandanāsutta

pucchā – gahaṭṭhavandanāsuttaṃ  panāvusau bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha ‘‘bhūtapubbaṃ bhikkhavai sak‍kau daivānamindau mātaliṃ saṅgāhakaṃ āmantaisī’’ti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

taṃ  namassanti taivij‍jā, sabbai bhummāca khattiyā|

cattārau ca mahārājā, tidasāca yasassinau|

athakau nāma sau yakkhau, yaṃ tvaṃ sak‍ka namassasi–

ahañ‍ca  sīlasampan‍nai, cirarattasamāhitai|

sammāpabbajitai vandai, brahmacariyaparāyanai||

yai gahaṭṭhā puñ‍ñakarā, sīlavantau upāsakā|

dhammaina dāraṃ pausainti, tai namassāmi mātali||

nidānavaggapāḷi

nidānasaṃyutta

paṭic‍casamuppādasutta

pucchā – taināvusau  bhagavatā…pai॰… sammāsambuddhaina nidānavaggasaṃyuttai paṭhamaṃ saṃgītaṃ paṭic‍casamuppādasuttaṃ kattha taṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai pañ‍casatai janapadavāsinau bhikkhū ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| pañ‍casatā bhantai janapadavāsinau bhikkhū sabbai ugghāṭitañ‍ñunau dhutaṅgadharā āraddhavīriyā yuttayaugā vipassakā saṇhaṃ sukhumaṃ suñ‍ñataṃ pac‍cayākāradhammadaisanaṃ patthayamānā bhagavantaṃ abhivādaitvā bhagavantaṃ parivāraitvā nisīdiṃsu| tasmiṃ vatthusmiṃ ‘‘paṭic‍casamuppādaṃ vau bhikkhavai daisaissāmi, taṃ suṇātha sādhukaṃ manasi karautha, bhāsissāmī’’ti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

katamau  ca bhikkhavai paṭic‍casamuppādau| avij‍jāpac‍cayā bhikkhavai saṅkhārā, saṅkhārapac‍cayā viñ‍ñāṇaṃ (paiyyāla) jātipac‍cayā jarāmaraṇaṃ saukaparidaivadukkhadaumanassupāsā sambhavanti, aivamaitassa kaivalassa dukkhandhassa samudayau hauti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati bhikkhavai paṭic‍casamuppādau|

avij‍jāya tvaiva asaisavirāganiraudhau saṅkhāraniraudhau (paiyyāla) jātiniraudhā jarāmaraṇaṃ saukaparidaivadukkhadaumanassupāyāsā nirujjhanti, aivamaitassa kaivalassa dukkhakkhandhassa niraudhau hauti –

vibhaṅgasutta

pucchā – tatthāvusau  dutiyaṃ saṃgītaṃ vibhaṅgasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai pañ‍casatai janapadavāsinau vipañ‍citañ‍ñunau bhikkhū ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| pañ‍casatā bhantai janapadavāsikā bhikkhū vipañ‍citañ‍ñunau dhutaṅgadharā āraddhavīriyā yuttayaugā vissakā saṇhaṃ sukhumaṃ suñ‍ñatapaṭisaṃyuttaṃ pac‍cayākāradhammadaisanaṃ patthayamānā bhagavantaṃ parivāraitvā nisīdiṃsu| tasmiṃ vatthusmiṃ ‘‘paṭic‍ca samuppādaṃ vau bhikkhavai daidaissāmi vibhajissāmi, taṃ suṇātha sādhukaṃ manasikarautha, bhāsissāmī’’ti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

pañ‍cavairabhayasutta

pucchā – pañ‍cavairabhayasuttaṃ  panāvusau bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai anāthapiṇḍikaṃ gahapatiṃ ārabbha ‘‘yatau khau gahapati ariyasāvakassa pañ‍ca bhayāni vairāni vūpasantāni haunti, catūhi ca sautāpattiyaṅgaihi saman‍nāgatau hauti, ariyau cassa ñāyau pañ‍ñāya sudiṭṭhau hauti suppaṭividdhau, sau ākaṅkhamānau attanāva attānaṃ byākaraiyya khīṇanirayaumi khīṇatiracchānayauni khīṇapaittivisayau khīṇāpāyaduggativinipātau, sautāpan‍nauhamasmi avinipātadhammau niyatau sambaudhiparāyanau’’ti aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

pucchā – kathañ‍cāvusautattha  bhagavatā ariyasāvakassa pañ‍can‍naṃ bhayānaṃ vairānaṃ vūpasantatā pakāsitā|

vissaj‍janā – ‘‘katamāni  pañ‍ca bhayāni vairāni vūpasantāni haunti| yaṃ gahapati pāṇātipātī pāṇātipātapac‍cayā diṭṭhadhammikampi bhayaṃ vairaṃ pasavati, samparāyikampi bhayaṃ vairaṃ pasavati, caitasikampi dukkhaṃ daumanassaṃ paṭisaṃvaidayati| pāṇātipātā ṭiviratassa aivaṃ taṃ bhayaṃ vairaṃ vūpasantaṃ hautī’’ti aivamādinā bhantai tattha bhagavatā ariyasāvakassa pañ‍can‍naṃ bhayānaṃ vairānaṃ vūpasantatā pakāsitā|

pucchā – kathañ‍cāvusau  tattha bhagavatā ariyasāvakassa catūhi sautāpattiyaṅgaihi saman‍nāgatatā pakāsitā|

vissaj‍janā – katamaihi catūhi sautāpattiyaṅgaihi saman‍nāgatau hauti| idha gahapati ariyasāvakau buddhai avaic‍cappasādaina saman‍nāgatau hauti ‘‘itipisau bhagavā arahaṃ sammāsambuddhau vij‍jācaraṇasampan‍nau sugatau laukavidū anuttaraupurisadammasārathi satthādaivamanussānaṃ buddhau bhagavā’’ti aivamādinā bhantai tattha bhagavatā ariyasāvakassa catūhi sautāpattiyaṅgaihi saman‍nāgatatā pakāsitā|

pucchā – kathañ‍cāvusau  tattha bhagavatā ariyasāvakaina ariyassa ñāyassa pañ‍ñāya sudiṭṭhatā suppaṭividdhatā pakāsitā|

vissaj‍janā – katamau cassa ariyau ñāyau pañ‍ñāya sudiṭṭhau hauti suppaṭividdhau| idha gahapati ariyasāvakau paṭic‍casamuppādañ‍ñaiva sādhukaṃ yaunisau manasi karauti ‘‘iti imasmiṃ sati idaṃ hauti, imasmiṃ asati idaṃ na hauti, imassuppādā idaṃ uppaj‍jati, imassa niraudhā idaṃ nirujjhati, yadidaṃ avij‍jāpac‍cayā (paiyyāla) aivamaitassa kaivalassa dukkhakkhandhassa niraudhau hautī’’ti, aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā tattha ariyasāvakaina ariyassa ñāyassa pañ‍ñāya sudiṭṭhatā suppaṭividdhatā pakāsitā|

yatau  khau gahapati ariyasāvakassa imāni pañ‍ca bhayāni vairāni vūpasantāni haunti| imaihi catūhi sautāpattiyaṅgaihi saman‍nāgatau hauti| ayañ‍cassa ariyau ñāyau pañ‍ñāya sudiṭṭhau hauti suppaṭividdhau–

dukkhasutta

pucchā – dukkhasuttaṃ  panāvusau bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha ‘‘dukkhassa bhikkhavai samudayañ‍ca atthaṅgamañ‍ca daisaissāmī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

pucchā – kathañ‍cāvusau  tattha bhagavatā dukkhasamudayau pakāsitau|

vissaj‍janā – katamau ca bhikkhavai dukkhassa samudayau| cakkhuñ‍ca paṭic‍ca rūpai ca uppaj‍jati cakkhuviñ‍ñāṇaṃ, tiṇṇaṃ saṅgati phassau, phassapac‍cayā vaidanā, vaidanāpac‍cayā taṇhā| ayaṃ khau bhikkhavai dukkhassa samudayau| sautañ‍ca paṭic‍ca saddai ca uppaj‍jati sautaviñ‍ñāṇaṃ…pai॰… ghānañ‍ca paṭic‍ca gandhai ca…pai॰… jivhañ‍ca paṭic‍ca rasai ca…pai॰… kāyañ‍ca paṭic‍ca phauṭṭhabbai ca…pai॰… manañ‍ca paṭic‍ca dhammai ca uppaj‍jati manauviñ‍ñāṇaṃ, tiṇṇaṃ saṅgati phassau, phassapac‍cayā vaidanā, vaidanāpac‍cayā taṇhā| ayaṃ khau bhikkhavai dukkhassa samudayauti aivaṃkhau bhantai tattha bhagavatā dukkhassa samudayau pakāsitau|

pucchā – kathañ‍cāvusau  tattha bhagavatā dukkhassa atthaṅgamau pakāsitau|

vissaj‍janā – katamau ca bhikkhavai dukkhassa atthaṅgamau| cakkhuñ‍ca paṭic‍ca rūpai ca uppaj‍jati cakkhuviñ‍ñāṇaṃ, tiṇṇaṃ saṅgati phassau, phassapac‍cayā vaidanā, vaidanāpac‍cayā taṇhā, tassāyaiva taṇhāya asaisavirāganiraudhā upādānaniraudhau upādānaniraudhā bhavaniraudhau, bhavaniraudhā jātiniraudhau, jātiniraudhā jarāmaraṇaṃ saukaparidaivadukkhadaumanassupāyāsā nirujjhanti| aivamaitassa kaivalassa dukkhakkhandhassa niraudhau hautīti aivamādinā bhantai tattha bhagavatā dukkhassa atthaṅgamau pakāsitau|

puttamaṃsūpamasutta

pucchā – puttamaṃsūpamasuttaṃ  panāvusau bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai sambahulai adhunā pabbajitai bhikkhū ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| bhagavatau ca bhantai bhikkhusaṅghassa ca mahālābhasak‍kārau udapādi, aikac‍cai ca bhantai bhikkhū navā acirapabbajitā kulaputtā āhāraṃ apac‍cavaikkhitvā paribhuñ‍jiṃsu| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘cattāraumai bhikkhavai āhārā bhūtānaṃ vā sattānaṃ ṭhitiyā sambhavaisīnaṃ vā anuggahāya| katamai cattārau kabaḷīkārau āhārau auḷārikau vā sukhumau vā phassau dutiyau manausañ‍caitanā tatiyā viñ‍ñāṇaṃ catuttha’’nti aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

pucchā – kathañ‍cāvusau  tattha kabaḷīkārassa āhārassa daṭṭhabbākārau bhagavatā pakāsitau|

vissaj‍janā – saiyyathāpi bhikkhavai dvai jāyampatikā parittaṃ sambalaṃ ādāya kantāramaggaṃ paṭipaj‍jaiyyuṃ| taisamassa aikaputtakau piyau manāpauti aivamādinā bhantai tattha bhagavatā  kabaḷīkārassa āhārassa daṭṭhabbākārau pakāsitau|

pucchā – kathañ‍cāvusau  tattha bhagavatā phassāhārassa daṭṭhabbākārau pakāsitau|

vissaj‍janā – saiyyathāpi bhikkhavai gāvī nic‍cammā kuṭṭaṃ cai nissāya tiṭṭhaiyya, yai kuṭṭanissitā pāṇā, tai naṃ khādaiyyuṃ| rukkhaṃ cai nissāya tiṭṭhaiyya| udakaṃ cai nissāya tiṭṭhaiyya| ākāsaṃ cai nissāya tiṭṭhaiyya, yai ākāsanissitā pāṇā, tai naṃ khādaiyyunti aivamādinā bhantai tattha bhagavatā phassassa āhārassa daṭṭhabbākārau vitthāraitvā pakāsitau|

aivamaiva  khvāhaṃ bhikkhavai phassāhārau daṭṭhabbauti vadāmi|

pucchā – kathañ‍cāvusau  tattha bhagavatā manausañ‍caitanāhārassa daṭṭhabbākārau pakāsitau|

vissaj‍janā – saiyyathāpi bhikkhavai aṅgārakāsu sādhikapaurisā puṇṇā aṅgārānaṃ vītac‍cikānaṃ vītadhūmānaṃ| atha purisau āgacchaiyya jīvitukāmau amaritukāmau sukha kāmau dukkhappaṭikūlauti aivamādinā bhantai tattha bhagavatā manausañ‍caitanāhārassa daṭṭhabbākārau vitthāraina pakāsitau|

pucchā – kathañ‍cāvusau  tattha bhagavatā viñ‍ñāṇāhārassa daṭṭhabbākārau pakāsitau|

vissaj‍janā – saiyyathāpi bhikkhavai cauraṃ āgucāriṃ gahaitvā rañ‍ñau dassaiyyuṃ ‘‘ayaṃ tai daivacaurau āgucārī, imassa yaṃ icchasi, taṃ daṇḍaṃ paṇaihī’’ti aivamādinā bhantai tattha bhagavatā viñ‍ñāṇāhārassa daṭṭhabbākārau pakāsitau|

susimaparibbājakasutta

pucchā – taināvusau  bhagavatā…pai॰… sammāsambuddhaina susimaparibbājaka suttaṃ kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – rājagahai bhantai āyasmantaṃ susimaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| āyasmā bhantai susimau yāvatakau bhikkhūhi saddhiṃ ahausi kathāsal‍lāpau, taṃ sabbaṃ bhagavatau āraucaisi| tasmiṃ vatthusmiṃ ‘‘pubbai khau susima dhammaṭṭhitiñāṇaṃ, pacchā nibbānai ñāṇa’’nti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

pucchā – kathañ‍cāvusau  tattha bhagavā āyasmatau susimatthairassa sampatiarahattapattassa anuyaugavasaina paṭipucchitvā paṭipucchitvā uttari dhammadaisanaṃ vitthāraitvā daisaisi|

vissaj‍janā – jātipac‍cayā jarāmaraṇanti susima passasīti| aivaṃ bhantai| bhavapac‍cayā jātīti susima passasīti| aivaṃ bhantaiti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavā tattha āyasmatau susimassa sampatiarahattapattassa uttaripi anuyaugavasaina paṭipucchitvā paṭipucchitvā vitthāratau dhammaṃ daisaisi|

tassa  mai bhantai bhagavā ac‍cayaṃ ac‍cayatau paṭiggaṇhātu āyatiṃsaṃvarāya–

abhisamayasaṃyutta

kassapasaṃyutta

candūpamasutta

pucchā – kassapasaṃyuttai  panāvusau candūpamasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha ‘‘candūpamā bhikkhavai kulāni upasaṅkamatha apakassaiva kāyaṃ apakassa cittaṃ nic‍canavakā kulaisu appagabbhā| saiyyathāpi bhikkhavai purisau jarudapānaṃ vā aulaukaiyya pabbatavisamaṃ vā nadīviduggaṃ vā apakassaiva kāyaṃ apakassa cittaṃ| aivamaiva khau bhikkhavai candūpamā kulāni upasaṅkamatha apakassaiva kāyaṃ apakassa cittaṃ nic‍canavakā kulaisu appagabbhā’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

taṃ  kiṃ mañ‍ñatha bhikkhavai, kathaṃ rūpā bhikkhu arahati kulāni upasaṅkamituṃ|

pucchā – kathañ‍cāvusau  tattha bhagavatā parisuddhāparisuddha dhammadaisanaṃ dassaitvā bhikkhūnaṃ auvādau din‍nau|

vissaj‍janā – ‘‘taṃ kiṃ mañ‍ñatha bhikkhavai, kathaṃ rūpassa bhikkhunau aparisuddhā dhammadaisanā hauti, kathaṃ rūpassa bhikkhunau parisuddhā dhammadaisanā  hautī’’ti aivamādinā parisuddhāparisuddhadhammadaisanā vitthāratau dassaitvā ‘‘kassapaina vā hi vau bhikkhavai auvadissāmi yau vā panassa kassapasadisau, auvaditaihi ca pana vau tathattāya paṭipaj‍jitabba’’nti| aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavatau bhikkhūnaṃ auvādau din‍nau|

yau  hi kauci bhikkhavai bhikkhu aivaṃcittau paraisaṃ dhammaṃ daisaiti ‘‘ahau vata mai dhammaṃ suṇaiyyuṃ, sutvā ca pana dhammaṃ pasīdaiyyuṃ, pasan‍nā ca mai pasan‍nākāraṃ karaiyyuṃ–

svākkhātau bhagavatā dhammau sandiṭṭhikau akālikau aihi passikau aupanaiyyikau pac‍cattaṃ vaiditabbau viñ‍ñūhi–

kassapaina  vā hi vau bhikkhavai auvadissāmi yau vā panassa kassapasadisau, auvaditaihi ca pana vau tathattāya paṭipaj‍jitabbaṃ–

lābhasak‍kārasaṃyutta

mīḷhakasutta

pucchā – taināvusau…pai॰…  sammāsambuddhaina lābhasak‍kāraṃyuttai pañ‍camaṃ saṃgītaṃ mīḷhakasuttaṃ kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha ‘‘dāruṇau bhikkhavai lābhasak‍kārasilaukau pharusau antarāyikau anuttarassa yaugakkhaimassa adhigamāya| saiyyathāpi bhikkhavai mīḷhakā gūthādī gūthapūrā puṇṇā gūthassa, puratau cassa mahāgūthapuñ‍jau, sā taina añ‍ñā mīḷhakā atimañ‍ñaiyya ‘‘ahamhi gūthādī gūthapūrā puṇṇā gūthassa, puratau ca myāyaṃ mahāgūthapuñ‍jau’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

taṃ  hi tassa bhikkhavai maughapurisassa hauti dīgharattaṃ ahitāya dukkhāya|

aivaṃ dāruṇau khau bhikkhavai lābhasak‍kārasilaukau|

tasmātiha  bhikkhavai aivaṃ sikkhitabbaṃ, ‘‘uppan‍naṃ lābhasak‍kārasi laukaṃ pajahissāma, na ca nau uppan‍nau lābhasak‍kārasilaukau cittaṃ pariyādāya ṭhassabhī’’ti, aivañhi khau bhikkhavai sikkhitabbaṃ|

aikaputtakasutta

pucchā – tatthau āvusau paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakaihi saṃgītaṃ aikaputtakasuttaṃ kattha kaṃ ārabbhaṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha ‘‘dāruṇau bhikkhavai lābhasak‍kārasilaukau kaṭukau pharusau antarāyikau anuttarassa yaugakkhaimassa adhigamāya, saddhā bhikkhavai upāsikā aikaputtakaṃ piyaṃ manāpaṃ aivaṃ sammāāyācamānā  āyācaiyya tādisau tāta bhavāhi, yādisau cittau ca gahapati hatthakau ca āḷavakau’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

aisā  bhikkhavai tulā aitaṃ pamāṇaṃ mama sāvakānaṃ upāsakānaṃ yadidaṃ cittau ca gahapati hatthakau ca āḷavakau|

sacai khau tvaṃ tāta agārasmā anagāriyaṃ pabbajasi, tādisau tāta bhavāhi, yādisā sāriputtamauggal‍lānā|

mā ca khau tvaṃ tāta saikhaṃ appattamānasaṃ lābhasak‍kārasilaukau anupāpuṇātu–

aivaṃ  dāruṇau bhikkhavai lābhasak‍kārasilaukau kaṭukau pharusau antarāyikau anuttarassa yaugakkhaimassa adhigamāya|

aikavītusutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāyakaihi saṃgītaṃ aikadhītusuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃyaiva bhantai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha ‘‘dāruṇau bhikkhavai lābhasak‍kārasilaukau kaṭukau pharusau antarāyikau anuttarassa yaugakkhaimassa adhigamāya| saddhā bhikkhavai upāsikā aikaṃ dhītaraṃ piyaṃ manāpaṃ aivaṃ sammā āyācamānā āyācaiyya ‘tādisā ayyai bhavāhi, yādisā khuj‍juttarā ca upāsikā vaiḷukaṇḍakiyā ca nandamātā’ti| aisā bhikkhavai tulā aitaṃ pamāṇaṃ mama sāvikānaṃ upāsikānaṃ, yadidaṃ khuj‍juttarā ca upāsikā vaiḷukaṇḍakiyā ca nandamātā’’ti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

sacai  khau tvaṃ ayyai agārasmā anagāriyaṃ pabbajasi, bhādisā ayyai bhavāhi, yādisā khaimā ca bhikkhunī uppalavaṇṇā ca–

tasmātiha  bhikkhavai aivaṃ sikkhitabbaṃ|

pucchā – taināvusau  bhagavatā jānatā…pai॰… sammāsambuddhaina khandhavaggasaṃyuttai paṭhamaṃ nakulapitusuttaṃ kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

tasmātiha  tai gahapati aivaṃ sikkhitabbaṃ|

pucchā – tañ‍cāvusau  bhagavatā saṃkhittaina bhāsitaṃ vitthāraina kaina kathañ‍ca vibhattaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – taṃ khau bhantai bhagavatā saṃkhittaina daisitaṃ ‘‘kathañ‍ca gahapati āturakāyau caiva hauti āturacittau ca| idha gahapati assutavā puthuj‍janau ariyānaṃ adassāvī ariyadhammassa akauvidau ariyadhammai avinītau, sappurisānaṃ adassāvī sappurisadhammassa akauvidau sappurisadhammai avinītau rūpaṃ attatau samanupassati rūpavantaṃ vā attānaṃ, attani vā rūpaṃ, rūpasmiṃ vā attānaṃ, ‘‘ahaṃ rūpaṃ mama rūpa’’nti pariyuṭṭhaṭṭhāyī hauti| tassa ‘‘ahaṃ rūpaṃ mama rūpa’’nti pariyuṭṭhaṭṭhāyinau taṃ rūpaṃ vipariṇamati añ‍ñathā hauti| tassa rūpavipariṇāmañ‍ñathābhāvā uppaj‍janti saukaparidaivadukkhadaumanassupāyāsā’’ti aivamādinā bhantai āyasmatā sāriputtaina dhammasaināpatinā vitthāraina vibhattaṃ|

‘‘yaṃ panānic‍caṃ, dukkhaṃ vā taṃ sukhaṃ vā’’–

dukkhaṃ bhantai|

yaṃ panānic‍caṃ dukkhaṃ vipariṇāmadhammaṃ, kal‍laṃ nu taṃ samanupassituṃ ‘‘aitaṃ mama, aisauhamasmi, aisau mai attā’’–

pucchā – kathañ‍cāvusau  punapi bhagavā bhikkhū paisaitvā āyasmatau vak‍kalitthairassa paggahavacanaṃ āraucāpaisi| saupi kathaṃ attanau pavattiṃ bhagavatau pac‍cāraucāpaisi| kathañ‍cassa abhisamparāyau ahausi|

vissaj‍janā – suṇāvusau tvaṃ vak‍kali bhagavatau vacanaṃ dvin‍nañ‍ca daivatānaṃ, imaṃ āvusau rattiṃ dvai daivatāyau abhik‍kantāya rattiyā abhik‍kantavaṇṇā kaivalakappaṃ gijjhakūṭaṃ aubhāsaitvā yaina

bhagavā  tainupasaṅkamiṃsu, upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ aṭṭhaṃsu, aikamantaṃ ṭhitā khau āvusau aikā daivatā bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca ‘vak‍kali bhantai bhikkhu vimaukkhāya caitaitī’ti| aparā daivatā bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca ‘sau hi nūna bhantai suvimuttau vimuc‍cissatī’ti| bhagavā ca taṃ āvusau vak‍kali aivamāha ‘mā bhāyi vak‍kali, mā bhāyi vak‍kali, apāpakaṃ tai maraṇaṃ bhavissati, apāpikā kālakiriyā’ti| aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavā punapi bhikkhū paisaitvā āyasmatau vak‍kalitthairassa paggahavacanaṃ āraucāpaisi| saupi bhantai āyasmā tainahāvusau mama vacanaina bhagavatau pādai sirasā vandatha ‘‘vak‍kali bhantai bhikkhu ābādhikau dukkhitau bāḷhagilānau, sau bhagavatau pādai sirasā vandatī’’ti, aivañ‍ca vadaitha ‘‘rūpaṃ anic‍caṃ, tāhaṃ bhantai na kaṅkhāmi, yadanic‍caṃ taṃ dukkhanti navicikicchāmi, yadanic‍caṃ dukkhaṃ vipariṇāmadhammaṃ, natthi mai tattha chandau vā rāgau vā paimaṃvāti na vicikicchāmī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatau attanau pavattiṃ pac‍cāraucāpaisi| sau hi bhantai āyasmā acirapak‍kantaisu taisu bhikkhūsu satthaṃ āharitvā vaidanaṃ vikkhambhaitvā mūlakammaṭṭhānaṃ ādāya sampajānau arahattaṃ sacchikatvā kālamakāsi| aivaṃ khau bhantai tassa āyasmatau abhisamparāyau ahausi|

pucchā – khaimakasuttaṃ  panāvusau kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kaina kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – kausambiyaṃ bhantai sambahulai thairai bhikkhū ārabbha āyasmatā khaimakatthairaina bhāsitaṃ| sambahulā bhantai thairā bhikkhū āyasmantaṃ dāsakaṃ paisaitvā catukkhattuṃ āyasmantaṃ khaimakaṃ ābādhikaṃ dukkhitaṃ bāḷhagilānaṃ gailañ‍ñakāraṇañ‍ca dhammañ‍ca pucchiṃsu, tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘pañ‍cimai āvusau upādānakkhandhā vuttā bhagavatā| saiyyathidaṃ, rūpupādānakkhandhau vaidanupādānakkhandhau sañ‍ñupādānakkhandhau saṅkhārupādānakkhandhau viñ‍ñāṇupādānakkhandhau, imaisu khvāhaṃ āvusau pañ‍casu upādānakkhandhaisu na kiñ‍ci attaṃ vā attaniyaṃ vā samanupassāmī’’ti aivamādinā bhantai āyasmatā khaimakatthairaina bhāsitaṃ|

pucchā – kathañ‍cāvusau  tattha āyasmā khaimakau uttari dhammadaisanaṃ pavaḍḍhaitvā vitthāraina thairānaṃ bhikkhūnaṃ daisaisi| kīdisau ca naisaṃ dhammadaisakadhammappaṭiggāhakānaṃ dhammadaisanāya ānisaṃsau adhigatau|

vissaj‍janā – catutthai bhantai vārai āyasmā khaimakau ‘‘alaṃ āvusau dāsaka kiṃ imāya sandhāvanikāya, āharāvusau daṇḍaṃ, ahamaiva thairānaṃ santikaṃ gamissāmī’’ti daṇḍamaulubbha āyasmā khaimakau yaina thairā bhikkhū tainupasaṅkami, upasaṅkamitvā thairaihi bhikkhūhi pucchitau ‘‘nakhvāhaṃ āvusau rūpaṃ asmīti vadāmi, napi añ‍ñatra rūpā asmīti vadāmi| na khvāhaṃ āvusau vaidanaṃ asmīti vadāmi, napi añ‍ñatra vaidanā asmīti vadāmī’’ti aivamādinā bhantai āyasmā khaimakau uttaripi dhammadaisanaṃ pavaḍḍhaitvā vitthāraina daisaisi| imasmiñ‍ca pana bhantai vaiyyākaraṇasmiṃ bhañ‍ñamānai saṭṭhimattānaṃ thairānaṃ bhikkhūnaṃ anupādāya āsavaihi cittāni vimuc‍ciṃsu āyasmatauca khaimakassa| aivaṃ khau bhantai taisaṃ thairānaṃ dhammadaisakadhammappaṭiggāhakānaṃ dhammābhisamayau ahausi|

pucchā – aivañ‍cāvusau  bhagavā āyasmatau puṇṇatthairassa saṃkhittaina auvādaṃ datvā kathañ‍ca naṃ paṭipucchi, kathañ‍ca sau bhagavatau āraucaisi, kathañ‍cassāyasmatau puṇṇatthairassa abhisamparāyau ahausi|

vissaj‍janā – aivaṃ khau bhantai bhagavā āyasmatau puṇṇatthairassa saṃkhittaina auvādaṃ datvā ‘‘iminā tvaṃ puṇṇa mayā saṃkhittaina auvādaina auvaditau katamasmiṃ janapadai viharissasī’’ti taṃ āyasmantaṃ puṇṇatthairaṃ paṭipucchi| sau ca bhantai āyasmā ‘‘atthi bhantai sunāparantau nāma janapadau, tatthāhaṃ viharissāmī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatau āraucaisi| sau hi bhantai āyasmā sunāparantai janapadai vasitvā tainaiva antaravassaina tissau vij‍jā sacchākāsi| tainaiva antaravassaina parinibbāyi| aivaṃ khau bhantai tassa āyasmatau abhisamparāyau ahausi|

santi  khau tassa bhagavatau sāvakā kāyaina ca jīvitaina ca aṭṭīyamānā harāyamānā jigucchamānā satthahārakaṃ pariyaisanti|

sakkhissasi khau tvaṃ puṇṇa iminā damūpasamaina saman‍nāgatau sunāparasantasmiṃ janapadai vatthuṃ|

pucchā – imasmiṃ  ca khau panāvusau bhagavatā auvādai saṃkhittaina bhāsitai sau āyasmā mālukyaputtatthairau bhagavantaṃ kiṃ avauca kathañ‍cassa bhagavā anuñ‍ñāsi| kīdisau cassāyasmatau mālukyaputtatthairassa dhammābhisamayau ahausi|

vissaj‍janā – imasmiṃ khau bhantai auvādai bhagavatā saṃkhittaina bhāsitai āyasmā mālukyaputtatthairau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca ‘‘imassa khvāhaṃ bhantai bhagavatā saṃkhittaina bhāsitassa vitthāraina atthaṃ ājānāmi|

sārattacittau vaidaiti, tañ‍ca ajjhausa tiṭṭhati||

tassa vaḍḍhanti vaidanā, anaikā rūpasambhavā|

abhijjhā ca vihaisā ca, cittamassūpahañ‍ñati|

aivaṃ ācinatau dukkhaṃ, ārā nibbāna vuc‍cati|

paiyyāla

na sau raj‍jati dhammaisu, dhammaṃ ñatvā paṭissatau|

virattacittau vaidaiti, tañ‍ca nājjhausa tiṭṭhati||

yathāssa jānatau dhammaṃ, saivatau cāpi vaidanaṃ|

khīyati naupacīyati, aivaṃ sau caratī satau|

aivaṃ apacinatau dukkhaṃ, santikai nibbāna vuc‍catīti||

imassa khvāhaṃ bhantai bhagavatā saṃkhittaina bhāsitassa aivaṃ vitthāraina atthaṃ ājānāmīti| bhagavā ca bhantai ‘‘sādhu sādhu mālukyaputta, sādhu khau tvaṃ mālukyaputta mayā saṃkhittaina bhāsitassa vitthāraina atthaṃ ājānāsī’’ti sādhukāraṃ datvā–

ādinā

vissaj‍janā – idha gāmaṇi aikac‍cau satthā aivaṃvādī hauti aivaṃdiṭṭhi yau kauci pāṇamatipātaiti, sabbau sau āpāyikau nairayikauti aivamādinā bhantai tattha bhagavatā anaikaṃsa kammavipākassa aikaṃsavipākavādai dausau vitthāraitvā pakāsitau|

vissaj‍janā – idha  pana gāmaṇi tathāgatau laukai uppaj‍jati arahaṃ sammāsambuddhau vij‍jācaraṇasampan‍nau sugatau laukavidū anuttaraupurisa dammasārathi satthādaivamanussānaṃ buddhau bhagavā, sau anaikapariyāyaina pāṇātipātaṃ garahati vigarahati| ‘‘pāṇātipātā viramathā’’ti cāhāti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā tattha yathādhammasāsanai guṇau vitthāraitvā pakāsitau|

vissaj‍janā – sak‍kaisu bhantai sak‍karai nāma sakyānaṃ nigamai āyasmantaṃ ānandatthairaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| āyasmā bhantai ānandatthairau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca ‘‘upaḍḍhamidaṃ bhantai brahmacariyaṃ yadidaṃ kalyāṇamittatā kalyāṇasahāyatā kalyāṇasampavaṅkatā’’ti| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘mā haivaṃ ānanda, mā haivaṃ ānanda, sakalamaividaṃ ānanda brahmacariyaṃ yadidaṃ kalyāṇamittatā kalyāṇasahāyatā kalyāṇasampavaṅkatā’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

mamaṃ  hi ānanda kalyāṇamittaṃ āgamma jātidhammā sattā jātiyā parimuc‍canti|

baujjhaṅgasaṃyutta

kuṇḍaliyasutta

pucchā – baujjhaṅgasaṃyuttai  panāvusau paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi chaṭṭhaṃ saṃgītaṃ kuṇḍaliyasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sākaitai bhantai añ‍janavanai migadāyai kuṇḍaliyaṃ paribbājakaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| kuṇḍaliyau bhantai paribbājakau bhagavantaṃ upasaṅkamitvā ‘‘ahamasmi bhau gautama ārāmanissayī parisāvacarau, tassa mayhaṃ bhau gautama pacchābhattaṃ bhuttapātarāsassa ayamācārau hauti ārāmaina ārāmaṃ uyyānaina uyyānaṃ anucaṅkamāmi anuvicarāmi, sau tattha passāmi aikai samaṇabrāhmaṇai itivādappamaukkhānisaṃsañ‍caiva kathaṃ kathantai upārambhānisaṃsañ‍ca, bhavaṃ pana gautamau kimānisaṃsau viharatī’’ti aitaṃ vacanaṃ avauca| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘vij‍jāvimuttiphalānisaṃsau  khau kuṇḍaliya tathāgatau viharatī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

paṭhama gilānasutta

pucchā – taināvusau  bhagavvatā jānatā…pai॰… sammāsambuddhaina baujjhaṅgasaṃyuttai dutiyai gilānavaggai paṭhamagilānasuttaṃ kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – rājagahai bhantai pippaliguhāyaṃ āyasmantaṃ mahākassapatthairaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| āyasmā bhantai mahākassapatthairau pippaliguhāyaṃ viharati ābādhikau dukkhitau bāḷhagilānau| atha khau bhantai bhagavā sāyanhasamayaṃ paṭisal‍lānā vuṭṭhitau yaināyasmā mahākassapau tainupasaṅkami, upasaṅkamitvā pañ‍ñattai āsanai nisīdi, nisaj‍ja khau bhagavā āyasmantaṃ mahākassapaṃ  aitadavauca– ‘‘kac‍ci tai kassapa khamanīyaṃ, kac‍ci yāpanīyaṃ, kac‍ci dukkhā vaidanā paṭik‍kamanti nau abhik‍kamanti, paṭik‍kamausānaṃ pañ‍ñāyati nau abhik‍kamauti| na mai bhantai khamanīyaṃ na yāpanīyaṃ, bāḷhā mai dukkhā vaidanā abhik‍kamanti nau paṭik‍kamanti, abhik‍kamausānaṃ pañ‍ñāyati nau paṭik‍kamau’’ti| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘sattimai kassapa baujjhaṅgā mayā sammadakkhātā bhāvitā bahulīkatā abhiñ‍ñāya sambaudhāya nibbānāya saṃvattantī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

udāyisutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau tatiyai udāyivaggai paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi dasamaṃ saṃgītaṃ udāyisuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sumbhaisu bhantai saitakaināma sumbhānaṃ nigamai āyasmantaṃ udāyitthairaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| āyasmā bhantai udāyitthairau bhagavantaṃ upasaṅkamitvā ‘‘acchariyaṃ bhantai, abbhutaṃ bhantai, yāva bahukatañ‍ca mai bhantai bhagavati paimañ‍ca gāravau ca hirī ca auttappañ‍cāti aivamādikaṃ vacanaṃ avauca| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘sādhu sādhu udāyi aisau hi tai udāyi maggau paṭiladdhau| yau tai bhāvitau bahulīkatau, tathā tathā viharantaṃ tathattāya upanaissati, yathā tvaṃ ‘khīṇā jāti, vusitaṃ brahmacariyaṃ, kataṃ karaṇīyaṃ, nāparaṃ itthattāyā’ti pajānissatī’’ti| aivaṃ khau bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

satipaṭṭhānasaṃyutta

satisutta

pucchā – satipaṭṭhānasaṃyuttai  āvusau paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhaka mahāthairaihi dutiyaṃ saṃgītaṃ satisuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ kārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – vaisāliyaṃ bhantai ambapālivanai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha ‘‘satau bhikkhavai bhikkhu viharaiyya sampajānau ayaṃ vau amhākaṃ anusāsanī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

sālasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi catutthaṃ saṃgītaṃ sālasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – kausalaisu bhantai sālāyanāma kaisalānaṃ brāhmaṇagāmai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha ‘‘yai tai bhikkhavai bhikkhū navā acirapabbajitā adhunāgatā imaṃ dhammavinayaṃ, tai vau bhikkhavai bhikkhū catun‍naṃ satipaṭṭhānānaṃ bhāvanāya samādapaitabbā nivaisaitabbā patiṭṭhāpaitabbā’’ti aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

sakuṇagghisutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi chaṭṭhaṃ saṃgītaṃ sakuṇagghisuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha ‘‘bhūtapubbaṃ bhikkhavai sakuṇagghi lāpaṃ sakuṇaṃ sahasā ajjhappattā aggahaisi| atha khau bhikkhavai lāpau sakuṇau sakuṇagghiyā hariyamānau aivaṃ paridaivasi mayamaivamha alakkhikā, mayaṃ appapuñ‍ñā, yai mayaṃ agaucarai carimha paravisayai, sacaij‍ja mayaṃ gaucarai caraiyyāma sakai paittikai visayai, na myāhaṃ sakuṇagghi alaṃ abhavissa yadidaṃ yuddhāyā’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

mayamaivamha  alakkhikā, mayaṃ appapuñ‍ñā|

kau pana tai lāpa gaucarau sakau paittikau visayau|

yadidaṃ naṅgalakaṭṭhakaraṇaṃ laiḍḍuṭṭhānaṃ|

aihi  khau dāni mai sakuṇagghi, aihi khau dāni mai sakuṇagghi,

(dāruguḷauviya vinivattitvā tassaiva laiḍḍussa antarai pac‍cupādi| aṭṭhakathā)

aivañhi taṃ bhikkhavai hauti yau agaucarai carati paravisayai|

tasmātiha  bhikkhavai mā agaucarai carittha paravisayai|

gaucarai bhikkhavai caratha sakai paittikai visayai|

gaucarai bhikkhavai carataṃ sakai paittikai visayai na lacchati mārau autāraṃ, na lacchati mārau ārammaṇaṃ|

bhikkhunupassayasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi dasamaṃ saṃgītaṃ bhikkhunupassayasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai āyasmantaṃ ānandatthairaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| āyasmā bhantai ānandau yāvattakau ahausi bhikkhunīhi saddhiṃ kathāsal‍lāpau taṃ sabbaṃ bhagavatau āraucaisi| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘aivamaitaṃ ānanda, aivamaitaṃ ānanda, yauhi kauci ānanda bhikkhu vā bhikkhunī vā catūsu satipaṭṭhānaisu suppatiṭṭhitacittau viharati, tassaitaṃ pāṭikaṅkhaṃ uḷāraṃ pubbaināparaṃ visaisaṃ sañ‍jānissatī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

cundasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau dutiyai nālandavaggai paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi tatiyaṃ saṃgītaṃ cundasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai āyasmantaṃyaiva ānandatthairaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| āyasmā bhantai ānandatthairau cundaina samaṇuddaisaina saddhiṃ yaina bhagavā tainupasaṅkami| upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdi| aikamantaṃ nisin‍nau khau bhantai āyasmā ānandau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca ‘‘ayaṃ bhantai cundau samaṇuddaisau aivamāha ‘āyasmā bhantai sāriputtau parinibbutau, idamassa pattacīvara’nti| api ca mai bhantai madhurakajātau viya kāyau, disāpi mai na pakkhāyanti, dhammāpi maṃ nappaṭibhanti ‘āyasmā sāriputtau parinibbutau’ti sutvā’’ti| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘kiṃ nu khau tai ānanda sāriputtau sīlakkhandhaṃ vā ādāya parinibbutau, samādhikkhandhaṃ vā, pañ‍ñākkhandhaṃvā, vimuttikkhandhaṃvā, vimuttiñāṇadassanakkhandhaṃ vā ādāya parinibbutau’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

ayaṃ  bhantai cundausamaṇuddaisau aivamāha|

tasmātihānanda  attadīpā viharatha attasaraṇā anañ‍ñasaraṇā dhammadīpā dhammasaraṇā anañ‍ñasaraṇā|

bāhiyasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi pañ‍camaṃ saṃgītaṃ bāhiyasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai āyasmantaṃ bāhiyaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| āyasmā bhantai bāhiyau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca ‘‘sādhu mai bhantai bhagavā saṃkhittaina dhammaṃ daisaitu, yamahaṃ bhagavatau dhammaṃ sutvā aikau vūpakaṭṭhau appamattau ātāpī pahitattau viharaiyya’’nti| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘tasmātiha tvaṃ bāhiya ādimaiva visaudhaihi kusalaisu dhammaisu| kau cādikusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ, sīlañ‍ca suvisuddhaṃ diṭṭhica ujukā’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

sīlaṭṭhitivagga

ciraṭṭhitisutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau tatiyai sīlaṭṭhitivaggai paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi dutiyaṃ saṃgītaṃ ciraṭṭhitisuttaṃ kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kaina kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – pāṭaliputtai bhantai āyasmantaṃ bhaddaṃ ārabbha āyasmatā ānandatthairaina dhammabhaṇḍāgārikaina bhāsitaṃ| āyasmā bhantai bhaddau sāyanhasamayaṃ paṭisal‍lānā vuṭṭhitau yaināyasmā ānandau tainupasaṅkami, upasaṅkamitvā āyasmantaṃ ānandaṃ aitadavauca ‘‘kau nu khau āvusau ānanda haitu kau pac‍cayau, yaina tathāgatai parinibbutai saddhammau na ciraṭṭhitikau hauti, kau panāvusau ānanda haitu kau pac‍cayau, yaina tathāgatai parinibbutai saddhammau ciraṭṭhitikau hautī’’ti| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘sādhu sādhu āvusau bhadda, bhaddakau khau tai āvusau bhadda ummaṅgau’’ti aivamādinā āyasmatā ānandatthairaina dhammabhaṇḍāgārikaina bhāsitaṃ|

bhaddakau  tai āvusau bhadda ummaṅgau|

‘‘catun‍naṃ  khau āvusau satipaṭṭhānānaṃ abhāvitattā abahulīkatattā tathāgatai parinibbutai saddhammau na ciraṭṭhitikau hautī’’ –

sirivaḍḍhasutta

pucchā – tassāvusau bhagavatā arahatau sammāsambuddhassa pāvacanasamudayabhūtāya mahāvaggapāḷiyā satipaṭṭhānasaṃyuttai sīlaṭṭhitivaggai navamaṃ sirivaḍḍhasuttaṃ kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kaina kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – rājagahai bhantai sirivaḍḍhaṃ gahapatiṃ ārabbha āyasmatā ānandatthairaina dhammabhaṇḍāgārikaina bhāsitaṃ| sirivaḍḍhau bhantai gahapati ābādhikau hauti dukkhitau bāḷhagilānau| athakhau bhantai āyasmā ānandau yainasirivaḍḍhassa gahapatissa nivaisanaṃ tainupasaṅkami, upasaṅkamitvā pañ‍ñattai āsanai nisīdi| nisaj‍ja khau āyasmā ānandau sirivaḍḍhaṃ gahapatiṃ aitadavauca ‘‘kac‍ci tai gahapati khamanīyaṃ, kac‍ci yāpanīyaṃ, kac‍ci dukkhā vaidanā paṭik‍kamanti nau abhik‍kamanti, paṭik‍kamausānaṃ pañ‍ñāyati nau abhik‍kamau’’ti| na mai bhantai khamanīyaṃ, na yāpanīyaṃ, bāḷhā mai dukkhā vaidanā abhik‍kamanti nau paṭik‍kamanti, abhik‍kamausānaṃ pañ‍ñāyati nau paṭik‍kamau’’ti| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘tasmātiha tai gahapati aivaṃ sikkhitabbaṃ ‘kāyai kāyānupassī viharissāmi ātāpī sampajānau satimā vinaiyya laukai abhijjhā daumanassaṃ| vaidanāsu| cittai| dhammaisu dhammānupassī viharissāmi ātāpī sampajānau satimā vinaiyya laukai abhijjhā daumanassa’nti| aivañhi tai gahapati sikkhitabba’’nti| aivaṃ khau āyasmatā ānandatthairaina dhammabhaṇḍāgārikaina bhāsitaṃ|

iddhipādasaṃyutta

mauggal‍lānasutta

pucchā – iddhipādasaṃyuttai  āvusau catutthaṃ mauggal‍lānasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| sambahulā bhantai bhikkhū haiṭṭhāmigāramātupāsādai viharanti uddhatā un‍naḷā capalā mukharā vikiṇṇavācā muṭṭhassatinau asampajānā asamāhitā bhantacittā pākatindriyā| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ āyasmantaṃ mahāmauggal‍lānaṃ tai bhikkhū saṃvaijaitvā ‘‘kiṃ nu tumhai bhikkhavai saṃviggā laumahaṭṭhajātā aikamantaṃ ṭhitā’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

acchariyaṃ  vata bhau, abbhutaṃ vata bhau|

nivātañ‍ca vata|

ayañ‍ca migāramātupāsādau gambhīranaimau|

tumhaiva  khau bhikkhavai saṃvaijaitukāmaina mauggal‍lānaina bhikkhunā pādaṅguṭṭhakaina migāramātupāsādau saṅkampitau sampakampitau sampadhālitau|

taṃ kiṃ mañ‍ñatha bhikkhavai, katamaisaṃ dhammānaṃ bhāvitattā bahulīkatattā mauggal‍lānau bhikkhu aivaṃ mahiddhikau aivaṃ mahānubhāvau|

icchānaṅgalasutta

dīghāvu upāsakasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi tatiyaṃ saṃgītaṃ dīghāvuupāsakasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – rājagahai  bhantai dīghāvuṃ upāsakaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| bhagavā bhantai dīghāvuṃ upāsakaṃ ābādhikaṃ dukkhitaṃ bāḷhagilānaṃ aitadavauca ‘‘kac‍ci tai dīghāvu khamanīyaṃ, kac‍ci yāpanīyaṃ, kac‍ci dukkhā vaidanā paṭik‍kamanti nau abhik‍kamanti, paṭik‍kamausānaṃ pañ‍ñāyati nau abhik‍kamau’’ti| na mai bhantai khamanīyaṃ, na yāpanīyaṃ bāḷhā mai dukkhā vaidanā abhik‍kamanti nau paṭik‍kamanti, abhik‍kamausānaṃ pañ‍ñāyati nau paṭik‍kamauti| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘tasmātiha tai dīghāvu aivaṃ sikkhitabbaṃ buddhai avaic‍cappasādaina saman‍nāgatau bhavissāmī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

yānimāni  bhantai bhagavatā cattāri sautāpattiyaṅgāni daisitāni, saṃvij‍jantai tai dhammā mayi–

tasmātiha tvaṃ dīghāvu imaisu catūsu sautāpattiyaṅgaisu patiṭṭhāya cha vij‍jābhāgiyai dhammai uttari bhāvaiyyāsi–

mā  tvaṃ tāta dīghāvu aivaṃ manasākāsi|

iṅgha tvaṃ tāta dīghāvu yadaiva tai bhagavā āha, tadaiva tvaṃ sādhukaṃ manasikarauhi|

paṇḍitau  bhikkhavai dīghāvu upāsakau|

pac‍capādi dhammassānudhammaṃ|

na  ca maṃ dhammādhikaraṇaṃ vihaiṭhaisi|

dīghāvu bhikkhavai upāsakau pañ‍can‍naṃ aurambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā aupapātikau tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammau tasmā laukā|

vaiḷudvāraiyyasutta

pucchā – taināvusau bhagavatā…pai॰… sammāsambuddhaina sautāpattisaṃyuttai sattamaṃ saṃgītaṃ vaiḷudvāraiyyasuttaṃ kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – kausalaisu bhantai vaiḷudvārai nāma kausalānaṃ brāhmaṇa gāmai vaiḷudvāraiyyakai brāhmaṇagahapatikai ārabbha bhāsitaṃ|

vaiḷudvāraiyyakā  bhantai brāhmaṇagahapatikā bhagavantaṃ aitadavaucuṃ ‘‘mayaṃ bhau gautama aivaṃkāmā aivaṃchandā aivaṃadhippāyā puttasambādhasayanaṃ ajjhāvasaiyyāma…pai॰… jātarūparajataṃ sādiyaiyyāma, kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā sugatiṃ saggaṃ laukaṃ upapaj‍jaiyyāma, taisaṃ nau bhavaṃ gautamau amhākaṃ aivaṃkāmānaṃ aivaṃchandānaṃ aivaṃadhippāyānaṃ tathā dammaṃ daisaitu, yathā mayaṃ puttasambādhasayanaṃ ajjhāvasaiyyāma…pai॰… kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā sugatiṃ saggaṃ laukaṃ upapaj‍jaiyyāmā’’ti| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘attūpanāyikaṃ vau gahapatayau dhammapariyāyaṃ daisaissāmi, taṃ suṇātha sādhukaṃ manasi karautha, bhāsissāmī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

saraṇānivagga

paṭhama mahānāmasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau tatiyai saraṇānivaggai paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi paṭhamaṃ saṃgītaṃ paṭhamamahānāmasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sak‍kaisu  bhantai kapilavatthusmiṃ nigraudhārāmai mahānāmaṃ sak‍kaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| mahānāmau bhantai sak‍kau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca ‘‘idaṃ bhantai kapilavatthu iddhañ‍caiva phītañ‍ca bāhujañ‍ñaṃ ākiṇṇamanussaṃ sambādhabyūhaṃ, sau khvāhaṃ bhantai bhagavantaṃ vā payirupāsitvā manaubhāvanīyai vā bhikkhū sāyanhasamayaṃ kapilavatthuṃ pavisantau bhantainapi hatthinā samāgacchāmi, bhantainapi assaina samāgacchāmi, bhantainapi rathaina samāgacchāmi, bhantainapi sakaṭaina samāgacchāmi, bhantainapi purisaina samāgacchāmi, tassa mayhaṃ bhantai tasmiṃ samayai mussataiva bhagavantaṃ ārabbha sati, mussati dhammaṃ ārabbha sati, mussati saṅghaṃ ārabbha sati, tassa mayhaṃ bhantai aivaṃ hauti imamhi cāhaṃ samayai kālaṃ karaiyyaṃ, kā mayhaṃ gati, kau abhisamparāyau’’ti| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘mā bhāyi mahānāma, mā bhāyi mahānāma, apāpakaṃ tai maraṇaṃ bhavissati apāpikā kālaṃ kiriyā’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

dutiya saraṇānisak‍kasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi pañ‍camaṃ saṃgītaṃ dutiyasaraṇānisak‍kasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sak‍kaisu bhantai kapilavatthusmiṃ nigraudhārāmai mahānāmaṃyaiva sak‍kaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ|

mahānāmau bhantai sak‍kau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca ‘‘idha bhantai saraṇāni sak‍kau kālaṅkatau, sau bhagavatā byākatau ‘sautāpan‍nau avinipātadhammau niyatau sambaudhiparāyaṇau’ti| tatra sudaṃ bhantai sambahulā sak‍kā saṅgamma samāgamma ujjhāyanti khīyanti vipācainti ‘acchariyaṃ vata bhau, abbhutaṃ vata

bhau, aittha dāni kau na sautāpan‍nau bhavissati, yatra hi nāma saraṇāni sak‍kau kālaṅkatau, sau bhagavatā byākatau ‘sautāpan‍nau avinipātadhammau niyatau sambaudhiparāyaṇau’ti, saraṇāni sak‍kau sikkhāya aparipūrakārī ahausī’’ti tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘yau sau mahānāma dīgharattaṃ upāsakau buddhaṃ saraṇaṃ gatau, dhammaṃ saraṇaṃ gatau, saṅghaṃ saraṇaṃ gatau, sau kathaṃ vinipātaṃ gacchaiyyā’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

paṭhama anāthapiṇḍikasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi chaṭṭhaṃ saṃgītaṃ paṭhamaanāthapiṇḍikasuttaṃ kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kaina kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai anāthapiṇḍikaṃ gahapatiṃ ārabbha āyasmatā sāriputtatthairaina dhammasaināpatinā bhāsitaṃ|

āyasmā bhantai sāriputtatthairau āyasmatā ānandatthairaina pacchāsamaṇaina saddhiṃ yaina anāthapiṇḍikassa gahapatissa nivaisanaṃ tainupasaṅkami, upasaṅkamitvā pañ‍ñattai āsanai nisīdi, nisaj‍ja khau āyasmā sāriputtau anāthapiṇḍikaṃ gahapatiṃ ābādhikaṃ dukkhitaṃ bāḷhagilānaṃ aitadavauca ‘‘kac‍ci tai gahapati khamanīyaṃ kac‍ci yāpanīyaṃ, kac‍ci dukkhā vaidanā paṭik‍kamanti, nau abhik‍kamanti, paṭik‍kamausānaṃ pañ‍ñāyati, nau abhik‍kamau’’ti, na mai bhantai khamanīyaṃ, na yāpanīyaṃ, bāḷhā mai dukkhā vaidanā abhik‍kamanti, nau paṭik‍kamanti, abhik‍kamausānaṃ pañ‍ñāyati, nau paṭik‍kamauti| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘yathā rūpaina khau gahapati buddhai appasādaina saman‍nāgatau assutavā puthuj‍janau kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ niyaraṃ upapaj‍jati tathārūpau tai buddhai appasādau natthi, atthi ca khau tai gahapati buddhai avaic‍cappasādau ‘itipi sau bhagavā arahaṃ sammāsambuddhau…pai॰… bhagavā’ti tañ‍ca pana tai buddhai avaic‍cappasādaṃ attani samanupassatau ṭhānasau vaidanā paṭippassambhaiyyā’’ti aivamādinā āyasmatā sāriputtatthairaina dhammasaināpatinā bhāsitaṃ|

yassa  saddhā tathāgatai, acalā suppatiṭṭhitā|

sīlañ‍ca yassa kalyāṇaṃ, ariyakantaṃ pasaṃsitaṃ||

saṅghai pasādau yassatthi, ujubhūtañ‍ca dassanaṃ|

adaliddauti taṃ āhu, amaughaṃ tassa jīvitaṃ||

tasmā saddhañ‍ca sīlañ‍ca, pasādaṃ dhammadassanaṃ|

anuyuñ‍jaitha maidhāvī, saraṃ buddhānasāsanaṃ||

puñ‍ñābhisandavagga

mahānāmasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva āvusau puñ‍ñābhisandavaggau paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi sattamaṃ saṃgītaṃ mahānāmasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sak‍kaisu  bhantai kapilavatthusmiṃ mahānāmaṃ sak‍kaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ|

mahānāmau bhantai sak‍kau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca ‘‘kittāvatā nu khau bhantai upāsakau hautī’’ti| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘yatau khau mahānāma buddhaṃ saraṇaṃ gatau hauti, dhammaṃ saraṇaṃ gatau hauti, saṅghaṃ saraṇaṃ gatau hautī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

kittāvatā nu khau bhantai upāsakau hauti|

kāḷigaudhasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi navamaṃ saṃgītaṃ kāḷigaudhasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ kārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sak‍kaisu bhantai kāḷigaudhaṃ nāma sākiyāniṃ ārabbha ‘‘catūhi khau gaudhai dhammaihi saman‍nāgatā ariyasāvikā sautāpan‍nā hauti avinipātadhammā niyatā sambaudhiparāyaṇā’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

lābhā  tai gaudhai, suladdhaṃ tai gaudhai, sautāpattiphalaṃ tayā gaudhai –

nandiyasak‍kasutta

pucchā – taināvusau jānatā…pai॰… sammāsambuddhaina sautāpattisaṃyuttai puñ‍ñābhisandavaggai dasamaṃ nandiyasak‍kasuttaṃ kattha kaṃ ārabbha kismiṃ vatthusmiṃ kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sak‍kaisu bhantai kapilavatthusmiṃ nigraudhārāmai nandiyaṃ sak‍kaṃ ārabbha bhāsitaṃ| nandiyau bhantai sak‍kau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca ‘‘yassaiva nu khau bhantai ariyasāvakassa cattāri sautāpattiyaṅgāni sabbaina sabbaṃ sabbathāsabbaṃ natthi, svaiva nu khau bhantai ariyasāvakau pamādavihārī’’ti| tasmiṃ bhantai vatthusmiṃ ‘‘yassa khau nandiya cattāri sautāpattiyaṅgāni sabbainasabbaṃ sabbathāsabbaṃ natthi, tamahaṃ bāhirau puthuj‍janapakkhai ṭhitauti vadāmī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

sac‍casaṃyutta

tiracchānakathāsutta

pucchā – sac‍casaṃyuttai  āvusau paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāyakamahāthairaihi dasamaṃ saṃgītaṃ tiracchānakathāsuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha mā bhikkhavai anaikavihitaṃ tiracchānakathaṃ kathaiyyātha| saiyyathidaṃ, rājakathaṃ caurakathaṃ mahāmattakathaṃ sainākathaṃ bhayakathaṃ yuddhakathaṃ an‍nakathaṃ pānakathaṃ vatthakathaṃ sayanakathaṃ aivamādinā bhantai bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

idaṃ  dukkhanti kathaiyyātha|

dhammacak‍kapavattanasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva āvusau dhammacak‍kappavattanavaggai paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi paṭhamaṃ saṃgītaṃ dhammacak‍kapavattanasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – bārāṇasiyaṃ bhantai isipatanai migadāyai pañ‍cavaggiyai bhikkhū ārabbha ‘‘dvai mai bhikkhavai antā pabbajitaina nasaivitabbā| katamai dvai, yau cāyaṃ kāmaisu kāmasukhal‍likānuyaugau hīnau gammau pauthuj‍janikau anariyau anatthasaṃhitau, yau cāyaṃ attakilamathānuyaugau  dukkhau anariyau anatthasaṃhitau, aitai khau bhikkhavai ubhau antai anupagamma majjhimāpaṭipadā tathāgataina abhisambuddhā’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

āsavakkhayasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau kauṭigāmavaggai paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi pañ‍camaṃ saṃgītaṃ āsavakkhayasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sāvatthiyaṃ bhantai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha ‘‘jānatauhaṃ bhikkhavai passatau āsavānaṃ khayaṃ vadāmi, nau ajānatau apassatau| kiñ‍ca bhikkhavai jānatau passatau āsavānaṃ khayau hauti, idaṃ dukkhanti bhikkhavai jānatau passatau āsavānaṃ khayau hauti, ayaṃ dukkhasamudayauti, ayaṃ dukkhaniraudhauti, ayaṃ dukkhaniraudhagāminīpaṭipadāti jānatau passatau āsavānaṃ khayau hauti| aivaṃ khau bhikkhavai jānatau aivaṃ passatau āsavānaṃ khayau hauti| tasmātiha bhikkhavai ‘‘idaṃ dukkha’’nti yaugau karaṇīyau, ayaṃ dukkhasamudayauti, ayaṃ dukkhaniraudhauti, ayaṃ dukkhaniraudhagāminīpaṭipadāti yaugau karaṇīyau’’ti aivaṃ khau bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

sīsapāvanasutta

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau sīsapāvanavaggai paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi paṭhamaṃ saṃgītaṃ sīsapāvanasuttaṃ bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – kausambiyaṃ  bhantai sīsapāvanai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha ‘‘taṃ kiṃ mañ‍ñatha bhikkhavai, katamaṃ nukhau bahutaraṃ yāni vā mayā parittāni sīsapāpaṇṇāni pāṇinā gahitāni yadidaṃ upari sīsapāvanai’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitaṃ|

pañ‍cagatipaiyyālavagga

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau paurāṇakaihi dhammasaṃgāhakamahāthairaihi pariyausānabhāvaina saṃgītau pañ‍cagatipaiyyālavaggai, bhagavatā kattha kaṃ ārabbha kathañ‍ca bhāsitau|

vissaj‍janā – vaisāliyaṃ bhantai sambahulai bhikkhū ārabbha ‘‘taṃ kiṃ mañ‍ñatha bhikkhavai, katamaṃ nu khau bahutaraṃ, yau vāyaṃ mayā parittau nakhasikhāyaṃ paṃsu āraupitau, ayaṃ vā mahāpathavī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā bhāsitau|
