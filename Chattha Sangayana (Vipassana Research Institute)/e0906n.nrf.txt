




|| namau tassa bhagavatau arahatau sammāsambuddhassa||

abhidhammapiṭaka

saṃgāyanassa pucchā vissaj‍janā

pucchā – paṭhamamahāsaṃgītikālai  āvusau dhammasaṃgāhakā mahākassapādayau mahāthairavarā paurāṇasaṃgītikārā paṭhamaṃ vinayapiṭakaṃ saṃgāyitvā suttantapiṭakai ca dīghamajjhimasaṃyuttaaṅguttarasaṃkhātai cattārau mahānikāyai saṃgāyitvā tadanantaraṃ kiṃ nāma pāvacanaṃ saṃgāyiṃsu|

vissaj‍janā – paṭhamamahāsaṃgītikālai bhantai dhammasaṃgāhakā mahākassapādayau mahāthairavarā paurāṇasaṃgītikārā paṭhamaṃ vinayapiṭakaṃ saṃgāyitvā suttantapiṭakai ca dīghamajjhimasaṃyuttaaṅguttarasaṃkhātai cattārau nikāyai saṃgāyitvā tadanantaraṃ abhidhammapiṭakaṃ nāma pāvacanaṃ saṃgāyiṃsu|

pucchā – imissampi  āvusau chaṭṭhasaṃgītiyaṃ sakalañ‍caiva vinayapiṭakaṃ saṃgītaṃ, tai ca cattārau mahānikāyā| kālau dāni āvusau sampattau abhidhammapiṭakaṃ saṃgāyituṃ, tasmāhaṃ taṃ tattha pucchitabbāni pucchissāmi| abhidhammau nāmaisa āvusau kainaṭṭhaina abhidhammauti vuc‍cati|

vissaj‍janā – dhammātiraika dhammavisaisaṭṭhaina bhantai abhidhammauti vuc‍cati|

pucchā – sau  panaisa āvusau abhidhammau kaina kattha kadā ca adhigatau|

vissaj‍janā – sau khau bhantai abhidhammau sabbañ‍ñubuddhaina mahābaudhimaṇḍamūlai vaisākhapuṇṇamiyaṃ yathābhūtaṃ adhigatau|

pucchā – kattha panaisa āvusau abhidhammau bhagavatā kadā ca vicitau|

vissaj‍janā – sau khau bhantai abhidhammau bhagavatā mahābaudhimaṇḍai ratanagharasattāhai vicitau|

pucchā – kattha  panaisa āvusau abhidhammau bhagavatā kadā kassatthāya ca daisitau|

vissaj‍janā – sau khau bhantai abhidhammau bhagavatā daivaisu tāvatiṃsaisu pāricchattakamūlamhi paṇḍukambalasilāyaṃ abhisambaudhitau sattamai vassai mātaraṃ pamukhaṃ katvā dasahi cak‍kavāḷasahassaihi āgamma san‍nisin‍nānaṃ daivatānaṃ caturaughanittharaṇatthāya antau vassaṃ daisitau|

pucchā – kainaisa  āvusau abhidhammau paṭhamaṃ manussalaukai patiggahitau, kassa ca puna taina daisitau|

vissaj‍janā – āyasmatā bhantai sāriputtatthairaina dhammasaināpatinā aisa abhidhammau paṭhamaṃ manussalaukai patiggahitau, tainaiva bhantai āyasmatā sāriputtatthairaina attanau saddhivihārikānaṃ pañ‍can‍naṃ bhikkhusatānaṃ daisitau|

pucchā – kai  āvusau sikkhanti|

vissaj‍janā – saikhā ca bhantai puthuj‍janā kalyāṇakā ca sikkhanti|

pucchā – kai āvusau sikkhitasikkhā|

vissaj‍janā – arahantau bhantai sikkhitasikkhā|

pucchā – kai  āvusau dhārainti|

vissaj‍janā – yaisaṃ bhantai vattati, tai dhārainti|

pucchā – kassa āvusau vacanaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – bhagavatau bhantai vacanaṃ arahatau sammāsambuddhassa|

pucchā – kaināvusau  ābhataṃ|

vissaj‍janā – paraṃparāya bhantai ābhataṃ|

dhammasaṅgaṇī

pucchā – sau  panaisa āvusau abhidhammau dhammasaṅgaṇivibhaṅgādipakaraṇaparicchaidavasaina sattavidhau| taisu paṭhamaṃ bhagavatā kataraṃ pakaraṇaṃ daisitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – paṭhamaṃ bhantai dhammasaṅgaṇipakaraṇaṃ bhagavatā daisitaṃ|

pucchā – dhammasaṅgaṇiyaṃ āvusau atthi mātikā, atthi padabhājanīyaṃ| taisu mātikaṃ nikkhipantaina bhagavatā kataṃ nikkhittā|

vissaj‍janā – mātikaṃ bhantai nikkhipantaina bhagavatā ‘‘kusalā dhammā akusalā dhammā abyākatā dhammā| sukhāya vaidanāya sampayuttā dhammā dukkhāya vaidanāya sampayuttā dhammā adukkhamasukhāya vaidanāya sampayuttā dhammā| vipākā dhammā vipākadhammadhammā naivavipākanavipākadhammadhammā’’ti aivamādinā dvāvīsatiyā tikānaṃ vasaina| ‘‘haitū dhammā na haitū dhammā| sahaitukā dhammā ahaitukā dhammā|

haitusampayuttā  dhammā haituvippayuttā dhammā| haitū caiva dhammā sahaitukā ca sahaitukā caiva dhammā na ca haitū| haitū caiva dhammā haitusampayuttā ca haitusampayuttā caiva dhammā na ca haitū| na haitū khau pana dhammā sahaitukāpi ahaitukāpī’’ti aivamādinā dukasatānañ‍ca vasaina mātikā nikkhittā|

kāmāvacarakusala

pucchā – abhidhammapiṭakai  āvusau dhammasaṅgaṇiyaṃ padabhājanīyai cittuppādakaṇḍai kusalā dhammātipadassa atthaṃ vibhajantaina bhagavatā kathaṃ kāmāvacarakusalaṃ vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai dhammasaṅgaṇipakaraṇai cittuppādakaṇḍai kusalā dhammāti padassa atthaṃ vibhajantaina bhagavatā ‘‘katamai dhammā kusalā| yasmiṃ samayai kāmāvacaraṃ kusalaṃ cittaṃ uppan‍naṃ hauti saumanassasahagataṃ ñāṇasampayuttaṃ rūpārammaṇaṃ vā saddārammaṇaṃ vā gandhārammaṇaṃ vā rasārammaṇaṃ vā phauṭṭhabbārammaṇaṃ vā dhammārammaṇaṃ vā yaṃ yaṃ vā panārabbha| tasmiṃ samayai phassau hauti vaidanā hauti sañ‍ñā hauti caitanā hauti cittaṃ hauti vitak‍kau hauti vicārau hauti pīti hauti sukhaṃ hauti cittassaikaggatā hautī’’ti aivamādinā kāmāvacarakusalaṃ tīhi mahāvāraihi vibhajitvā daisitaṃ|

rūpāvacarakusala

pucchā – abhidhammapiṭakai  āvusau dhammasaṅgaṇīpāḷiyaṃ cittuppādakaṇḍai kusalā dhammāti padassa atthaṃ vibhajantaina bhagavatā kathaṃ rūpāvacarakusalañ‍ca arūpāvacarakusalañ‍ca vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – rūpāvacarakusalañ‍ca arūpāvacarakusalañ‍ca bhantai ‘‘katamai dhammā kusalā| yasmiṃ samayai rūpūpapattiyā maggaṃ bhāvaiti vivic‍caiva kāmaihi vivic‍ca akusalaihi dhammaihi savitak‍kaṃ savitak‍kaṃ vivaikajaṃ pītisukhaṃ paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ upasampaj‍ja viharati pathavīkasiṇa’’nti aivamādinā bhagavatā vitthāratau daisitaṃ|

laukuttarākusala

pucchā – abhidhammapiṭakai  āvusau dhammasaṅgaṇīpāḷiyaṃ cittuppādakaṇḍai kusalā dhammāti padassa atthaṃ vibhajantaina bhagavatā kathaṃ laukuttarakusalaṃ vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai dhammasaṅgaṇiyaṃ cittuppādakaṇḍai kusalā dhammāti padassa atthaṃ vibhajantaina bhagavatā ‘‘katamai dhammā kusalā| yasmiṃ samayai laukuttaraṃ jhānaṃ bhāvaiti niyyānikaṃ apacayagāmiṃ diṭṭhigatānaṃ pahānāya paṭhamāya bhūmiyā pattiyā vivic‍caiva kāmaihi…pai॰… paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ upasampaj‍ja viharati dukkhapaṭipadaṃ dandhābhiñ‍ña’’nti aivamādinā bhagavatā laukuttarakusalaṃ vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitaṃ|

akusala

pucchā – tatthaiva  āvusau akusalā dhammāti mātikāpadassa atthaṃ vibhajantaina bhagavatā kathaṃ akusalaṃ vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – tatthaiva bhantai akusalā dhammāti mātikāpadassa atthaṃ vibhajantaina bhagavatā ‘‘katamai dhammā akusalā| yasmiṃ samayai akusalaṃ cittaṃ uppan‍naṃ hauti saumanassasahagataṃ diṭṭhigatasampayuttaṃ rūpārammaṇaṃ vā saddhārammaṇaṃ vā gandhārammaṇaṃ vā rasārammaṇaṃ vā phauṭṭhabbārammaṇaṃ vā dhammārammaṇaṃ vā yaṃ yaṃ vā panārabbha| tasmiṃ samayai phassau hauti vaidanā hauti sañ‍ñā hauti caitanā hauti cittaṃ hautī’’ti aivamādinā akusalaṃ vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitaṃ|

abyākata

pucchā – abhidhammapiṭakai āvusau dhammasaṅgaṇīpāḷiyaṃ cittuppādakaṇḍarūpakaṇḍaisu abyākatā dhammāti mātikāpadassa atthaṃ vibhajantaina  bhagavatā kathaṃ abyākatā dhammā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai dhammasaṅgaṇiyaṃ abyākatā dhammāti padassa atthaṃ vibhajantaina bhagavatā ‘‘katamai dhammā abyākatā| yasmiṃ samayai kāmāvacarassa kusalassa kammassa katattā upacitattā vipākaṃ cakkhuviñ‍ñāṇaṃ uppan‍naṃ hauti upaikkhāsahagataṃ rūpārammaṇaṃ| tasmiṃ samayai phassau hauti vaidanā hauti sañ‍ñā hauti caitanā hauti cittaṃ hautī’’ti aivamādinā ca cittuppādakaṇḍai, rūpakaṇḍai ca ‘‘katamai dhammā abyākatā| kusalākusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ vipākā kāmāvacarā rūpāvacarā arūpāvacarā apariyāpan‍nā vaidanākkhandhau sañ‍ñākkhandhau saṅkhārakkhandhau viñ‍ñāṇakkhandhau| yai ca dhammā kiriyā naiva kusalā nākusalā na ca kammavipākā| sabbañ‍ca rūpaṃ asaṅkhatā ca dhātu| imai dhammā abyākatā’’ti aivamādinā ca abyākatā dhammā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

vibhaṅga

pucchā – sattasu  āvusau abhidhammappakaraṇaisu vibhaṅgappakaraṇai bhagavatā kati vibhaṅgā daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai sattasu pakaraṇaisu dutiyai vibhaṅgappakaraṇai khandhavibhaṅgau āyatanavibhaṅgau dhātuvibhaṅgau sac‍cavibhaṅgau indriyavibhaṅgau paṭic‍casamuppādavibhaṅgau satipaṭṭhānavibhaṅgau sammappadhānavibhaṅgau iddhipādavibhaṅgau baujjhaṅgavibhaṅgau maggaṅgavibhaṅgau jhānavibhaṅgau appamañ‍ñāvibhaṅgau sikkhāpadavibhaṅgau paṭisambhidāvibhaṅgau ñāṇavibhaṅgau khuddakavatthuvibhaṅgau dhammahadayavibhaṅgauti bhagavatā aṭṭhārasa vibhaṅgā daisitā|

khandhavibhaṅga

pucchā – taisu āvusau aṭṭhārasasu vibhaṅgaisu paṭhamai khandhavibhaṅgai bhagavatā kathaṃ khandhā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – taisu  bhantai aṭṭhārasasu vibhaṅgaisu paṭhamai khandhavibhaṅgai ‘‘pañ‍cakkhandhā rūpakkhandhau vaidanākkhandhau sañ‍ñākkhandhau saṅkhārakkhandhau viñ‍ñāṇakkhandhau| tattha katamau rūpakkhandhau, yaṃkiñ‍ci rūpaṃ atītānāgatapac‍cuppan‍naṃ ajjhattaṃ vā bahiddhā vā auḷārikaṃ vā sukhumaṃ vā hīnaṃ vā paṇītaṃ vā yaṃ dūrai santikai vā, tadaikajjhaṃ abhisañ‍ñūhitvā abhisaṅkhipitvā ayaṃ vuc‍cati rūpakkhandhau’’ti aivamādinā suttantabhājanīyaabhidhammabhājanīyapañhāpucchakasaṅkhātaihi tīhi mahānayaihi bhagavatā khandhā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

āyatanavibhaṅga

pucchā – dutiyai  pana āvusau āyatanavibhaṅgai bhagavatā kathaṃ āyatanāni vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitāni|

vissaj‍janā – dutiyai bhantai āyatanavibhaṅgai ‘‘dvādasāyatanāni cakkhāyatanaṃ rūpāyatanaṃ sautāyatanaṃ saddāyatanaṃ ghānāyatanaṃ gandhāyatanaṃ jivhāyatanaṃ rasāyatanaṃ kāyāyatanaṃ phauṭṭhabbāyatanaṃ manāyatanaṃ dhammāyatanaṃ| cakkhuṃ anic‍caṃ dukkhaṃ anattā vipariṇāmadhammaṃ, rūpā anic‍cā dukkhā anattā vipariṇāmadhammā’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā suttantabhājanīyaabhidhammabhājanīyapañhāpucchakasaṅkhātaihi tīhaiva āyatanāni vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitāni|

dhātuvibhaṅga

pucchā – abhidhammapiṭakai  āvusau vibhaṅgappakaraṇai tatiyai dhātuvibhaṅgai bhagavatā kathaṃ dhātuyau vitthāraina vibhajitvā daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – tatiyai bhantai dhātuvibhaṅgai ‘‘cha dhātuyau pathavīdhātu āpaudhātu taijaudhātu vāyaudhātu ākāsadhātu viñ‍ñāṇadhātū’’ti bhantai suttantabhājanīyaabhidhammabhājanīyapañhāpucchakasaṅkhātaihi tīhi mahānayaihi bhagavatā dhātuyau vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

sac‍cavibhaṅga

pucchā – catutthai  pana āvusau sac‍cavibhaṅgai bhagavatā kathaṃ sac‍cāni vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitāni|

vissaj‍janā – catutthai bhantai sac‍cavibhaṅgai ‘‘cattāri ariyasac‍cāni dukkhaṃ ariyasac‍caṃ, dukkhasamudayaṃ ariyasac‍caṃ, dukkhaniraudhaṃ ariyasac‍caṃ, dukkhaniraudhagāminī paṭipadā ariyasac‍caṃ| tattha katamaṃ dukkhaṃ ariyasac‍caṃ, jātipi dukkhā, jarāpi dukkhā, maraṇampi dukkhaṃ, saukaparidaivadukkhadaumanassupāyāsāpi dukkhā, appiyaihi sampayaugau dukkhau, piyaihi vippayaugau dukkhau, yampicchaṃ na labhati, tampi dukkhaṃ, saṃkhittaina pañ‍cupādānakkhandhā dukkhā’’ti aivamādinā suttantabhājanīyaabhidhammabhājanīyapañhāpucchakasaṅkhātaihi tīhi mahānayaihi bhagavatā sac‍cāni vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitāni|

indriyavibhaṅga

pucchā – pañ‍camai  pana āvusau indriyavibhaṅgai bhagavatā kathaṃ indriyāni vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitāni|

vissaj‍janā – pañ‍camai pana bhantai indriyavibhaṅgai ‘‘bāvīsatindriyāni cakkhundriyaṃ sautindriyaṃ ghānindriyaṃ jivhindriyaṃ kāyindriyaṃ manindriyaṃ…pai॰… añ‍ñātāvindriya’’nti aivamādinā abhidhammabhājanīyapañhāpucchakasaṅkhātaihi dvīhi mahānayaihi bhagavatā indriyāni vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitāni|

paṭic‍casamuppāda vibhaṅga

pucchā – abhidhammapiṭakai  āvusau vibhaṅgappakaraṇai chaṭṭhai paṭic‍casamuppādavibhaṅgai bhagavatā kathaṃ paṭic‍casamuppādau vitthāraina vibhajitvā daisitau|

vissaj‍janā – chaṭṭhai bhantai paṭic‍casamuppādavibhaṅgai ‘‘avij‍jāpac‍cayā saṅkhārā…pai॰… samudayau hautī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā paṭic‍casamuppādau suttantabhājanīyaabhidhammabhājanīyasaṅkhātaihi dvīhi mahānayaihi vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitau|

satipaṭṭhānavibhaṅga

pucchā – sattamai  pana āvusau satipaṭṭhānavibhaṅgai bhagavatā kathaṃ satipaṭṭhānā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – sattamai bhantai satipaṭṭhānavibhaṅgai ‘‘cattārau satipaṭṭhānā idha bhikkhu ajjhattaṃ kāyai kāyānupassī viharati, bahiddhā kāyai kāyānupassī viharati, ajjhattabahiddhā kāyai kāyānupassī viharati ātāpī sampajānau satimā vinaiyya laukai abhijjhādaumanassa’’nti aivamādinā suttantabhājanīyaabhidhammabhājanīyapañhāpucchakasaṅkhātaihi tīhi mahānayaihi bhagavatā satipaṭṭhānā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

sammappadhānavibhaṅga

pucchā – aṭṭhamai  pana āvusau sammappadhānavibhaṅgai bhagavatā kathaṃ sammappadhānā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – aṭṭhamai bhantai sammappadhānavibhaṅgai ‘‘cattārau sammappadhānā idha bhikkhu anuppan‍nānaṃ pāpakānaṃ akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ anuppādāya chandaṃ janaiti vāyamati vīriyaṃ ārabhati cittaṃ paggaṇhāti padahati, uppan‍nānaṃ pāpakānaṃ akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ pahānāya chandaṃ janaiti vāyamati vīriyaṃ ārabhati cittaṃ paggaṇhāti padahati, anuppan‍nānaṃ kusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ uppādāya chandaṃ janaiti vāyamati vīriyaṃ ārabhati cittaṃ paggaṇhāti padahati, uppan‍nānaṃ kusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ ṭhitiyā asammausāya bhiyyaubhāvāya vaipul‍lāya bhāvanāya pāripūriyā chandaṃ janaiti vāyamati vīriyaṃ ārabhati cittaṃ paggaṇhāti padahatī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā sammappadhānā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

iddhipādavibhaṅga

pucchā – navamai  pana āvusau iddhipādavibhaṅgai bhagavatā kathaṃ iddhipādā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – navamai bhantai iddhipādavibhaṅgai ‘‘cattārau iddhipādā idha bhikkhu chandasamādhipadhānasaṅkhārasaman‍nāgataṃ iddhipādaṃ bhāvaiti…pai॰… vīmaṃsāsamādhipadhānasaṅkhārasaman‍nāgataṃ iddhipādaṃ bhāvaitī’’ti aivamādinā tīhi mahānayaihi bhagavatā iddhipādā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

baujjhaṅgavibhaṅga

pucchā – abhidhammapiṭakai  āvusau vibhaṅgappakaraṇai dasamai baujjhaṅgavibhaṅgai bhagavatā kathaṃ baujjhaṅgā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – dasamai bhantai baujjhaṅgavibhaṅgai ‘‘satta baujjhaṅgā satisambaujjhaṅgau dhammavicayasambaujjhaṅgau vīriyasambaujjhaṅgau pītisambaujjhaṅgau passaddhisambaujjhaṅgau samādhisambaujjhaṅgau upaikkhāsambaujjhaṅgau| tattha katamau satisambaujjhaṅgau, idha bhikkhu satimā hauti paramaina satinaipak‍kaina saman‍nāgatau cirakatampi cirabhāsitampi saritā hauti anussaritā, ayaṃ vuc‍cati satisambaujjhaṅgau’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā suttantabhājanīyaabhidhammabhājanīyapañhāpucchakasaṅkhātaihi tīhi nayaihi baujjhaṅgā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

maggaṅgavibhaṅga

pucchā – aikādasamai  pana āvusau maggaṅgavibhaṅgai bhagavatā kathaṃ maggaṅgā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – aikādasamai  bhantai maggaṅgavibhaṅgai ‘‘ariyau aṭṭhaṅgikau maggau| saiyyathidaṃ, sammādiṭṭhi sammāsaṅkappau sammāvācā sammākammantau sammāājīvau sammāvāyāmau sammāsati sammāsamādhī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā suttantabhājanīyaabhidhammabhājanīyapañhāpucchakasaṅkhātaihi tīhi mahānayaihi maggaṅgā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

jhānavibhaṅga

pucchā – dvādasamau  pana āvusau jhānavibhaṅgau bhagavatā kathaṃ vitthāraina vibhajitvā daisitau|

vissaj‍janā – dvādasamau bhantai jhānavibhaṅgau ‘‘idha bhikkhu pātimaukkhasaṃvarasaṃvutau viharati ācāragaucarasampan‍nau aṇumattaisu vaj‍jaisu bhayadassāvī samādāya sikkhati sikkhāpadaisu indriyaisu guttadvārau bhaujanai mattañ‍ñū pubbarattāpararattaṃ jāgariyānuyaugamanuyuttau’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitau|

appamañ‍ñāvibhaṅga

pucchā – tairasamai  pana āvusau appamañ‍ñāvibhaṅgai bhagavatā kathaṃ appamañ‍ñāyau vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – tairasamai bhantai appamañ‍ñāvibhaṅgai ‘‘catassau appamañ‍ñāyau idha bhikkhu maittāsahagataina caitasā aikaṃ disaṃ pharitvā viharati, tathā dutiyaṃ| tathā tatiyaṃ| tathā catutthaṃ| iti uddhamadhau tiriyaṃ sabbadhi sabbattatāya sabbāvantaṃ laukaṃ maittāsahagataina caitasā vipulaina mahaggataina appamāṇaina avairaina abyāpaj‍jaina pharitvā viharatī’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā suttantabhājanīyaabhidhammabhājanīyapañhāpucchakasaṅkhātaihi tīhi mahānayaihi appamañ‍ñāyau vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

sikkhāpadavibhaṅga

pucchā – abhidhammapiṭakai  āvusau vibhaṅgappakaraṇai cuddasamai sikkhāpadavibhaṅgai bhagavatā kathaṃ sikkhāpadāni vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitāni|

vissaj‍janā – cuddasamai bhantai sikkhāpadavibhaṅgai ‘‘pañ‍ca sikkhāpadāni pāṇātipātā vairamaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ, adin‍nādānā vairamaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ, kāmaisumicchācārā vairamaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ, musāvādā vairamaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ, surāmairayamaj‍japamādaṭṭhānā vairamaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ| tattha katamaṃ pāṇātipātā vairamaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ, yasmiṃ samayai kāmāvacaraṃ kusalaṃ cittaṃ uppan‍naṃ hauti saumanassasahagataṃ ñāṇasampayuttaṃ pāṇātipātā viramantassā’’ti aivamādinā abhidhammabhājanīyapañhāpucchakasaṅkhātaihi dvīhi mahānayaihi bhagavatā sikkhāpadāni vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitāni|

paṭisambhidāvibhaṅga

pucchā – pan‍narasamai  pana āvusau paṭisambhidāvibhaṅgai bhagavatā kathaṃ paṭisambhidāyau vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – pan‍narasamai bhantai paṭisambhidāvibhaṅgai ‘‘catassau paṭisambhidā atthapaṭisambhidā dhammapaṭisambhidā niruttipaṭisambhidā paṭibhānapaṭisambhidā| atthai ñāṇaṃ atthapaṭisambhidā, dhammai ñāṇaṃ dhammapaṭisambhidā, tatra dhammaniruttābhilāpai ñāṇaṃ niruttipaṭisambhidā, ñāṇaisu ñāṇaṃ paṭibhānapaṭisambhidā’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā catassau paṭisambhidāyau suttantabhājanīyaabhidhammabhājanīyapañhāpucchakasaṅkhātaihi  tīhi mahānayaihi vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

ñāṇavibhaṅga

pucchā – sauḷasamai pana āvusau ñāṇavibhaṅgai bhagavatā kathaṃ ñāṇāni vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitāni|

vissaj‍janā – sauḷasamai bhantai ñāṇavibhaṅgai ‘‘aikavidhaina ñāṇavatthu pañ‍caviñ‍ñāṇā na haitū, ahaitukā, haituvippayuttā, sappac‍cayā, saṅkhatā, arūpā, laukiyā, sāsavā’’ti aivamādinā aikakatau paṭṭhāya yāva dasakā bhagavatā ñāṇāni vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitāni|

khuddakavatthuvibhaṅga

pucchā – abhidhammapiṭakai āvusau vibhaṅgappakaraṇai sattarasamau khuddakavatthuvibhaṅgau bhagavatā kathaṃ daisitau|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai dutiyai vibhaṅgappakaraṇai sattarasamau khuddakavatthuvibhaṅgau ‘‘jātimadau, gauttamadau, āraugyamadau, yaubbanamadau, jīvitamadau’’ti aivamādinā aikakatau paṭṭhāya yāva aṭṭhasatataṇhāvicaritaṃ bhagavatā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitau|

dhammahadayavibhaṅga

pucchā – tattha  āvusau aṭṭhārasamau dhammahadayavibhaṅgau bhagavatā kathaṃ vibhajitvā daisitau|

vissaj‍janā – aṭṭhārasamau  bhantai dhammahadayavibhaṅgau ‘‘kati khandhā, kati āyatanāni, kati dhātuyau, kati sac‍cāni, kati indriyāni, kati haitū, kati āhārā, kati phassā, kati vaidanā, kati sañ‍ñā, kati caitanā, kati cittāni| pañ‍cakkhandhā, dvādasāyatanāni, aṭṭhārasa dhātuyau, cattāri sac‍cāni, bāvīsatindriyāni, nava haitū, cattārau āhārā, satta phassā, satta vaidanā, satta sañ‍ñā, satta caitanā, satta cittānī’’ti aivamādinā sabbasaṅgāhakādīhi dasahi vāraihi bhagavatā vitthāraina vibhajitvā daisitau|

pucchā – kathañ‍cāvusau tattha bhagavatā uppādakakammaāyuppamāṇavārau vitthāraina vibhajitvā daisitau|

vissaj‍janā – dānaṃ datvā sīlaṃ samādiyitvā upausathakammaṃ katvā kattha upapaj‍janti| dānaṃ datvā, sīlaṃ samādiyitvā upausathakammaṃ katvā  appaikac‍cai khattiyamahāsālānaṃ sahabyataṃ upapaj‍janti, appaikac‍cai brāhmaṇamahāsālānaṃ, appaikac‍cai gahapatimahāsālānaṃ, appaikac‍cai cātumahārājikānaṃ daivānaṃ, appaikac‍cai tāvatiṃsānaṃ daivānaṃ, appaikac‍cai yāmānaṃ daivānaṃ, appaikac‍cai tusitānaṃ daivānaṃ sahabyataṃ upapaj‍jantīti aivamādinā bhantai tattha bhagavatā uppādakakammaāyuppamāṇavārau vitthāraina vibhajitvā daisitau|

ukkhittā  puñ‍ñataijaina, kāmarūpagatiṃ gatā|

bhavaggatampi sampattā, punāgacchanti duggatiṃ –

dhātukathā

pucchā – taināvusau  bhagavatā aṭṭhārasahi buddhadhammaihi saman‍nāgataina abhidhammai aṭṭhārasahi vibhaṅgaihi paṭimaṇḍitaṃ dutiyaṃ vibhaṅgappakaraṇaṃ daisaitvā tadanantaraṃ dhātukathaṃ nāma tatiyaṃ pakaraṇaṃ kathaṃ vibhajitvā daisitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – tatiyaṃ bhantai dhātukathaṃ nāma abhidhammapiṭakaṃ ‘‘saṅgahau asaṅgahau, saṅgahitaina asaṅgahitaṃ, asaṅgahitaina saṅgahitaṃ, saṅgahitaina saṅgahitaṃ, asaṅgahitaina asaṅgahitaṃ| sampayaugau vippayaugau, sampayuttaina vippayuttaṃ, vippayuttaina sampayuttaṃ, sampayuttaina sampayuttaṃ, vippayuttaina vippayuttaṃ| saṅgahitaina sampayuttaṃ vippayuttaṃ, sampayuttaina saṅgahitaṃ asaṅgahitaṃ, asaṅgahitaina sampayuttaṃ vippayuttaṃ, vippayuttaina saṅgahitaṃ asaṅgahita’’nti aivamādinā pañ‍ca mātikāyau paṭhamaṃ nikkhipitvā saṅgahāsaṅgahādīhi cuddasahi nayaihi bhagavatā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitaṃ|

puggalapañ‍ñatti

pucchā – taināvusau  bhagavatā sabbadhātukusalaina abhidhammapiṭakai tatiyaṃ dhātukathāpakaraṇaṃ daisaitvā tadanantaraṃ catutthaṃ puggalapañ‍ñattippakaraṇaṃ kathaṃ vibhajitvā daisitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai catutthaṃ puggalapañ‍ñattippakaraṇaṃ ‘‘cha pañ‍ñattiyau khandhapañ‍ñatti āyatanapañ‍ñatti dhātupañ‍ñatti sac‍capañ‍ñatti indriyapañ‍ñatti puggalapañ‍ñatti| kittāvatā khandhānaṃ khandhapañ‍ñatti, yāvatā pañ‍cakkhandhā, rūpakkhandhau vaidanākkhandhau sañ‍ñākkhandhau saṅkhārakkhandhau viñ‍ñāṇakkhandhau| aittāvatā khandhānaṃ khandhapañ‍ñattī’’ti aivamādinā khandhāyatanadhātusac‍caindriyapañ‍ñattiyau saṃkhittaina uddisitvā puggalapañ‍ñattiṃ ca aikakadukatikacatuk‍kapañ‍cakachak‍kasattakaaṭṭhakanavakadasakavasaina vitthāratau vibhajitvā bhagavatā daisitā|

kathāvatthu

pucchā – taināvusau  bhagavatā sabbaparamatthapañ‍ñattidhammakusalaina abhidhammapiṭakai catutthaṃ puggalapañ‍ñattippakaraṇaṃ daisaitvā tadanantaraṃ pañ‍camaṃ kathāvatthuppakaraṇaṃ kathaṃ daisitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – sabbaparamatthapañ‍ñattidhammakusalaina bhantai bhagavatā laukai aggapuggalaina abhidhammapiṭakai catutthaṃ puggalapañ‍ñattippakaraṇaṃ daisaitvā tadanantaraṃ kathāvatthudaisanāya vārai sampattai ‘‘anāgatai mama sāvakau mauggaliputtatissatthairau nāma uppan‍naṃ sāsanamalaṃ saudhaitvā tatiyasaṅgītiṃ karauntau bhikkhusaṅghassa majjhai nisin‍nau sakavādai pañ‍ca suttasatāni paravādai pañ‍cāti suttasahassaṃ samaudhānaitvā, imaṃ pakaraṇaṃ bhājaissatī’’ti tassaukāsaṃ karauntaina puggalavārai aṭṭhamukhā vādayuttiṃ ādiṃ katvā sabbakathāmaggaisu asampuṇṇabhāṇavāratthāya tantiyā mātikā nikkhaipamattaina pañ‍camaṃ kathāvatthuppakaraṇaṃ saṃkhaipatau daisitaṃ|

pucchā – adhunā  pākaṭaṃ panāvusau vitthāraṃ kathāvatthuppakaraṇaṃ kadā kaina kattha kathañ‍ca vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – taṃ khau bhantai bhagavatau nayamukhaṃ nissāya āyasmatā mahāmauggaliputtatissatthairaina tatiyasaṃgītikālai pāṭaliputtai asaukārāmai saṭṭhibhikkhusatasahassānaṃ samāgamai bhagavatā din‍nanayavasaina tathāgataina ṭhapitamātikaṃ vibhajantaina sakavādai pañ‍casuttasatāni paravādai pañ‍cāti suttasahassaṃ āharitvā idaṃ paravādamathanaṃ adhunā pākaṭaṃ kathāvatthuppakaraṇaṃ vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitaṃ|

pucchā – kati  āvusau tattha kathāyau taina āyasmatā mahāmauggaliputtatissatthairaina vitthāratau vibhajitvā kathitā|

vissaj‍janā – tattha bhantai vācanāmaggatau dvai ca satāni sattavīsati ca kathāyau āyasmatā mahāmauggaliputtatissatthairaina vitthāratau vibhajitvā kathitā|

puggalakathā

pucchā – kathañ‍cāvusau  tattha paṭhamā puggalakathā āyasmatā mahāmauggaliputtatissatthairaina vitthāratau vibhajitvā kathitā|

vissaj‍janā – puggalau upalabbhati sac‍cikaṭṭhaparamatthaināti, āmantā| yau sac‍cikaṭṭhau paramatthau, tatau sau puggalau upalabbhati sac‍cikaṭṭhaparamatthaināti, na haivaṃ vattabbai| ājānāhi niggahaṃ hañ‍ci puggalau upalabbhati sac‍cikaṭṭhaparamatthaina, taina vata rai vattabbai ‘‘yau sac‍cikaṭṭhau paramatthau, tatau sau puggalau upalabbhati sac‍cikaṭṭhaparamatthainā’’ti| yaṃ tattha vadaisi ‘‘vattabbai khau ‘puggalau upalabbhati sac‍cikaṭṭhaparamatthaina’, nau ca vattabbai ‘yau sac‍cikaṭṭhau paramatthau, tatau sau puggalau upalabbhati sac‍cikaṭṭhaparamatthainā’’ti, micchāti aivamādinā bhantai tattha paṭhamā puggalakathā pañ‍ca bhāṇavāramattāya tantiyā āyasmatā mahāmauggaliputtatissatthairaina vitthāratau vibhajitvā kathitā|

añ‍ñāṇakathā

pucchā – abhidhammapiṭakai  āvusau kathāvatthuppakaraṇai dutiyavaggai dutiyā añ‍ñāṇakathā āyasmatā mahāmauggaliputtatissatthairaina kathaṃ vitthāratau vibhajitvā kathitā|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai pañ‍camai kathāvatthuppakaraṇai dutiyavaggai dutiyā añ‍ñāṇakathā ‘‘atthi arahatau añ‍ñāṇanti, āmantā| atthi arahatau avij‍jā avij‍jaughau avij‍jāyaugau avij‍jānusayau avij‍jāpariyuṭṭhānaṃ avij‍jāsaṃyaujanaṃ avij‍jānīvaraṇanti, na haivaṃ vattabbai| natthi arahatau avij‍jā avij‍jaughau avij‍jāyaugau avij‍jānusayau avij‍jāpariyuṭṭhānaṃ avij‍jāsaṃyaujanaṃ avij‍jānivaraṇanti, āmantā| hañ‍ci natthi arahatau avij‍jā avij‍jaughau avij‍jāyaugau avij‍jānusayau avij‍jāpariyuṭṭhānaṃ avij‍jāsaṃyaujanaṃ avij‍jānīvaraṇaṃ, nau ca vata rai vattabbai ‘‘atthi arahatau añ‍ñāṇa’’nti aivamādinā āyasmatā mahāmauggaliputtatissatthairaina vitthāratau vibhajitvā bhāsitā|

asañ‍ñakathā

pucchā – abhidhammapiṭakai  āvusau pañ‍camai kathāvatthuppakaraṇai tatiyavaggai aikādasamā asañ‍ñakathā āyasmatā mahāmauggaliputtatissatthairaina kathaṃ vitthāratau vibhajitvā kathitā|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai pañ‍camai kathāvatthuppakaraṇai tatiyavaggai aikādasamā asañ‍ñakathā ‘‘asañ‍ñasattaisu sañ‍ñā atthīti, āmantā| sañ‍ñābhavau sañ‍ñāgati sañ‍ñāsattāvāsau sañ‍ñāsaṃsārau sañ‍ñāyauni sañ‍ñattabhāvapaṭilābhauti, na haivaṃ vattabbai’’ti aivamādinā āyasmatā mahāmauggaliputtatissatthairaina vitthāratau vibhajitvā kathitā|

asañ‍ñasattaisu  sañ‍ñā atthi|

sañ‍ñābhavau  sañ‍ñāgati sañ‍ñāsattāvāsau sañ‍ñāsaṃsārau sañ‍ñāyauni sañ‍ñattabhāvapaṭilābhau|

na haivaṃ vattabbai|

nanu asañ‍ñabhavau asañ‍ñagati asañ‍ñasattāvāsau asañ‍ñasaṃsārau asañ‍ñayauni asañ‍ñattabhāvapaṭilābhau|

hañ‍ci  asañ‍ñabhavau asañ‍ñagati asañ‍ñasattāvāsau asañ‍ñasaṃsārau asañ‍ñayauni asañ‍ñattabhāvapaṭilābhau, nau ca vata rai vattabbai asañ‍ñasattaisu sañ‍ñā atthi|

asañ‍ñasattaisu sañ‍ñā atthi|

pañ‍cavaukārabhavau gati sattāvāsau saṃsārau yauni attabhāvapaṭilābhau|

nanu  aikavaukārabhavau gati sattāvāsau saṃsārau yauni attabhāvapaṭilābhau|

hañ‍ci aikavaukārabhavau gati sattāvāsau saṃsārau yauni attabhāvapaṭilābhau, nau ca vata rai vattabbai asañ‍ñasattaisu sañ‍ñā atthi|

pucchā – chaṭṭhavaggai  pana āvusau aṭṭhamā pathavīdhātu sanidassanātiādikathā āyasmatā mahāmauggaliputtatissatthairaina kathaṃ vitthāratau vibhajitvā kathitā|

vissaj‍janā – chaṭṭhavaggai bhantai aṭṭhamā pathavīdhātu sanidassanātiādikathā ‘‘pathavīdhātu sanidassanāti āmantā| rūpaṃ rūpāyatanaṃ rūpadhātu nīlaṃ pītakaṃ lauhitakaṃ audātaṃ cakkhuviñ‍ñaiyyaṃ cakkhusmiṃ paṭihañ‍ñati cakkhussa āpāthaṃ āgacchatīti, na haivaṃ vattabbai’’ti aivamādinā āyasmatā mahāmauggali puttatissatthairaina vitthāratau vibhajitvā kathitā|

antarābhavakathā

pucchā – abhidhammapiṭakai āvusau pañ‍camai kathāvatthuppakaraṇai aṭṭhamavaggai dutiyā antarābhavakathā āyasmatā mahāmauggaliputtatissatthairaina  kathaṃ vitthāratau vibhajitvā kathitā|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai pañ‍camai kathāvatthuppakaraṇai aṭṭhamavaggai dutiyā antarābhavakathā ‘‘atthi antarābhavauti, āmantā| kāmabhavauti, na haivaṃ vattabbai…pai॰… atthi antarābhavauti, āmantā| rūpabhavauti, na haivaṃ vattabbai…pai॰… atthi antarābhavauti, āmantā| arūpabhavauti, na haivaṃ vattabbai’’ti aivamādinā āyasmatā mahāmauggaliputtatissatthairaina vitthāratau vibhajitvā kathitā|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai pañ‍camai kathāvatthuppakaraṇai cuddasamaivaggai dutiyā saḷāyatanuppattikathā ‘‘saḷāyatanaṃ apubbaṃ acarimaṃ mātukucchismiṃ saṇṭhātīti, āmantā| sabbaṅgapac‍caṅgī ahīnindriyau mātukucchismiṃ auk‍kamatīti, na haivaṃ vattabbai’’ti aivamādinā  āyasmatā mahāmauggaliputtatissatthairaina vitthāratau vibhajitvā kathitā|

vissaj‍janā – taisu bhantai dvāvīsatiyā tikaisu paṭhamai kusalattikai sattasu mahāvāraisu paṭhamau paṭic‍camahāvārau siyā kusalaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca kusalau dhammau uppaj‍jaiyya haitupac‍cayā| siyā kusalaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca akusalau dhammau uppaj‍jaiyya haitupac‍cayā| siyā kusalaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca abyākatau dhammau uppaj‍jaiyya haitupac‍cayā| siyā kusalaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca kusalā ca abyākatā ca dhammā uppaj‍jaiyyuṃ haitupac‍cayā| siyā kusalaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca akusalau ca abyākatau ca dhammā uppaj‍jaiyyuṃ haitupac‍cayā| siyā kusalaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca kusalau ca akusalau ca dhammā uppaj‍jaiyyuṃ haitupac‍cayā| siyā kusalaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca kusalau ca akusalau ca abyākatau ca dhammā uppaj‍jaiyyuṃ haitupac‍cayā’’ti aivamādinā pucchāvāraina ca| ‘‘kusalaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca kusalau dhammau uppaj‍jati haitupac‍cayā| kusalaṃ aikaṃ khandhaṃ paṭic‍ca tayau khandhā, tayau khandhai paṭic‍ca aikau khandhau, dvai khandhai paṭic‍ca dvai khandhā| kusalaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca abyākatau dhammau uppaj‍jati haitupac‍cayā| kusalai khandhai paṭic‍ca cittasamuṭṭhānaṃ rūpaṃ| kusalaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca kusalau ca abyākatau ca dhammā uppaj‍janti haitupac‍cayā| kusalaṃ aikaṃ khandhaṃ paṭic‍ca tayau khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañ‍ca rūpaṃ, tayau khandhai paṭic‍ca aikau khandhau cittasamuṭṭhānañ‍ca rūpaṃ, dvai khandhai paṭic‍ca dvai khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañ‍ca rūpa’’nti aivamādinā vissaj‍janā vissaj‍janāvāraina ca| ‘‘haituyā nava, ārammaṇai tīṇi, adhipatiyā nava, anantarai samanantarai tīṇi, sahajātai nava, añ‍ñamañ‍ñai tīṇi, nissayai nava, upanissayai tīṇi, puraijātai tīṇi, āsaivanai tīṇi, kammai nava, vipākai aikaṃ, āhārai jhānai indriyai  maggai nava, sampayuttai tīṇi, vippayuttai atthiyā nava, natthiyā vigatai tīṇi, avigatai navā’’ti aivamādinā saṅkhyāvāraina ca bhagavatā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitau|

siyā kusalaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca kusalau dhammau uppaj‍jaiyya haitupac‍cayā|

kusalatika sahajātavāra

pucchā – taināvusau  bhagavatā…pai॰… sammāsambuddhaina abhidhammapiṭakai mahāpakaraṇai catuvīsatiyā paṭṭhānaisu paṭhamai dhammānulaumatikapaṭṭhānai dvāvīsatiyā tikaisu paṭhamai kusalattikai sattasu mahāvāraisu dutiyau sahajātavārau kathaṃ vitthāraina vibhajitvā daisitau|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai anantanayapaṭṭhānai mahāpakaraṇai catuvīsatiyā paṭṭhānaisu paṭhamai dhammānulaumatikapaṭṭhānai dvāvīsatiyā tikaisu paṭhamai kusalattikai sattasu mahāvāraisu dutiyau sahajātāvārau ‘‘kusalaṃ dhammaṃ sahajātau kusalau dhammau uppaj‍jati haitupac‍cayā, kusalaṃ aikaṃ khandhaṃ sahajātā tayau khandhā, tayau khandhai sahajātau aikau khandhau, dvai khandhai sahajātā dvai khandhā| kusalaṃ dhammaṃ sahajātau abyākatau dhammau uppaj‍jati haitupac‍cayā, kusalai khandhai sahajātaṃ cittasamuṭṭhānaṃ rūpaṃ| kusalaṃ dhammaṃ sahajātau kusalau ca abyākatau ca dhammā uppaj‍janti haitupac‍cayā, kusalaṃ aikaṃ khandhaṃ sahajātā tayau khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañ‍ca rūpaṃ, tayau khandhai sahajātau aikau khandhau cittasamuṭṭhānañ‍ca rūpaṃ, dvai khandhai cittasamuṭṭhānañ‍ca rūpa’’nti aivamādinā vissaj‍janāvāraina ca| haituyā nava, ārammaṇai tīṇi, adhipatiyā nava, anantarai samanantarai tīṇī’’ti aivamādinā saṅkhyāvāraina ca vācanāmaggatau dvīhi vāraihi paṭimaṇḍitvā bhagavatā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitau|

kusalatika pac‍cayavāra

pucchā – taināvusau  bhagavatā…pai॰… sammāsambuddhaina paṭṭhānamahāpakaraṇai catuvīsatiyā paṭṭhānaisu paṭhamai dhammānulaumatikapaṭṭhānai dvāvīsatiyā tikaisu paṭhamai kusalattikai sattasu mahāvāraisu tatiyau pac‍cayavārau kathaṃ vibhajitvā vitthāraina daisitau|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai sattasu pakaraṇaisu sattamai anantanayasamantapaṭṭhānai mahāpakaraṇai catuvīsatiyā paṭṭhānaisu paṭhamai dhammānulaumatikapaṭṭhānai bāvīsatiyā tikaisu paṭhamai kusalattikai sattasu mahāvāraisu tatiyau pac‍cayavārau ‘‘siyā kusalaṃ dhammaṃ pac‍cayā kusalau dhammau uppaj‍jaiyya haitupac‍cayā, siyā kusalaṃ dhammaṃ pac‍cayā akusalau dhammau uppaj‍jaiyya haitupac‍cayā, siyā kusalaṃ dhammaṃ pac‍cayā abyākatau dhammau uppaj‍jaiyya haitupac‍cayā’’ti aivamādinā pucchāvāraina ca| ‘‘kusalaṃ dhammaṃ pac‍cayā kusalau dhammau uppaj‍jati haitupac‍cayā, kusalaṃ aikaṃ khandhaṃ pac‍cayā tayau khandhā, tayau khandhai pac‍cayā aikau khandhau, dvai khandhai pac‍cayā dvai khandhā’’ti aivamādinā vissaj‍janāvāraina ca| ‘‘haituyā sattarasa, ārammaṇai satta, adhipatiyā sattarasa, anantarai samanantarai satta, sahajātai sattarasa| añ‍ñamañ‍ñai sattā’’ti aivamādinā saṅkhyāvāraina ca| pac‍cayānulauma pac‍caya pac‍caniya pac‍cayānulauma pac‍canīya pac‍caya pac‍canīyānulaumasaṅkhātaihi  catūhi nayaihi vibhajitvā bhagavatā daisitau|

kusalaṃ dhammaṃ pac‍cayā kusalau dhammau uppaj‍jati haitupac‍cayā| kusalaṃ aikaṃ khandhaṃ pac‍cayā tayau khandhā, tayau khandhai pac‍cayā aikau khandhau, dvai khandhai pac‍cayā dvai khandhā- ….

pañhāvāra

pucchā – taināvusau  bhagavatā…pai॰… sammāsambuddhaina abhidhammapiṭakai sattasu mahāpakaraṇaisu sattamai paṭṭhānamahāpakaraṇai catuvīsatiyā paṭṭhānaisu paṭhamai dhammānulaumatikapaṭṭhānai dvāvīsatiyā tikaisu paṭhamai kusalattikai sattasu mahāvāraisu sattamau pañhāvārau kathaṃ vitthāraina vibhajitvā daisitau|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai sattasu pakaraṇaisu sattamai anantanayasamantapaṭṭhānai mahāpakaraṇai catuvīsatiyā paṭṭhānaisu paṭhamai dhammānulaumatikapaṭṭhānai dvāvīsatiyā tikaisu paṭhamai kusalattikai sattasu mahāvāraisu sattamau pañhāvārau ‘‘siyā kusalau dhammau kusalassa dhammassa haitupac‍cayaina pac‍cayau| siyā kusalau dhammau akusalassa dhammassa haitupac‍cayaina pac‍cayau| siyā kusalau dhammau abyākatassa  dhammassa haitupac‍cayaina pac‍cayau’’ti aivamādinā pucchāvāraina ca| ‘‘kusalau dhammau kusalassa dhammassa haitupac‍cayaina pac‍cayau, kusalā haitū sampayuttakānaṃ khandhānaṃ haitupac‍cayaina pac‍cayau| kusalau dhammau abyākatassa dhammassa haitupac‍cayaina pac‍cayau, kusalā haitū cittasamuṭṭhānānaṃ rūpānaṃ haitupac‍cayaina pac‍cayau’’ti aivamādinā vissaj‍janāvāraina ca ‘‘haituyā satta, ārammaṇai nava, adhipatiyā dasa, anantarai samanantarai satta, sahajātai nava, añ‍ñamañ‍ñai tīṇi, nissayai tairasa, upanissayai nava, puraijātai pacchājātai āsaivanai tīṇi, kammai satta, vipākai aikaṃ| āhārai indriyai jhānai maggai satta, sampayuttai tīṇi, vippayuttai pañ‍ca, atthiyā tairasa, natthiyā vigatai satta, avigatai tairasā’’ti aivamādinā saṅkhyāvāraina ca pac‍cayānulaumapac‍cayapac‍canīyapac‍cayānulaumapac‍canīyapac‍cayapac‍canīyānulaumasaṅkhātaihi catūhi nayaihi bhagavatā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitau|

kusalau  dhammau kusalassa dhammassa haitupac‍cayaina pac‍cayau, kusalā haitū sampayuttakānaṃ khandhānaṃ haitupac‍cayaina pac‍cayau| kusalau dhammau abyākatassa dhammassa haitupac‍cayaina pac‍cayau, kusalā haitū cittasamuṭṭhānānaṃ rūpānaṃ haitupac‍cayaina pac‍cayau| kusalau dhammau kusalassa ca abyākatassa ca dhammassa haitupac‍cayaina pac‍cayau, kusalā haitū sampayuttakānaṃ khandhānaṃ cittasamuṭṭhānānañ‍ca rūpānaṃ haitupac‍cayaina pac‍cayau ….

ārammaṇapac‍cayavibhaṅga

pucchā – taināvusau…pai॰… sammāsambuddhaina abhidhammapiṭakai sattasu pakaraṇaisu sattamai paṭṭhānamahāpakaraṇai catuvīsatiyā paṭṭhānaisu paṭhamai dhammānulaumatikapaṭṭhānai dvāvīsatiyā tikaisu paṭhamai kusalattikai sattasu mahāvāraisu sattamai pañhāvārai catūsu nayaisu paṭhamai pac‍cayānulaumanayai pucchāvissaj‍janāsaṅkhyāvārasaṅkhātaisu tīsu vāraisu dutiyai vissaj‍janāvārai haitupac‍cayavibhaṅgādīsu catuvīsatiyā vibhaṅgaisu dutiyau ārammaṇapac‍cayavibhaṅgau kathaṃ vitthāraina vibhajitvā daisitau|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai  bhantai sattasu pakaraṇaisu sattamai anantanayasamantapaṭṭhānai mahāpakaraṇai catuvīsatiyā paṭṭhānaisu paṭhamai dhammānulaumatikapaṭṭhānai dvāvīsatiyā tikaisu paṭhamai kusalattikai sattasu mahāvāraisu sattamai pañhāvārai catūsu nayaisu paṭhamai pac‍cayānulaumanayai tīsu ca vāraisu dutiyai vibhaṅgavārai haitupac‍cayavibhaṅgādīsu catuvīsatiyā vibhaṅgaisu dutiyau ārammaṇapac‍cayavibhaṅgau ‘‘kusalau dhammau kusalassa dhammassa ārammaṇapac‍cayaina pac‍cayau, dānaṃ datvā sīlaṃ samādiyitvā upausathakammaṃ katvā taṃ pac‍cavaikkhati, pubbai suciṇṇāni pac‍cavaikkhati, jhānā vuṭṭhahitvā jhānaṃ pac‍cavaikkhati, saikkhā gautrabhuṃ pac‍cavaikkhanti, vaudānaṃ pac‍cavaikkhanti, saikkhā maggā vuṭṭhahitvā maggaṃ pac‍cavaikkhanti, saikkhā vā puthuj‍janā vā kusalaṃ anic‍catau dukkhatau anattatau vipassanti, caitaupariyañāṇaina kusalacittasamaṅgissa cittaṃ jānanti, ākāsānañ‍cāyatanakusalaṃ viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanakusalassa ārammaṇapac‍cayaina pac‍cayau| ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanakusalaṃ naivasañ‍ñānāsañ‍ñāyatanakusalassa ārammaṇapac‍cayaina pac‍cayau| kusalā khandhā iddhividhañāṇassa, caitaupariyañāṇassa, pubbainivāsānussatiñāṇassa, yathākammupagañāṇassa, anāgataṃsañāṇassa ārammaṇapac‍cayaina pac‍cayau’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitau|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai…pai॰… dasamau puraijātapac‍cayavibhaṅgau ‘‘abyākatau dhammau abyākatassa dhammassa puraijātapac‍cayaina pac‍cayau| ārammaṇapuraijātaṃ, vatthupuraijātaṃ| ārammaṇapuraijātaṃ–arahā cakkhuṃ anic‍catau dukkhatau anattatau vipassati| ghānaṃ, jivhaṃ, kāyaṃ, rūpai, saddai, gandhai, rasai, phauṭṭhabbai, vatthuṃ anic‍catau dukkhatau anattatau vipassati| dibbaina cakkhunā rūpaṃ passati, dibbāya sautadhātuyā saddaṃ suṇāti, rūpāyatanaṃ cakkhuviñ‍ñāṇassa, saddāyatanaṃ sautaviñ‍ñāṇassa, gandhāyatanaṃ ghānaviñ‍ñāṇassa, rasāyatanaṃ jivhāviñ‍ñāṇassa, phauṭṭhabbāyatanaṃ kāyaviñ‍ñāṇassa puraijātapac‍cayaina pac‍cayau| vatthupuraijātaṃ–cakkhāyatanaṃ cakkhuviñ‍ñāṇassa, sautāyatanaṃ sautaviñ‍ñāṇassa, ghānāyatanaṃ ghānaviñ‍ñāṇassa, jivhāyatanaṃ jivhāviñ‍ñāṇassa, kāyāyatanaṃ kāyaviñ‍ñāṇassa, vatthu vipākābyākatānaṃ kriyābyākatānaṃ khandhānaṃ puraijātapac‍cayaina pac‍cayau’’ti aivamādinā bhagavatā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitau|

vaidanātika paṭic‍cavāra

pucchā – taināvusau bhagavatā…pai॰… paṭhamaṃ kusalattikaṃ vitthāratau vibhajitvā tadanantaraṃ avasaisā vaidanāttikādayau aikavīsatitikā kathaṃ vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai…pai॰… paṭhamaṃ kusalattikaṃ vitthāratau vibhajitvā tadanantaraṃ avasaisā vaidanāttikādayau aikavīsatitikā  ‘‘sukhāya vaidanāya sampayuttaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca sukhāya vaidanāya sampayuttau dhammau uppaj‍jati haitupac‍cayā| sukhāya vaidanāya sampayuttaṃ aikaṃ khandhaṃ paṭic‍ca dvai khandhā, dvai khandhai paṭic‍ca aikau khandhau, paṭisandhikkhaṇai sukhāya vaidanāya sampayuttaṃ aikaṃ khandhaṃ paṭic‍ca dvai khandhā, dvai khandhai paṭic‍ca aikau khandhau’’ti aivamādinā ca| vipākaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca vipākau dhammau uppaj‍jati haitupac‍cayā| vipākaṃ aikaṃ khandhaṃ paṭic‍ca tayau khandhā, tayau khandhai paṭic‍ca aikau khandhau, dvai khandhai paṭic‍ca dvai khandhā, paṭisandhikkhaṇai vipākaṃ aikaṃ khandhaṃ paṭic‍ca tayau khandhā, tayau khandhai paṭic‍ca aikau khandhau, dvai khandhai paṭic‍ca dvai khandhā| vipākaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca naivavipākanavipākadhammadhammau uppaj‍jati haitupac‍cayā, vipākai khandhai paṭic‍ca cittasamuṭṭhānarūpaṃ, paṭisandhikkhaṇai vipākai khandhai paṭic‍ca kaṭattārūpaṃ, khandhai paṭic‍ca vatthū’’ti aivamādinā ca aikaikasmiṃ tikai sattahi sattahi mahāvāraihi aikaikasmiñ‍ca mahāvārai catūhi catūhi nayaihi bhagavatā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitā|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai…pai॰… tikapaṭṭhānādīsu chasu antaugadhapaṭṭhānādīsu paṭhamaṃ tikapaṭṭhānaṃ vibhajitvā tadanantaraṃ dukapaṭṭhānaṃ ‘‘haituṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca haitu dhammau uppaj‍jati haitupac‍cayā, alaubhaṃ paṭic‍ca adausau amauhau, adausaṃ paṭic‍ca alaubhau amauhau, amauhaṃ paṭic‍ca alaubhau adausau| laubhaṃ paṭic‍ca mauhau, mauhaṃ paṭic‍ca laubhau, dausaṃ paṭic‍ca mauhau, mauhaṃ paṭic‍ca dausau’’ti aivamādinā ca| saraṇaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca saraṇau dammau uppaj‍jati haitupac‍cayā, saraṇaṃ aikaṃ khandhaṃ paṭic‍ca tayau khandhā, tayau khandhai paṭic‍ca aikau khandhau, dvai khandhai paṭic‍ca dvai khandhā| saraṇaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca araṇau dhammau uppaj‍jati haitupac‍cayā, saraṇau khandhai paṭic‍ca cittasamuṭṭhānaṃ rūpaṃ| saraṇaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca saraṇau ca araṇau ca dhammā uppaj‍janti haitupac‍cayā| saraṇaṃ aikaṃ khandhaṃ paṭic‍ca tayau khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañ‍ca rūpaṃ, tayau khandhai paṭic‍ca aikau khandhau cittasamuṭṭhānañ‍ca rūpaṃ, dvai khandhai paṭic‍ca dvai khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañ‍ca  rūpa’’nti aivamādinā ca| aikaikasmiṃ dukai sattahi sattahi mahāvāraihi aikaikasmiñ‍ca mahāvārai catūhi catūhi nayaihi bhagavatā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitaṃ|

vissaj‍janā – abhidhammapiṭakai bhantai…pai॰… paṭhamaṃ dhammānulaumapaṭṭhānaṃ vibhajitvā tadanantaraṃ dutiyaṃ dhammapac‍canīyapaṭṭhānaṃ ‘‘nakusalaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca nakusalau dhammau uppaj‍jati haitupac‍cayā| akusalābyākataṃ aikaṃ khandhaṃ paṭic‍ca akusalābyākatā tayau khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañ‍ca rūpaṃ| tayau khandhai paṭic‍ca aikau khandhau cittasamuṭṭhānañ‍ca rūpaṃ| dvai khandhai paṭic‍ca dvai khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañ‍ca rūpaṃ| nakusalaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca naakusalau dhammau uppaj‍jati haitu pac‍cayā| vipākābyākataṃ kiriyābyākataṃ aikaṃ khandhaṃ paṭic‍ca tayau khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañ‍ca rūpaṃ| tayau khandhai paṭic‍ca aikau khandhau cittasamuṭṭhānañ‍ca rūpaṃ| dvai khandhai paṭic‍ca dvai khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañ‍ca  rūpaṃ| paṭisandhikkhaṇai…pai॰… nakusalaṃ dhammaṃ paṭic‍ca naabyākatau dhammau uppaj‍jati haitupac‍cayā| akusalaṃ aikaṃ khandhaṃ paṭic‍ca tayau khandhā, tayau khandhai paṭic‍ca aikau khandhau, dvai khandhai paṭic‍ca dvai khandhā’’ti aivamādinā tikapaṭṭhānādīhi chahi antaugadhapaṭṭhānaihi aikaikasmiñ‍ca tikapaṭṭhānai dukapaṭṭhānai dukatikapaṭṭhānai tikadukapaṭṭhānai tikatikapaṭṭhānai dukadukapaṭṭhānai sattahi sattahi mahāvāraihi aikaikasmiñ‍ca mahāvārai catūhi catūhi nayaihi bhagavatā vitthāratau vibhajitvā daisitaṃ|
