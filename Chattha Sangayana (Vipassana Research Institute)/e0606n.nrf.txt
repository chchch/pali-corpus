




|| namau tassa bhagavatau arahatau sammāsambuddhassa||

kamalāñ‍jali

1.

 kamalāsana kamalāpati pamathādhipa vajirā,

yudhadānava manujauraga bhujagāsana patinaṃ|

makuṭāhita manidīdhiti bhamarāvali bhajitaṃ,

paṇamā’mahaṃ anaghaṃ muni caraṇāmala kamalaṃ||

2.

 kamalālaya sadi’saṅkita kalamaṅgala ruciraṃ,

jutirañ‍jitaṃ abhinij‍jita subhakañ‍cana nalinaṃ|

janitā cira samayai nija pitu bhūpati namitaṃ,

paṇamā’mahaṃ anaghaṃ muni caraṇāmala kamalaṃ||

3.

 saraṇāgata rajanīpati dinasaikhara namitaṃ,

bhajitākhila janapāvanaṃ abhikaṅkhita sukhadaṃ|

vasudhātala sayamuggata sarasīruha mahitaṃ,

paṇamā’mahaṃ anaghaṃ muni caraṇāmala kamalaṃ||

4.

 diradāsana tarupantika ghaṭitāsanaṃ abhayaṃ,

vijayāsana samadhiṭṭhita caturaṅgika viriyaṃ|

dharamānaka suriyaihani vijitantaka dhajiniṃ,

paṇamā’mahaṃ abhipātita namuciddhaja vibhavaṃ||

5.

 sadayaudita piyabhārati vijitantaka samaraṃ,

dasapārami balakampita sadharādhara dharaniṃ|

girimaikhala varavārana sirasānata caraṇaṃ,

paṇamā’mahaṃ abhinandita sanarāmara bhuvanaṃ||

6.

 nikhilāsava vigamai’nativimalīkata hadayaṃ,

tadanantara viditākhila matigaucara visayaṃ|

visayīkata bhuvanattayaṃ atilaukiya caritaṃ,

paṇamā’mahaṃ aparājitaṃ arahaṃ muniṃ asamaṃ||

7.

 mudubhārati madhupāsita nalinaupama vadanaṃ,

rucirāyata nalinīdala nibha laucana yugalaṃ|

udayaudita ravimaṇḍala jalitāmala niṭilaṃ,

paṇamā’maha akutaubhayaṃ anaghaṃ muni pamukhaṃ||

8.

 asitambuda rucikuñ‍cita mudu kuntala lalitaṃ,

bhuvanaudara vitatāmita jutisañ‍caya jalitaṃ|

madamaudita diradaupama gativibbhama ruciraṃ,

paṇamā’mahaṃ amatandada munipuṅgavaṃ asamaṃ||

9.

 karuṇārasa paribhāvita savaṇāmata vacanaṃ,

virudāvali sataghausita yasapūrita bhuvanaṃ|

sumanauhara varalakkhaṇa sirisañ‍caya sadanaṃ,

paṇamā’mahaṃ uditāmala sasimaṇḍala vadanaṃ||

10.

 vinayāraha janamānasa kumudākara sasinaṃ,

tasināpaga parisausana satadīdhiti tulitaṃ|

tamanāsava munisaivitaṃ apalaukita sukhadaṃ,

paṇamā’mahaṃ anikaitanaṃ akhilāgati vigataṃ||

11.

 sahitākhila bhayabhairavaṃ abhayāgata saranaṃ,

ajarāmara sukhadāyakaṃ anirākata karuṇaṃ|

tamupāsaka janasaivita supatiṭṭhita caraṇaṃ,

paṇamā’mahaṃ ahitāpahaṃ anaghuttama caraṇaṃ||

12.

 karuṇāmata rasapurita vīmalākhila hadayaṃ,

vihitāmita janatāhitaṃ anukampita bhuvanaṃ|

bhuvanai sutaṃ avanīpati sata saivita caraṇaṃ,

paṇamā’mahaṃ anaghaṃ muniṃ aghanāsana caturaṃ||

13.

 aratīrati paripīlita yatimānasa damanaṃ,

nijasāsana vinivārita puthutitthiya samaṇaṃ|

paravādika janatākata paribhāsita khamanaṃ,

paṇamā’mahaṃ atidaivata vara gautama samaṇaṃ||

14.

 saraṇāgata bhayanāsana vajirālaya paṇibhaṃ,

bhavasāgara patitāmita janatārana nirataṃ|

sirasāvahaṃ amalañ‍jali puṭapaṅkaja makulaṃ,

paṇamā’mahaṃ akhilalāya vigataṃ muniṃ atulaṃ||

15.

 vimalīkata janamānasa vigatāsava bhagavaṃ,

bhavapāraga vibhavāmata sukhadāyaka satataṃ|

paramādara garugārava vinataṃ jina payataṃ,

padapaṅkaja rajasā mama samalaṅkuru sirasaṃ||

16.

 pavanāhata dumapal‍lavaṃ iva nārata capalaṃ,

bhavalālasa malinīkataṃ ajitindriya nivahaṃ|

cira sañ‍cita duritāhataṃ anivārita timisaṃ,

vimalīkuru karuṇābhara sutaraṃ mama hadayaṃ||

17.

 adayai dayaṃ anayai nayaṃ api yau guṇaṃ aguṇai,

ahitai hitaṃ akarau k‍vacida api kainaci nakataṃ|

sadayai jina sunayai guṇasadanai tayi nitaraṃ,

suhitai hitacaritai’nagha ramatai mama hadayaṃ||

18.

 bhavasaṅkaṭa patitainapi bhavatā cira caritaṃ,

visamai sama caraṇaṃ khalu dasapārami bharaṇaṃ|

saratau’hani saratau nisi supinainapi satataṃ,

ramatai jina sumatai tvayi sadayaṃ mama hadayaṃ||

19.

 atiduddadaṃ adadī bhavaṃ atiduk‍karaṃ akarī|

atidukkhamaṃ akhamī vata karuṇānidhi’rasamau,

iti tai guṇaṃ anaghaṃ muni saratau mama hadayaṃ,

ramatai’hani ramatai nisi ramatai tvayi satataṃ||

20.

 atiduc‍caraṃ acarī bhavaṃ atiduddamaṃ adamī,

atiduddayaṃ adayī vata sadayāpara hadayau|

iti tai guṇaṃ anaghaṃ muni saratau mama hadayaṃ,

ramatai’hani ramatai nisi ramatai tvayi satataṃ||

21.

 atiduggamaṃ agamī bhavaṃ atiduj‍jayaṃ ajayī,

atidussahaṃ asahī vata samupaikkhita manasau|

iti tai guṇaṃ anaghaṃ muni saratau mama hadayaṃ,

ramatai’hani ramatai nisi ramatai tvayi satataṃ||

22.

 atidāruna palayānala sadisānala jalitai,

nirayai vinipatitau ciraṃ aghatāpita manasau|

na sariṃ sakida api tai pita bhajituṃ padanalinaṃ,

khama gautama narasārathi tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

23.

 tiriyaggata-gatiyaṃ ciraṃ anavaṭṭhita caritau,

atiniṭṭhura vadhataj‍jita bhayakampita hadayau|

na sariṃ sakidapi tai pita bhajituṃ padanalinaṃ,

khama gautama purisuttama tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

24.

 paridaivana niratau ciraṃ atha paittiya visayai,

sujighacchita supipāsita parisausita jaṭharai|

na sariṃ sakidapi tai pita bhajituṃ padanalinaṃ,

khama gautama dipaduttama tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

25.

 vivasau bhusaṃ aghadūsita manasāsura visayai,

janitau ghanatimirai ciraṃ atidukkhita hadayau|

na sariṃ sakidapi tai pita bhajituṃ padanalinaṃ,

khama gautama vasisattama tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

26.

 manasā cira vihitaṃ saraṃ atikibbisa caritaṃ,

samathainatha suvirājiya taṃ asañ‍ñitaṃ upagau|

na sariṃ sakida api tai pita bhajituṃ pada nalinaṃ,

khama gautama vasipuṅgava tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

27.

 vijigucchiya duritaṃ nija vapusā kataṃ amitaṃ,

tanu vaj‍jitaṃ upagau bhavaṃ iha bhāvita samathau|

na sariṃ sakidapi tai pita bhajituṃ padanalinaṃ,

khama gautama yatikuñ‍jara tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

28.

 ratanattaya rahitai bhusa bahulīkata duritai,

janitau paravisayai budhajana nindiya caritai|

na sariṃ sakidapi tai pita bhajituṃ padanalinaṃ,

khama gautama karuṇānidhi tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

29.

 janitau yadi manujaisupi vikalindriya nivahau,

tanunā karacaraṇādihi vikatai’niha dukhitau|

na sariṃ sakidapi tai pita bhajituṃ padanalinaṃ,

khama gautama matisāgara tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

30.

 vidhināhita matibhāvana rahitau tamapihitau,

visadaisupi kusalādisu tathadassana vimukhau|

na sariṃ sakidapi tai pita bhajituṃ padanalinaṃ,

khama gautama narakaisari tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

31.

 sucirainapi bhuvi dul‍labhaṃ asamaṃ khaṇaṃ alabhaṃ,

sanarāmara janatāhita sukhadaṃ muni jananaṃ|

na sariṃ sakidapi tai pita bhajituṃ padanalinaṃ,

khama gautama vadataṃvara tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

32.

 nisitāyudha vadhasaj‍jita khaḷaniddaya hadayau,

parahiṃsana ruci bhiṃsana yamasaudara sadisau|

na sariṃ sakidapi tai pita bhajituṃ padanalinaṃ,

khama gautama munisattama tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

33.

 parasantaka haraṇai katamati bañ‍cana bahulau,

gharasandhika paripanthika sahasākati niratau|

na sariṃ sakidapi tai pita bhajituṃ padanalinaṃ,

khama gautama munipuṅgava tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

34.

 navayaubbana madagabbita parimucchita hadayau,

sucisaj‍jana vijigucchiya paradārika niratau|

na sariṃ sakidapi tai pita bhajituṃ padanalinaṃ,

khama gautama karuṇābhara tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

35.

 madirāsavarata nāgarajana santata bhajitau,

garugārava hiridūrita tiriyaggata caritau|

na sariṃ sakidapi tai pita bhajituṃ padanalinaṃ,

khama gautama vihatāsava tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

36.

 sapitāmaha papitāmaha nicitaṃ dhanaṃ amitaṃ,

pitusañ‍citamapi nāsiya kitavau hatavibhavau|

na sariṃ sakidapi tai pita bhajituṃ padanalinaṃ,

khama gautama vijitantaka tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

37.

 vitathālika vacanau parapiyasuñ‍ñata karaṇau,

pharusaṃ bhaṇaṃ atinipphala bahubhāsana nipuṇau|

na sariṃ sakidapi tai pita bhajituṃ padanalinaṃ,

khama gautama bhuvanaisuta tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

38.

 parasampadaṃ abhijhāyana nirantara dukhitau,

nabhirajjhana paramau k‍vaci phaladassana rahitau|

na sariṃ sakidapi tai pita bhajituṃ padanalinaṃ,

khama gautama guṇasāgara tamidaṃ mama khalitaṃ||

39.

 bhavatau bhavaṃ aparāparaṃ ayatāciraṃ iti mai,

vapusā atha vacasāpi ca manasā kataṃ amitaṃ|

khama gautama duritāpaha duritaṃ bahuvihitaṃ,

dada mai sivapadaṃ ac‍cutaṃ amataṃ bhavavigataṃ||

40.

 timirāvuta kuṇapākula vijigucchiya pavanai,

janikāsuci jaṭharai bahu kimisantati sadanai|

asayiṃ bhavagahaṇai caraṃ ahaṃ appaṭisaraṇai,

saranaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

41.

 bahi nikkhamaṃ asakiṃ bhagatiriyaṃ patha patitau,

agadaṅkara kata sal‍laka satakhaṇḍita karaṇau|

amariṃ bhavagahaṇai caraṃ ahaṃ appaṭisaraṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taranai||

42.

 patitau bahi ratipil‍laka tanurāmaya mathitau,

vadituṃ kimu viditumpi ca na sahaṃ matirahitau|

amariṃ bhavagahaṇai caraṃ ahaṃ appaṭisaraṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

43.

 janitau yadi sukhitau janadayitau piyajanakau,

puthukau bahuvidha-kīlananiratau gadagahitau|

amariṃ bhavagahaṇai caraṃ ahaṃ appaṭisaraṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

44.

 tarunaupi hi gharabandhana gathitaumita vibhavau,

sahasā gadagahitau piyabhariyāsuta viyutau|

amariṃ bhavagahaṇai caraṃ ahaṃ appaṭisaraṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

45.

 ajaraṃ tanuṃ abhimañ‍ñiya navayaubbana vasikau,

jarasā parimathitau paraṃ ananuṭṭhita kusalau|

amariṃ bhavagahaṇai caraṃ ahaṃ appaṭisaraṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

46.

 nirujau dhuvaṃ arujaṃ tanuṃ abhimañ‍ñiya samadau,

kusalāsaya vimukhau bhusaṃ avasau gadanihatau|

amariṃ bhavagahaṇai caraṃ ahaṃ appaṭisaraṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

47.

 pavicintiya sakajīvitaṃ amaraṃ dhuvaṃ anighaṃ,

iti jīvitamadagabbita matirujjhita kusalau|

amariṃ bhavagahaṇai caraṃ ahaṃ appaṭisaraṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

48.

 parahiṃsana dhanamausana paradārika niratau,

dharanīpati gahitau bahu vadhabandhana nihatau|

amariṃ bhavagahaṇai caraṃ ahaṃ appaṭisaraṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

49.

 narakaudaka patitau giritarumatthaka galitau,

migavāḷaka gahitau visadhara jātihi ḍasitau|

amariṃ bhavagahaṇai caraṃ ahaṃ appaṭisaraṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

50.

 abhicāraka vidhikaupita nisicāraka gahitau,

savisaudana sahasādana pabhutīhi ca khalitau|

amariṃ bhavagahaṇai caraṃ ahaṃ appaṭisaraṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

51.

 sayamaivaca sajanaupari kupitau mativiyutau,

savisādana galakantana pabhutāmita khalitau|

amariṃ bhavagahaṇai caraṃ ahaṃ appaṭisaraṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

52.

 pitupūjana niratāsaya sukhitau pitudayitau,

maraṇainaca pitunau bhusaṃ anusaucana niratau|

parudiṃ ciraṃ atidussaha kasirai bhavagahaṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

53.

 samupaṭṭhiya janikaṃ nijaṃ abhivādana paramau,

maraṇainaca janikāyanusaritā guṇamahimaṃ|

parudiṃ ciraṃ atidussaha kasirai bhavagahaṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

54.

 gurudaivata patimānana paramau piyasuvacau,

samupāsitacaraṇau gurumaraṇainatidukhitau|

parudiṃ ciraṃ atidussaha kasirai bhavagahaṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

55.

 gharamaidhitaṃ upagaumitavibhavau ratibahulau,

maraṇai piyabhariyāsutaduhitū’natikaruṇaṃ|

parudiṃ ciraṃ atidussaha kasirai bhavagahaṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

56.

 piyapubbaja sahajānuja bhagiṇidvaya maraṇai,

nija bandhava-sakha-sissaka maraṇai pyatikaruṇaṃ|

parudiṃ ciraṃ atidussaha kasirai bhavagahaṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

57.

 jagatīpati gahaṇā ripujanatak‍kara haraṇā,

saritaudaka vahaṇā puthujalitānala dahaṇā|

parudiṃ hatavibhavau ciramiha duggatigahaṇai,

saraṇaṃ bhava bhagavaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇai||

58.

 atiduggama visamākula bhavasaṅkaṭa patitai,

byasanaṃ ciramiti dussahaṃ anubhūyapi vimitaṃ|

na jahai sukhalavavañ‍citahadayau bhavatasinaṃ,

tamapākuru karuṇānidhi tasinaṃ mama kasiṇaṃ||

59.

 jananāvadhi maraṇaṃ viya maraṇāvadhi jananaṃ,

ubhayainapi bhayamaivahi bhavatau mama niyataṃ|

sivamaivaca jananāvadhi maraṇāvadhi rahitaṃ,

dada mai sivaṃ amatandada taṃ anāsava bhagavaṃ||

60.

 ciradikkhitamapi mai manaṃ anivārita tasinaṃ,

bhavatau bhava ratipīlitaṃ ahahau kalighaṭitaṃ!

tamatau pita bhayatau mamamava mai bhava saraṇaṃ,

bhagavaṃ paṭisaraṇaṃ mama bhavanīradhi taraṇaṃ||
