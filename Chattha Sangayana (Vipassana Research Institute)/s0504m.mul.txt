1. laubhasuttaṃ

1. vuttañhaitaṃ  bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘aikadhammaṃ, bhikkhavai, pajahatha; ahaṃ vau pāṭibhaugau anāgāmitāya| katamaṃ aikadhammaṃ? laubhaṃ, bhikkhavai, aikadhammaṃ pajahatha; ahaṃ vau pāṭibhaugau anāgāmitāyā’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘yaina  laubhaina luddhāsai, sattā gacchanti duggatiṃ|

taṃ laubhaṃ sammadañ‍ñāya, pajahanti vipassinau|

pahāya na punāyanti, imaṃ laukaṃ kudācana’’nti||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| paṭhamaṃ|

4. avij‍jānīvaraṇasuttaṃ

14. vuttañhaitaṃ bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti  mai sutaṃ –

‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikanīvaraṇampi samanupassāmi yaina yainaivaṃ (?) nīvaraṇaina nivutā pajā dīgharattaṃ  sandhāvanti saṃsaranti yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, avij‍jānīvaraṇaṃ avij‍jānīvaraṇaina (?)| avij‍jānīvaraṇaina hi, bhikkhavai, nivutā pajā dīgharattaṃ sandhāvanti saṃsarantī’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘natthañ‍ñau  aikadhammaupi, yainaivaṃ yainaiva (sī॰ pī॰ ka॰) nivutā pajā|

saṃsaranti  ahaurattaṃ, yathā mauhaina āvutā||

‘‘yai ca mauhaṃ pahantvāna, tamaukhandhaṃ tamaukkhandhaṃ (sī॰ syā॰ pī॰) padālayuṃ|

na tai puna saṃsaranti, haitu taisaṃ na vij‍jatī’’ti||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| catutthaṃ|

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| pañ‍camaṃ|

‘‘yaunisau  manasikārau, dhammau saikhassa bhikkhunau|

natthañ‍ñau aivaṃ bahukārau, uttamatthassa pattiyā|

yaunisau padahaṃ bhikkhu, khayaṃ dukkhassa pāpuṇai’’ti||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| chaṭṭhaṃ|

8. saṅghabhaidasuttaṃ

18. vuttañhaitaṃ  bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘aikadhammau, bhikkhavai, laukai uppaj‍jamānau  uppaj‍jati bahujanāhitāya bahujanāsukhāya bahunau janassa anatthāya ahitāya dukkhāya daivamanussānaṃ| katamau aikadhammau? saṅghabhaidau| saṅghai khau pana, bhikkhavai, bhin‍nai añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ bhaṇḍanāni caiva haunti, añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ paribhāsā ca haunti, añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ parikkhaipā ca haunti, añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ paric‍cajanā ca haunti| tattha appasan‍nā caiva nappasīdanti, pasan‍nānañ‍ca aikac‍cānaṃ añ‍ñathattaṃ hautī’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘āpāyikau  nairayikau, kappaṭṭhau saṅghabhaidakau|

vaggārāmau adhammaṭṭhau, yaugakkhaimā padhaṃsati yaugakkhaimatau dhaṃsati (syā॰ pī॰), yaugakkhaimā vimaṃsati (sī॰ ka॰)|

saṅghaṃ samaggaṃ bhaitvāna bhitvāna (sī॰ ka॰), bhinditvā (cūḷava॰ 354; a॰ ni॰ 10.39), kappaṃ nirayamhi pac‍catī’’ti||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| aṭṭhamaṃ|

9. saṅghasāmaggīsuttaṃ

19. vuttañhaitaṃ bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘aikadhammau, bhikkhavai, laukai uppaj‍jamānau uppaj‍jati bahujanahitāya bahujanasukhāya bahunau janassa atthāya hitāya sukhāya daivamanussānaṃ| katamau aikadhammau? saṅghasāmaggī| saṅghai khau pana, bhikkhavai, samaggai na caiva añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ bhaṇḍanāni haunti, na ca añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ paribhāsā haunti, na ca añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ parikkhaipā haunti, na ca añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ paric‍cajanā haunti| tattha appasan‍nā caiva pasīdanti, pasan‍nānañ‍ca bhiyyaubhāvau hautī’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘sukhā saṅghassa sāmaggī, samaggānañ‍canuggahau|

samaggaratau dhammaṭṭhau, yaugakkhaimā na dhaṃsati|

saṅghaṃ samaggaṃ katvāna, kappaṃ saggamhi maudatī’’ti||

ayampi  atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| navamaṃ|

10. paduṭṭhacittasuttaṃ

20. vuttañhaitaṃ  bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘idhāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, aikac‍caṃ puggalaṃ paduṭṭhacittaṃ aivaṃ caitasā caitau paric‍ca pajānāmi – ‘imamhi cāyaṃ samayai puggalau kālaṅkaraiyya yathābhataṃ nikkhittau aivaṃ nirayai’| taṃ kissa haitu? cittaṃ hissa, bhikkhavai, paduṭṭhaṃ| caitaupadausahaitu khau pana, bhikkhavai, aivamidhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapaj‍jantī’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘paduṭṭhacittaṃ  ñatvāna, aikac‍caṃ idha puggalaṃ|

aitamatthañ‍ca byākāsi, buddhau bhikkhūna santikai||

‘‘imamhi cāyaṃ samayai, kālaṃ kayirātha puggalau|

nirayaṃ upapaj‍jaiyya, cittaṃ hissa padūsitaṃ||

‘‘yathā haritvā nikkhipaiyya, aivamaiva tathāvidhau|

caitaupadausahaitu hi, sattā gacchanti duggati’’nti||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| dasamaṃ|

dutiyau vaggau niṭṭhitau|

tassuddānaṃ –

mauhau kaudhau atha makkhau, vij‍jā taṇhā saikhaduvai ca|

bhaidau sāmaggipuggalau mauhakaudha atha makkhāgatau, mūhā kāmasaikkhaduvai| bhaidasāmaggapuggalau ca (sī॰ ka॰) mauhakaudhā atha makkhau mauhakāmā saikkhā duvai| bhaidamaudā puggalau ca (syā॰ pī॰), vaggamāhu dutiyanti vuc‍catīti||

21. vuttañhaitaṃ  bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘idhāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, aikac‍caṃ puggalaṃ pasan‍nacittaṃ aivaṃ caitasā caitau paric‍ca pajānāmi  – ‘imamhi cāyaṃ samayai puggalau kālaṃ karaiyya yathābhataṃ nikkhittau aivaṃ saggai’| taṃ kissa haitu? cittaṃ hissa, bhikkhavai, pasan‍naṃ| caitaupasādahaitu khau pana, bhikkhavai, aivamidhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā sugatiṃ saggaṃ laukaṃ upapaj‍jantī’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘pasan‍nacittaṃ ñatvāna, aikac‍caṃ idha puggalaṃ|

aitamatthañ‍ca byākāsi, buddhau bhikkhūna santikai||

2. maittasuttaṃ

‘‘mā, bhikkhavai, puñ‍ñānaṃ bhāyittha| sukhassaitaṃ, bhikkhavai, adhivacanaṃ iṭṭhassa kantassa piyassa manāpassa yadidaṃ puñ‍ñāni puñ‍ñānanti, (a॰ ni॰ 7.62)| abhijānāmi khau panāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, dīgharattaṃ katānaṃ puñ‍ñānaṃ iṭṭhaṃ kantaṃ piyaṃ manāpaṃ vipākaṃ pac‍canubhūtaṃ| satta vassāni maittacittaṃ bhāvaitvā satta  saṃvaṭṭavivaṭṭakappai  nayimaṃ laukaṃ punarāgamāsiṃ| saṃvaṭṭamānai sudaṃ, bhikkhavai, kappai ābhassarūpagau haumi; vivaṭṭamānai kappai suñ‍ñaṃ brahmavimānaṃ upapaj‍jāmi|

dānañ‍ca  samacariyañ‍ca, maittacittañ‍ca bhāvayai||

‘‘saiyyathāpi, bhikkhavai, rattiyā pac‍cūsasamayaṃ ausadhitārakā bhāsatai ca tapatai ca viraucati ca; aivamaiva khau, bhikkhavai, yāni kānici aupadhikāni puñ‍ñakiriyavatthūni sabbāni tāni maittāya caitauvimuttiyā kalaṃ nāgghanti sauḷasiṃ, maittāyaiva tāni caitauvimutti adhiggahaitvā bhāsatai ca tapatai ca viraucati cā’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

tanū tanu (sī॰) saṃyaujanā haunti, passatau upadhikkhayaṃ||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| sattamaṃ|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, aulīyanti aikai? bhavārāmā, bhikkhavai, daivamanussā bhavaratā bhavasammuditā taisaṃ bhavaniraudhāya dhammai daisiyamānai cittaṃ na pakkhandati na pasīdati na santiṭṭhati nādhimuc‍cati| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, aulīyanti aikai|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, cakkhumantau passanti? idha bhikkhu bhūtaṃ bhūtatau passati; bhūtaṃ bhūtatau disvā bhūtassa nibbidāya virāgāya niraudhāya paṭipan‍nau hauti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, cakkhumantau passantī’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘yai  yau (syā॰ ka॰) bhūtaṃ bhūtatau disvā, bhūtassa ca atik‍kamaṃ|

‘‘yadā, bhikkhavai, daivau daivakāyā cavanadhammau hauti, pañ‍cassa pubbanimittāni pātubhavanti – mālā milāyanti, vatthāni kilissanti, kacchaihi saidā muc‍canti, kāyai dubbaṇṇiyaṃ auk‍kamati, sakai daivau daivāsanai nābhiramatīti| tamainaṃ, bhikkhavai, daivā ‘cavanadhammau ayaṃ daivaputtau’ti iti viditvā tīhi vācāhi anumaudainti anumaudanti (sī॰ syā॰ pī॰) – ‘itau, bhau, sugatiṃ gaccha, sugatiṃ gantvā  suladdhalābhaṃ labha, suladdhalābhaṃ labhitvā suppatiṭṭhitau bhavāhī’’’ti|

aivaṃ vuttai, añ‍ñatarau bhikkhu bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca – ‘‘kin‍nu khau, bhantai, daivānaṃ sugatigamanasaṅkhātaṃ; kiñ‍ca, bhantai, daivānaṃ suladdhalābhasaṅkhātaṃ; kiṃ pana, bhantai, daivānaṃ suppatiṭṭhitasaṅkhāta’’nti?

10. daivadattasuttaṃ

89. vuttañhaitaṃ bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘tīhi, bhikkhavai, asaddhammaihi abhibhūtau pariyādin‍nacittau daivadattau āpāyikau nairayikau kappaṭṭhau ataikicchau| katamaihi tīhi? pāpicchatāya, bhikkhavai, abhibhūtau pariyādin‍nacittau daivadattau āpāyikau nairayikau kappaṭṭhau ataikicchau| pāpamittatāya, bhikkhavai, abhibhūtau pariyādin‍nacittau daivadattau āpāyikau nairayikau kappaṭṭhau ataikicchau| sati khau pana uttarikaraṇīyai uttariṃ karaṇīyai (syā॰) auramattakaina visaisādhigamaina visaisādhigamaina ca (syā॰ pī॰) antarā vausānaṃ āpādi| imaihi khau, bhikkhavai, tīhi asaddhammaihi abhibhūtau pariyādin‍nacittau daivadattau āpāyikau nairayikau  kappaṭṭhau ataikicchau’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘mā jātu kauci laukasmiṃ, pāpicchau udapaj‍jatha|

tadamināpi jānātha, pāpicchānaṃ yathā gati||

‘‘paṇḍitauti  samañ‍ñātau, bhāvitattauti sammatau|

jalaṃva yasasā aṭṭhā, daivadattauti vissutau mai sutaṃ (pāḷiyaṃ)||

‘‘sau pamāṇamanuciṇṇau pamādamanuciṇṇau (ka॰ sī॰ syā॰ pī॰), samānamanuciṇṇau (aṭṭha॰), āsaj‍ja naṃ tathāgataṃ|

avīcinirayaṃ pattau, catudvāraṃ bhayānakaṃ||

‘‘aduṭṭhassa hi yau dubbhai, pāpakammaṃ akubbatau|

tamaiva pāpaṃ phusati phussaiti (syā॰), duṭṭhacittaṃ anādaraṃ||

‘‘samuddaṃ visakumbhaina, yau mañ‍ñaiyya padūsituṃ|

na sau taina padūsaiyya, bhaismā hi udadhi mahā||

‘‘aivamaiva  aivamaitaṃ (syā॰) tathāgataṃ, yau vādaina vihiṃsati|

sammaggataṃ  samaggataṃ (sī॰ ka॰) santacittaṃ, vādau tamhi na rūhati||

‘‘tādisaṃ mittaṃ kubbaitha, tañ‍ca saivaiyya paṇḍitau|

yassa maggānugau bhikkhu, khayaṃ dukkhassa pāpuṇai’’ti||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| dasamaṃ|

catutthau vaggau niṭṭhitau|

tassuddānaṃ –

vitak‍kāsak‍kārasadda, cavanalaukai asubhaṃ|

dhammaandhakāramalaṃ, daivadattaina tai dasāti||

5. pañ‍camavaggau

1. aggappasādasuttaṃ

90. vuttañhaitaṃ  bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘tayaumai, bhikkhavai, aggappasādā| katamai tayau? yāvatā, bhikkhavai, sattā apadā vā dvipadā vā catuppadā vā bahuppadā bahupadā (ka॰) vā rūpinau vā arūpinau vā sañ‍ñinau vā asañ‍ñinau vā naivasañ‍ñināsañ‍ñinau vā, tathāgatau taisaṃ aggamakkhāyati arahaṃ sammāsambuddhau| yai, bhikkhavai, buddhai pasan‍nā, aggai tai pasan‍nā| aggai khau pana pasan‍nānaṃ aggau vipākau hauti|

‘‘yāvatā, bhikkhavai, dhammā saṅkhatā vā asaṅkhatā vā, virāgau taisaṃ aggamakkhāyati, yadidaṃ madanimmadanau pipāsavinayau ālayasamugghātau vaṭṭupacchaidau taṇhakkhayau virāgau niraudhau nibbānaṃ| yai, bhikkhavai, virāgai dhammai pasan‍nā, aggai tai pasan‍nā| aggai khau pana pasan‍nānaṃ aggau vipākau hauti|

‘‘yāvatā, bhikkhavai, saṅghā vā gaṇā vā, tathāgatasāvakasaṅghau taisaṃ aggamakkhāyati, yadidaṃ cattāri purisayugāni aṭṭha purisapuggalā  aisa  bhagavatau sāvakasaṅghau āhunaiyyau pāhunaiyyau dakkhiṇaiyyau añ‍jalikaraṇīyau anuttaraṃ puñ‍ñakkhaittaṃ laukassa| yai, bhikkhavai, saṅghai pasan‍nā, aggai tai pasan‍nā| aggai khau pana pasan‍nānaṃ aggau vipākau hauti| imai khau, bhikkhavai, tayau aggappasādā’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘aggatau vai pasan‍nānaṃ, aggaṃ dhammaṃ vijānataṃ|

aggai buddhai pasan‍nānaṃ, dakkhiṇaiyyai anuttarai||

‘‘aggai dhammai pasan‍nānaṃ, virāgūpasamai sukhai|

aggai saṅghai pasan‍nānaṃ, puñ‍ñakkhaittai anuttarai||

‘‘aggasmiṃ  dānaṃ dadataṃ, aggaṃ puñ‍ñaṃ pavaḍḍhati|

aggaṃ āyu ca vaṇṇau ca, yasau kitti sukhaṃ balaṃ||

‘‘aggassa dātā maidhāvī, aggadhammasamāhitau|

daivabhūtau manussau vā, aggappattau pamaudatī’’ti||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| paṭhamaṃ|

2. jīvikasuttaṃ

91. vuttañhaitaṃ bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘antamidaṃ, bhikkhavai, jīvikānaṃ yadidaṃ piṇḍaulyaṃ| abhisāpauyaṃ abhisāpāyaṃ (sī॰), abhilāpāyaṃ (syā॰ pī॰), abhisapāyaṃ (ka॰), bhikkhavai, laukasmiṃ – ‘piṇḍaulau vicarasi pattapāṇī’ti| tañ‍ca khau aitaṃ, bhikkhavai, kulaputtā upainti atthavasikā, atthavasaṃ paṭic‍ca; naiva rājābhinītā, na caurābhinītā, na iṇaṭṭā, na bhayaṭṭā, na ājīvikāpakatā| api ca khau ‘autiṇṇamhā jātiyā jarāya maraṇaina saukaihi paridaivaihi dukkhaihi daumanassaihi upāyāsaihi dukkhautiṇṇā dukkhaparaitā, appaiva nāma imassa kaivalassa dukkhakkhandhassa antakiriyā pañ‍ñāyaithā’ti| aivaṃ pabbajitau cāyaṃ, bhikkhavai, kulaputtau sau  ca hauti abhijjhālu kāmaisu tibbasārāgau, byāpan‍nacittau paduṭṭhamanasaṅkappau, muṭṭhassati  asampajānau asamāhitau vibbhantacittau pākatindriyau| saiyyathāpi, bhikkhavai, chavālātaṃ ubhataupadittaṃ majjhai gūthagataṃ naiva gāmai kaṭṭhatthaṃ pharati  na arañ‍ñaiḥ tathūpamāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, imaṃ puggalaṃ vadāmi gihibhaugā parihīnau sāmañ‍ñatthañ‍ca na paripūraitī’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘gihibhaugā parihīnau, sāmañ‍ñatthañ‍ca dubbhagau|

paridhaṃsamānau pakiraiti, chavālātaṃva nassati||

‘‘kāsāvakaṇṭhā bahavau, pāpadhammā asañ‍ñatā|

pāpā pāpaihi kammaihi, nirayaṃ tai upapaj‍jarai||

‘‘saiyyau ayauguḷau bhuttau, tattau aggisikhūpamau|

yañ‍cai bhuñ‍jaiyya dussīlau, raṭṭhapiṇḍamasañ‍ñatau’’ti||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| dutiyaṃ|

3. saṅghāṭikaṇṇasuttaṃ

92. vuttañhaitaṃ bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘saṅghāṭikaṇṇai caipi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu gahaitvā piṭṭhitau piṭṭhitau anubandhau assa pādai pādaṃ nikkhipantau, sau ca hauti abhijjhālu kāmaisu tibbasārāgau byāpan‍nacittau paduṭṭhamanasaṅkappau muṭṭhassati asampajānau asamāhitau vibbhantacittau pākatindriyau; atha khau sau ārakāva mayhaṃ, ahañ‍ca tassa| taṃ kissa haitu? dhammañhi sau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na passati| dhammaṃ apassantau  na maṃ passati maṃ na passati (syā॰)|

‘‘yaujanasatai caipi sau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu viharaiyya| sau ca hauti anabhijjhālu kāmaisu na tibbasārāgau abyāpan‍nacittau apaduṭṭhamanasaṅkappau upaṭṭhitassati sampajānau samāhitau aikaggacittau saṃvutindriyau; atha khau sau santikaiva mayhaṃ, ahañ‍ca tassa| taṃ kissa haitu? dhammaṃ hi sau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu passati; dhammaṃ passantau maṃ passatī’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘anubandhaupi  cai assa, mahicchau ca vighātavā|

aijānugau anaijassa, nibbutassa anibbutau|

giddhau sau vītagaidhassa, passa yāvañ‍ca ārakā||

‘‘yau  ca dhammamabhiñ‍ñāya, dhammamañ‍ñāya paṇḍitau|

rahadauva  nivātai ca, anaijau vūpasammati||

‘‘anaijau sau anaijassa, nibbutassa ca nibbutau|

agiddhau vītagaidhassa, passa yāvañ‍ca santikai’’ti||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| tatiyaṃ|

4. aggisuttaṃ

93. vuttañhaitaṃ bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘tayaumai, bhikkhavai, aggī| katamai tayau? rāgaggi, dausaggi, mauhaggi – imai khau, bhikkhavai, tayau aggī’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘rāgaggi dahati mac‍cai, rattai kāmaisu mucchitai|

dausaggi pana byāpan‍nai, narai pāṇātipātinau||

‘‘mauhaggi pana sammūḷhai, ariyadhammai akauvidai|

aitai aggī ajānantā, sak‍kāyābhiratā pajā||

‘‘tai vaḍḍhayanti nirayaṃ, tiracchānañ‍ca yauniyau|

tai nibbāpainti rāgaggiṃ, nic‍caṃ asubhasañ‍ñinau||

‘‘ariyaddasā vaidagunau, sammadañ‍ñāya paṇḍitā|

kalyāṇasīlasuttaṃ

97. vuttañhaitaṃ bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘kalyāṇasīlau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu kalyāṇadhammau kalyāṇapañ‍ñau imasmiṃ dhammavinayai ‘kaivalī vusitavā uttamapurisau’ti vuc‍cati –

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu kalyāṇasīlau hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu sīlavā hauti, pātimaukkhasaṃvarasaṃvutau viharati, ācāragaucarasampan‍nau aṇumattaisu vaj‍jaisu bhayadassāvī, samādāya sikkhati sikkhāpadaisu| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu kalyāṇasīlau hauti| iti kalyāṇasīlau|

‘‘kalyāṇadhammau  ca kathaṃ hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu sattan‍naṃ baudhipakkhiyānaṃ dhammānaṃ bhāvanānuyaugamanuyuttau viharati| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu kalyāṇadhammau hauti| iti kalyāṇasīlau, kalyāṇadhammau|

‘‘kalyāṇapañ‍ñau ca kathaṃ hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu āsavānaṃ khayā anāsavaṃ caitauvimuttiṃ pañ‍ñāvimuttiṃ diṭṭhaiva dhammai sayaṃ abhiñ‍ñā sacchikatvā upasampaj‍ja viharati| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu kalyāṇapañ‍ñau hauti|

‘‘iti kalyāṇasīlau kalyāṇadhammau kalyāṇapañ‍ñau imasmiṃ dhammavinayai ‘kaivalī vusitavā uttamapurisau’ti vuc‍catī’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘yassa  kāyaina vācāya, manasā natthi duk‍kaṭaṃ|

taṃ vai kalyāṇasīlauti, āhu bhikkhuṃ hirīmanaṃ hirīmataṃ (syā॰ ka॰)||

‘‘yassa dhammā subhāvitā, satta patta (sabbattha) sambaudhigāminau|

taṃ vai kalyāṇadhammauti, āhu bhikkhuṃ anussadaṃ||

‘‘yau dukkhassa pajānāti, idhaiva khayamattanau|

taṃ vai kalyāṇapañ‍ñauti, āhu bhikkhuṃ anāsavaṃ||

‘‘taihi dhammaihi sampan‍naṃ, anīghaṃ chin‍nasaṃsayaṃ|

asitaṃ  sabbalaukassa, āhu sabbapahāyina’’nti||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| aṭṭhamaṃ|

9. dānasuttaṃ

98. vuttañhaitaṃ bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘dvaimāni, bhikkhavai, dānāni – āmisadānañ‍ca dhammadānañ‍ca| aitadaggaṃ, bhikkhavai, imaisaṃ dvin‍naṃ dānānaṃ yadidaṃ – dhammadānaṃ|

‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, saṃvibhāgā – āmisasaṃvibhāgau ca dhammasaṃvibhāgau ca| aitadaggaṃ, bhikkhavai, imaisaṃ dvin‍naṃ saṃvibhāgānaṃ yadidaṃ – dhammasaṃvibhāgau|

‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, anuggahā – āmisānuggahau ca dhammānuggahau ca| aitadaggaṃ, bhikkhavai, imaisaṃ dvin‍naṃ anuggahānaṃ yadidaṃ – dhammānuggahau’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘yamāhu dānaṃ paramaṃ anuttaraṃ, yaṃ saṃvibhāgaṃ bhagavā avaṇṇayi avaṇṇayī (sī॰)|

aggamhi khaittamhi pasan‍nacittau, viñ‍ñū pajānaṃ kau na yajaitha kālai||

‘‘yai  caiva bhāsanti suṇanti cūbhayaṃ, pasan‍nacittā sugatassa sāsanai|

taisaṃ sau atthau paramau visujjhati, yai appamattā sugatassa sāsanai’’ti||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| navamaṃ|

10. taivij‍jasuttaṃ

99. vuttañhaitaṃ bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

9. kuhasuttaṃ

108. vuttañhaitaṃ bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘yai kaici, bhikkhavai, bhikkhū kuhā thaddhā lapā siṅgī un‍naḷā asamāhitā, na mai tai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhū māmakā| apagatā ca tai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhū imasmā dhammavinayā; na ca tai na ca tai bhikkhavai bhikkhū (sī॰ pī॰ ka॰) imasmiṃ dhammavinayai  vuddhiṃ virūḷhiṃ vaipul‍laṃ āpaj‍janti| yai ca khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhū nik‍kuhā nil‍lapā dhīrā atthaddhā susamāhitā, tai khau mai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhū māmakā| anapagatā ca tai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhū imasmā dhammavinayā; tai ca imasmiṃ dhammavinayai imasmiṃ ca tai dhammavinayai (syā॰), tai bhikkhavai bhikkhū imasmiṃ dhammavinayai (ka॰) vuddhiṃ virūḷhiṃ vaipul‍laṃ āpaj‍jantī’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘kuhā thaddhā lapā siṅgī, un‍naḷā asamāhitā|

na tai dhammai virūhanti, sammāsambuddhadaisitai||

‘‘nik‍kuhā nil‍lapā dhīrā, atthaddhā susamāhitā|

tai vai dhammai virūhanti, sammāsambuddhadaisitai’’ti||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| navamaṃ|

10. nadīsautasuttaṃ

109. vuttañhaitaṃ  bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘saiyyathāpi, bhikkhavai, purisau nadiyā sautaina  auvuyhaiyya piyarūpasātarūpaina| tamainaṃ cakkhumā purisau tīrai ṭhitau disvā aivaṃ vadaiyya – ‘kiñ‍cāpi khau tvaṃ, ambhau purisa, nadiyā sautaina auvuyhasi piyarūpasātarūpaina, atthi caittha haiṭṭhā rahadau saūmi sāvaṭṭau sagahau sarakkhasau yaṃ tvaṃ, ambhau purisa, rahadaṃ pāpuṇitvā maraṇaṃ vā nigacchasi maraṇamattaṃ vā dukkha’nti| atha khau sau, bhikkhavai, purisau tassa purisassa saddaṃ sutvā hatthaihi ca pādaihi ca paṭisautaṃ vāyamaiyya|

‘‘upamā khau mai ayaṃ, bhikkhavai, katā atthassa viñ‍ñāpanāya| ayaṃ caittha ayaṃ caivaittha (syā॰) atthau – ‘nadiyā sautau’ti khau, bhikkhavai, taṇhāyaitaṃ adhivacanaṃ|

‘‘‘piyarūpaṃ sātarūpa’nti khau, bhikkhavai, chan‍naitaṃ ajjhattikānaṃ āyatanānaṃ adhivacanaṃ|

‘‘‘haiṭṭhā  rahadau’ti khau, bhikkhavai, pañ‍can‍naṃ aurambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ adhivacanaṃ|

‘‘‘ūmibhaya’nti khau sañīmīti khau (bahūsu), bhikkhavai, kaudhupāyāsassaitaṃ adhivacanaṃ|

‘‘‘āvaṭṭa’nti khau sāvaṭṭauti khau (bahūsu), bhikkhavai, pañ‍can‍naitaṃ kāmaguṇānaṃ adhivacanaṃ|

‘‘‘gaharakkhasau’ti khau sagahau sarakkhasauti khau (bahūsu), bhikkhavai, mātugāmassaitaṃ adhivacanaṃ|

‘‘‘paṭisautau’ti  khau, bhikkhavai, naikkhammassaitaṃ adhivacanaṃ|

‘‘‘hatthaihi ca pādaihi ca vāyāmau’ti khau, bhikkhavai, vīriyārambhassaitaṃ adhivacanaṃ|

‘‘‘cakkhumā purisau tīrai ṭhitauti khau, bhikkhavai, tathāgatassaitaṃ adhivacanaṃ arahatau sammāsambuddhassā’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘sahāpi  dukkhaina jahaiyya kāmai, yaugakkhaimaṃ āyatiṃ patthayānau|

sammappajānau  suvimuttacittau, vimuttiyā phassayai tattha tattha|

sa vaidagū vūsitabrahmacariyau, laukantagū pāragatauti vuc‍catī’’ti||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| dasamaṃ|

11. carasuttaṃ

110. vuttañhaitaṃ bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘caratau caipi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhunau uppaj‍jati kāmavitak‍kau vā byāpādavitak‍kau vā vihiṃsāvitak‍kau vā| tañ‍cai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu adhivāsaiti nappajahati na vinaudaiti na byantīkarauti byantikarauti (sī॰ pī॰), byantaṃ karauti (ka॰) anabhāvaṃ gamaiti| carampi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu aivaṃbhūtau anātāpī anauttāpī  anauttappī (sabbattha) dukanipātai, aṅguttarai 1.4.11 passitabbaṃ satataṃ samitaṃ kusītau hīnavīriyauti vuc‍cati|

‘‘ṭhitassa caipi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhunau uppaj‍jati kāmavitak‍kau vā byāpādavitak‍kau vā vihiṃsāvitak‍kau vā| tañ‍cai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu adhivāsaiti nappajahati na vinaudaiti na byantīkarauti na anabhāvaṃ gamaiti| ṭhitaupi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu aivaṃbhūtau anātāpī anauttāpī satataṃ samitaṃ kusītau hīnavīriyauti vuc‍cati|

‘‘nisin‍nassa  caipi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhunau uppaj‍jati kāmavitak‍kau vā byāpādavitak‍kau vā vihiṃsāvitak‍kau vā| tañ‍cai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu adhivāsaiti nappajahati na vinaudaiti na byantīkarauti na anabhāvaṃ gamaiti| nisin‍naupi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu aivaṃbhūtau anātāpī anauttāpī satataṃ samitaṃ kusītau hīnavīriyauti vuc‍cati|

‘‘sayānassa caipi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhunau jāgarassa uppaj‍jati kāmavitak‍kau vā byāpādavitak‍kau vā vihiṃsāvitak‍kau vā| tañ‍cai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu adhivāsaiti  nappajahati  na vinaudaiti na byantīkarauti na anabhāvaṃ gamaiti| sayānaupi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu jāgarau aivaṃbhūtau anātāpī anauttāpī satataṃ samitaṃ kusītau hīnavīriyauti vuc‍cati|

‘‘caratau caipi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhunau uppaj‍jati kāmavitak‍kau vā byāpādavitak‍kau vā vihiṃsāvitak‍kau vā| tañ‍cai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu nādhivāsaiti pajahati vinaudaiti byantīkarauti anabhāvaṃ gamaiti| carampi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu aivaṃbhūtau ātāpī auttāpī auttappī (sabbattha) satataṃ samitaṃ āraddhavīriyau pahitattauti vuc‍cati|

‘‘ṭhitassa caipi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhunau uppaj‍jati kāmavitak‍kau vā byāpādavitak‍kau vā vihiṃsāvitak‍kau vā| tañ‍cai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu nādhivāsaiti pajahati vinaudaiti byantīkarauti anabhāvaṃ gamaiti| ṭhitaupi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu aivaṃbhūtau ātāpī  auttāpī satataṃ samitaṃ āraddhavīriyau pahitattauti vuc‍cati|

‘‘nisin‍nassa caipi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhunau uppaj‍jati kāmavitak‍kau vā byāpādavitak‍kau vā vihiṃsāvitak‍kau vā| tañ‍cai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu nādhivāsaiti pajahati vinaudaiti byantīkarauti anabhāvaṃ gamaiti| nisin‍naupi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu aivaṃbhūtau ātāpī auttāpī satataṃ samitaṃ āraddhavīriyau pahitattauti vuc‍cati|

‘‘sayānassa caipi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhunau jāgarassa uppaj‍jati kāmavitak‍kau vā byāpādavitak‍kau vā vihiṃsāvitak‍kau vā| tañ‍cai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu nādhivāsaiti pajahati vinaudaiti byantīkarauti anabhāvaṃ gamaiti| sayānaupi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu jāgarau aivaṃbhūtau ātāpī auttāpī satataṃ samitaṃ  āraddhavīriyau pahitattauti vuc‍catī’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘caraṃ  vā yadi vā tiṭṭhaṃ, nisin‍nau uda vā sayaṃ|

yau vitak‍kaṃ vitak‍kaiti, pāpakaṃ gaihanissitaṃ||

‘‘kummaggaṃ paṭipan‍nau kummaggappaṭipan‍nau (a॰ ni॰ 4.11) sau, mauhanaiyyaisu mucchitau|

abhabbau tādisau bhikkhu, phuṭṭhuṃ sambaudhimuttamaṃ||

‘‘yau  ca caraṃ vā tiṭṭhaṃ vā yau caraṃ vā yadi vā tiṭṭhaṃ (syā॰), yau caraṃ vātha tiṭṭhaṃ vā (sī॰ ka॰), nisin‍nau uda vā sayaṃ|

vitak‍kaṃ samayitvāna, vitak‍kūpasamai  ratau|

bhabbau sau tādisau bhikkhu, phuṭṭhuṃ sambaudhimuttama’’nti||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| aikādasamaṃ|

12. sampan‍nasīlasuttaṃ

111. vuttañhaitaṃ bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘sampan‍nasīlā, bhikkhavai, viharatha hautha (syā॰) sampan‍napātimaukkhā; pātimaukkhasaṃvarasaṃvutā viharatha ācāragaucarasampan‍nā aṇumattaisu vaj‍jaisu bhayadassāvinau; samādāya sikkhatha sikkhāpadaisu|

‘‘sampan‍nasīlānaṃ vau, bhikkhavai, viharataṃ bhavataṃ (syā॰) sampan‍napātimaukkhānaṃ pātimaukkhasaṃvarasaṃvutānaṃ viharataṃ ācāragaucarasampan‍nānaṃ aṇumattaisu vaj‍jaisu bhayadassāvīnaṃ samādāya sikkhataṃ sikkhāpadaisu kimassa uttari karaṇīyaṃ kimassa bhikkhavai uttari karaṇīyaṃ (sabbattha)?

‘‘caratau caipi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhunau abhijjhā byāpādau vigatau (a॰ ni॰ 4.12) aṭṭhakathāya samaiti bhijjhā vigatā abhijjhā byāpādau vigatau (a॰ ni॰ 4.12) aṭakathāya samaiti hauti, byāpādau vigatau hauti thinamiddhaṃ uddhac‍cakuk‍kuc‍caṃ vicikicchā (a॰ ni॰ 4.12), thinamiddhaṃ vigataṃ hauti, uddhac‍cakuk‍kuc‍caṃ  vigataṃ hauti, vicikicchā thinamiddhaṃ uddhac‍cakuk‍kuc‍caṃ vicikic‍cā (a॰ ni॰ 4.12) pahīnā hauti, āraddhaṃ hauti vīriyaṃ asal‍līnaṃ, upaṭṭhitā sati asammuṭṭhā appamuṭṭhā (syā॰), passaddhau kāyau asāraddhau, samāhitaṃ cittaṃ aikaggaṃ| carampi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu aivaṃbhūtau ātāpī auttāpī satataṃ samitaṃ āraddhavīriyau pahitattauti vuc‍cati|

‘‘ṭhitassa caipi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhunau abhijjhā vigatā hauti byāpādau…pai॰… thinamiddhaṃ… uddhac‍cakuk‍kuc‍caṃ… vicikicchā pahīnā hauti, āraddhaṃ hauti  vīriyaṃ asal‍līnaṃ, upaṭṭhitā sati asammuṭṭhā, passaddhau kāyau asāraddhau, samāhitaṃ cittaṃ aikaggaṃ| ṭhitaupi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu aivaṃbhūtau ātāpī auttāpī satataṃ samitaṃ āraddhavīriyau pahitattauti vuc‍cati|

‘‘nisin‍nassa caipi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhunau abhijjhā vigatā hauti, byāpādau…pai॰… thinamiddhaṃ… uddhac‍cakuk‍kuc‍caṃ… vicikicchā pahīnā hauti, āraddhaṃ hauti vīriyaṃ asal‍līnaṃ, upaṭṭhitā sati asammuṭṭhā, passaddhau kāyau asāraddhau, samāhitaṃ cittaṃ aikaggaṃ| nisin‍naupi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu aivaṃbhūtau ātāpī auttāpī satataṃ samitaṃ āraddhavīriyau pahitattauti vuc‍cati|

‘‘sayānassa caipi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhunau jāgarassa abhijjhā vigatā hauti byāpādau…pai॰… thinamiddhaṃ… uddhac‍cakuk‍kuc‍caṃ… vicikicchā pahīnā hauti, āraddhaṃ hauti vīriyaṃ asal‍līnaṃ, upaṭṭhitā sati asammuṭṭhā, passaddhau kāyau asāraddhau, samāhitaṃ cittaṃ aikaggaṃ| sayānaupi, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu jāgarau aivaṃbhūtau ātāpī auttāpī satataṃ samitaṃ āraddhavīriyau pahitattauti vuc‍catī’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘yataṃ  carai yataṃ tiṭṭhai, yataṃ acchai yataṃ sayai|

yataṃ samiñ‍jayai sammiñjayai (sī॰ syā॰) bhikkhu, yatamainaṃ pasārayai||

‘‘uddhaṃ tiriyaṃ apācīnaṃ, yāvatā jagatau gati|

samavaikkhitā ca dhammānaṃ, khandhānaṃ udayabbayaṃ||

‘‘aivaṃ  vihārimātāpiṃ, santavuttimanuddhataṃ|

caitausamathasāmīciṃ, sikkhamānaṃ sadā sataṃ|

satataṃ pahitattauti, āhu bhikkhuṃ tathāvidha’’nti||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| dvādasamaṃ|

13. laukasuttaṃ

112. vuttañhaitaṃ  bhagavatā, vuttamarahatāti mai sutaṃ –

‘‘laukau, bhikkhavai, tathāgataina abhisambuddhauḥ laukasmā tathāgatau visaṃyuttau| laukasamudayau, bhikkhavai, tathāgataina abhisambuddhau ḥ laukasamudayau tathāgatassa pahīnau| laukaniraudhau, bhikkhavai, tathāgataina abhisambuddhauḥ laukaniraudhau tathāgatassa sacchikatau| laukaniraudhagāminī paṭipadā, bhikkhavai, tathāgataina abhisambuddhāḥ laukaniraudhagāminī paṭipadā tathāgatassa bhāvitā|

‘‘yaṃ, bhikkhavai, sadaivakassa laukassa samārakassa sabrahmakassa sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiyā pajāya sadaivamanussāya diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā yasmā taṃ tathāgataina abhisambuddhaṃ, tasmā tathāgatauti vuc‍cati|

‘‘yañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, rattiṃ tathāgatau anuttaraṃ sammāsambaudhiṃ abhisambujjhati, yañ‍ca rattiṃ anupādisaisāya nibbānadhātuyā parinibbāyati, yaṃ aitasmiṃ antarai bhāsati lapati  niddisati, sabbaṃ taṃ tathaiva hauti nau añ‍ñathā, tasmā tathāgatauti  vuc‍cati|

‘‘yathāvādī, bhikkhavai, tathāgatau tathākārī, yathākārī tathāvādī, iti yathāvādī tathākārī yathākārī tathāvādī, tasmā tathāgatauti vuc‍cati|

‘‘sadaivakai, bhikkhavai, laukai samārakai sabrahmakai sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiyā pajāya sadaivamanussāya tathāgatau abhibhū anabhibhūtau añ‍ñadatthudasau vasavattī, tasmā tathāgatauti vuc‍catī’’ti| aitamatthaṃ bhagavā avauca| tatthaitaṃ iti vuc‍cati –

‘‘sabbalaukaṃ sabbaṃ laukaṃ (a॰ ni॰ 4.23) abhiñ‍ñāya, sabbalaukai yathātathaṃ|

sabbalaukavisaṃyuttau, sabbalaukai anūpayau anusayau (sī॰), anupayau (syā॰)||

‘‘sa vai sabbai (sabbattha) a॰ ni॰ 4.23 passitabbaṃ sabbābhibhū dhīrau, sabbaganthappamaucanau|

phuṭṭhāssa paramā santi, nibbānaṃ akutaubhayaṃ||

‘‘aisa  khīṇāsavau buddhau, anīghau chin‍nasaṃsayau|

sabbakammakkhayaṃ pattau, vimuttau upadhisaṅkhayai||

‘‘aisa  sau bhagavā buddhau, aisa sīhau anuttarau|

sadaivakassa laukassa, brahmacak‍kaṃ pavattayi||

‘‘iti daivā manussā ca, yai buddhaṃ saraṇaṃ gatā|

saṅgamma taṃ namassanti, mahantaṃ vītasāradaṃ||

‘‘dantau damayataṃ saiṭṭhau, santau samayataṃ isi|

muttau maucayataṃ aggau, tiṇṇau tārayataṃ varau||

‘‘iti haitaṃ namassanti, mahantaṃ vītasāradaṃ|

sadaivakasmiṃ  laukasmiṃ, natthi tai paṭipuggalau’’ti||

ayampi atthau vuttau bhagavatā, iti mai sutanti| tairasamaṃ|
