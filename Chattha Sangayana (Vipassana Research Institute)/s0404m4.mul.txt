1. nissayavaggau

1. kimatthiyasuttaṃ

1. a॰ ni॰ 10.1 aivaṃ  mai sutaṃ – aikaṃ  samayaṃ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṃ viharati jaitavanai anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāmai| atha  khau āyasmā ānandau yaina bhagavā tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdi| aikamantaṃ nisin‍nau khau āyasmā ānandau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca – ‘‘kimatthiyāni, bhantai, kusalāni sīlāni kimānisaṃsānī’’ti? ‘‘avippaṭisāratthāni khau, ānanda, kusalāni sīlāni avippaṭisārānisaṃsānī’’ti|

‘‘avippaṭisārau  pana, bhantai, kimatthiyau kimānisaṃsau’’? ‘‘avippaṭisārau khau, ānanda, pāmauj‍jatthau pāmauj‍jānisaṃsau’’|

‘‘pāmauj‍jaṃ pana, bhantai, kimatthiyaṃ kimānisaṃsaṃ’’? ‘‘pāmauj‍jaṃ khau, ānanda, pītatthaṃ pītānisaṃsaṃ’’|

‘‘pīti pana, bhantai, kimatthiyā kimānisaṃsā’’? ‘‘pīti khau, ānanda, passaddhatthā passaddhānisaṃsā’’|

‘‘passaddhi pana, bhantai, kimatthiyā kimānisaṃsā’’? ‘‘passaddhi khau, ānanda, sukhatthā sukhānisaṃsā’’|

‘‘sukhaṃ pana, bhantai, kimatthiyaṃ kimānisaṃsaṃ’’? ‘‘sukhaṃ khau, ānanda, samādhatthaṃ samādhānisaṃsaṃ’’|

‘‘samādhi  pana, bhantai, kimatthiyau kimānisaṃsau’’? ‘‘samādhi khau, ānanda, yathābhūtañāṇadassanatthau yathābhūtañāṇadassanānisaṃsau’’|

‘‘yathābhūtañāṇadassanaṃ pana, bhantai, kimatthiyaṃ kimānisaṃsaṃ’’? ‘‘yathābhūtañāṇadassanaṃ khau, ānanda, nibbidatthaṃ nibbidānisaṃsaṃ’’|

‘‘nibbidā, pana, bhantai, kimatthiyā kimānisaṃsā’’? ‘‘nibbidā khau, ānanda, virāgatthā virāgānisaṃsā ’’|

‘‘virāgau pana, bhantai, kimatthiyau kimānisaṃsau’’? ‘‘virāgau khau, ānanda, vimuttiñāṇadassanatthau vimuttiñāṇadassanānisaṃsau|

‘‘iti khau, ānanda, kusalāni sīlāni avippaṭisāratthāni avippaṭisārānisaṃsāni, avippaṭisārau  pāmauj‍jatthau pāmauj‍jānisaṃsau, pāmauj‍jaṃ pītatthaṃ pītānisaṃsaṃ, pīti passaddhatthā passaddhānisaṃsā, passaddhi sukhatthā sukhānisaṃsā, sukhaṃ samādhatthaṃ samādhānisaṃsaṃ, samādhi yathābhūtañāṇadassanatthau yathābhūtañāṇadassanānisaṃsau, yathābhūtañāṇadassanaṃ nibbidatthaṃ nibbidānisaṃsaṃ, nibbidā virāgatthā virāgānisaṃsā, virāgau vimuttiñāṇadassanatthau vimuttiñāṇadassanānisaṃsau| iti khau, ānanda, kusalāni sīlāni anupubbaina aggāya paraintī’’ti| paṭhamaṃ|

2. caitanākaraṇīyasuttaṃ

2. a॰ ni॰ 10.2 ‘‘sīlavatau, bhikkhavai, sīlasampan‍nassa na caitanāya karaṇīyaṃ – ‘avippaṭisārau mai uppaj‍jatū’ti| dhammatā aisā, bhikkhavai, yaṃ sīlavatau sīlasampan‍nassa avippaṭisārau uppaj‍jati|

‘‘avippaṭisārissa, bhikkhavai, na caitanāya karaṇīyaṃ – ‘pāmauj‍jaṃ mai uppaj‍jatū’ti| dhammatā aisā, bhikkhavai, yaṃ avippaṭisārissa pāmauj‍jaṃ uppaj‍jati|

‘‘pamuditassa, bhikkhavai, na caitanāya karaṇīyaṃ – ‘pīti mai uppaj‍jatū’ti| dhammatā aisā, bhikkhavai, yaṃ pamuditassa pīti uppaj‍jati|

‘‘pītimanassa, bhikkhavai, na caitanāya karaṇīyaṃ – ‘kāyau mai  passambhatū’ti| dhammatā aisā, bhikkhavai, yaṃ pītimanassa kāyau passambhati|

‘‘passaddhakāyassa, bhikkhavai, na caitanāya karaṇīyaṃ – ‘sukhaṃ vaidiyāmī’ti| dhammatā aisā, bhikkhavai, yaṃ passaddhakāyau sukhaṃ vaidiyati|

‘‘sukhinau, bhikkhavai, na caitanāya karaṇīyaṃ – ‘cittaṃ mai samādhiyatū’ti| dhammatā aisā, bhikkhavai, yaṃ sukhinau cittaṃ samādhiyati|

‘‘samāhitassa, bhikkhavai, na caitanāya karaṇīyaṃ – ‘yathābhūtaṃ jānāmi passāmī’ti| dhammatā aisā, bhikkhavai, yaṃ samāhitau yathābhūtaṃ jānāti passati|

‘‘yathābhūtaṃ, bhikkhavai, jānatau passatau na caitanāya karaṇīyaṃ – ‘nibbindāmī’ti| dhammatā aisā, bhikkhavai, yaṃ yathābhūtaṃ jānaṃ passaṃ nibbindati|

‘‘nibbin‍nassa, bhikkhavai, na caitanāya karaṇīyaṃ – ‘viraj‍jāmī’ti| dhammatā aisā, bhikkhavai, yaṃ nibbin‍nau viraj‍jati|

‘‘virattassa, bhikkhavai, na caitanāya karaṇīyaṃ – ‘vimuttiñāṇadassanaṃ sacchikaraumī’ti| dhammatā aisā, bhikkhavai, yaṃ virattau vimuttiñāṇadassanaṃ sacchikarauti|

‘‘iti khau, bhikkhavai, virāgau vimuttiñāṇadassanatthau vimuttiñāṇadassanānisaṃsau, nibbidā virāgatthā virāgānisaṃsā, yathābhūtañāṇadassanaṃ nibbidatthaṃ nibbidānisaṃsaṃ, samādhi yathābhūtañāṇadassanatthau yathābhūtañāṇadassanānisaṃsau, sukhaṃ samādhatthaṃ samādhānisaṃsaṃ, passaddhi sukhatthā sukhānisaṃsā, pīti passaddhatthā passaddhānisaṃsā, pāmauj‍jaṃ pītatthaṃ pītānisaṃsaṃ, avippaṭisārau pāmauj‍jatthau pāmauj‍jānisaṃsau, kusalāni sīlāni avippaṭisāratthāni avippaṭisārānisaṃsāni| iti khau, bhikkhavai, dhammā dhammai  abhisandainti, dhammā dhammai paripūrainti apārā pāraṃ gamanāyā’’ti| dutiyaṃ|

3. paṭhamaupanisāsuttaṃ

3. a॰ ni॰ 5.24; 10.3 ‘‘dussīlassa, bhikkhavai, sīlavipan‍nassa hatūpanisau hauti avippaṭisārau| avippaṭisārai asati avippaṭisāravipan‍nassa hatūpanisaṃ hauti pāmauj‍jaṃ| pāmauj‍jai asati pāmauj‍javipan‍nassa hatūpanisā hauti pīti| pītiyā asati pītivipan‍nassa hatūpanisā hauti passaddhi| passaddhiyā asati  passaddhivipan‍nassa hatūpanisaṃ  hauti sukhaṃ| sukhai asati sukhavipan‍nassa hatūpanisau hauti sammāsamādhi| sammāsamādhimhi asati sammāsamādhivipan‍nassa hatūpanisaṃ hauti yathābhūtañāṇadassanaṃ| yathābhūtañāṇadassanai asati yathābhūtañāṇadassanavipan‍nassa hatūpanisā  hauti nibbidā| nibbidāya asati nibbidāvipan‍nassa hatūpanisau hauti virāgau| virāgai asati virāgavipan‍nassa hatūpanisaṃ hauti vimuttiñāṇadassanaṃ|

‘‘saiyyathāpi, bhikkhavai, rukkhau sākhāpalāsavipan‍nau| tassa papaṭikāpi na pāripūriṃ gacchati, tacaupi… phaiggupi… sāraupi na pāripūriṃ gacchati| aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, dussīlassa sīlavipan‍nassa hatūpanisau hauti avippaṭisārau, avippaṭisārai asati avippaṭisāravipan‍nassa hatūpanisaṃ hauti pāmauj‍jaṃ…pai॰… vimuttiñāṇadassanaṃ|

‘‘sīlavatau, bhikkhavai, sīlasampan‍nassa upanisasampan‍nau hauti avippaṭisārau, avippaṭisārai sati avippaṭisārasampan‍nassa upanisasampan‍naṃ hauti pāmauj‍jaṃ, pāmauj‍jai sati pāmauj‍jasampan‍nassa upanisasampan‍nā hauti pīti, pītiyā sati pītisampan‍nassa upanisasampan‍nā hauti passaddhi, passaddhiyā sati passaddhisampan‍nassa upanisasampan‍naṃ hauti sukhaṃ, sukhai sati sukhasampan‍nassa upanisasampan‍nau  hauti sammāsamādhi, sammāsamādhimhi sati sammāsamādhisampan‍nassa upanisasampan‍naṃ hauti yathābhūtañāṇadassanaṃ, yathābhūtañāṇadassanai sati yathābhūtañāṇadassanasampan‍nassa upanisasampan‍nā hauti nibbidā, nibbidāya sati nibbidāsampan‍nassa upanisasampan‍nau hauti virāgau, virāgai sati virāgasampan‍nassa upanisasampan‍naṃ hauti vimuttiñāṇadassanaṃ|

‘‘saiyyathāpi, bhikkhavai, rukkhau sākhāpalāsasampan‍nau| tassa papaṭikāpi pāripūriṃ gacchati, tacaupi… phaiggupi… sāraupi pāripūriṃ gacchati| aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, sīlavatau sīlasampan‍nassa upanisasampan‍nau hauti avippaṭisārau, avippaṭisārai sati avippaṭisārasampan‍nassa upanisasampan‍naṃ hauti…pai॰… vimuttiñāṇadassana’’nti| tatiyaṃ|

4. dutiyaupanisāsuttaṃ

‘‘dussīlassa, āvusau, sīlavipan‍nassa hatūpanisau hauti avippaṭisārau, avippaṭisārai asati avippaṭisāravipan‍nassa hatūpanisaṃ hauti pāmauj‍jaṃ, pāmauj‍jai asati pāmauj‍javipan‍nassa hatūpanisā hauti pīti, pītiyā asati pītivipan‍nassa hatūpanisā hauti passaddhi, passaddhiyā asati passaddhivipan‍nassa hatūpanisaṃ hauti sukhaṃ, sukhai asati sukhavipan‍nassa hatūpanisau hauti sammāsamādhi, sammāsamādhimhi asati sammāsamādhivipan‍nassa hatūpanisaṃ hauti yathābhūtañāṇadassanaṃ, yathābhūtañāṇadassanai asati yathābhūtañāṇadassanavipan‍nassa hatūpanisā hauti nibbidā, nibbidāya asati nibbidāvipan‍nassa hatūpanisau hauti virāgau, virāgai asati virāgavipan‍nassa hatūpanisaṃ hauti vimuttiñāṇadassanaṃ|

‘‘saiyyathāpi, āvusau, rukkhau sākhāpalāsasampan‍nau| tassa papaṭikāpi pāripūriṃ gacchati, tacaupi… phaiggupi… sāraupi pāripūriṃ gacchati| aivamaivaṃ khau, āvusau, sīlavatau sīlasampan‍nassa upanisasampan‍nau hauti avippaṭisārau, avippaṭisārai  sati avippaṭisārasampan‍nassa upanisasampan‍naṃ hauti pāmauj‍jaṃ…pai॰… vimuttiñāṇadassana’’nti| catutthaṃ|

6. byasanasuttaṃ

6. ‘‘yau sau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu ak‍kausakau paribhāsakau ariyūpavādau sabrahmacārīnaṃ, ṭhānamaitaṃ avakāsau yaṃ sau aikādasan‍naṃ byasanānaṃ añ‍ñataraṃ byasanaṃ nigacchaiyya|

katamaisaṃ aikādasan‍naṃ? anadhigataṃ nādhigacchati, adhigatā parihāyati, saddhammassa na vaudāyanti, saddhammaisu vā adhimānikau hauti, anabhiratau vā brahmacariyaṃ carati, añ‍ñataraṃ vā saṃkiliṭṭhaṃ  āpattiṃ āpaj‍jati, sikkhaṃ vā pac‍cakkhāya hīnāyāvattati, gāḷhaṃ vā raugātaṅkaṃ phusati, ummādaṃ vā pāpuṇāti cittakkhaipaṃ vā, sammūḷhau kālaṃ karauti, kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapaj‍jati – yau sau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu ak‍kausakau paribhāsakau ariyūpavādau sabrahmacārīnaṃ, ṭhānamaitaṃ avakāsau yaṃ sau imaisaṃ aikādasan‍naṃ byasanānaṃ añ‍ñataraṃ byasanaṃ nigacchaiyya| ( ) aitthantarai pāṭhau sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰ pautthakaisu na dissati

‘‘yau sau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu ak‍kausakau paribhāsakau ariyūpavādau sabrahmacārīnaṃ, aṭṭhānamaitaṃ anavakāsau yaṃ sau aikādasan‍naṃ byasanānaṃ añ‍ñataraṃ byasanaṃ na nigacchaiyya|

katamaisaṃ  aikādasan‍naṃ? anadhigataṃ nādhigacchati, adhigatā parihāyati, saddhammassa na vaudāyanti, saddhammaisu vā adhimānikau hauti, anabhiratau vā brahmacariyaṃ carati, añ‍ñataraṃ vā saṃkiliṭṭhaṃ āpattiṃ āpaj‍jati, sikkhaṃ vā pac‍cakkhāya hīnāyāvattati, gāḷhaṃ vā raugātaṅkaṃ phusati, ummādaṃ vā pāpuṇāti cittakkhaipaṃ vā, sammūḷhau kālaṃ karauti, kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ  nirayaṃ upapaj‍jati – yau sau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu ak‍kausakau paribhāsakau ariyūpavādau sabrahmacārīnaṃ, aṭṭhānamaitaṃ anavakāsau yaṃ sau imaisaṃ aikādasan‍naṃ byasanānaṃ añ‍ñataraṃ byasanaṃ na nigacchaiyyā’’ti| chaṭṭhaṃ|

7. sañ‍ñāsuttaṃ

7. atha  khau āyasmā ānandau yaina bhagavā tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdi| aikamantaṃ nisin‍nau khau āyasmā ānandau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca –

‘‘siyā nu khau, bhantai, bhikkhunau tathārūpau samādhipaṭilābhau yathā naiva pathaviyaṃ pathavisañ‍ñī assa, na āpasmiṃ āpausañ‍ñī assa, na taijasmiṃ taijausañ‍ñī assa, na vāyasmiṃ vāyausañ‍ñī assa, na ākāsānañ‍cāyatanai ākāsānañ‍cāyatanasañ‍ñī assa, na viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanai viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanasañ‍ñī assa, na ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanai ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanasañ‍ñī assa, na naivasañ‍ñānāsañ‍ñāyatanai naivasañ‍ñānāsañ‍ñāyatanasañ‍ñī assa, na idhalaukai idhalaukasañ‍ñī assa, na paralaukai paralaukasañ‍ñī assa, yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tatrāpi na sañ‍ñī assa; sañ‍ñī ca pana assāti?

‘‘siyā, ānanda, bhikkhunau tathārūpau samādhipaṭilābhau yathā naiva pathaviyaṃ pathavisañ‍ñī assa, na āpasmiṃ āpausañ‍ñī assa, na taijasmiṃ taijausañ‍ñī assa, na vāyasmiṃ vāyausañ‍ñī assa, na ākāsānañ‍cāyatanai ākāsānañ‍cāyatanasañ‍ñī assa, na viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanai viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanasañ‍ñī assa, na ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanai ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanasañ‍ñī  assa, na naivasañ‍ñānāsañ‍ñāyatanai naivasañ‍ñānāsañ‍ñāyatanasañ‍ñī assa, na idhalaukai idhalaukasañ‍ñī assa, na paralaukai paralaukasañ‍ñī  assa, yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tatrāpi na sañ‍ñī assa; sañ‍ñī ca pana assā’’ti|

‘‘yathā kathaṃ pana, bhantai, siyā bhikkhunau tathārūpau samādhipaṭilābhau yathā naiva pathaviyaṃ pathavisañ‍ñī assa, na āpasmiṃ āpausañ‍ñī assa, na taijasmiṃ taijausañ‍ñī assa, na vāyasmiṃ vāyausañ‍ñī assa, na ākāsānañ‍cāyatanai ākāsānañ‍cāyatanasañ‍ñī assa, na viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanai viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanasañ‍ñī assa, na ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanai ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanasañ‍ñī  assa, na naivasañ‍ñānāsañ‍ñāyatanai naivasañ‍ñānāsañ‍ñāyatanasañ‍ñī assa, na idhalaukai idhalaukasañ‍ñī assa, na paralaukai paralaukasañ‍ñī assa, yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tatrāpi na sañ‍ñī assa, sañ‍ñī ca pana assāti|

‘‘idhānanda, bhikkhu aivaṃsañ‍ñī hauti – ‘aitaṃ santaṃ aitaṃ paṇītaṃ, yadidaṃ sabbasaṅkhārasamathau sabbūpadhipaṭinissaggau taṇhākkhayau virāgau niraudhau nibbāna’nti| aivaṃ khau, ānanda, siyā bhikkhunau tathārūpau samādhipaṭilābhau yathā naiva pathaviyaṃ pathavisañ‍ñī assa, na āpasmiṃ āpausañ‍ñī assa, na taijasmiṃ taijausañ‍ñī assa, na vāyasmiṃ vāyausañ‍ñī assa, na ākāsānañ‍cāyatanai ākāsānañ‍cāyatanasañ‍ñī assa, na viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanai viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanasañ‍ñī  assa, na ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanai ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanasañ‍ñī assa, na naivasañ‍ñānāsañ‍ñāyatanai naivasañ‍ñānāsañ‍ñāyatanasañ‍ñī assa, na idhalaukai idhalaukasañ‍ñī assa, na paralaukai paralaukasañ‍ñī assa, yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tatrāpi na sañ‍ñī assa, sañ‍ñī ca pana assā’’ti|

atha khau āyasmā ānandau bhagavatau bhāsitaṃ abhinanditvā anumauditvā uṭṭhāyāsanā bhagavantaṃ abhivādaitvā padakkhiṇaṃ  katvā yaināyasmā sāriputtau tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā āyasmatā sāriputtaina saddhiṃ sammaudi| sammaudanīyaṃ kathaṃ sāraṇīyaṃ vītisāraitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdi| aikamantaṃ nisin‍nau khau āyasmā ānandau āyasmantaṃ sāriputtaṃ aitadavauca –

‘‘siyā  nu khau, āvusau sāriputta, bhikkhunau tathārūpau samādhipaṭilābhau yathā naiva pathaviyaṃ pathavisañ‍ñī assa…pai॰… yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tatrāpi na sañ‍ñī assa, sañ‍ñī pana assāti| ‘‘siyā, āvusau ānanda, bhikkhunau tathārūpau samādhipaṭilābhau yathā naiva pathaviyaṃ pathavisañ‍ñī assa…pai॰… yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tatrāpi na sañ‍ñī assa, sañ‍ñī ca pana assā’’ti|

‘‘yathā kathaṃ panāvusau sāriputta, siyā bhikkhunau tathārūpau samādhipaṭilābhau yathā naiva pathaviyaṃ  pathavisañ‍ñī assa…pai॰… yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tatrāpi na sañ‍ñī assa, sañ‍ñī ca pana assā’’ti?

‘‘idha, āvusau ānanda, bhikkhu aivaṃsañ‍ñī hauti – ‘aitaṃ santaṃ aitaṃ paṇītaṃ, yadidaṃ sabbasaṅkhārasamathau sabbūpadhipaṭinissaggau taṇhākkhayau virāgau niraudhau nibbāna’nti| aivaṃ khau, āvusau ānanda, siyā bhikkhunau tathārūpau samādhipaṭilābhau yathā naiva pathaviyaṃ pathavisañ‍ñī assa…pai॰… yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tatrāpi na sañ‍ñī assa, sañ‍ñī ca pana assā’’ti|

‘‘acchariyaṃ, āvusau, abbhutaṃ, āvusau! yatra hi nāma satthu caiva sāvakassa ca atthaina atthau byañ‍janaina byañ‍janaṃ saṃsandissati samaissati na viggayhissati, yadidaṃ aggapadasmiṃ! idānāhaṃ, āvusau, bhagavantaṃ upasaṅkamitvā  aitamatthaṃ apucchiṃ| bhagavāpi mai aitaihi akkharaihi aitaihi padaihi aitaihi byañ‍janaihi aitamatthaṃ byākāsi, saiyyathāpi āyasmā sāriputtau| acchariyaṃ, āvusau, abbhutaṃ, āvusau, yatra hi nāma satthu caiva sāvakassa ca atthaina atthau byañ‍janaina byañ‍janaṃ saṃsandissati samaissati na viggayhissati, yadidaṃ aggapadasmi’’nti! sattamaṃ|

8. manasikārasuttaṃ

8. atha khau āyasmā ānandau yaina bhagavā tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdi| aikamantaṃ nisin‍nau khau āyasmā ānandau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca –

‘‘siyā  nu khau, bhantai, bhikkhunau tathārūpau samādhipaṭilābhau yathā na cakkhuṃ manasi karaiyya, na rūpaṃ manasi karaiyya, na sautaṃ manasi karaiyya, na saddaṃ manasi karaiyya, na  ghānaṃ manasi karaiyya, na gandhaṃ manasi karaiyya, na jivhaṃ manasi karaiyya, na rasaṃ manasi karaiyya, na kāyaṃ manasi karaiyya, na phauṭṭhabbaṃ manasi karaiyya, na pathaviṃ manasi karaiyya, na āpaṃ manasi karaiyya, na taijaṃ manasi karaiyya, na vāyaṃ manasi karaiyya, na ākāsānañ‍cāyatanaṃ manasi  karaiyya, na viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanaṃ manasi karaiyya, na ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanaṃ manasi karaiyya, na naivasañ‍ñānāsañ‍ñāyatanaṃ manasi karaiyya, na idhalaukaṃ manasi karaiyya, na paralaukaṃ manasi karaiyya, yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tampi na manasi karaiyya; manasi ca pana karaiyyā’’ti?

‘‘siyā, ānanda, bhikkhunau tathārūpau samādhipaṭilābhau yathā na cakkhuṃ manasi karaiyya, na rūpaṃ manasi karaiyya, na sautaṃ manasi karaiyya, na saddaṃ manasi karaiyya, na ghānaṃ manasi karaiyya, na gandhaṃ manasi karaiyya, na jivhaṃ manasi karaiyya, na rasaṃ manasi karaiyya, na kāyaṃ manasi karaiyya, na phauṭṭhabbaṃ manasi karaiyya, na pathaviṃ manasi karaiyya, na āpaṃ manasi karaiyya, na taijaṃ manasi  karaiyya, na vāyaṃ manasi karaiyya, na ākāsānañ‍cāyatanaṃ manasi karaiyya, na viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanaṃ manasi karaiyya, na ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanaṃ manasi karaiyya, na naivasañ‍ñānāsañ‍ñāyatanaṃ manasi karaiyya, na idhalaukaṃ manasi karaiyya, na paralaukaṃ manasi karaiyya, yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tampi na manasi karaiyya; manasi ca pana karaiyyā’’ti|

‘‘yathā kathaṃ pana, bhantai, siyā bhikkhunau tathārūpau samādhipaṭilābhau yathā na cakkhuṃ manasi karaiyya, na rūpaṃ manasi karaiyya… yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ  anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tampi na manasi karaiyya; manasi ca pana karaiyyā’’ti?

‘‘idhānanda, bhikkhu aivaṃ manasi karauti – ‘aitaṃ santaṃ aitaṃ paṇītaṃ, yadidaṃ sabbasaṅkhārasamathau sabbūpadhipaṭinissaggau taṇhākkhayau virāgau niraudhau nibbāna’nti| aivaṃ khau, ānanda, siyā bhikkhunau tathārūpau samādhipaṭilābhau yathā na cakkhuṃ manasi karaiyya, na rūpaṃ manasi karaiyya…pai॰… yampidaṃ  diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tampi na manasi karaiyya; manasi ca pana karaiyyā’’ti| aṭṭhamaṃ|

9. saddhasuttaṃ

9. aikaṃ samayaṃ bhagavā nātikai viharati giñ‍jakāvasathai| atha khau āyasmā saddhau yaina  bhagavā tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdi| aikamantaṃ nisin‍naṃ khau āyasmantaṃ saddhaṃ bhagavā aitadavauca –

‘‘ājānīyajhāyitaṃ khau, saddha, jhāya; mā khaḷuṅkajhāyitaṃ ājānīyajjhāyitaṃ khau saddha jhāyatha, mā khaḷuṅkajjhāyitaṃ (sī॰ pī॰)| kathañ‍ca, khaḷuṅkajhāyitaṃ hauti? assakhaḷuṅkau hi, saddha, dauṇiyā baddhau bandhau (syā॰ ka॰) ‘yavasaṃ yavasa’nti jhāyati| taṃ kissa haitu? na hi, saddha, assakhaḷuṅkassa dauṇiyā baddhassa aivaṃ hauti – ‘kiṃ nu khau maṃ aj‍ja assadammasārathi kāraṇaṃ kāraissati, kimassāhaṃ kammassāhaṃ (ka॰) paṭikaraumī’ti| sau dauṇiyā baddhau ‘yavasaṃ yavasa’nti jhāyati| aivamaivaṃ khau, saddha, idhaikac‍cau purisakhaḷuṅkau arañ‍ñagataupi rukkhamūlagataupi suñ‍ñāgāragataupi kāmarāgapariyuṭṭhitaina caitasā viharati kāmarāgaparaitaina uppan‍nassa ca kāmarāgassa nissaraṇaṃ yathābhūtaṃ nappajānāti| sau kāmarāgaṃyaiva antaraṃ katvā jhāyati pajjhāyati nijjhāyati avajjhāyati, byāpādapariyuṭṭhitaina caitasā viharati… thinamiddhapariyuṭṭhitaina caitasā viharati… uddhac‍cakuk‍kuc‍capariyuṭṭhitaina caitasā viharati… vicikicchāpariyuṭṭhitaina caitasā viharati vicikicchāparaitaina, uppan‍nāya ca vicikicchāya nissaraṇaṃ yathābhūtaṃ nappajānāti| sau vicikicchaṃyaiva antaraṃ katvā jhāyati pajjhāyati nijjhāyati  avajjhāyati| sau pathavimpi nissāya jhāyati, āpampi nissāya jhāyati, taijampi nissāya jhāyati, vāyampi nissāya jhāyati, ākāsānañ‍cāyatanampi nissāya jhāyati, viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanampi nissāya jhāyati, ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanampi nissāya jhāyati, naivasañ‍ñānāsañ‍ñāyatanampi nissāya jhāyati, idhalaukampi nissāya jhāyati, paralaukampi nissāya jhāyati, yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tampi nissāya jhāyati| aivaṃ khau, saddha, purisakhaḷuṅkajhāyitaṃ hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, saddha, ājānīyajhāyitaṃ hauti? bhadrau hi, saddha, assājānīyau dauṇiyā baddhau na ‘yavasaṃ yavasa’nti jhāyati| taṃ kissa haitu? bhadrassa hi, saddha, assājānīyassa dauṇiyā baddhassa aivaṃ hauti – ‘kiṃ nu khau maṃ aj‍ja assadammasārathi kāraṇaṃ kāraissati, kimassāhaṃ paṭikaraumī’ti| sau dauṇiyā baddhau na ‘yavasaṃ yavasa’nti jhāyati| bhadrau hi, saddha, assājānīyau yathā iṇaṃ yathā bandhaṃ yathā jāniṃ yathā kaliṃ aivaṃ pataudassa ajjhauharaṇaṃ samanupassati| aivamaivaṃ khau, saddha, bhadrau purisājānīyau arañ‍ñagataupi rukkhamūlagataupi suñ‍ñāgāragataupi na kāmarāgapariyuṭṭhitaina caitasā viharati na kāmarāgaparaitaina, uppan‍nassa ca kāmarāgassa  nissaraṇaṃ yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti, na byāpādapariyuṭṭhitaina caitasā viharati… na thinamiddhapariyuṭṭhitaina caitasā viharati… na uddhac‍cakuk‍kuc‍capariyuṭṭhitaina caitasā viharati… na vicikicchāpariyuṭṭhitaina caitasā viharati na vicikicchāparaitaina, uppan‍nāya ca vicikicchāya nissaraṇaṃ yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti| sau naiva pathaviṃ nissāya jhāyati, na āpaṃ nissāya jhāyati, na taijaṃ nissāya jhāyati, na vāyaṃ nissāya jhāyati, na ākāsānañ‍cāyatanaṃ nissāya jhāyati, na viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanaṃ nissāya jhāyati, na ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanaṃ nissāya jhāyati, na naivasañ‍ñānāsañ‍ñāyatanaṃ nissāya jhāyati, na idhalaukaṃ  nissāya jhāyati, na paralaukaṃ nissāya jhāyati, yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tampi nissāya na jhāyati; jhāyati ca pana| aivaṃ  jhāyiñ‍ca pana, saddha, bhadraṃ purisājānīyaṃ saindā daivā sabrahmakā sapajāpatikā ārakāva namassanti –

‘‘namau tai purisājañ‍ña, namau tai purisuttama|

yassa tai nābhijānāma, yampi nissāya jhāyasī’’ti||

aivaṃ vuttai āyasmā saddhau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca – ‘‘kathaṃ jhāyī pana, bhantai, bhadrau purisājānīyau purisājānīyau jhāyati, sau (sī॰ syā॰ pī॰), purisājānīyau, sau (ka॰) naiva pathaviṃ nissāya jhāyati, na āpaṃ nissāya jhāyati, na taijaṃ nissāya jhāyati, na vāyaṃ nissāya jhāyati, na ākāsānañ‍cāyatanaṃ nissāya jhāyati, na viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanaṃ nissāya jhāyati, na ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanaṃ nissāya jhāyati, na naivasañ‍ñānāsañ‍ñāyatanaṃ nissāya jhāyati, na idhalaukaṃ nissāya jhāyati, na paralaukaṃ nissāya jhāyati, yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tampi nissāya na jhāyati; jhāyati ca pana? kathaṃ jhāyiñ‍ca pana, bhantai, bhadraṃ purisājānīyaṃ saindā daivā sabrahmakā sapajāpatikā ārakāva namassanti –

‘‘namau tai purisājañ‍ña, namau tai purisuttama|

yassa tai nābhijānāma, yampi nissāya jhāyasī’’ti||

‘‘idha, saddha, bhadrassa purisājānīyassa pathaviyaṃ pathavisañ‍ñā vibhūtā hauti, āpasmiṃ āpausañ‍ñā  vibhūtā hauti, taijasmiṃ taijausañ‍ñā vibhūtā hauti, vāyasmiṃ vāyausañ‍ñā vibhūtā hauti, ākāsānañ‍cāyatanai ākāsānañ‍cāyatanasañ‍ñā  vibhūtā hauti, viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanai viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanasañ‍ñā vibhūtā  hauti, ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanai ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanasañ‍ñā vibhūtā hauti, naivasañ‍ñānāsañ‍ñāyatanai naivasañ‍ñānāsañ‍ñāyatanasañ‍ñā vibhūtā hauti, idhalaukai idhalaukasañ‍ñā vibhūtā hauti, paralaukai paralaukasañ‍ñā vibhūtā hauti, yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tatrāpi sañ‍ñā vibhūtā hauti| aivaṃ jhāyī khau, saddha, bhadrau purisājānīyau naiva pathaviṃ nissāya jhāyati…pai॰… yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā, tampi nissāya na jhāyati; jhāyati ca pana| aivaṃ jhāyiñ‍ca pana, saddha, bhadraṃ purisājānīyaṃ saindā daivā sabrahmakā sapajāpatikā ārakāva namassanti –

‘‘namau tai purisājañ‍ña, namau tai purisuttama|

yassa tai nābhijānāma, yampi nissāya jhāyasī’’ti|| navamaṃ|

10. mauranivāpasuttaṃ

11. aikaṃ  samayaṃ bhagavā sak‍kaisu viharati kapilavatthusmiṃ nigraudhārāmai| taina khau pana samayaina sambahulā bhikkhū bhagavatau cīvarakammaṃ karaunti – ‘‘niṭṭhitacīvarau bhagavā taimāsac‍cayaina cārikaṃ pak‍kamissatī’’ti| assausi khau mahānāmau sak‍kau – ‘‘sambahulā kira bhikkhū bhagavatau cīvarakammaṃ karaunti – ‘niṭṭhitacīvarau bhagavā taimāsac‍cayaina cārikaṃ pak‍kamissatī’’’ti|

atha khau mahānāmau sak‍kau yaina bhagavā tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdi| aikamantaṃ nisin‍nau khau mahānāmau sak‍kau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca – ‘‘sutaṃ maitaṃ, bhantai – ‘sambahulā kira bhikkhū bhagavatau cīvarakammaṃ karaunti – niṭṭhitacīvarau bhagavā taimāsac‍cayaina cārikaṃ pak‍kamissatī’ti| taisaṃ nau, bhantai, nānāvihāraihi viharataṃ kainassa kaina (syā॰ kaṃ॰) vihāraina vihātabba’’nti?

‘‘sādhu  sādhu, mahānāma! aitaṃ khau, mahānāma, tumhākaṃ patirūpaṃ kulaputtānaṃ, yaṃ tumhai tathāgataṃ upasaṅkamitvā pucchaiyyātha – ‘taisaṃ nau, bhantai, nānāvihāraihi viharataṃ kainassa vihāraina vihātabba’’’nti? saddhau khau, mahānāma, ārādhakau hauti, nau assaddhau; āraddhavīriyau ārādhakau hauti, nau kusītau; upaṭṭhitassati ārādhakau hauti, nau muṭṭhassati; samāhitau ārādhakau hauti, nau asamāhitau; pañ‍ñavā ārādhakau hauti, nau duppañ‍ñau| imaisu khau tvaṃ, mahānāma, pañ‍casu dhammaisu patiṭṭhāya cha dhammai uttari uttariṃ (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) bhāvaiyyāsi| a॰ ni॰ 6.10 ‘‘idha tvaṃ, mahānāma, tathāgataṃ anussaraiyyāsi – ‘itipi sau bhagavā  arahaṃ sammāsambuddhau vij‍jācaraṇasampan‍nau sugatau laukavidū anuttarau purisadammasārathi satthā daivamanussānaṃ buddhau bhagavā’ti| yasmiṃ, mahānāma, samayai ariyasāvakau tathāgataṃ anussarati, naivassa tasmiṃ  samayai rāgapariyuṭṭhitaṃ cittaṃ hauti, na dausapariyuṭṭhitaṃ cittaṃ hauti, na mauhapariyuṭṭhitaṃ cittaṃ hauti; ujugatamaivassa tasmiṃ samayai cittaṃ hauti tathāgataṃ ārabbha| ujugatacittau khau pana, mahānāma, ariyasāvakau labhati atthavaidaṃ, labhati dhammavaidaṃ, labhati dhammūpasaṃhitaṃ pāmauj‍jaṃ| pamuditassa pīti jāyati, pītimanassa kāyau passambhati, passaddhakāyau sukhaṃ vaidiyati, sukhinau cittaṃ samādhiyati| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, mahānāma, ariyasāvakau visamagatāya pajāya samappattau viharati, sabyāpaj‍jāya pajāya abyāpaj‍jau viharati, dhammasautasamāpan‍nau buddhānussatiṃ bhāvaiti|

‘‘puna caparaṃ tvaṃ, mahānāma, dhammaṃ anussaraiyyāsi – ‘svākkhātau bhagavatā dhammau sandiṭṭhikau akālikau aihipassikau aupanaiyyikau aupanayikau (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) pac‍cattaṃ vaiditabbau viñ‍ñūhī’ti| yasmiṃ, mahānāma, samayai ariyasāvakau dhammaṃ anussarati, naivassa tasmiṃ samayai rāgapariyuṭṭhitaṃ cittaṃ  hauti, na dausapariyuṭṭhitaṃ cittaṃ hauti, na mauhapariyuṭṭhitaṃ cittaṃ hauti; ujugatamaivassa tasmiṃ samayai cittaṃ hauti dhammaṃ ārabbha| ujugatacittau khau pana, mahānāma, ariyasāvakau labhati atthavaidaṃ, labhati dhammavaidaṃ, labhati dhammūpasaṃhitaṃ pāmauj‍jaṃ| pamuditassa pīti jāyati, pītimanassa kāyau passambhati, passaddhakāyau sukhaṃ vaidiyati, sukhinau cittaṃ samādhiyati| ayaṃ  vuc‍cati, mahānāma, ariyasāvakau visamagatāya pajāya samappattau viharati, sabyāpaj‍jāya pajāya abyāpaj‍jau viharati, dhammasautasamāpan‍nau dhammānussatiṃ bhāvaiti|

‘‘puna caparaṃ tvaṃ, mahānāma, saṅghaṃ anussaraiyyāsi – ‘suppaṭipan‍nau bhagavatau sāvakasaṅghau, ujuppaṭipan‍nau bhagavatau sāvakasaṅghau, ñāyappaṭipan‍nau bhagavatau  sāvakasaṅghau, sāmīcippaṭipan‍nau bhagavatau sāvakasaṅghau, yadidaṃ cattāri purisayugāni aṭṭha purisapuggalā, aisa bhagavatau sāvakasaṅghau āhunaiyyau pāhunaiyyau dakkhiṇaiyyau añ‍jalikaraṇīyau anuttaraṃ puñ‍ñakkhaittaṃ laukassā’ti| yasmiṃ, mahānāma, samayai ariyasāvakau saṅghaṃ anussarati, naivassa tasmiṃ samayai rāgapariyuṭṭhitaṃ cittaṃ hauti, na dausapariyuṭṭhitaṃ cittaṃ hauti, na mauhapariyuṭṭhitaṃ cittaṃ hauti; ujugatamaivassa tasmiṃ samayai cittaṃ hauti saṅghaṃ ārabbha| ujugatacittau khau pana, mahānāma, ariyasāvakau labhati atthavaidaṃ, labhati dhammavaidaṃ, labhati dhammūpasaṃhitaṃ pāmauj‍jaṃ| pamuditassa pīti jāyati, pītimanassa kāyau passambhati, passaddhakāyau sukhaṃ vaidiyati, sukhinau cittaṃ samādhiyati| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, mahānāma, ariyasāvakau visamagatāya pajāya samappattau  viharati, sabyāpaj‍jāya pajāya abyāpaj‍jau viharati, dhammasautasamāpan‍nau saṅghānussatiṃ bhāvaiti|

13. aikaṃ samayaṃ bhagavā sak‍kaisu viharati kapilavatthusmiṃ nigraudhārāmai| taina khau pana samayaina bhagavā sāvatthiyaṃ vassāvāsaṃ upagantukāmau hauti ahausi (ka॰)|

assausi  khau nandiyau sak‍kau – ‘‘bhagavā kira sāvatthiyaṃ vassāvāsaṃ upagantukāmau’’ti| atha khau nandiyassa sak‍kassa  aitadahausi – ‘‘yaṃnūnāhampi sāvatthiyaṃ vassāvāsaṃ upagacchaiyyaṃ| tattha kammantañ‍caiva adhiṭṭhahissāmi, bhagavantañ‍ca lacchāmi kālaina kālaṃ dassanāyā’’ti|

atha khau bhagavā sāvatthiyaṃ vassāvāsaṃ upagacchi upagañchi (sī॰ pī॰)| nandiyaupi khau sak‍kau sāvatthiyaṃ vassāvāsaṃ upagacchi| tattha kammantañ‍caiva adhiṭṭhāsi  adhiṭṭhāya (syā॰), adhiṭṭhāti (ka॰), bhagavantañ‍ca labhi lacchati (syā॰ ka॰) kālaina kālaṃ dassanāya| taina khau pana samayaina sambahulā bhikkhū bhagavatau cīvarakammaṃ karaunti – ‘‘niṭṭhitacīvarau bhagavā taimāsac‍cayaina cārikaṃ pak‍kamissatī’’ti|

assausi khau nandiyau sak‍kau – ‘‘sambahulā kira bhikkhū bhagavatau cīvarakammaṃ karaunti – ‘niṭṭhitacīvarau bhagavā taimāsac‍cayaina cārikaṃ pak‍kamissatī’’’ti| atha khau nandiyau sak‍kau yaina bhagavā tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdi| aikamantaṃ nisin‍nau khau nandiyau sak‍kau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca – ‘‘sutaṃ maitaṃ, bhantai  – ‘sambahulā kira bhikkhū bhagavatau cīvarakammaṃ karaunti – niṭṭhitacīvarau bhagavā taimāsac‍cayaina cārikaṃ pak‍kamissatī’ti| taisaṃ nau, bhantai, nānāvihāraihi viharataṃ kainassa vihāraina vihātabba’’nti?

‘‘sādhu sādhu, nandiya! aitaṃ khau, nandiya, tumhākaṃ patirūpaṃ kulaputtānaṃ, yaṃ tumhai tathāgataṃ upasaṅkamitvā pucchaiyyātha – ‘taisaṃ nau, bhantai, nānāvihāraihi viharataṃ kainassa vihāraina vihātabba’nti? saddhau khau, nandiya, ārādhakau hauti, nau assaddhau; sīlavā ārādhakau hauti, nau dussīlau; āraddhavīriyau ārādhakau hauti, nau kusītau; upaṭṭhitassati ārādhakau hauti, nau muṭṭhassati; samāhitau ārādhakau hauti, nau asamāhitau; pañ‍ñavā ārādhakau hauti, nau duppañ‍ñau| imaisu khau tai, nandiya, chasu dhammaisu patiṭṭhāya pañ‍casu dhammaisu ajjhattaṃ sati upaṭṭhāpaitabbā|

‘‘idha tvaṃ, nandiya, tathāgataṃ anussaraiyyāsi – ‘itipi  sau bhagavā arahaṃ sammāsambuddhau vij‍jācaraṇasampan‍nau sugatau laukavidū  anuttarau purisadammasārathi, satthā daivamanussānaṃ buddhau bhagavā’ti| iti khau tai, nandiya, tathāgataṃ ārabbha ajjhattaṃ sati upaṭṭhāpaitabbā|

‘‘puna caparaṃ tvaṃ, nandiya, dhammaṃ anussaraiyyāsi – ‘svākkhātau bhagavatā dhammau sandiṭṭhikau akālikau aihipassikau aupanaiyyikau pac‍cattaṃ vaiditabbau viñ‍ñūhī’ti| iti khau tai, nandiya, dhammaṃ ārabbha ajjhattaṃ sati upaṭṭhāpaitabbā|

‘‘puna caparaṃ tvaṃ, nandiya, kalyāṇamittai anussaraiyyāsi – ‘lābhā vata mai, suladdhaṃ vata mai, yassa mai kalyāṇamittā anukampakā atthakāmā auvādakā anusāsakā’ti| iti khau tai, nandiya, kalyāṇamittai ārabbha ajjhattaṃ sati upaṭṭhāpaitabbā|

‘‘puna caparaṃ tvaṃ, nandiya, attanau cāgaṃ anussaraiyyāsi – ‘lābhā vata mai, suladdhaṃ vata mai, yauhaṃ macchairamalapariyuṭṭhitāya pajāya vigatamalamacchairaina caitasā agāraṃ ajjhāvasāmi muttacāgau  payatapāṇi vaussaggaratau yācayaugau dānasaṃvibhāgaratau’ti| iti khau tai, nandiya, cāgaṃ ārabbha ajjhattaṃ sati upaṭṭhāpaitabbā|

‘‘puna caparaṃ tvaṃ, nandiya, daivatā anussaraiyyāsi – ‘yā daivatā atik‍kammaiva kabaḷīkārāhārabhakkhānaṃ kabaḷiṃkārabhakkhānaṃ (sī॰), kabaḷīkārabhakkhānaṃ (syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) daivatānaṃ sahabyataṃ añ‍ñataraṃ manaumayaṃ kāyaṃ upapan‍nā, tā karaṇīyaṃ attanau na samanupassanti katassa vā paticayaṃ| saiyyathāpi, nandiya, bhikkhu asamayavimuttau karaṇīyaṃ attanau na samanupassati katassa vā paticayaṃ; aivamaivaṃ khau, nandiya, yā tā daivatā  atik‍kammaiva kabaḷīkārāhārabhakkhānaṃ daivatānaṃ sahabyataṃ añ‍ñataraṃ manaumayaṃ kāyaṃ upapan‍nā, tā karaṇīyaṃ  attanau na samanupassanti katassa vā paticayaṃ| iti khau tai, nandiya, daivatā ārabbha ajjhattaṃ sati upaṭṭhāpaitabbā|

‘‘imaihi khau, nandiya, aikādasahi dhammaihi saman‍nāgatau ariyasāvakau pajahataiva pāpakai akusalai dhammai, na upādiyati| saiyyathāpi, nandiya, kumbhau nik‍kuj‍jau nikuj‍jau (ka॰) vamataiva udakaṃ, nau vantaṃ pac‍cāvamati pac‍cāmasati (syā॰); saiyyathāpi vā pana, nandiya, sukkhai tiṇadāyai aggi muttau ḍahañ‍ñaiva gacchati, nau daḍḍhaṃ pac‍cudāvattati; aivamaivaṃ khau, nandiya, imaihi aikādasahi dhammaihi saman‍nāgatau ariyasāvakau pajahataiva pāpakai akusalai dhammai, na upādiyatī’’ti| tatiyaṃ|

4. subhūtisuttaṃ

14. atha khau āyasmā subhūti saddhaina bhikkhunā saddhiṃ yaina bhagavā tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdi| aikamantaṃ nisin‍naṃ khau āyasmantaṃ subhūtiṃ bhagavā aitadavauca – ‘‘kau nāmāyaṃ kau nāmau ayaṃ (sī॰ ka॰), kau nāma ayaṃ (syā॰ kaṃ॰), subhūti, bhikkhū’’ti? ‘‘saddhau nāmāyaṃ, bhantai, bhikkhu, sudattassa saddhassa (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) upāsakassa puttau, saddhā agārasmā anagāriyaṃ pabbajitau’’ti|

‘‘kac‍ci panāyaṃ, subhūti, saddhau bhikkhu sudattassa upāsakassa puttau saddhā agārasmā anagāriyaṃ pabbajitau sandissati saddhāpadānaisū’’ti? ‘‘aitassa, bhagavā, kālau; aitassa, sugata, kālau, yaṃ bhagavā saddhassa saddhāpadānāni bhāsaiyya| idānāhaṃ jānissāmi yadi vā ayaṃ bhikkhu sandissati saddhāpadānaisu yadi vā nau’’ti|

‘‘taina  hi, subhūti, suṇāhi, sādhukaṃ manasi karauhi; bhāsissāmī’’ti| ‘‘aivaṃ, bhantai’’ti khau āyasmā subhūti bhagavatau pac‍cassausi| bhagavā aitadavauca –

‘‘idha, subhūti, bhikkhu sīlavā hauti, pātimaukkhasaṃvarasaṃvutau viharati ācāragaucarasampan‍nau aṇumattaisu vaj‍jaisu bhayadassāvī, samādāya sikkhati sikkhāpadaisu| yampi, subhūti, bhikkhu sīlavā hauti…pai॰… samādāya sikkhati sikkhāpadaisu, idampi, subhūti, saddhassa saddhāpadānaṃ hauti|

‘‘puna caparaṃ, subhūti, bhikkhu bahussutau hauti sutadharau sutasan‍nicayau; yai tai dhammā ādikalyāṇā majjhaikalyāṇā pariyausānakalyāṇā sātthaṃ sabyañ‍janaṃ kaivalaparipuṇṇaṃ parisuddhaṃ brahmacariyaṃ abhivadanti, tathārūpāssa dhammā bahussutā haunti dhātā vacasā paricitā manasānupaikkhitā diṭṭhiyā suppaṭividdhā| yampi, subhūti, bhikkhu bahussutau  hauti…pai॰… diṭṭhiyā suppaṭividdhā, idampi, subhūti, saddhassa saddhāpadānaṃ hauti|

‘‘puna caparaṃ, subhūti, bhikkhu kalyāṇamittau hauti kalyāṇasahāyau kalyāṇasampavaṅkau| yampi, subhūti, bhikkhu kalyāṇamittau hauti kalyāṇasahāyau kalyāṇasampavaṅkau, idampi, subhūti, saddhassa saddhāpadānaṃ hauti|

‘‘puna caparaṃ, subhūti, bhikkhu suvacau hauti sauvacassakaraṇaihi dhammaihi saman‍nāgatau khamau padakkhiṇaggāhī anusāsaniṃ| yampi, subhūti, bhikkhu suvacau hauti sauvacassakaraṇaihi dhammaihi saman‍nāgatau khamau padakkhiṇaggāhī anusāsaniṃ, idampi, subhūti, saddhassa saddhāpadānaṃ hauti|

‘‘puna caparaṃ, subhūti, bhikkhu yāni tāni sabrahmacārīnaṃ uc‍cāvacāni kiṃkaraṇīyāni tatra dakkhau hauti analasau tatrupāyāya vīmaṃsāya saman‍nāgatau  alaṃ kātuṃ alaṃ saṃvidhātuṃ| yampi, subhūti, bhikkhu yāni tāni sabrahmacārīnaṃ uc‍cāvacāni kiṃkaraṇīyāni tatra dakkhau hauti analasau tatrupāyāya vīmaṃsāya saman‍nāgatau alaṃ kātuṃ alaṃ saṃvidhātuṃ, idampi, subhūti, saddhassa saddhāpadānaṃ hauti|

‘‘puna  caparaṃ, subhūti, bhikkhu dhammakāmau hauti piyasamudāhārau abhidhammai abhivinayai uḷārapāmauj‍jau| yampi, subhūti, bhikkhu dhammakāmau hauti piyasamudāhārau abhidhammai abhivinayai uḷārapāmauj‍jau, idampi, subhūti, saddhassa saddhāpadānaṃ hauti|

‘‘puna caparaṃ, subhūti, bhikkhu āraddhavīriyau viharati akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ pahānāya, kusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ upasampadāya, thāmavā daḷhaparak‍kamau anikkhittadhurau kusalaisu dhammaisu| yampi, subhūti, bhikkhu āraddhavīriyau viharati akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ pahānāya kusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ upasampadāya thāmavā daḷhaparak‍kamau anikkhittadhurau kusalaisu dhammaisu, idampi, subhūti, saddhassa saddhāpadānaṃ hauti|

‘‘puna caparaṃ, subhūti, bhikkhu catun‍naṃ jhānānaṃ ābhicaitasikānaṃ diṭṭhadhammasukhavihārānaṃ nikāmalābhī hauti akicchalābhī akasiralābhī| yampi, subhūti, bhikkhu catun‍naṃ jhānānaṃ ābhicaitasikānaṃ diṭṭhadhammasukhavihārānaṃ nikāmalābhī hauti akicchalābhī akasiralābhī, idampi, subhūti, saddhassa saddhāpadānaṃ hauti|

‘‘puna caparaṃ, subhūti, bhikkhu anaikavihitaṃ pubbainivāsaṃ anussarati, saiyyathidaṃ – aikampi jātiṃ dvaipi jātiyau tissaupi jātiyau catassaupi jātiyau pañ‍capi jātiyau dasapi jātiyau vīsampi jātiyau tiṃsampi jātiyau cattārīsampi jātiyau pañ‍ñāsampi jātiyau jātisatampi jātisahassampi jātisatasahassampi anaikaipi saṃvaṭṭakappai anaikaipi vivaṭṭakappai anaikaipi  saṃvaṭṭavivaṭṭakappai – ‘amutrāsiṃ aivaṃnāmau aivaṃgauttau aivaṃvaṇṇau aivamāhārau aivaṃsukhadukkhappaṭisaṃvaidī aivamāyupariyantau, sau tatau cutau amutra udapādiṃ; tatrāpāsiṃ aivaṃnāmau aivaṃgauttau aivaṃvaṇṇau aivamāhārau aivaṃsukhadukkhappaṭisaṃvaidī aivamāyupariyantau, sau tatau cutau idhūpapan‍nau’ti| iti sākāraṃ sauddaisaṃ anaikavihitaṃ pubbainivāsaṃ anussarati| yampi, subhūti, bhikkhu  anaikavihitaṃ pubbainivāsaṃ anussarati, saiyyathidaṃ, aikampi jātiṃ dvaipi jātiyau…pai॰… iti sākāraṃ sauddaisaṃ anaikavihitaṃ pubbainivāsaṃ anussarati| idampi, subhūti, saddhassa saddhāpadānaṃ hauti|

‘‘puna caparaṃ, subhūti, bhikkhu dibbaina cakkhunā visuddhaina atik‍kantamānusakaina sattai passati  cavamānai upapaj‍jamānai hīnai paṇītai suvaṇṇai dubbaṇṇai, sugatai duggatai yathākammūpagai sattai pajānāti – ‘imai vata bhauntau sattā kāyaduc‍caritaina saman‍nāgatā vacīduc‍caritaina saman‍nāgatā manauduc‍caritaina saman‍nāgatā ariyānaṃ upavādakā micchādiṭṭhikā micchādiṭṭhikammasamādānā, tai kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapan‍nā| imai vā pana bhauntau sattā kāyasucaritaina saman‍nāgatā vacīsucaritaina saman‍nāgatā manausucaritaina saman‍nāgatā ariyānaṃ anupavādakā sammādiṭṭhikā sammādiṭṭhikammasamādānā, tai kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā sugatiṃ saggaṃ laukaṃ upapan‍nā’ti| iti dibbaina cakkhunā visuddhaina atik‍kantamānusakaina sattai passati cavamānai upapaj‍jamānai hīnai paṇītai suvaṇṇai dubbaṇṇai, sugatai duggatai yathākammūpagai  sattai pajānāti| yampi, subhūti, bhikkhu dibbaina cakkhunā visuddhaina…pai॰… yathākammūpagai sattai pajānāti, idampi, subhūti, saddhassa saddhāpadānaṃ hauti|

‘‘puna  caparaṃ, subhūti, bhikkhu āsavānaṃ khayā anāsavaṃ caitauvimuttiṃ pañ‍ñāvimuttiṃ diṭṭhaiva dhammai sayaṃ abhiñ‍ñā sacchikatvā upasampaj‍ja viharati| yampi, subhūti, bhikkhu āsavānaṃ khayā…pai॰… sacchikatvā upasampaj‍ja viharati, idampi, subhūti, saddhassa saddhāpadānaṃ hautī’’ti|

aivaṃ vuttai āyasmā subhūti bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca – ‘‘yānimāni, bhantai, bhagavatā saddhassa saddhāpadānāni bhāsitāni, saṃvij‍janti tāni imassa bhikkhunau, ayañ‍ca bhikkhu aitaisu sandissati|

‘‘ayaṃ, bhantai, bhikkhu sīlavā hauti, pātimaukkhasaṃvarasaṃvutau viharati ācāragaucarasampan‍nau aṇumattaisu vaj‍jaisu bhayadassāvī, samādāya sikkhati  sikkhāpadaisu|

‘‘ayaṃ, bhantai, bhikkhu bahussutau hauti sutadharau sutasan‍nicayau; yai tai dhammā ādikalyāṇā majjhaikalyāṇā pariyausānakalyāṇā sātthaṃ sabyañ‍janaṃ kaivalaparipuṇṇaṃ parisuddhaṃ brahmacariyaṃ abhivadanti, tathārūpāssa dhammā bahussutā haunti dhātā vacasā paricitā manasānupaikkhitā diṭṭhiyā suppaṭividdhā|

‘‘ayaṃ, bhantai, bhikkhu kalyāṇamittau hauti kalyāṇasahāyau kalyāṇasampavaṅkau|

‘‘ayaṃ, bhantai, bhikkhu suvacau hauti…pai॰… anusāsaniṃ|

‘‘ayaṃ, bhantai, bhikkhu yāni tāni  sabrahmacārīnaṃ uc‍cāvacāni kiṃkaraṇīyāni tattha dakkhau hauti analasau tatrupāyāya vīmaṃsāya saman‍nāgatau alaṃ kātuṃ alaṃ saṃvidhātuṃ|

‘‘ayaṃ, bhantai, bhikkhu dhammakāmau hauti piyasamudāhārau abhidhammai abhivinayai uḷārapāmauj‍jau|

‘‘ayaṃ, bhantai, bhikkhu āraddhavīriyau viharati…pai॰… thāmavā daḷhaparak‍kamau anikkhittadhurau kusalaisu dhammaisu|

‘‘ayaṃ, bhantai, bhikkhu catun‍naṃ jhānānaṃ ābhicaitasikānaṃ diṭṭhadhammasukhavihārānaṃ nikāmalābhī hauti akicchalābhī akasiralābhī|

‘‘ayaṃ, bhantai, bhikkhu anaikavihitaṃ pubbainivāsaṃ anussarati, saiyyathidaṃ – aikampi jātiṃ dvaipi jātiyau…pai॰… iti sākāraṃ sauddaisaṃ anaikavihitaṃ pubbainivāsaṃ anussarati|

‘‘ayaṃ, bhantai, bhikkhu dibbaina cakkhunā visuddhaina atik‍kantamānusakaina…pai॰… yathākammūpagai sattai pajānāti|

‘‘ayaṃ, bhantai, bhikkhu āsavānaṃ khayā…pai॰… sacchikatvā upasampaj‍ja viharati| yānimāni, bhantai, bhagavatā saddhassa saddhāpadānāni bhāsitāni, saṃvij‍janti tāni imassa bhikkhunau, ayañ‍ca bhikkhu aitaisu sandissatī’’ti|

‘‘sādhu sādhu, subhūti! taina hi tvaṃ, subhūti, iminā ca saddhaina bhikkhunā saddhiṃ viharaiyyāsi| yadā ca tvaṃ, subhūti, ākaṅkhaiyyāsi tathāgataṃ dassanāya, iminā saddhaina bhikkhunā saddhiṃ upasaṅkamaiyyāsi tathāgataṃ dassanāyā’’ti| catutthaṃ|

5. maittāsuttaṃ

15. paṭi॰ ma॰ 2.22; mi॰ pa॰ 4.4.6 ‘‘maittāya, bhikkhavai, caitauvimuttiyā āsaivitāya bhāvitāya bahulīkatāya yānīkatāya vatthukatāya anuṭṭhitāya paricitāya susamāraddhāya aikādasānisaṃsā pāṭikaṅkhā|

katamai aikādasa? sukhaṃ supati, sukhaṃ paṭibujjhati, na pāpakaṃ supinaṃ passati, manussānaṃ piyau hauti, amanussānaṃ piyau hauti, daivatā rakkhanti, nāssa aggi vā visaṃ vā satthaṃ vā kamati, tuvaṭaṃ cittaṃ samādhiyati, mukhavaṇṇau vippasīdati, asammūḷhau kālaṃ karauti, uttari appaṭivijjhantau brahmalaukūpagau hauti| maittāya, bhikkhavai, caitauvimuttiyā āsaivitāya bhāvitāya bahulīkatāya yānīkatāya vatthukatāya anuṭṭhitāya paricitāya susamāraddhāya imai aikādasānisaṃsā pāṭikaṅkhā’’ti| pañ‍camaṃ|

6. aṭṭhakanāgarasuttaṃ

16. aikaṃ samayaṃ āyasmā ānandau vaisāliyaṃ viharati bailuvagāmakai vaiḷuvagāmakai (syā॰ kaṃ॰ ka॰)| taina khau pana samayaina dasamau gahapati aṭṭhakanāgarau pāṭaliputtaṃ anuppattau hauti kainacidaiva karaṇīyaina|

atha  khau dasamau gahapati aṭṭhakanāgarau yaina kuk‍kuṭārāmau yaina añ‍ñatarau bhikkhu tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā taṃ bhikkhuṃ aitadavauca – ‘‘kahaṃ nu khau, bhantai, āyasmā ānandau aitarahi viharati? dassanakāmā hi mayaṃ, bhantai, āyasmantaṃ ānanda’’nti| ‘‘aisau, gahapati, āyasmā ānandau vaisāliyaṃ viharati bailuvagāmakai’’ti|

atha khau dasamau gahapati aṭṭhakanāgarau pāṭaliputtai taṃ karaṇīyaṃ tīraitvā yaina vaisālī bailuvagāmakau yaināyasmā ānandau tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā āyasmantaṃ ānandaṃ abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdi| aikamantaṃ nisin‍nau khau dasamau gahapati aṭṭhakanāgarau āyasmantaṃ  ānandaṃ aitadavauca – ‘‘atthi nu khau, bhantai ānanda, taina bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhaina aikadhammau sammadakkhātau, yattha bhikkhunau  appamattassa ātāpinau pahitattassa viharatau avimuttaṃ vā cittaṃ vimuc‍cati, aparikkhīṇā vā āsavā parikkhayaṃ  gacchanti, ananuppattaṃ vā anuttaraṃ yaugakkhaimaṃ anupāpuṇātī’’ti? ‘‘atthi khau, gahapati, taina bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhaina aikadhammau sammadakkhātau, yattha bhikkhunau appamattassa ātāpinau pahitattassa viharatau avimuttaṃ vā cittaṃ vimuc‍cati, aparikkhīṇā vā āsavā parikkhayaṃ gacchanti, ananuppattaṃ vā anuttaraṃ yaugakkhaimaṃ anupāpuṇātī’’ti|

‘‘katamau pana, bhantai ānanda, taina bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhaina aikadhammau sammadakkhātau, yattha bhikkhunau appamattassa ātāpinau pahitattassa viharatau avimuttaṃ vā cittaṃ vimuc‍cati, aparikkhīṇā vā āsavā parikkhayaṃ gacchanti, ananuppattaṃ vā anuttaraṃ yaugakkhaimaṃ anupāpuṇātī’’ti? ‘‘idha, gahapati, bhikkhu vivic‍caiva kāmaihi vivic‍ca akusalaihi dhammaihi savitak‍kaṃ savicāraṃ vivaikajaṃ pītisukhaṃ paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ upasampaj‍ja viharati| sau iti paṭisañ‍cikkhati – ‘idampi khau paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ abhisaṅkhataṃ abhisañ‍caitayitaṃ’| ‘yaṃ khau pana kiñ‍ci abhisaṅkhataṃ abhisañ‍caitayitaṃ, tadanic‍caṃ niraudhadhamma’nti pajānāti| sau tattha ṭhitau āsavānaṃ khayaṃ pāpuṇāti; nau cai āsavānaṃ khayaṃ pāpuṇāti, tainaiva dhammarāgaina tāya dhammanandiyā pañ‍can‍naṃ aurambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā aupapātikau hauti tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammau tasmā laukā| ayampi khau, gahapati, taina bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhaina  aikadhammau sammadakkhātau, yattha bhikkhunau appamattassa ātāpinau pahitattassa viharatau avimuttaṃ vā cittaṃ vimuc‍cati, aparikkhīṇā vā āsavā parikkhayaṃ gacchanti, ananuppattaṃ vā anuttaraṃ yaugakkhaimaṃ anupāpuṇāti|

‘‘puna  caparaṃ, gahapati, bhikkhu vitak‍kavicārānaṃ vūpasamā ajjhattaṃ sampasādanaṃ caitasau aikaudibhāvaṃ avitak‍kaṃ avicāraṃ samādhijaṃ pītisukhaṃ dutiyaṃ jhānaṃ…pai॰… tatiyaṃ jhānaṃ…pai॰… catutthaṃ jhānaṃ upasampaj‍ja viharati| sau iti paṭisañ‍cikkhati – ‘idampi khau catutthaṃ jhānaṃ abhisaṅkhataṃ abhisañ‍caitayitaṃ’| ‘yaṃ khau pana kiñ‍ci  abhisaṅkhataṃ abhisañ‍caitayitaṃ tadanic‍caṃ niraudhadhamma’nti pajānāti| sau tattha ṭhitau āsavānaṃ khayaṃ pāpuṇāti; nau cai āsavānaṃ khayaṃ pāpuṇāti, tainaiva dhammarāgaina tāya dhammanandiyā pañ‍can‍naṃ aurambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā aupapātikau hauti tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammau tasmā laukā| ayampi khau, gahapati, taina bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhaina aikadhammau sammadakkhātau, yattha bhikkhunau appamattassa ātāpinau pahitattassa viharatau avimuttaṃ vā cittaṃ vimuc‍cati aparikkhīṇā vā āsavā parikkhayaṃ gacchanti, ananuppattaṃ vā anuttaraṃ yaugakkhaimaṃ anupāpuṇāti|

‘‘puna caparaṃ, gahapati, bhikkhu maittāsahagataina caitasā aikaṃ disaṃ pharitvā viharati tathā dutiyaṃ tathā tatiyaṃ tathā catutthaṃ| iti uddhamadhau  tiriyaṃ sabbadhi sabbattatāya sabbāvantaṃ laukaṃ maittāsahagataina caitasā aikaṃ disaṃ pharitvā viharati vipulaina mahaggataina appamāṇaina avairaina abyāpaj‍jaina pharitvā viharati| sau iti paṭisañ‍cikkhati – ‘ayampi khau maittā caitauvimutti abhisaṅkhatā abhisañ‍caitayitā’| ‘yaṃ khau pana kiñ‍ci abhisaṅkhataṃ abhisañ‍caitayitaṃ tadanic‍caṃ niraudhadhamma’nti pajānāti| sau tattha ṭhitau āsavānaṃ khayaṃ pāpuṇāti; nau cai āsavānaṃ khayaṃ pāpuṇāti, tainaiva dhammarāgaina tāya dhammanandiyā pañ‍can‍naṃ aurambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā aupapātikau hauti tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammau tasmā laukā| ayampi khau, gahapati, taina bhagavatā jānatā…pai॰  … ananuppattaṃ vā anuttaraṃ yaugakkhaimaṃ anupāpuṇāti|

‘‘puna caparaṃ, gahapati, bhikkhu karuṇāsahagataina caitasā…pai॰… muditāsahagataina caitasā…pai॰… upaikkhāsahagataina caitasā aikaṃ disaṃ pharitvā viharati  tathā dutiyaṃ tathā tatiyaṃ tathā catutthaṃ| iti uddhamadhau tiriyaṃ sabbadhi sabbattatāya sabbāvantaṃ laukaṃ upaikkhāsahagataina caitasā vipulaina mahaggataina appamāṇaina avairaina abyāpaj‍jaina pharitvā viharati| sau iti paṭisañ‍cikkhati – ‘ayampi khau upaikkhācaitauvimutti abhisaṅkhatā abhisañ‍caitayitā’| ‘yaṃ khau pana kiñ‍ci abhisaṅkhataṃ abhisañ‍caitayitaṃ tadanic‍caṃ niraudhadhamma’nti  pajānāti| sau tattha ṭhitau āsavānaṃ khayaṃ pāpuṇāti; nau cai āsavānaṃ khayaṃ pāpuṇāti, tainaiva dhammarāgaina tāya dhammanandiyā pañ‍can‍naṃ aurambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā aupapātikau hauti tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammau tasmā laukā| ayampi khau, gahapati, taina bhagavatā jānatā…pai॰… ananuppattaṃ vā anuttaraṃ yaugakkhaimaṃ anupāpuṇāti|

‘‘puna caparaṃ, gahapati, bhikkhu sabbasau rūpasañ‍ñānaṃ samatik‍kamā paṭighasañ‍ñānaṃ atthaṅgamā nānattasañ‍ñānaṃ amanasikārā ‘anantau ākāsau’ti ākāsānañ‍cāyatanaṃ upasampaj‍ja viharati| sau iti paṭisañ‍cikkhati – ‘ayampi khau ākāsānañ‍cāyatanasamāpatti abhisaṅkhatā abhisañ‍caitayitā’| ‘yaṃ khau pana kiñ‍ci abhisaṅkhataṃ abhisañ‍caitayitaṃ tadanic‍caṃ niraudhadhamma’nti pajānāti| sau tattha ṭhitau āsavānaṃ khayaṃ pāpuṇāti; nau cai āsavānaṃ  khayaṃ pāpuṇāti, tainaiva dhammarāgaina tāya dhammanandiyā pañ‍can‍naṃ aurambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā aupapātikau hauti tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammau tasmā laukā| ayampi khau, gahapati, taina bhagavatā jānatā…pai॰… ananuppattaṃ  vā anuttaraṃ yaugakkhaimaṃ anupāpuṇāti|

‘‘puna caparaṃ, gahapati, bhikkhu sabbasau ākāsānañ‍cāyatanaṃ samatik‍kamma ‘anantaṃ viñ‍ñāṇa’nti viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanaṃ upasampaj‍ja viharati…pai॰… sabbasau viñ‍ñāṇañ‍cāyatanaṃ samatik‍kamma ‘natthi kiñ‍cī’ti ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanaṃ upasampaj‍ja viharati| sau iti paṭisañ‍cikkhati – ‘ayampi khau ākiñ‍cañ‍ñāyatanasamāpatti abhisaṅkhatā abhisañ‍caitayitā’| ‘yaṃ khau pana kiñ‍ci abhisaṅkhataṃ abhisañ‍caitayitaṃ tadanic‍caṃ niraudhadhamma’nti pajānāti| sau tattha ṭhitau āsavānaṃ khayaṃ pāpuṇāti; nau cai āsavānaṃ khayaṃ pāpuṇāti, tainaiva dhammarāgaina tāya dhammanandiyā pañ‍can‍naṃ aurambhāgiyānaṃ saṃyaujanānaṃ parikkhayā aupapātikau hauti tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammau tasmā laukā| ayampi  khau, gahapati, taina bhagavatā jānatā…pai॰… ananuppattaṃ vā anuttaraṃ yaugakkhaimaṃ anupāpuṇātī’’ti|

aivaṃ vuttai dasamau gahapati aṭṭhakanāgarau āyasmantaṃ ānandaṃ aitadavauca – ‘‘saiyyathāpi, bhantai ānanda, purisau aikaṃ nidhimukhaṃ gavaisantau sakidaiva sabbatthapi aivamaiva dissati aikādasa nidhimukhāni adhigacchaiyya; aivamaivaṃ khau ahaṃ, bhantai, aikaṃ amatadvāraṃ gavaisantau sakidaiva aikādasa amatadvārāni aikādasan‍naṃ amatadvārānaṃ (sabbattha) ma॰ ni॰ 2.21 passitabbaṃ alatthaṃ saivanāya savanāya (syā॰) sī॰ pī॰ majjhimapaṇṇāsakadutiyasuttaipi, bhāvanāya (ma॰ ni॰ 2.21)| saiyyathāpi, bhantai, purisassa agāraṃ aikādasa dvāraṃ| sau  tasmiṃ  agārai ādittai  aikamaikainapi dvāraina sak‍kuṇaiyya attānaṃ sautthiṃ kātuṃ; aivamaivaṃ khau ahaṃ, bhantai, imaisaṃ aikādasan‍naṃ amatadvārānaṃ aikamaikainapi amatadvāraina sak‍kuṇissāmi attānaṃ sautthiṃ kātuṃ| imai hi nāma, bhantai, añ‍ñatitthiyā ācariyassa ācariyadhanaṃ pariyaisissanti| kiṃ kimaṅgaṃ (ma॰ ni॰ 2.21) panāhaṃ āyasmatau ānandassa pūjaṃ na karissāmī’’ti!

atha khau dasamau gahapati aṭṭhakanāgarau vaisālikañ‍ca pāṭaliputtakañ‍ca bhikkhusaṅghaṃ san‍nipātāpaitvā paṇītaina khādanīyaina bhaujanīyaina sahatthā santappaisi sampavāraisi| aikamaikañ‍ca bhikkhuṃ pac‍caikaṃ dussayugaina acchādaisi, āyasmantañ‍ca ānandaṃ ticīvaraina| āyasmatau ānandassa pañ‍casataṃ vihāraṃ kārāpaisīti| chaṭṭhaṃ|

7. gaupālasuttaṃ

17. ‘‘aikādasahi, bhikkhavai, aṅgaihi saman‍nāgatau gaupālakau abhabbau gaugaṇaṃ pariharituṃ phātiṃ kātuṃ phātikattuṃ (sī॰), phātikātuṃ (syā॰ pī॰)| katamaihi aikādasahi? idha, bhikkhavai, gaupālakau na rūpañ‍ñū hauti, na lakkhaṇakusalau hauti, na āsāṭikaṃ hāraitā sāṭaitā (sī॰ syā॰ pī॰) hauti, na vaṇaṃ paṭicchādaitā hauti, na dhūmaṃ kattā hauti, na titthaṃ jānāti, na pītaṃ jānāti, na vīthiṃ jānāti, na gaucarakusalau hauti, anavasaisadauhī ca hauti, yai tai usabhā gaupitarau gaupariṇāyakā tai na atiraikapūjāya pūjaitā hauti| imaihi khau, bhikkhavai, aikādasahi aṅgaihi saman‍nāgatau gaupālakau abhabbau gaugaṇaṃ pariharituṃ phātiṃ kātuṃ|

‘‘aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, aikādasahi dhammaihi saman‍nāgatau bhikkhu abhabbau imasmiṃ dhammavinayai vuddhiṃ virūḷhiṃ vaipul‍laṃ āpaj‍jituṃ| katamaihi aikādasahi? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na rūpañ‍ñū hauti, na lakkhaṇakusalau hauti, na āsāṭikaṃ  hāraitā hauti, na vaṇaṃ paṭicchādaitā hauti, na dhūmaṃ kattā hauti, na titthaṃ jānāti, na pītaṃ jānāti, na vīthiṃ jānāti, na gaucarakusalau hauti, anavasaisadauhī ca hauti, yai tai bhikkhū thairā rattañ‍ñū cirapabbajitā saṅghapitarau saṅghapariṇāyakā tai na atiraikapūjāya pūjaitā hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na rūpañ‍ñū hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu yaṃ kiñ‍ci rūpaṃ  ( ) (sabbaṃ rūpaṃ) ma॰ ni॰ 1.347 ( ) katthaci dissati ‘cattāri mahābhūtāni, catun‍nañ‍ca mahābhūtānaṃ upādāyarūpa’nti yathābhūtaṃ nappajānāti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na rūpañ‍ñū hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na lakkhaṇakusalau hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu ‘kammalakkhaṇau bālau, kammalakkhaṇau paṇḍitau’ti yathābhūtaṃ nappajānāti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na lakkhaṇakusalau hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na āsāṭikaṃ hāraitā hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu uppan‍naṃ kāmavitak‍kaṃ adhivāsaiti nappajahati na vinaudaiti na byantīkarauti na anabhāvaṃ gamaiti, uppan‍naṃ byāpādavitak‍kaṃ… uppan‍naṃ vihiṃsāvitak‍kaṃ… uppan‍nuppan‍nai pāpakai akusalai dhammai adhivāsaiti nappajahati na vinaudaiti na byantīkarauti na anabhāvaṃ gamaiti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na āsāṭikaṃ hāraitā hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na vaṇaṃ paṭicchādaitā hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu cakkhunā rūpaṃ disvā nimittaggāhī hauti anubyañ‍janaggāhī; yatvādhikaraṇamainaṃ cakkhundriyaṃ asaṃvutaṃ viharantaṃ abhijjhādaumanassā pāpakā akusalā  dhammā anvāssavaiyyuṃ, tassa saṃvarāya na paṭipaj‍jati; na rakkhati cakkhundriyaṃ, cakkhundriyai saṃvaraṃ nāpaj‍jati| sautaina saddaṃ sutvā… ghānaina gandhaṃ  ghāyitvā… jivhāya rasaṃ sāyitvā… kāyaina phauṭṭhabbaṃ phusitvā… manasā  dhammaṃ viñ‍ñāya nimittaggāhī hauti anubyañ‍janaggāhī; yatvādhikaraṇamainaṃ manindriyaṃ asaṃvutaṃ viharantaṃ abhijjhādaumanassā pāpakā akusalā dhammā anvāssavaiyyuṃ, tassa saṃvarāya na paṭipaj‍jati; na rakkhati manindriyaṃ, manindriyai saṃvaraṃ nāpaj‍jati| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na vaṇaṃ paṭicchādaitā hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na dhūmaṃ kattā hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na ma॰ ni॰ 1.346-347 pana ayaṃ nakārau dhammantipadassa anantaraṃ dissati yathāsutaṃ yathāpariyattaṃ dhammaṃ vitthāraina paraisaṃ daisaitā hauti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na dhūmaṃ kattā hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na titthaṃ jānāti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu yai tai bhikkhū  bahussutā āgatāgamā dhammadharā vinayadharā mātikādharā, tai kālaina kālaṃ upasaṅkamitvā na paripucchati na paripañhati – ‘idaṃ, bhantai, kathaṃ, imassa kau atthau’ti? tassa tai āyasmantau avivaṭañ‍caiva na vivaranti, anuttānīkatañ‍ca na uttānīkaraunti, anaikavihitaisu ca kaṅkhāṭhāniyaisu dhammaisu kaṅkhaṃ na paṭivinaudainti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na titthaṃ jānāti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na pītaṃ jānāti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu tathāgatappavaiditai dhammavinayai daisiyamānai na labhati atthavaidaṃ, na labhati dhammavaidaṃ, na labhati dhammūpasaṃhitaṃ pāmauj‍jaṃ| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na pītaṃ jānāti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na vīthiṃ jānāti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu ariyaṃ aṭṭhaṅgikaṃ maggaṃ yathābhūtaṃ nappajānāti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na vīthiṃ jānāti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na gaucarakusalau hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu cattārau satipaṭṭhānai yathābhūtaṃ nappajānāti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu na gaucarakusalau hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu anavasaisadauhī hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhuṃ saddhā gahapatikā abhihaṭṭhuṃ pavārainti cīvarapiṇḍapātasaināsanagilānapac‍cayabhaisaj‍japarikkhāraihi| tatra bhikkhu mattaṃ na jānāti paṭiggahaṇāya| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu anavasaisadauhī hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu yai tai bhikkhū thairā rattañ‍ñū cirapabbajitā saṅghapitarau saṅghapariṇāyakā, tai na atiraikapūjāya pūjaitā hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu yai tai bhikkhū thairā rattañ‍ñū cirapabbajitā saṅghapitarau saṅghapariṇāyakā, taisu na maittaṃ kāyakammaṃ pac‍cupaṭṭhāpaiti āvi caiva rahau ca, na maittaṃ vacīkammaṃ… na maittaṃ manaukammaṃ pac‍cupaṭṭhāpaiti āvi caiva rahau ca| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu yai tai bhikkhū thairā rattañ‍ñū cirapabbajitā saṅghapitarau saṅghapariṇāyakā, na tai atiraikapūjāya pūjaitā hauti|

‘‘imaihi  khau, bhikkhavai, aikādasahi dhammaihi saman‍nāgatau bhikkhu  abhabbau imasmiṃ dhammavinayai vuddhiṃ virūḷhiṃ vaipul‍laṃ āpaj‍jituṃ|

‘‘aikādasahi, bhikkhavai, aṅgaihi saman‍nāgatau gaupālakau bhabbau gaugaṇaṃ pariharituṃ phātiṃ kātuṃ| katamaihi aikādasahi? idha, bhikkhavai, gaupālakau rūpañ‍ñū hauti, lakkhaṇakusalau hauti, āsāṭikaṃ hāraitā hauti, vaṇaṃ paṭicchādaitā hauti, dhūmaṃ kattā hauti, titthaṃ jānāti, pītaṃ jānāti, vīthiṃ jānāti, gaucarakusalau hauti, sāvasaisadauhī ca hauti, yai tai usabhā gaupitarau gaupariṇāyakā tai atiraikapūjāya pūjaitā hauti – imaihi khau, bhikkhavai, aikādasahi aṅgaihi saman‍nāgatau gaupālakau bhabbau gaugaṇaṃ pariharituṃ phātiṃ kātuṃ|

‘‘aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, aikādasahi dhammaihi saman‍nāgatau bhikkhu bhabbau imasmiṃ dhammavinayai vuddhiṃ virūḷhiṃ vaipul‍laṃ āpaj‍jituṃ| katamaihi aikādasahi? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu rūpañ‍ñū hauti, lakkhaṇakusalau hauti, āsāṭikaṃ hāraitā hauti, vaṇaṃ paṭicchādaitā hauti, dhūmaṃ kattā hauti, titthaṃ jānāti, pītaṃ jānāti, vīthiṃ jānāti, gaucarakusalau hauti, sāvasaisadauhī ca hauti, yai tai bhikkhū thairā rattañ‍ñū cirapabbajitā saṅghapitarau saṅghapariṇāyakā tai atiraikapūjāya pūjaitā hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu rūpañ‍ñū hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu yaṃ kiñ‍ci rūpaṃ ‘cattāri mahābhūtāni, catun‍nañ‍ca mahābhūtānaṃ upādāyarūpa’nti yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu rūpañ‍ñū hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu lakkhaṇakusalau hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu ‘kammalakkhaṇau bālau, kammalakkhaṇau paṇḍitau’ti yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu lakkhaṇakusalau  hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu āsāṭikaṃ hāraitā hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu uppan‍naṃ kāmavitak‍kaṃ nādhivāsaiti pajahati vinaudaiti byantīkarauti anabhāvaṃ gamaiti, uppan‍naṃ byāpādavitak‍kaṃ… uppan‍naṃ vihiṃsāvitak‍kaṃ… uppan‍nuppan‍nai pāpakai akusalai dhammai nādhivāsaiti pajahati  vinaudaiti byantīkarauti anabhāvaṃ gamaiti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu āsāṭikaṃ hāraitā hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu vaṇaṃ paṭicchādaitā hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu cakkhunā rūpaṃ disvā na nimittaggāhī hauti nānubyañ‍janaggāhī; yatvādhikaraṇamainaṃ cakkhundriyaṃ asaṃvutaṃ viharantaṃ abhijjhādaumanassā pāpakā akusalā dhammā anvāssavaiyyuṃ, tassa saṃvarāya paṭipaj‍jati; rakkhati cakkhundriyaṃ, cakkhundriyai saṃvaraṃ āpaj‍jati| sautaina saddaṃ sutvā… ghānaina gandhaṃ ghāyitvā… jivhāya rasaṃ sāyitvā… kāyaina phauṭṭhabbaṃ phusitvā… manasā dhammaṃ viñ‍ñāya na nimittaggāhī hauti nānubyañ‍janaggāhī; yatvādhikaraṇamainaṃ manindriyaṃ asaṃvutaṃ viharantaṃ abhijjhādaumanassā pāpakā akusalā dhammā anvāssavaiyyuṃ, tassa saṃvarāya  paṭipaj‍jati; rakkhati manindriyaṃ, manindriyai saṃvaraṃ āpaj‍jati| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu vaṇaṃ paṭicchādaitā hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu dhūmaṃ kattā hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu yathāsutaṃ yathāpariyattaṃ dhammaṃ vitthāraina paraisaṃ daisaitā hauti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu dhūmaṃ kattā hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu titthaṃ jānāti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu yai tai bhikkhū bahussutā āgatāgamā dhammadharā vinayadharā mātikādharā, tai  kālaina kālaṃ upasaṅkamitvā paripucchati paripañhati – ‘idaṃ, bhantai, kathaṃ, imassa kau atthau’ti? tassa tai āyasmantau avivaṭañ‍caiva vivaranti, anuttānīkatañ‍ca uttānīkaraunti, anaikavihitaisu ca kaṅkhāṭhāniyaisu dhammaisu kaṅkhaṃ paṭivinaudainti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu titthaṃ jānāti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu pītaṃ jānāti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu tathāgatappavaiditai dhammavinayai daisiyamānai labhati atthavaidaṃ, labhati dhammavaidaṃ, labhati dhammūpasaṃhitaṃ pāmauj‍jaṃ| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu pītaṃ jānāti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu vīthiṃ jānāti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu ariyaṃ aṭṭhaṅgikaṃ maggaṃ yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu vīthiṃ jānāti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu gaucarakusalau hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu cattārau satipaṭṭhānai yathābhūtaṃ pajānāti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu gaucarakusalau hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu sāvasaisadauhī hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhuṃ saddhā gahapatikā abhihaṭṭhuṃ pavārainti cīvarapiṇḍapātasaināsanagilānapac‍cayabhaisaj‍japarikkhāraihi| tatra bhikkhu mattaṃ jānāti paṭiggahaṇāya| aivaṃ  khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu sāvasaisadauhī hauti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu yai tai bhikkhū thairā rattañ‍ñū cirapabbajitā saṅghapitarau saṅghapariṇāyakā, tai atiraikapūjāya pūjaitā hauti? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu yai tai thairā rattañ‍ñū cirapabbajitā saṅghapitarau saṅghapariṇāyakā, taisu maittaṃ kāyakammaṃ pac‍cupaṭṭhāpaiti āvi caiva rahau ca, maittaṃ vacīkammaṃ… maittaṃ manaukammaṃ pac‍cupaṭṭhāpaiti āvi caiva rahau ca| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu yai tai bhikkhū thairā rattañ‍ñū cirapabbajitā saṅghapitarau saṅghapariṇāyakā, tai atiraikapūjāya pūjaitā hauti|

‘‘imaihi khau, bhikkhavai, aikādasahi dhammaihi saman‍nāgatau bhikkhu bhabbau imasmiṃ dhammavinayai vuddhiṃ virūḷhiṃ vaipul‍laṃ āpaj‍jitu’’nti| sattamaṃ|

8. paṭhamasamādhisuttaṃ

18. a॰ ni॰ 10.6 atha khau sambahulā bhikkhū yaina bhagavā tainupasaṅkamiṃsu; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdiṃsu| aikamantaṃ nisin‍nā khau tai bhikkhū bhagavantaṃ aitadavaucuṃ –

‘‘yathā kathaṃ pana, bhantai, siyā bhikkhunau tathārūpau samādhipaṭilābhau  yathā naiva pathaviyaṃ pathavisañ‍ñī assa…pai॰… yampidaṃ diṭṭhaṃ sutaṃ mutaṃ viñ‍ñātaṃ pattaṃ pariyaisitaṃ anuvicaritaṃ manasā tatrāpi na sañ‍ñī assa; sañ‍ñī ca pana assā’’ti?

9. dutiyasamādhisuttaṃ

10. tatiyasamādhisuttaṃ

20. a॰ ni॰ 10.7 atha  khau sambahulā bhikkhū yaināyasmā sāriputtau tainupasaṅkamiṃsu; upasaṅkamitvā āyasmatā sāriputtaina saddhiṃ sammaudiṃsu| sammaudanīyaṃ  kathaṃ sāraṇīyaṃ vītisāraitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdiṃsu| aikamantaṃ nisin‍nā khau tai bhikkhū āyasmantaṃ sāriputtaṃ aitadavaucuṃ –
