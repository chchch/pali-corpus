




|| namau tassa bhagavatau arahatau sammāsambuddhassa||

suttantanīti

1.

 parā  bhavantaṃ purisaṃ,

mayaṃ pucchāma gautamaṃ|

bhavantaṃ puṭṭhu māgamma,

kiṃ parābhavatau mukhaṃ||

2.

 suvijānau  bhavaṃhauti,

duvijānau parābhavau|

dhammakāmau bhavaṃ hauti,

dhammadaissī parābhavau||

3.

 itihai  taṃ vijānāma,

pathamau sau parābhavau|

dutīyaṃ bhagavā brūhi,

kiṃ parābhavatau mukhaṃ||

4.

 asantassa  piyau hauti,

santai na kurutai piyaṃ|

asataṃ dhammaṃ raucaiti,

taṃ parābhavatau mukhaṃ||

kammāparādhasattānaṃ,

vināsai pac‍cupaṭṭhitai|

anayau nayarūpaina,

buddhimākamya tiṭṭhati||

5.

 niddāsīlī  sabhāsīlī,

anuṭṭhātā ca yau narau|

alasau kaudhapañ‍ñāṇau,

taṃ parābhavatau mukhaṃ||

6.

 yau  mātaraṃ pitaraṃ vā,

jiṇṇakaṃ gatayaubbanaṃ|

pahusantau na bharati,

taṃ parābhavatau mukhaṃ||

7.

 yau  brāhmaṇaṃ samaṇaṃ vā,

añ‍ñaṃ vāpi vaṇibbakaṃ|

musāvādaina vañ‍caiti,

taṃ parābhavatau mukhaṃ||

8.

 pahutavittau  purisau,

sahirañ‍ñau sabhaujanau|

aikau bhuñ‍jati sādūni,

taṃ parābhavatau mukhaṃ||

9.

 jātithaddhau  dhanathaddhau,

gauttathaddhau ca yau narau|

sañātiṃ atimañ‍ñaiti,

taṃ parābhavatau mukhaṃ||

10.

 itthidhuttau  surādhuttau,

akkhadhuttau ca yau narau|

laddhaṃladdhaṃ vināsaiti,

taṃ parābhavatau mukhaṃ||

na  bāḷhaṃ itthiṃ gacchaiyya,

sampassaṃ taijasaṅkhayaṃ|

kāsaṃ sāsaṃ daraṃ bālyaṃ,

khīṇamaidau nigacchati||

(ka)

māyācaitā  marīcī ca,

saukau raugau upaddavau|

kharā ca bandhanācaitā,

mac‍cupāsau guhāsayau||

(kha)

balavantau dubbalā haunti,

thāmavantaupi hāyarai|

cakkhumā andhakā haunti,

mātugāmavasaṃgatā||

(ga)

guṇavantau  nigguṇā haunti,

pañ‍ñavantaupi hāyarai|

pamuttā bandhanā sainti,

mātugāmavasaṃgatā||

(gha)

yasaṃ  kittiṃ dhitiṃ sūraṃ|

bāhussac‍caṃ pajānanaṃ|

hāpayanti pamattassa|

kaṭṭhapuñ‍caṃva pāvakau||

11.

 saihi  dāraihi santuṭṭhau,

vaisiyāsu padussati|

dussati paradāraisu,

taṃ parābhavatau mukhaṃ||

(ka)

mayañ‍ca  bhariyaṃ nātik‍kamāma,

amhaica bhariyā nātik‍kamanti|

añ‍ñatra tāhi brahmacariyaṃ carāma,

tasmā hi amhaṃ daharā na miyyarai||

(kha)

aitāsu  jāyarai suttamāsu,

maidhāvinau haunti pahutapañ‍ñā|

bahussutā thairaguṇā ca haunti,

tasmā hi amhaṃ daharā na miyyarai||

12.

 atītayaubbanau  pausau,

ānaiti timbarutthaniṃ|

tassa issā na supati,

taṃ parābhavatau mukhaṃ||

(ka)

na  dukkhaṃ ahinā daṭṭhaṃ,

na dukkhaṃ sattiyā hataṃ|

tañ‍ca dukkhañ‍ca tibbañ‍ca,

yaṃ passai jiṇṇakaṃ patiṃ||

(kha)

natthi  khiṭṭā natthi rati,

jiṇṇaina patinā saha|

natthi al‍lāpasal‍lāpau,

jagghituṃpi na saubhati||

(ga)

yadā ca daharau daharī,

mantayiṃsu rahaugatā|

sabbai saukā vinassanti,

yaikaici hadayassitā||

13.

 itthiṃ  sauṇḍiṃ vikiraṇiṃ,

purisaṃ vāpi tādisaṃ|

issariyasmiṃ thapaiti,

taṃ parābhavatau mukhaṃ||

14.

 appabhaugau  mahātaṇhau,

khattiyai jāyatai kulai|

sau ca raj‍jaṃ patthayati,

taṃ parābhavatau mukhaṃ||

‘‘suvijānau  bhavaṃhauti,

duvijānau parābhavau’’|

appabhaugau mahātaṇhau||

15.

 aitai  parābhavai laukai,

paṇḍitau samavaikkhiya|

ariyau dassanasampan‍nau,

sa laukaṃ bhajatai sivaṃ||

vasalasutta

1.

 kaudhanau  upanāhīca,

pāpamakkhī ca yau narau|

vipan‍nadiṭṭhī māyāvī,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

2.

 aikajaṃ  vā dvijaṃ vāpi,

yaudha pāṇaṃ vihiṃsati|

yassa pāṇai dayā natthi,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

3.

 yau  hanti uparundhati,

gāmāni nigamāni ca|

niggāhakau samañ‍ñātau,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

4.

 gāmai  vā yadi vā rañ‍ñai,

yaṃ paraisaṃ mamāyitaṃ|

thaiyyā adin‍naṃ ādaiti,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

5.

 yau  havai iṇamādāya,

vuc‍camānau palāyati|

na hi tai iṇamatthīti,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

6.

 yaudha kiñ‍cikkhakamyatā,

pathasmiṃ vajataṃ janaṃ|

hantvā kiñ‍cikkha mādaiti|

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

7.

 yau  attahaitu parahaitu,

dhanahaitu ca yau narau|

sakkhipuṭṭhau musābrūti,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

8.

 yau  ñātīnaṃ sakhīnaṃ vā,

dāraisu paṭidissati|

sahasā sampiyaina vā,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

9.

 yau 4 mātaraṃ pitaraṃ vā,

jiṇṇakaṃ gatayaubbanaṃ|

pahusantau na bharati,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

10.

 yau  mātaraṃ pitaraṃ vā,

bhātaraṃ bhaginiṃ sassuṃ|

hanti rausaiti vācāya,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

11.

 yau  atthaṃ pucchitau santau,

anattha manusāsati|

paṭicchan‍naina mantaiti,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

12.

 yau  katvā pāpakaṃ kammaṃ,

māmaṃ jañ‍ñāti icchati|

yau paṭicchan‍nakammantau,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

13.

 yau  vai parakulaṃ gantvā,

bhutvāna sucibhaujanaṃ|

āgataṃ nappaṭipūjaiti,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

14.

 yau  samaṇaṃ vā brāhmaṇaṃ,

añ‍ñaṃ vāpi vaṇibbakaṃ|

musāvādaina vañ‍caiti,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

15.

 yau samaṇaṃ vā brāhmaṇaṃ,

bhattakālai upaṭṭhitaṃ|

rausaiti vā na ca daiti,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

16.

 asantaṃ  yaudha pabrūti,

mauhaina paliguṇṭhitau|

kiñ‍canaṃ nijigīsānau,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

17.

 yaucattānaṃ  samuk‍kaṃsai,

paraica mavajānāti|

nihīnau saina mānaina,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

18.

 rausakau  kadarīyau ca,

pāpicchau maccharī saṭhau|

ahirīkau anauttappī,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

19.

 yau  buddhaṃ paribhāsati,

athavā tassa sāvakaṃ|

paribbajaṃ gahaṭṭhaṃ vā,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

aṭṭhahi  bhikkhavai aṅgaihi sampan‍nāgatassa upāsakassa pattau nikuj‍jitabbau| bhikkhūnaṃ alābhāya parisak‍kati, bhikkhūnaṃ anatthāya parisak‍kati, bhikkhūnaṃ anāvāsāya parisak‍kati, bhikkhūnaṃ ak‍kausati paribhāsati, bhikkhūbhikkhūhi bhaidaiti, buddhassa avaṇṇaṃ bhāsati, dhammassa avaṇṇaṃ bhāsati, saṅghassa avaṇṇaṃ bhāsati, anujānāmi bhikkhavai imaihi aṭṭhahi aṅgaihi sampan‍nāgatassa upāsakassa pattaṃ nikuj‍jituṃ|

20.

 yau  anarahaṃ santau,

arahāti paṭijānāti|

caurau sabrahmakai laukai,

taṃ jañ‍ñā vasalau iti||

21.

 aitai  khau vasalā vuttā,

mayā yai tai pakāsitā|

na jac‍cā vasalau hauti,

na jac‍cā hauti brāhmaṇau||

22.

 kammunā vasalau hauti,

kammunā hauti brāhmaṇau||

23.

 tadamināpi  jānātha,

yathāhaitaṃ nidassanaṃ|

caṇḍālaputtau saupākau,

mātaṅgau iti vissutau||

24.

 sau  yasaṃ paramaṃ pattau,

mātaṅgau yaṃ sudul‍labhaṃ|

āgacchuṃ tassupaṭṭhānaṃ,

khattiyā brāhmaṇā bahū||

25.

 sau  daivayānaṃ abhiruyha,

virajaṃ sau mahāpathaṃ|

kāmarāgaṃ virājaitvā,

brahmalaukūpagau ahu||

giriṃ  nakhaina khaṇasi,

ayau dantaibhi khādasi|

jātavaidaṃ padahasi,

yau isiṃ paribhāsati||

āvailitaṃ  piṭṭhitau uttamaṅgaṃ,

bāhuṃ pasāraiti akampaṇaiyyaṃ|

saitāni akkhīni yathā matassa,

kau mai imaṃ puttamakāsi aivaṃ||
