aṅguttaranikāyau


aikakanipātapāḷi


1. rūpādivaggau

1. aivaṃ  mai sutaṃ – aikaṃ samayaṃ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṃ viharati jaitavanai anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāmai| tatra khau bhagavā bhikkhū āmantaisi – ‘‘bhikkhavau’’ti| ‘‘bhadantai’’ti tai bhikkhū bhagavatau pac‍cassausuṃ| bhagavā aitadavauca –

‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikarūpampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ purisassa cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, itthirūpaṃ| itthirūpaṃ, bhikkhavai, purisassa cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhatī’’ti| paṭhamaṃ|

2. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikasaddampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ purisassa cittaṃ pariyādāya  tiṭṭhati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, itthisaddau| itthisaddau, bhikkhavai, purisassa cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhatī’’ti| dutiyaṃ|

3. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikagandhampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ purisassa cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, itthigandhau| itthigandhau, bhikkhavai, purisassa cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhatī’’ti| tatiyaṃ|

4. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikarasampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ purisassa cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, itthirasau| itthirasau, bhikkhavai, purisassa cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhatī’’ti| catutthaṃ|

5. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikaphauṭṭhabbampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ purisassa cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, itthiphauṭṭhabbau| itthiphauṭṭhabbau, bhikkhavai, purisassa cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhatī’’ti| pañ‍camaṃ|

6. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikarūpampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ itthiyā cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, purisarūpaṃ| purisarūpaṃ, bhikkhavai, itthiyā cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhatī’’ti| chaṭṭhaṃ|

7. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikasaddampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ itthiyā cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, purisasaddau| purisasaddau, bhikkhavai, itthiyā cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhatī’’ti| sattamaṃ|

8. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikagandhampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ itthiyā cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, purisagandhau| purisagandhau, bhikkhavai, itthiyā cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhatī’’ti| aṭṭhamaṃ|

9. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikarasampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ itthiyā cittaṃ pariyādāya  tiṭṭhati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, purisarasau| purisarasau, bhikkhavai, itthiyā cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhatī’’ti| navamaṃ|

10. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikaphauṭṭhabbampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ  itthiyā cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, purisaphauṭṭhabbau| purisaphauṭṭhabbau, bhikkhavai, itthiyā cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhatī’’ti| dasamaṃ|

rūpādivaggau paṭhamau|



2. nīvaraṇappahānavaggau

11. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaina anuppan‍nau vā kāmacchandau uppaj‍jati uppan‍nau vā kāmacchandau bhiyyaubhāvāya vaipul‍lāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, subhanimittaṃ| subhanimittaṃ, bhikkhavai, ayaunisau manasi karautau anuppan‍nau caiva kāmacchandau uppaj‍jati uppan‍nau ca kāmacchandau bhiyyaubhāvāya vaipul‍lāya saṃvattatī’’ti| paṭhamaṃ|

12. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaina anuppan‍nau vā byāpādau uppaj‍jati uppan‍nau vā byāpādau bhiyyaubhāvāya vaipul‍lāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, paṭighanimittaṃ| paṭighanimittaṃ, bhikkhavai, ayaunisau manasi karautau anuppan‍nau caiva byāpādau uppaj‍jati uppan‍nau ca byāpādau bhiyyaubhāvāya vaipul‍lāya saṃvattatī’’ti| dutiyaṃ|

13. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaina anuppan‍naṃ vā thinamiddhaṃ thīnamiddhaṃ (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) uppaj‍jati uppan‍naṃ vā thinamiddhaṃ bhiyyaubhāvāya vaipul‍lāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, arati tandī tandi (ka॰) vijambhitā vijambhikā (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) bhattasammadau caitasau ca līnattaṃ| līnacittassa, bhikkhavai, anuppan‍nañ‍caiva thinamiddhaṃ  uppaj‍jati uppan‍nañ‍ca thinamiddhaṃ bhiyyaubhāvāya vaipul‍lāya saṃvattatī’’ti| tatiyaṃ|

14. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaina anuppan‍naṃ vā uddhac‍cakuk‍kuc‍caṃ uppaj‍jati uppan‍naṃ vā uddhac‍cakuk‍kuc‍caṃ bhiyyaubhāvāya vaipul‍lāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, caitasau avūpasamau| avūpasantacittassa, bhikkhavai, anuppan‍nañ‍caiva uddhac‍cakuk‍kuc‍caṃ uppaj‍jati uppan‍nañ‍ca uddhac‍cakuk‍kuc‍caṃ bhiyyaubhāvāya vaipul‍lāya saṃvattatī’’ti| catutthaṃ|

15. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaina anuppan‍nā vā vicikicchā uppaj‍jati uppan‍nā vā vicikicchā bhiyyaubhāvāya vaipul‍lāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, ayaunisaumanasikārau| ayaunisau, bhikkhavai, manasi karautau anuppan‍nā caiva vicikicchā uppaj‍jati uppan‍nā ca vicikicchā bhiyyaubhāvāya vaipul‍lāya saṃvattatī’’ti| pañ‍camaṃ|

16. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaina anuppan‍nau vā kāmacchandau nuppaj‍jati uppan‍nau vā kāmacchandau pahīyati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, asubhanimittaṃ| asubhanimittaṃ, bhikkhavai, yaunisau manasi karautau anuppan‍nau caiva kāmacchandau nuppaj‍jati uppan‍nau ca kāmacchandau pahīyatī’’ti| chaṭṭhaṃ|

17. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaina anuppan‍nau vā byāpādau nuppaj‍jati uppan‍nau vā byāpādau pahīyati  yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, maittā  caitauvimutti| maittaṃ, bhikkhavai, caitauvimuttiṃ yaunisau manasi karautau anuppan‍nau caiva byāpādau nuppaj‍jati uppan‍nau ca byāpādau pahīyatī’’ti| sattamaṃ|

18. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaina anuppan‍naṃ vā thinamiddhaṃ nuppaj‍jati uppan‍naṃ vā thinamiddhaṃ pahīyati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, ārambhadhātu nik‍kamadhātu parak‍kamadhātu| āraddhavīriyassa, bhikkhavai, anuppan‍nañ‍caiva thinamiddhaṃ nuppaj‍jati uppan‍nañ‍ca thinamiddhaṃ pahīyatī’’ti| aṭṭhamaṃ|

19. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaina anuppan‍naṃ vā uddhac‍cakuk‍kuc‍caṃ nuppaj‍jati uppan‍naṃ vā uddhac‍cakuk‍kuc‍caṃ pahīyati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, caitasau vūpasamau| vūpasantacittassa, bhikkhavai, anuppan‍nañ‍caiva uddhac‍cakuk‍kuc‍caṃ nuppaj‍jati uppan‍nañ‍ca uddhac‍cakuk‍kuc‍caṃ pahīyatī’’ti| navamaṃ|

20. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi  yaina anuppan‍nā vā vicikicchā nuppaj‍jati uppan‍nā vā vicikicchā pahīyati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, yaunisaumanasikārau| yaunisau, bhikkhavai, manasi karautau anuppan‍nā caiva vicikicchā nuppaj‍jati uppan‍nā ca vicikicchā pahīyatī’’ti| dasamaṃ|

nīvaraṇappahānavaggau dutiyau|



3. akammaniyavaggau

21. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ abhāvitaṃ  akammaniyaṃ hauti yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ yathayidaṃ cittaṃ (sī॰ pī॰) aivamuparipi| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, abhāvitaṃ akammaniyaṃ hautī’’ti| paṭhamaṃ|

22. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ bhāvitaṃ kammaniyaṃ hauti yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, bhāvitaṃ kammaniyaṃ hautī’’ti| dutiyaṃ|

23. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ abhāvitaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, abhāvitaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattatī’’ti| tatiyaṃ|

24. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ bhāvitaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, bhāvitaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattatī’’ti| catutthaṃ|

25. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ abhāvitaṃ apātubhūtaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, abhāvitaṃ apātubhūtaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattatī’’ti| pañ‍camaṃ|

26. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi  yaṃ aivaṃ bhāvitaṃ pātubhūtaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, bhāvitaṃ pātubhūtaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattatī’’ti| chaṭṭhaṃ|

27. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ abhāvitaṃ abahulīkataṃ  mahatau anatthāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, abhāvitaṃ abahulīkataṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattatī’’ti| sattamaṃ|

28. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ bhāvitaṃ bahulīkataṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, bhāvitaṃ bahulīkataṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattatī’’ti| aṭṭhamaṃ|

29. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ abhāvitaṃ abahulīkataṃ dukkhādhivahaṃ hauti yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, abhāvitaṃ abahulīkataṃ dukkhādhivahaṃ hautī’’ti| navamaṃ|

30. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ bhāvitaṃ bahulīkataṃ sukhādhivahaṃ hauti yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, bhāvitaṃ bahulīkataṃ sukhādhivahaṃ hautī’’ti| dasamaṃ|

akammaniyavaggau tatiyau|



4. adantavaggau

31. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ adantaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, adantaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattatī’’ti| paṭhamaṃ|

32. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ dantaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, dantaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattatī’’ti| dutiyaṃ|

33. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi  yaṃ aivaṃ aguttaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, aguttaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattatī’’ti| tatiyaṃ|

34. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ guttaṃ  mahatau atthāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, guttaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattatī’’ti| catutthaṃ|

35. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ arakkhitaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, arakkhitaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattatī’’ti| pañ‍camaṃ|

36. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ rakkhitaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, rakkhitaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattatī’’ti| chaṭṭhaṃ|

37. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ asaṃvutaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, asaṃvutaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattatī’’ti| sattamaṃ|

38. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ saṃvutaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, saṃvutaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattatī’’ti| aṭṭhamaṃ|

39. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ adantaṃ aguttaṃ arakkhitaṃ asaṃvutaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, adantaṃ aguttaṃ arakkhitaṃ asaṃvutaṃ mahatau anatthāya saṃvattatī’’ti| navamaṃ|

40. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ dantaṃ guttaṃ rakkhitaṃ saṃvutaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattati yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ| cittaṃ, bhikkhavai, dantaṃ guttaṃ rakkhitaṃ saṃvutaṃ mahatau atthāya saṃvattatī’’ti| dasamaṃ|

adantavaggau catutthau|



5. paṇihitaacchavaggau

41. ‘‘saiyyathāpi, bhikkhavai, sālisūkaṃ vā yavasūkaṃ vā micchāpaṇihitaṃ hatthaina vā pādaina vā ak‍kantaṃ hatthaṃ vā pādaṃ vā bhaicchati bhij‍jissati (syā॰ kaṃ॰ ka॰), bhaij‍jati (sī॰) mauggal‍lānabyākaraṇaṃ passitabbaṃ lauhitaṃ vā uppādaissatīti naitaṃ ṭhānaṃ vij‍jati| taṃ kissa haitu? micchāpaṇihitattā, bhikkhavai, sūkassa| aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, sau vata bhikkhu micchāpaṇihitaina cittaina avij‍jaṃ bhaicchati, vij‍jaṃ uppādaissati, nibbānaṃ sacchikarissatīti naitaṃ ṭhānaṃ vij‍jati| taṃ kissa haitu? micchāpaṇihitattā, bhikkhavai, cittassā’’ti| paṭhamaṃ|

42. ‘‘saiyyathāpi, bhikkhavai, sālisūkaṃ vā yavasūkaṃ vā sammāpaṇihitaṃ hatthaina vā pādaina vā ak‍kantaṃ hatthaṃ vā pādaṃ vā bhaicchati lauhitaṃ vā uppādaissatīti ṭhānamaitaṃ vij‍jati| taṃ kissa haitu? sammāpaṇihitattā, bhikkhavai, sūkassa| aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, sau vata bhikkhu sammāpaṇihitaina cittaina avij‍jaṃ bhaicchati, vij‍jaṃ uppādaissati, nibbānaṃ sacchikarissatīti ṭhānamaitaṃ vij‍jati| taṃ kissa haitu? sammāpaṇihitattā, bhikkhavai, cittassā’’ti| dutiyaṃ|

43. ‘‘idhāhaṃ  idāhaṃ (sī॰), bhikkhavai, aikac‍caṃ puggalaṃ paduṭṭhacittaṃ aivaṃ caitasā caitau paric‍ca pajānāmi – ‘imamhi cai ayaṃ samayai puggalau kālaṃ karaiyya, yathābhataṃ nikkhittau aivaṃ nirayai’| taṃ kissa haitu? cittaṃ hissa, bhikkhavai, paduṭṭhaṃ| ‘‘caitaupadausahaitu pana, bhikkhavai, aivamidhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapaj‍jantī’’ti| tatiyaṃ|

44. ‘‘idhāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, aikac‍caṃ puggalaṃ pasan‍nacittaṃ aivaṃ caitasā caitau paric‍ca pajānāmi – ‘imamhi cai ayaṃ  samayai puggalau kālaṃ karaiyya, yathābhataṃ nikkhittau aivaṃ saggai’| taṃ kissa haitu? cittaṃ hissa, bhikkhavai, pasan‍naṃ| ‘‘caitaupasādahaitu pana, bhikkhavai, aivamidhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā sugatiṃ saggaṃ laukaṃ upapaj‍jantī’’ti| catutthaṃ|

45. ‘‘saiyyathāpi, bhikkhavai, udakarahadau āvilau luḷitau kalalībhūtau tattha cakkhumā purisau tīrai ṭhitau na passaiyya sippisambukampi sippikasambukampi (ka॰) sakkharakaṭhalampi macchagumbampi carantampi tiṭṭhantampi| taṃ kissa haitu? āvilattā, bhikkhavai, udakassa| aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, sau vata bhikkhu āvilaina cittaina attatthaṃ vā ñassati paratthaṃ vā ñassati ubhayatthaṃ vā ñassati uttariṃ vā manussadhammā alamariyañāṇadassanavisaisaṃ sacchikarissatīti naitaṃ ṭhānaṃ vij‍jati| taṃ kissa haitu? āvilattā, bhikkhavai, cittassā’’ti| pañ‍camaṃ|

46. ‘‘saiyyathāpi, bhikkhavai, udakarahadau acchau vippasan‍nau anāvilau tattha cakkhumā purisau tīrai ṭhitau passaiyya sippisambukampi sakkharakaṭhalampi macchagumbampi carantampi tiṭṭhantampi| taṃ kissa haitu? anāvilattā, bhikkhavai, udakassa| aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, sau vata bhikkhu anāvilaina  cittaina attatthaṃ vā ñassati paratthaṃ vā ñassati ubhayatthaṃ vā ñassati uttariṃ vā manussadhammā alamariyañāṇadassanavisaisaṃ sacchikarissatīti ṭhānamaitaṃ vij‍jati| taṃ kissa haitu? anāvilattā, bhikkhavai, cittassā’’ti| chaṭṭhaṃ|

306. ‘‘micchādiṭṭhikassa, bhikkhavai, purisapuggalassa yañ‍caiva kāyakammaṃ yathādiṭṭhi samattaṃ samādin‍naṃ yañ‍ca vacīkammaṃ…pai॰… yañ‍ca manaukammaṃ yathādiṭṭhi samattaṃ samādin‍naṃ yā ca caitanā yā ca patthanā yau ca paṇidhi yai ca saṅkhārā sabbai tai dhammā aniṭṭhāya akantāya amanāpāya ahitāya dukkhāya saṃvattanti| taṃ kissa haitu? diṭṭhi hissa diṭṭhi hi (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰), bhikkhavai, pāpikā| saiyyathāpi, bhikkhavai, nimbabījaṃ vā kausātakibījaṃ vā tittakalābubījaṃ vā al‍lāya pathaviyā paṭhaviyā (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) nikkhittaṃ yañ‍caiva pathavirasaṃ upādiyati yañ‍ca āpaurasaṃ upādiyati sabbaṃ taṃ tittakattāya kaṭukattāya asātattāya saṃvattati| taṃ kissa haitu? bījaṃ hissa vījaṃ (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰), bhikkhavai, pāpakaṃ| aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, micchādiṭṭhikassa purisapuggalassa yañ‍caiva kāyakammaṃ yathādiṭṭhi samattaṃ samādin‍naṃ yañ‍ca vacīkammaṃ…pai॰…  yañ‍ca manaukammaṃ yathādiṭṭhi samattaṃ samādin‍naṃ yā ca caitanā yā ca patthanā yau ca paṇidhi yai ca saṅkhārā sabbai tai dhammā aniṭṭhāya akantāya amanāpāya ahitāya dukkhāya saṃvattanti| taṃ kissa haitu? diṭṭhi hissa, bhikkhavai, pāpikā’’ti|

307. ‘‘sammādiṭṭhikassa, bhikkhavai, purisapuggalassa yañ‍caiva kāyakammaṃ yathādiṭṭhi samattaṃ samādin‍naṃ yañ‍ca vacīkammaṃ…pai॰… yañ‍ca manaukammaṃ yathādiṭṭhi samattaṃ samādin‍naṃ yā ca caitanā yā ca patthanā yau ca paṇidhi yai ca saṅkhārā sabbai tai dhammā iṭṭhāya kantāya manāpāya hitāya sukhāya saṃvattanti| taṃ kissa haitu? diṭṭhi hissa, bhikkhavai, bhaddikā| saiyyathāpi, bhikkhavai, ucchubījaṃ vā sālibījaṃ vā muddikābījaṃ vā al‍lāya pathaviyā nikkhittaṃ yañ‍caiva pathavirasaṃ upādiyati yañ‍ca āpaurasaṃ upādiyati sabbaṃ taṃ madhurattāya sātattāya asaicanakattāya saṃvattati| taṃ kissa haitu? bījaṃ hissa, bhikkhavai, bhaddakaṃ| aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, sammādiṭṭhikassa purisapuggalassa yañ‍caiva kāyakammaṃ yathādiṭṭhi samattaṃ samādin‍naṃ yañ‍ca vacīkammaṃ…pai॰… yañ‍ca manaukammaṃ yathādiṭṭhi samattaṃ samādin‍naṃ yā ca caitanā yā ca patthanā yau ca paṇidhi yai ca saṅkhārā sabbai tai dhammā iṭṭhāya kantāya manāpāya  hitāya sukhāya saṃvattanti| taṃ kissa haitu? diṭṭhi hissa, bhikkhavai, bhaddikā’’ti|

3. tatiyavaggau

308. ‘‘aikapuggalau, bhikkhavai, laukai uppaj‍jamānau uppaj‍jati bahujanaahitāya bahujanaasukhāya, bahunau janassa anatthāya ahitāya dukkhāya  daivamanussānaṃ| katamau aikapuggalau? micchādiṭṭhikau hauti viparītadassanau| sau bahujanaṃ saddhammā vuṭṭhāpaitvā asaddhammai patiṭṭhāpaiti| ayaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, aikapuggalau laukai uppaj‍jamānau uppaj‍jati bahujanaahitāya bahujanaasukhāya, bahunau janassa anatthāya ahitāya dukkhāya daivamanussāna’’nti|

309. ‘‘aikapuggalau, bhikkhavai, laukai uppaj‍jamānau uppaj‍jati bahujanahitāya bahujanasukhāya, bahunau janassa atthāya hitāya sukhāya daivamanussānaṃ| katamau aikapuggalau? sammādiṭṭhikau hauti aviparītadassanau| sau bahujanaṃ asaddhammā vuṭṭhāpaitvā saddhammai patiṭṭhāpaiti| ayaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, aikapuggalau laukai upapaj‍jamānau uppaj‍jati bahujanahitāya bahujanasukhāya, bahunau janassa atthāya hitāya sukhāya daivamanussāna’’nti|

310. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikadhammampi samanupassāmi yaṃ aivaṃ mahāsāvaj‍jaṃ yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, micchādiṭṭhi| micchādiṭṭhiparamāni, bhikkhavai, mahāsāvaj‍jānī’’ti vaj‍jānīti (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰)|

311. ‘‘nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, añ‍ñaṃ aikapuggalampi samanupassāmi yau aivaṃ bahujanaahitāya paṭipan‍nau bahujanaasukhāya, bahunau janassa anatthāya ahitāya dukkhāya daivamanussānaṃ yathayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, makkhali maughapurisau| saiyyathāpi, bhikkhavai, nadīmukhai khippaṃ khipaṃ (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) uḍḍaiyya auḍḍaiyya (sī॰), ujjhaiyya (ka॰) bahūnaṃ macchānaṃ ahitāya dukkhāya anayāya byasanāya; aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, makkhali maughapurisau manussakhippaṃ  mañ‍ñai laukai uppan‍nau bahūnaṃ sattānaṃ ahitāya dukkhāya anayāya byasanāyā’’ti|

312. ‘‘durakkhātai, bhikkhavai, dhammavinayai yau ca samādapaiti samādāpaiti (?) yañ‍ca samādapaiti yau ca samādapitau tathattāya paṭipaj‍jati sabbai tai bahuṃ apuñ‍ñaṃ pasavanti| taṃ kissa haitu? durakkhātattā, bhikkhavai, dhammassā’’ti|

314. ‘‘durakkhātai, bhikkhavai, dhammavinayai dāyakaina mattā jānitabbā, nau paṭiggāhakaina| taṃ kissa haitu? durakkhātattā, bhikkhavai, dhammassā’’ti|

4. catutthavaggau

322. ‘‘saiyyathāpi, bhikkhavai, appamattakaṃ imasmiṃ jambudīpai ārāmarāmaṇaiyyakaṃ vanarāmaṇaiyyakaṃ bhūmirāmaṇaiyyakaṃ paukkharaṇirāmaṇaiyyakaṃ; atha khau aitadaiva bahutaraṃ yadidaṃ uk‍kūlavikūlaṃ nadīviduggaṃ khāṇukaṇṭakaṭṭhānaṃ khāṇukaṇḍakadhānaṃ (sī॰ pī॰) pabbatavisamaṃ; aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, appakā tai sattā yai thalajā, atha khau aitaiva sattā bahutarā yai audakā’’|

323. … aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, appakā tai sattā yai manussaisu pac‍cājāyanti; atha khau aitaiva sattā bahutarā yai añ‍ñatra manussaihi pac‍cājāyanti|

… aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, appakā tai sattā yai majjhimaisu janapadaisu pac‍cājāyanti; atha  khau aitaiva sattā bahutarā yai pac‍cantimaisu janapadaisu pac‍cājāyanti aviñ‍ñātāraisu milakkhaisu milakkhūsu (ka॰)|

324. … aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, appakā tai sattā yai pañ‍ñavantau ajaḷā anaiḷamūgā paṭibalā subhāsitadubbhāsitassa atthamañ‍ñātuṃ; atha khau aitaiva sattā bahutarā yai duppañ‍ñā jaḷā aiḷamūgā na paṭibalā subhāsitadubbhāsitassa atthamañ‍ñātuṃ|

325. … aivamaivaṃ  khau, bhikkhavai, appakā tai sattā yai ariyaina pañ‍ñācakkhunā saman‍nāgatā; atha khau aitaiva sattā bahutarā yai avij‍jāgatā sammūḷhā|

326. … aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, appakā tai sattā yai labhanti tathāgataṃ dassanāya; atha khau aitaiva sattā bahutarā yai na labhanti tathāgataṃ dassanāya|

327. … aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, appakā tai sattā yai labhanti tathāgatappavaiditaṃ dhammavinayaṃ savanāya; atha khau aitaiva  sattā bahutarā yai na labhanti tathāgatappavaiditaṃ dhammavinayaṃ savanāya|

328. … aivamaivaṃ  khau, bhikkhavai, appakā tai sattā yai sutvā dhammaṃ dhārainti; atha khau aitaiva sattā bahutarā yai sutvā dhammaṃ na dhārainti|

329. … aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, appakā tai sattā yai dhātānaṃ dhatānaṃ (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) dhammānaṃ atthaṃ upaparikkhanti; atha khau aitaiva sattā bahutarā yai dhātānaṃ dhammānaṃ atthaṃ na upaparikkhanti|

330. … aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, appakā tai sattā yai atthamañ‍ñāya dhammamañ‍ñāya dhammānudhammaṃ paṭipaj‍janti; atha khau aitaiva sattā bahutarā yai atthamañ‍ñāya dhammamañ‍ñāya dhammānudhammaṃ na paṭipaj‍janti|

331. … aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, appakā tai sattā yai saṃvaijaniyaisu ṭhānaisu saṃvij‍janti; atha khau aitaiva sattā  bahutarā yai saṃvaijaniyaisu ṭhānaisu na saṃvij‍janti|

332. … aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, appakā tai sattā yai saṃviggā  yaunisau padahanti; atha khau aitaiva sattā bahutarā yai saṃviggā yaunisau na padahanti|

333. … aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, appakā tai sattā yai vavassaggārammaṇaṃ karitvā labhanti samādhiṃ cittassa samādhiṃ (sī॰) labhanti cittassaikaggataṃ cittassaikaggaṃ (sī॰); atha khau aitaiva sattā bahutarā yai vavassaggārammaṇaṃ karitvā na labhanti samādhiṃ na labhanti cittassaikaggataṃ|

334. … aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, appakā tai sattā yai an‍naggarasaggānaṃ lābhinau; atha khau aitaiva sattā bahutarā yai an‍naggarasaggānaṃ na lābhinau, uñchaina kapālābhataina yāpainti|

335. … aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, appakā tai sattā yai attharasassa dhammarasassa vimuttirasassa lābhinau; atha khau aitaiva sattā bahutarā yai attharasassa dhammarasassa vimuttirasassa na lābhinau| tasmātiha, bhikkhavai, aivaṃ sikkhitabbaṃ – attharasassa dhammarasassa vimuttirasassa lābhinau bhavissāmāti| aivañhi vau, bhikkhavai, sikkhitabbanti|
