khuddakanikāyai


dhammapadapāḷi


1. yamakavaggau

1.

 manaupubbaṅgamā  dhammā, manausaiṭṭhā manaumayā|

manasā cai paduṭṭhaina, bhāsati vā karauti vā|

tatau naṃ dukkhamanvaiti, cak‍kaṃva vahatau padaṃ||

2.

 manaupubbaṅgamā dhammā, manausaiṭṭhā manaumayā|

manasā cai pasan‍naina, bhāsati vā karauti vā|

tatau naṃ sukhamanvaiti, chāyāva anapāyinī anupāyinī (ka॰)||

3.

 ak‍kaucchi  maṃ avadhi maṃ, ajini ajinī (?) maṃ ahāsi mai|

yai ca taṃ upanayhanti, vairaṃ taisaṃ na sammati||

4.

 ak‍kaucchi maṃ avadhi maṃ, ajini maṃ ahāsi mai|

yai ca taṃ nupanayhanti, vairaṃ taisūpasammati||

5.

 na  hi vairaina vairāni, sammantīdha kudācanaṃ|

avairaina ca sammanti, aisa dhammau sanantanau||

6.

 parai  ca na vijānanti, mayamaittha yamāmasai|

yai ca tattha vijānanti, tatau sammanti maidhagā||

7.

 subhānupassiṃ viharantaṃ, indriyaisu asaṃvutaṃ|

bhaujanamhi cāmattañ‍ñuṃ, kusītaṃ hīnavīriyaṃ|

taṃ vai pasahati mārau, vātau rukkhaṃva dubbalaṃ||

8.

 asubhānupassiṃ viharantaṃ, indriyaisu susaṃvutaṃ|

bhaujanamhi ca mattañ‍ñuṃ, saddhaṃ āraddhavīriyaṃ|

taṃ vai nappasahati mārau, vātau sailaṃva pabbataṃ||

9.

 anik‍kasāvau kāsāvaṃ, yau vatthaṃ paridahissati|

apaitau damasac‍caina, na sau kāsāvamarahati||

10.

 yau ca vantakasāvassa, sīlaisu susamāhitau|

upaitau damasac‍caina, sa vai kāsāvamarahati||

11.

 asārai sāramatinau, sārai cāsāradassinau|

tai sāraṃ nādhigacchanti, micchāsaṅkappagaucarā||

12.

 sārañ‍ca  sāratau ñatvā, asārañ‍ca asāratau|

tai sāraṃ adhigacchanti, sammāsaṅkappagaucarā||

13.

 yathā agāraṃ ducchan‍naṃ, vuṭṭhī samativijjhati|

aivaṃ abhāvitaṃ cittaṃ, rāgau samativijjhati||

14.

 yathā  agāraṃ suchan‍naṃ, vuṭṭhī na samativijjhati|

aivaṃ subhāvitaṃ cittaṃ, rāgau na samativijjhati||

15.

 idha  saucati paic‍ca saucati, pāpakārī ubhayattha saucati|

sau saucati sau vihañ‍ñati, disvā kammakiliṭṭhamattanau||

16.

 idha maudati paic‍ca maudati, katapuñ‍ñau ubhayattha maudati|

sau maudati sau pamaudati, disvā kammavisuddhimattanau||

17.

 idha tappati paic‍ca tappati, pāpakārī pāpakāri (?) ubhayattha tappati|

‘‘pāpaṃ mai kata’’nti tappati, bhiyyau bhīyau (sī॰) tappati duggatiṃ gatau||

18.

 idha nandati paic‍ca nandati, katapuñ‍ñau ubhayattha nandati|

‘‘puñ‍ñaṃ mai kata’’nti nandati, bhiyyau nandati suggatiṃ gatau||

19.

 bahumpi cai saṃhita sahitaṃ (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) bhāsamānau, na tak‍karau hauti narau pamattau|

gaupauva  gāvau gaṇayaṃ paraisaṃ, na bhāgavā sāmañ‍ñassa hauti||

20.

 appampi cai saṃhita bhāsamānau, dhammassa hauti hautī (sī॰ pī॰) anudhammacārī|

rāgañ‍ca dausañ‍ca pahāya mauhaṃ, sammappajānau suvimuttacittau|

anupādiyānau idha vā huraṃ vā, sa bhāgavā sāmañ‍ñassa hauti||

yamakavaggau paṭhamau niṭṭhitau|



2. appamādavaggau

21.

 appamādau  amatapadaṃ amataṃ padaṃ (ka॰), pamādau mac‍cunau padaṃ|

appamattā na mīyanti, yai pamattā yathā matā||

22.

 aivaṃ aitaṃ (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) visaisatau ñatvā, appamādamhi paṇḍitā|

appamādai pamaudanti, ariyānaṃ gaucarai ratā||

23.

 tai jhāyinau sātatikā, nic‍caṃ daḷhaparak‍kamā|

phusanti dhīrā nibbānaṃ, yaugakkhaimaṃ anuttaraṃ||

24.

 uṭṭhānavatau satīmatau satimatau (sī॰ syā॰ ka॰), sucikammassa nisammakārinau|

sañ‍ñatassa dhammajīvinau, appamattassa apamattassa (?) yasaubhivaḍḍhati||

25.

 uṭṭhānainappamādaina, saṃyamaina damaina ca|

dīpaṃ kayirātha maidhāvī, yaṃ aughau nābhikīrati||

26.

 pamādamanuyuñ‍janti, bālā dummaidhinau janā|

appamādañ‍ca maidhāvī, dhanaṃ saiṭṭhaṃva rakkhati||

27.

 mā pamādamanuyuñ‍jaitha, mā kāmaratisanthavaṃ sandhavaṃ (ka)|

appamattau hi jhāyantau, pappauti vipulaṃ sukhaṃ||

28.

 pamādaṃ appamādaina, yadā nudati paṇḍitau|

pañ‍ñāpāsādamāruyha, asaukau saukiniṃ pajaṃ|

pabbataṭṭhauva bhūmaṭṭhai bhummaṭṭhai (sī॰ syā॰), dhīrau bālai avaikkhati||

29.

 appamattau  pamattaisu, suttaisu bahujāgarau|

abalassaṃva  sīghassau, hitvā yāti sumaidhasau||

30.

 appamādaina maghavā, daivānaṃ saiṭṭhataṃ gatau|

appamādaṃ pasaṃsanti, pamādau garahitau sadā||

31.

 appamādaratau bhikkhu, pamādai bhayadassi vā|

saṃyaujanaṃ aṇuṃ thūlaṃ, ḍahaṃ aggīva gacchati||

32.

 appamādaratau bhikkhu, pamādai bhayadassi vā|

abhabbau parihānāya, nibbānassaiva santikai||

appamādavaggau dutiyau niṭṭhitau|



3. cittavaggau

33.

 phandanaṃ  capalaṃ cittaṃ, dūrakkhaṃ durakkhaṃ (sabbattha) dun‍nivārayaṃ|

ujuṃ karauti maidhāvī, usukārauva taijanaṃ||

34.

 vārijauva thalai khittau, aukamaukataubbhatau|

pariphandatidaṃ cittaṃ, māradhaiyyaṃ pahātavai||

35.

 dun‍niggahassa lahunau, yatthakāmanipātinau|

cittassa damathau sādhu, cittaṃ dantaṃ sukhāvahaṃ||

36.

 sududdasaṃ  sunipuṇaṃ, yatthakāmanipātinaṃ|

cittaṃ rakkhaitha maidhāvī, cittaṃ guttaṃ sukhāvahaṃ||

37.

 dūraṅgamaṃ aikacaraṃ aikacāraṃ (ka॰), asarīraṃ guhāsayaṃ|

yai  cittaṃ saṃyamaissanti, maukkhanti mārabandhanā||

38.

 anavaṭṭhitacittassa, saddhammaṃ avijānatau|

parip‍lavapasādassa, pañ‍ñā na paripūrati||

39.

 anavassutacittassa, ananvāhatacaitasau|

puñ‍ñapāpapahīnassa, natthi jāgaratau bhayaṃ||

40.

 kumbhūpamaṃ kāyamimaṃ viditvā, nagarūpamaṃ cittamidaṃ ṭhapaitvā|

yaudhaitha māraṃ pañ‍ñāvudhaina, jitañ‍ca rakkhai anivaisanau siyā||

41.

 aciraṃ  vatayaṃ kāyau, pathaviṃ adhisaissati|

chuddhau apaitaviñ‍ñāṇau, niratthaṃva kaliṅgaraṃ||

42.

 disau disaṃ yaṃ taṃ kayirā, vairī vā pana vairinaṃ|

micchāpaṇihitaṃ cittaṃ, pāpiyau pāpiyaṃ (?) naṃ tatau karai||

43.

 na taṃ mātā pitā kayirā, añ‍ñai vāpi ca ñātakā|

sammāpaṇihitaṃ cittaṃ, saiyyasau naṃ tatau karai||

cittavaggau tatiyau niṭṭhitau|



4. pupphavaggau

44.

 kau  imaṃ kaumaṃ (ka॰) pathaviṃ vicaissati vijaissati (sī॰ syā॰ pī॰), yamalaukañ‍ca imaṃ sadaivakaṃ|

kau dhammapadaṃ sudaisitaṃ, kusalau pupphamiva pacaissati pupphamivappacaissati (ka॰)||

45.

 saikhau pathaviṃ vicaissati, yamalaukañ‍ca imaṃ sadaivakaṃ|

saikhau dhammapadaṃ sudaisitaṃ, kusalau pupphamiva pacaissati||

46.

 phaiṇūpamaṃ  kāyamimaṃ viditvā, marīcidhammaṃ abhisambudhānau|

chaitvāna mārassa papupphakāni sapupphakāni (ṭīkā), adassanaṃ mac‍curājassa gacchai||

47.

 pupphāni haiva pacinantaṃ, byāsattamanasaṃ byāsattamānasaṃ (ka॰) naraṃ|

suttaṃ gāmaṃ mahaughauva, mac‍cu ādāya gacchati||

48.

 pupphāni haiva pacinantaṃ, byāsattamanasaṃ naraṃ|

atittañ‍ñaiva kāmaisu, antakau kurutai vasaṃ||

49.

 yathāpi bhamarau pupphaṃ, vaṇṇagandhamahaiṭhayaṃ vaṇṇagandhamapauṭhayaṃ (ka॰)|

palaiti rasamādāya, aivaṃ gāmai munī carai||

50.

 na paraisaṃ vilaumāni, na paraisaṃ katākataṃ|

attanauva avaikkhaiyya, katāni akatāni ca||

51.

 yathāpi  ruciraṃ pupphaṃ, vaṇṇavantaṃ agandhakaṃ|

aivaṃ subhāsitā vācā, aphalā hauti akubbatau||

52.

 yathāpi  ruciraṃ pupphaṃ, vaṇṇavantaṃ sugandhakaṃ sagandhakaṃ (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰)|

aivaṃ subhāsitā vācā, saphalā hauti kubbatau sakubbatau (sī॰ pī॰), pakubbatau (sī॰ aṭṭha॰), sukubbatau (syā॰ kaṃ॰)||

53.

 yathāpi  puppharāsimhā, kayirā mālāguṇai bahū|

aivaṃ jātaina mac‍caina, kattabbaṃ kusalaṃ bahuṃ||

54.

 na pupphagandhau paṭivātamaiti, na candanaṃ tagaramal‍likā tagaramal‍likā (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰)|

satañ‍ca gandhau paṭivātamaiti, sabbā disā sappurisau pavāyati||

55.

 candanaṃ tagaraṃ vāpi, uppalaṃ atha vassikī|

aitaisaṃ gandhajātānaṃ, sīlagandhau anuttarau||

56.

 appamattau ayaṃ gandhau, yvāyaṃ tagaracandanaṃ yāyaṃ tagaracandanī (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰)|

yau ca sīlavataṃ gandhau, vāti daivaisu uttamau||

57.

 taisaṃ sampan‍nasīlānaṃ, appamādavihārinaṃ|

sammadañ‍ñā vimuttānaṃ, mārau maggaṃ na vindati||

58.

 yathā saṅkāraṭhānasmiṃ saṅkāradhānasmiṃ (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰), ujjhitasmiṃ mahāpathai|

padumaṃ tattha jāyaitha, sucigandhaṃ manauramaṃ||

59.

 aivaṃ  saṅkārabhūtaisu, andhabhūtai andhībhūtai (ka॰) puthuj‍janai|

atiraucati pañ‍ñāya, sammāsambuddhasāvakau||

pupphavaggau catutthau niṭṭhitau|



5. bālavaggau

60.

 dīghā  jāgaratau ratti, dīghaṃ santassa yaujanaṃ|

dīghau bālānaṃ saṃsārau, saddhammaṃ avijānataṃ||

61.

 carañ‍cai nādhigacchaiyya, saiyyaṃ sadisamattanau|

aikacariyaṃ aikacariyaṃ (ka॰) daḷhaṃ kayirā, natthi bālai sahāyatā||

62.

 puttā matthi dhanammatthi puttamatthi dhanamatthi (ka॰), iti bālau vihañ‍ñati|

attā hi attāpi (?) attanau natthi, kutau puttā kutau dhanaṃ||

63.

 yau bālau mañ‍ñati bālyaṃ, paṇḍitau vāpi taina sau|

bālau ca paṇḍitamānī, sa vai ‘‘bālau’’ti vuc‍cati||

64.

 yāvajīvampi cai bālau, paṇḍitaṃ payirupāsati|

na sau dhammaṃ vijānāti, dabbī sūparasaṃ yathā||

65.

 muhuttamapi  cai viñ‍ñū, paṇḍitaṃ payirupāsati|

khippaṃ dhammaṃ vijānāti, jivhā sūparasaṃ yathā||

66.

 caranti bālā dummaidhā, amittainaiva attanā|

karauntā pāpakaṃ kammaṃ, yaṃ hauti kaṭukapphalaṃ||

67.

 na  taṃ kammaṃ kataṃ sādhu, yaṃ katvā anutappati|

yassa assumukhau raudaṃ, vipākaṃ paṭisaivati||

68.

 tañ‍ca  kammaṃ kataṃ sādhu, yaṃ katvā nānutappati|

yassa patītau sumanau, vipākaṃ paṭisaivati||

69.

 madhuvā  madhuṃ vā (dī॰ ni॰ ṭīkā 1) mañ‍ñati bālau, yāva pāpaṃ na pac‍cati|

yadā ca pac‍cati pāpaṃ, bālau atha bālau (sī॰ syā॰) atha (?) dukkhaṃ nigacchati||

70.

 māsai māsai kusaggaina, bālau bhuñ‍jaiyya bhaujanaṃ|

na sau saṅkhātadhammānaṃ saṅkhatadhammānaṃ (sī॰ pī॰ ka॰), kalaṃ agghati sauḷasiṃ||

71.

 na hi pāpaṃ kataṃ kammaṃ, saj‍ju khīraṃva muc‍cati|

ḍahantaṃ bālamanvaiti, bhasmacchan‍nauva bhasmāchan‍nauva (sī॰ pī॰ ka॰) pāvakau||

72.

 yāvadaiva anatthāya, ñattaṃ ñātaṃ (?) bālassa jāyati|

hanti bālassa suk‍kaṃsaṃ, muddhamassa vipātayaṃ||

73.

 asantaṃ  bhāvanamicchaiyya asantaṃ bhāvamicchaiyya (syā॰), asantabhāvanamicchaiyya (ka॰), puraikkhārañ‍ca bhikkhusu|

āvāsaisu ca issariyaṃ, pūjā parakulaisu ca||

74.

 mamaiva  kata mañ‍ñantu, gihīpabbajitā ubhau|

mamaivātivasā assu, kic‍cākic‍caisu kismici|

iti bālassa saṅkappau, icchā mānau ca vaḍḍhati||

75.

 añ‍ñā hi lābhūpanisā, añ‍ñā nibbānagāminī|

aivamaitaṃ abhiñ‍ñāya, bhikkhu buddhassa sāvakau|

sak‍kāraṃ nābhinandaiyya, vivaikamanubrūhayai||

bālavaggau pañ‍camau niṭṭhitau|



6. paṇḍitavaggau

76.

 nidhīnaṃva  pavattāraṃ, yaṃ passai vaj‍jadassinaṃ|

niggayhavādiṃ maidhāviṃ, tādisaṃ paṇḍitaṃ bhajai|

tādisaṃ bhajamānassa, saiyyau hauti na pāpiyau||

77.

 auvadaiyyānusāsaiyya, asabbhā ca nivārayai|

satañhi sau piyau hauti, asataṃ hauti appiyau||

78.

 na bhajai pāpakai mittai, na bhajai purisādhamai|

bhajaitha mittai kalyāṇai, bhajaitha purisuttamai||

79.

 dhammapīti  sukhaṃ saiti, vippasan‍naina caitasā|

ariyappavaiditai dhammai, sadā ramati paṇḍitau||

80.

 udakañhi  nayanti naittikā, usukārā namayanti damayanti (ka॰) taijanaṃ|

dāruṃ namayanti tacchakā, attānaṃ damayanti paṇḍitā||

81.

 sailau yathā aikaghanau aikagghanau (ka॰), vātaina na samīrati|

aivaṃ nindāpasaṃsāsu, na samiñ‍janti paṇḍitā||

82.

 yathāpi rahadau gambhīrau, vippasan‍nau anāvilau|

aivaṃ dhammāni sutvāna, vippasīdanti paṇḍitā||

83.

 sabbattha vai sappurisā cajanti, na  kāmakāmā lapayanti santau|

sukhaina phuṭṭhā atha vā dukhaina, na uc‍cāvacaṃ nauc‍cāvacaṃ (sī॰ aṭṭha॰) paṇḍitā dassayanti||

84.

 na  attahaitu na parassa haitu, na puttamicchai na dhanaṃ na raṭṭhaṃ|

na icchaiyya nayicchai (pī॰), nicchai (?) adhammaina samiddhimattanau, sa sīlavā pañ‍ñavā dhammikau siyā||

85.

 appakā tai manussaisu, yai janā pāragāminau|

athāyaṃ itarā pajā, tīramaivānudhāvati||

86.

 yai  ca khau sammadakkhātai, dhammai dhammānuvattinau|

tai janā pāramaissanti, mac‍cudhaiyyaṃ suduttaraṃ||

87.

 kaṇhaṃ  dhammaṃ vippahāya, suk‍kaṃ bhāvaitha paṇḍitau|

aukā anaukamāgamma, vivaikai yattha dūramaṃ||

88.

 tatrābhiratimicchaiyya, hitvā kāmai akiñ‍canau|

pariyaudapaiyya pariyaudāpaiyya (?) attānaṃ, cittak‍laisaihi paṇḍitau||

89.

 yaisaṃ sambaudhiyaṅgaisu, sammā cittaṃ subhāvitaṃ|

ādānapaṭinissaggai, anupādāya yai ratā|

khīṇāsavā jutimantau, tai laukai parinibbutā||

paṇḍitavaggau chaṭṭhau niṭṭhitau|



7. arahantavaggau

90.

 gataddhinau  visaukassa, vippamuttassa sabbadhi|

sabbaganthappahīnassa, pariḷāhau na vij‍jati||

91.

 uyyuñ‍janti  satīmantau, na nikaitai ramanti tai|

haṃsāva pal‍lalaṃ hitvā, aukamaukaṃ jahanti tai||

92.

 yaisaṃ  san‍nicayau natthi, yai pariñ‍ñātabhaujanā|

suñ‍ñatau animittau ca, vimaukkhau yaisaṃ gaucarau|

ākāsai va sakuntānaṃ sakuṇānaṃ (ka॰), gati taisaṃ duran‍nayā||

93.

 yassāsavā  parikkhīṇā, āhārai ca anissitau|

suñ‍ñatau animittau ca, vimaukkhau yassa gaucarau|

ākāsai va sakuntānaṃ, padaṃ tassa duran‍nayaṃ||

94.

 yassindriyāni samathaṅgatāni samathaṃ gatāni (sī॰ pī॰), assā yathā sārathinā sudantā|

pahīnamānassa anāsavassa, daivāpi tassa pihayanti tādinau||

95.

 pathavisamau nau virujjhati, indakhilupamau indakhīlūpamau (sī॰ syā॰ ka॰) tādi subbatau|

rahadauva apaitakaddamau, saṃsārā na bhavanti tādinau||

96.

 santaṃ  tassa manaṃ hauti, santā vācā ca kamma ca|

sammadañ‍ñā vimuttassa, upasantassa tādinau||

97.

 assaddhau akatañ‍ñū ca, sandhicchaidau ca yau narau|

hatāvakāsau vantāsau, sa vai uttamapaurisau||

98.

 gāmai vā yadi vārañ‍ñai, nin‍nai vā yadi vā thalai|

yattha arahantau viharanti, taṃ bhūmirāmaṇaiyyakaṃ||

99.

 ramaṇīyāni  arañ‍ñāni, yattha na ramatī janau|

vītarāgā ramissanti, na tai kāmagavaisinau||

arahantavaggau sattamau niṭṭhitau|



8. sahassavaggau

100.

 sahassamapi  cai vācā, anatthapadasaṃhitā|

aikaṃ atthapadaṃ saiyyau, yaṃ sutvā upasammati||

101.

 sahassamapi cai gāthā, anatthapadasaṃhitā|

aikaṃ gāthāpadaṃ saiyyau, yaṃ sutvā upasammati||

102.

 yau ca gāthā sataṃ bhāsai, anatthapadasaṃhitā anatthapadasañhitaṃ (ka॰) visaisanaṃ haitaṃ gāthātipadassa|

aikaṃ dhammapadaṃ saiyyau, yaṃ sutvā upasammati||

103.

 yau sahassaṃ sahassaina, saṅgāmai mānusai jinai|

aikañ‍ca jaiyyamattānaṃ attānaṃ (sī॰ pī॰), sa vai saṅgāmajuttamau||

104.

 attā  havai jitaṃ saiyyau, yā cāyaṃ itarā pajā|

attadantassa pausassa, nic‍caṃ sañ‍ñatacārinau||

105.

 naiva daivau na gandhabbau, na mārau saha brahmunā|

jitaṃ apajitaṃ kayirā, tathārūpassa jantunau||

106.

 māsai  māsai sahassaina, yau yajaitha sataṃ samaṃ|

aikañ‍ca bhāvitattānaṃ, muhuttamapi pūjayai|

sāyaiva pūjanā saiyyau, yañ‍cai vassasataṃ hutaṃ||

107.
