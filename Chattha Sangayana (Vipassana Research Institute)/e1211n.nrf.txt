




padamañ‍jarī 

||namau tassa bhagavatau arahatau sammāsambuddhassa||

niruttikāyaudadhipāragaṃ jinaṃ,

visuddhadhammañ‍ca gaṇaṃ anuttaraṃ|

tidhā namitvā padamañ‍jarī mayā,

karīyatau bālakabuddhivuddhiyāti||

atha akārantapul‍liṅgau buddhasaddau vuc‍catai|

buddhau daivamanussānaṃ dhammaṃ daisaisi

buddhā daivamanussānaṃ dhammaṃ adaisiṃsu

bhau buddha tvampikhau maṃ pālaya

bhau buddhā tumhaipi khau maṃ pālaitha|

buddhaṃ bhagavantaṃ sak‍kac‍caṃ vandāmahaṃ

buddhai bhagavantai sak‍kac‍caṃ vandāma mayaṃ

buddhaina bhagavatā dhammau daisīyatai

buddhaihi bhagavantaihi dhammau daisitau

buddhaina bhagavatā mahājanau sucarati

buddhaihi bhagavantaihi sivapadaṃ yanti

buddhassa bhagavatau pupphaṃ yajati

buddhānaṃ bhagavataṃ pupphāni yajati

buddhā bhagavatā sivapadaṃ labhaiyya

buddhaihi bhagavantaihi pabhā niccharati

buddhassa bhagavatau iddhi kiṃ na karai

buddhānaṃ bhagavantānaṃ iddhipāṭihāriyāni

buddhai bhagavantai mahājanau pasīdati|

buddhaisu bhagavantaisu manaṃ patiṭṭhitaṃ|

iti paṭhamau pāṭhau|

akārantapul‍liṅgau attasaddau vuc‍catai|

attā saṃsāradukkhaṃ pāpuṇāti

attānau sukhadukkhaṃ pāpuṇanti

bhau atta sukhadukkhaṃ anubhausi

bhau attanau sukhadukkhaṃ anubhautha

attānaṃ passati buddhau bhagavā

attānau  passati sammā sambuddhau

attanā sukhadukkhaṃ anubhuttaṃ

attanaihi kammaphalaṃ anubhuttaṃ

attanā saṃsārasukhaṃ nāma natthi

attanaihi bhavasukhaṃ nāma natthi

attanau attāva patiṭṭhā siyā

attānaṃ kammamaiva patiṭṭhā siyā

attanāpi mahabbhayaṃ uppaj‍jati

attanaihi mahabbhayāni jāyanti

attanau mātāpitarau haunti

attānaṃ pañ‍cagatiyau haunti

attani sabbaṃ balaṃ harati raugau

attanaisu sabbaṃ balaṃ haranti raugā|

iti dutiyau pāṭhau|

akāranta pul‍liṅgau rājasaddau vuc‍catai|

rājāpi mahājanaṃ tausaiti

rājānau mahājanaṃ tausainti

bhau rājā mahājanaṃ pālaya

bhau rājānau mahājanaṃ pālaitha

rājānaṃ passati mahājanau

rājānau passanti mahājanā

rañ‍ñā kārīyatai mahāpāsādau

rājūhi kārāpitā mahāvihārā

rañ‍ñā mahājanau sukhaṃ carati

rājūhī mahājanā sukhaṃ caranti

rañ‍ñau paṇṇākāraṃ daiti mahājanau

rājūnaṃ paṇṇākāraṃ dainti mahājanā

rañ‍ñā mahabbhayaṃ uppaj‍jati

rājūhi mahabbhayāni uppaj‍janti

rañ‍ñau vappamaṅgalaṃ hauti

rājūnaṃ sabbābharaṇāni haunti

rañ‍ñai mahājanau pana pasīdati

rājusu mahājanā pana pasīdanti|

iti tatiyau pāṭhau|

akārantapul‍liṅgau guṇavattusaddau vuc‍catai|

guṇavā purisau sundaraṃ nibbāṇaṃ gatau

guṇavantau  purisā sundaraṃ nibbāṇaṃ gatā

bhau guṇavaṃ purisa tvampi dānaṃ daihi

bhau guṇavantau purisā tumhai dānaṃ daitha

guṇavantaṃ purisaṃ dhammaṃ baudhaiti ācariyau

guṇavantai purisai dhammaṃ pāṭhayati ācariyau

guṇavantaina purisaina kāritau vihārau

guṇavantaihi purisaihi kāritā vihārā

guṇavantaina purisaina laukau sucarati

guṇavantaihi purisaihi laukā sucaranti

guṇavatau purisassa sak‍kāraṃ karauti

guṇavataṃ purisānaṃ sak‍kāraṃ karaunti

guṇavatā purisasmā bhayaṃ nūppaj‍jati

guṇavantaihi purisaihi bhayāni nūppaj‍janti

guṇavatau purisassa kittisaddau aṅguggacchati

guṇavataṃ purisānaṃ guṇaghausau hauti

guṇavantai purisai mai ramati matau

guṇavantaisu purisaisu sappurisau pasīdati|

iti catutthau pāṭhau|

akārantapul‍liṅgau gacchantasaddau vuc‍catai|

gacchaṃ yañ‍ñadattau purisaṃ bhāraṃ hārayati

gacchantā yañ‍ñadattā purisaṃ kammaṃ kārayanti

bhau gacchaṃ yañ‍ñadatta tvaṃ maṃ pālaya

bhau gacchantā yañ‍ñadattā maṃ pālaitha

gacchantaṃ yañ‍ñadattaṃ kambalaṃyācayati dvijau

gacchantai yañ‍ñadattai kambalaṃ yācayati dvijau

gacchatā yañ‍ñadattaina rukkhau pupphāni avacīyatai

gacchantaihi yañ‍ñadattaihi rukkhau pupphāni avacitau

gacchatā yañ‍ñadattaina kauci maggaṃ jānāti

gacchantaihi yañ‍ñadattaihi kaicimaggaṃ jānanti

gacchatau yañ‍ñadattassa chattaṃ dhārayatai

gacchataṃ yañ‍ñadattānaṃ chattāni dhārayantai

gacchatā yañ‍ñadattasmā bhayaṃ nūppaj‍jati

gacchantaihi yañ‍ñadattaihi bhayāni nūppaj‍janti

gacchatau yañ‍ñadattassa chattau hauti

gacchataṃ yañ‍ñadattānaṃ ābharaṇāni haunti

gacchantai yañ‍ñadattai kauci pasīdati

gacchantaisu yañ‍ñadattaisu kaici pasīdanti|

iti  pañ‍camau pāṭhau|

ikārantapul‍liṅgau aggisaddau vuc‍catai|

aggi pana kaṭṭhamaṅgāraṃ karauti

aggayau kaṭṭhamaṅgāraṃ karaunti

bhau aggi tvaṃ sītaṃ vinaudaihi

bhau aggī tumhai sītaṃ vinaudaitha

aggiṃ nibbāpaiti yau kauci

aggayau nibbāpainti yai kaici

agginā kauci akārau daḍḍhau

aggīhi kaici agārā daḍḍhā

agginā kiñ‍ci āgāraṃ jhāpaiti

aggīhi kaici agārai jhāpaiti

agginau upādānaṃ dadāti brāhmaṇau

aggīnaṃ upādānaṃ dadanti brāhmaṇā

agginā dhūmau apaiti nic‍caṃ

aggīhi’dhūmāpi apainti nic‍caṃ

agginau ālaukau ca hauti

aggīnaṃ ālaukāpi haunti

aggimhi yau kauci pasīdati

aggīsu yai kaici pasīdanti|

iti chaṭṭhau pāṭhau|

ikārantapul‍liṅgau ādisaddau vuc‍catai|

ādi bandhīyatai samaggaina saṅghaina

ādayau bandhīyantai samaggaihi bhikkhuhi

bhau ādi tvaṃ dīghakālaṃ pavattaihi

bhau ādī tumhai dīghakālaṃ pavattaitha

ādiṃ passati samaggau saṅghau ca

ādayau passati bhikkhu saṅghau ca

ādinā parisuddhaina pana bhūyatai

ādīhi parisuddhaihi pana bhūyatai

ādinā samaggau saṅghau sucarati

ādīhi bhikkhu saṅghau sucarati

ādinau kauci upakaraṇaṃ daiti

ādīnaṃ kaici upakaraṇaṃ dainti

ādinā samaggau saṅghau apaiti

ādīhi bhikkhu’saṅghau apaiti

ādissa  upakārikā khau pana hauti

ādīnaṃ upakārikāyaupi haunti

ādimhi samaggau saṅghau nisīdati

ādīsū bhikkhu saṅghaupi nisīdati|

iti sattamau pāṭhau|

īkārantapul‍liṅgau daṇḍīsaddau vuc‍catai|

daṇḍī purisaupi daṇḍaṃ chaḍḍaitu

daṇḍinau purisā daṇḍaṃ chaḍḍaintu

bhau daṇḍī purisa daṇḍaṃ chaḍḍaihi

bhau daṇḍinau purisā daṇḍaṃ chaḍḍaitha

daṇḍiṃ purisaṃ kammaṃ kārayati purisau

daṇḍī purisai kammai kārainti purisā

daṇḍinā purisaina daṇḍau chaḍḍīyatu

daṇḍīhi purisaihi daṇḍā chaḍḍīyantu

daṇḍinā purisaina purisau tiṭṭhati

daṇḍīhi purisaihi purisā tiṭṭhanti

daṇḍinau purisassa cittaṃ na ruc‍cati

daṇḍīnaṃ purisānaṃ cittaṃ na ruc‍cati

daṇḍinā purisasmā bhayaṃ uppaj‍jati

daṇḍīhi purisaihi bhayāni uppaj‍janti

daṇḍinau purisassa pariggahau hauti

daṇḍīnaṃ purisānaṃ pariggahā haunti

daṇḍismiṃ purisai cittaṃ na ramati

daṇḍīsu purisaisu aikac‍cau nappasīdati|

iti aṭṭhamau pāṭhau|

ukārantapul‍liṅgau bhikkhusaddau vuc‍catai|

bhikkhu mahārājānaṃ dhammaṃ bhaṇati

bhikkhū mahārājānaṃ dhammaṃ bhaṇanti

bhau bhikkhu paṇītaṃ dhammaṃ daisaihi

bhau bhikkhū paṇītaṃ dhammaṃ daisaitha

bhikkhuṃ sak‍kac‍caṃ paṇamāmahaṃ

bhikkhū sak‍kac‍caṃ paṇamāma mayaṃ

bhikkhunā saddhammau daisīyatai

bhikkhūhi saddhammau sudaisitau

bhikkhunā laukau saggaṃ gacchati

bhikkhūhi mahājanā saggaṃ tacchanti

bhikkhussa  dānaṃ daiti sappurisau

bhikkhūnaṃ dānaṃ dainti sappurisā

bhikkhunā saggaṃ labhaiyya saṅau

bhikkhūhi saggaṃ labhaiyyuṃ sappurisā

bhikkhunau pattacīvarampi bhavati

bhikkhūnaṃ pattacīvarāni bhavanti

bhikkhusmiṃ mai ramati manau

bhikkhūsu saddhau sappurisau pasīdati|

iti navamau pāṭhau|

ukārantapul‍liṅgau jantusaddau vuc‍catai|

jantu daivadattaṃ kaṭaṃ kārāpaiti

jantunau daivadattai kaṭai kārāpainti

bhaujantu tvampi daivadattaṃ kaṭaṃ kāraisi

bhau jantunau daivadattai kaṭai kāraitha|

jantumpaitaṃ kaṭaṃ kāraimi tainaiva kaṭai vā

jantupaitai kaṭaṃ kāraima tainaiva kaṭai vā

jantunā purisau vihāraṃ vihāraivā kārāpīyatai

jantūhi purisau vihāraṃ vihāraivā kāritau

jantunā riyaina yau kauci sukhaṃ pāpuṇati

jantūhi ariyaihi yai kaici sukhaṃ pāpuṇanti

jantunau ariyassa dhanaṃ dadāti dhanavantau

jantunamariyānaṃ dhanaṃ dadanti dhanavantā

jantunāriyamhā antaradhāyati yau kauci

jantūhi ariyaihi antaradhāyanti yai kaici

jantunau sakalassa phalaṃvipākau hauti

jantūnaṃ sakalānaṃ pañ‍cagatiyau haunti

jantumhi ariyai yau kauci pasīdati

jantusu ariyaisu yai kaici pasīdanti|

iti dasamau pāṭhau|

ukārantapul‍liṅgau satthusaddau vuc‍catai|

satthā daivamanussānaṃ dhammadaisanaṃ akāsi

satthārau daivamanussānaṃ dhammadaisanaṃ akāsuṃ

bhau satthā tvaṃ sadaivakaṃ laukaṃ pālaya

bhau satthārau sadaivakaṃ laukaṃ pālaitha

satthāraṃ dhammarājānaṃ sak‍kac‍caṃ paṇamā mahaṃ

satthārai dhammarājai sak‍kac‍caṃ paṇamāma mayaṃ

satthārā  dhammarājaina sīvaṃ baudhīyatai laukau

satthāraihi dhammarājaihi sivaṃ baudhitau laukau

satthārā dhammarājaina ac‍cutaṃ padaṃ gacchati

satthāraihi dhammarājaihi ac‍cutaṃ padaṃ labhaiyya

satthu dhammarājassa pupphāni yajati laukau

satthānaṃ dhammarājānaṃ pupphāni yajati laukau

satthārā dhammarājasmā parājainti añ‍ñatitthiyā

satthāraihi dhammarājaihi charaṃsiyau niccharanti

satthunau dhammarājassa caraṇaṃ paṇamāmyahaṃ

satthānaṃ dhammarājānaṃ pādai sirasā paṇāma

satthari dhammarājai kau bhattiṃ na ghaṭīyati

satthāraisu dhammarājaisu bhatti bhavabhavai atthu|

iti aikādasamau pāṭhau|

ukārantapul‍liṅgau nantusaddau vuc‍catai|

tattā pitāmahaṃ bhaujanaṃ bhaujayati

nattārau pitāmahaṃ bhaujanaṃ bhaujayanti

bhau nattā tvampi sippaṃ uggaṇhāhi

bhau nattārau tumhai sippāni uggaṇhātha

nattāraṃ sippaṃ pāṭhaiti ācariyau

nattārai sippāni pāṭhainti ācariyā

nattārā rukkhau pupphāni avacīyatai

nattāraihi rukkhau pupphāni avacitau

nattārā pitāmahau sukhī jātau

nattāraihi pittāmahā sukhījātā

nattussa khaittavatthuṃ dadāti pitāmahau

nattārānaṃ khaittavatthuṃ dadanti pitāmahā

nattārā pitāmahau apaitau hauti

nattāraihi pitāmahau apaitau hauti

nantunau vatthābharanaṃ pana hauti

nattārānaṃ khau pana vatthābharanāni haunti

nattari yau kauci pitāmahau nappasīdati

nattāraisu yai kaici pitāmahā nappasīdanti|

iti dvādasamau pāṭhau|

ukāranta pul‍liṅgau pitusaddau vuc‍catai|

pitāpi puttaṃ bhaujanaṃ bhaujayati

pitarau puttai bhaujanaṃ bhaujayanti

bhaupitā  tvampi bhaujanaṃ bhaujaihi

bhau pitarau tumhai bhaujanaṃ bhaujaitha

pitaraṃ sak‍kac‍caṃ pausaiti puttau

pitarai sak‍kac‍caṃ pausainti puttā

pitarā puttau kusalaṃ kārāpīyatai

pitaraihi puttau kusalaṃ kārāpīyatai

pitarā puttaupi sukhaṃ pāpuṇāti

pitaraihi bhaginiyau sukhaṃ pāpuṇanti

pitussa an‍napānaṃdaiti puttau

pitarānaṃ an‍napānaṃ dainti puttā

pitarā antaradhāyāti puttau

pitunau pādai abhivandati puttau

pitarānaṃ pādai abhivandati puttā

pitari puttau trajau pasīdati

pitaraisu puttaurasau pasīdati|

iti tairasamau pāṭhau|

ukāranta pul‍liṅgau bhātusaddau vuc‍catai|

bhātā bhaginiṃ kusalaṃ kārāpayati

bhātarau bhaginī kusalāni kārāpayanti

bhau bhātā tvampi kusalaṃ karauhi

bhau bhātarau tumhai kusalāni karautha

bhātaraṃ kammaṃ kārayati pubbajau

bhātarai kammai kārayanti pubbajā

bhātarā saṅghau bhattaṃ bhaujāpīyatai

bhātaraihi saṅghau bhattaṃ bhaujāpitau

bhātarā bhaginīpi sukhaṃ pāpuṇāti

bhātaraihi bhaginiyau sukhaṃ pāpuṇanti

bhātussa vatthañ‍ca dadāti pubbajau

bhātarānaṃ vatthāni dadanti pubbajā

bhātarā antaradhāyati bhaginipi

bhātaraihi antaradhāyanti bhaginiyau

bhātunau khaittavatthūni pana vij‍janti

bhātarānaṃ khaittavatthūni pana vij‍janti

bhātari jaiṭṭhau sammā pasīdati

bhātaraisu jaiṭṭhā sammā pasīdanti|

iti cuddasamau pāṭhau|

ūkāranta  pul‍liṅgau abhibhūsaddau vuc‍catai|

abhibhū tathāgatau dhammadaisanaṃ akāsi

abhibhū tathāgatā dhammadaisanaṃ akāsuṃ

bhau abhibhū tathāgata dhammaṃ daisaihi

bhau abhibhū tathāgatā dhammaṃ daisaitha

abhibhuṃ tathāgataṃ sirasā namāmyahaṃ

abhibhuvau tathāgatai sirasā namāma mayaṃ

abhibhunā tathāgataina dhammau daisitau

abhibhūhi tathāgataihi dhammā daisitā

abhibhunā tathāgataina munayau sukhījātā

abhibhūhi tathāgataihi laukā sukhījātā

abhibhūnau tathāgatassa pupphāni yajati

abhibhūnaṃ tathāgatānaṃ pupphāni yajanti

abhibhunā tathāgatamhā pabhā niccharati

abhibhūhi tathāgataihi pabhāyau niccharanti

abhibhunau tathāgatassa pādai paṇamāmi

abhibhūnaṃ tathāgatānaṃ caraṇaṃ paṇamāma

abhibhumhi tathāgatai mai ramati manau

abhibhūsu tathāgataisu manaṃ patiṭṭhitaṃ|

iti pañ‍cadasamau pāṭhau|

ūkāranta pul‍liṅgau sabbañ‍ñusaddau vuc‍catai|

sabbañ‍ñu laukanāthau dhammaṃ daisaiti

sabbañ‍ñu laukanāthā dhammaṃ daisainti

bhau sabbañ‍ñu laukanātha dhammaṃ daisaihi

bhau sabbañ‍ñu laukanāthā dhammaṃ daisaitha

sabbañ‍ñuṃ laukanāthaṃ passati mahājanau

sabbañ‍ñunau laukanāthai passati mahājanau

sabbañ‍ñunā laukanāthaina dhammau daisīyatai

sabbañ‍ñūhi laukanāthaihi dhammau daisitau

sabbañ‍ñunā laukanāthaina sivapadaṃ yanti

sabbañ‍ñūhi laukanāthaihi laukā sukhījātā

sabbañ‍ñunau laukanāthassa jīvitaṃ paric‍cajāmi

sabbañ‍ñūnaṃ laukanāthānaṃ jīvitaṃ paric‍cajāmi

sabbañ‍ñunā laukanāthasmā sivapadaṃ labhaiyya

sabbañ‍ñūhi laukanāthaihi sivapadi labhaiyyuṃ

sabbañ‍ñunau  laukanāthassa caraṇaṃ vandāmi

sabbañuñūnaṃ laukanāthānaṃ pādai vandāma

sabbañ‍ñusmiṃ laukanāthai laukau pasīdati

sabbañ‍ñūsu laukanāthaisu laukā pasīdanti|

iti saulasamau pāṭhau|

aukāranta pul‍liṅgau gausaddau vuc‍catai|

gau usabhau ujuṃ gacchati

gāvau usabhā ujuṃ gacchanti

bhau gau usabhā ujuṃ gacchāhi

gāvuṃ vajaṃ rundhati gaupālau

gāvau vajai rundhati gaupālā

gāvaina sakaṭau ānīyatai

gauhi sakaṭā ānīyantai

gāvaina gaumikau jīvikaṃ kappaiti

gauhi gaumikā jīvikaṃ kappainti

gāvassa tiṇaṃ dadāti gaupālau

gaunaṃ tiṇaṃ dadanti gaupālā

gāvā usabhasmā bhayaṃ upjaj‍jati

gauhi usabhaihi bhayāni jāyanti

gāvassa usabhassa dhavalau guṇau

gavaṃ usabhānaṃ guṇā pamāṇaṃ

bhāvai usabhai gaumikau pasīdati

gausu usabhaisu gaumikā pasīdanti|

iti sattadasamau pāṭhau|

iti padamañ‍jariyā pul‍liṅganāmānaṃ|

paṭhamau paricchaidau|

atha akārantaitthiliṅgau kañ‍ñāsaddau vuc‍catai|

kañ‍ñā dāsiṃ kammaṃ kārāpayati

kañ‍ñāyau dāsī kammai kārāpayanti

bhau kañ‍ñai tvampi kusalaṃ karauhi

bhau kañ‍ñāyau tumhai kusalaṃ karautha

kañ‍ñaṃ kusalaṃ kārāpaiti mātā

kañ‍ñāyau kusalāni kārainti mātāpitarau

kañ‍ñāya  tilānipi bhaj‍jīyantai

kañ‍ñāhi dhañ‍ñānipi bhaj‍jīyantai

kañ‍ñāya kauci purisau sucarati

kañ‍ñāhi kaici purisā sucaranti

kañ‍ñāya ābharaṇāni dadāti purisau

kañ‍ñānaṃ ābharaṇāni dadāti purisau

kañ‍ñāya apaiti kauci purisau

kañ‍ñāhi apainti kaici purisā

kañ‍ñāya vatthābharaṇānipi haunti

kañ‍ñānaṃ vatthābharaṇānipi haunti

kañ‍ñāyaṃ kauci purisau pasīdati

kañ‍ñāsu kaici purisā pasīdanti|

iti paṭhamau pāṭhau|

ikāranta itthiliṅgau rattisaddau vuc‍catai|

ratti juṇhā sammā viraucati

rattiyau juṇhāyau viraucanti

bhau ratti juṇhā sammā virauca

bhau rattiyau juṇhāyau viraucatha

yai  kaici dāsai gāmaṃ gamayati sāmikau

yaihi kaihici sūdaihi audanau pācāpitau

yaihi kaihici sukhaṃ pāpuṇāti bhikkhusaṅghau

yaisu kaisuci āsanaisu nisīdanti kaici|

itthiliṅgarūpāni vuc‍cantai|

yāhi kāhici vanitāhi sāmi bhattaṃ bhaujāpitau

yāya kāyaci vanitāya sukhaṃ pāpuṇāti sāmikau

yāhi kāhici vanitāhi sukhaṃ pāpuṇanti sāmikā

yāya kāyaci vanitāya ābharaṇaṃ daiti sāmikau

yāhi kāhici vanitāhi apaiti yau kauci

yāya kāyaci vanitāya puttāpi bahavau

yāsaṃ kāsaṃci vanitānaṃ ābharaṇāni haunti

yāyaṃ kāyaṃci vanitāyaṃ mai cittaṃ na ramati

yāsu kāsuci vanitāsu mai cittāni na ramanti|

iti aikādasamau pāṭhau|

yaihi kaihici kulaihi sak‍kārā katā

yaina kainaci kulaina aikac‍cau jīvati

yaihi kaihici kulaihi aikac‍cai jīvanti

yaisaṃ kaisaṃci kulānaṃ upakāraṃ akāsuṃ

iti dvādasamau pāṭhau|

pul‍liṅgarūpāni vuc‍cantai|

sau sūdajaiṭṭhau sūdaina audanaṃ pācaiti

tai sūdajaiṭṭhā sūdaihi audanaṃ pācainti

taṃ yañ‍ñadattaṃ kambalaṃ yācatai brāhmaṇau

tai yañ‍ñadattaina kambalaṃ yācantai brāhmaṇā

taina brāhmaṇaina gahapati dhanaṃ yācīyatai

taihi sissaihi garu satthaṃ pucchīyatai

taina pupphaina buddhaṃ yajati sappurisau

taihi pupphaihi buddhaṃ yajanti sappurisā

tassa bhikkhussa dānaṃ daiti sappurisau

taisaṃ yācakānaṃ dhanaṃ dadāti dhanavā

tamhā himavatā pabhavanti pañ‍camahā nadiyau

taihi laubhanīyaihi dhammaihi suddhau asaṃsaṭṭhau

tassa buddhassa pacchatau pacchatau anubandhiṃsu

taisaṃ bhikkhūnaṃyaiva pattacīvarāni haunti

tasmiṃ āsanaiyaiva nisīdati bhikkhu

taisu bhikkhūsu pana mai manau ramati|

iti tairasamau pāṭhau|

itthiliṅgarūpāni vuc‍cantai|

sā khattiyakañ‍ñā pana bahuṃ puñ‍ñaṃ pasavati
