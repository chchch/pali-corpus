




tailakaṭāhagāthā 

||namau tassa bhagavatau arahatau sammāsambuddhassa||

1.

 laṃkissarau jayatu vāraṇarājagāmī

bhaugindabhauga rucirāyata pīṇa bāhu,

sādhupacāraniratau guṇasan‍nivāsau

dhammai ṭhitau vīgatakaudhamadāvalaipau|

2.

 yau sabbalaukamahitau karuṇādhivāsau

maukkhākarau ravikulambara puṇṇa candau,

ñaiyyaudadhiṃ suvipulaṃ sakalaṃ vibuddhau

laukuttamaṃ namatha taṃ sirasā munindaṃ|

3.

 saupānamālamamalaṃ tidasālayassa

saṃsāra sāgarasamuttaraṇāya saituṃ,

sabbāgatībhaya vivaj‍jita khaima maggaṃ

dhammaṃ namassatha sadā muninā paṇītaṃ|

4. 

 daiyyaṃ tadappamapi yattha pasan‍na cittā

datvā narā phalamuḷārataraṃ labhantai,

taṃ sabbadā dasabalainapi suppasatthaṃ

saṅghaṃ namassatha sadāmitapuñ‍ñakhaittaṃ|

5.

 taijau balaina mahatā ratanattayassa

laukattayaṃ samadhigacchati yaina maukkhaṃ,

rakkhā na catthi ca samā ratanattayassa

tasmā sadā bhajatha taṃ ratanattayaṃ bhau|

6.

 laṃkissarau parahitaikaratau nirāsau

rattimpi jāgararatau karuṇādhivāsau,

laukaṃ vibaudhayati laukahitāya kāmaṃ

dhammaṃ samācaratha jāgariyānuyuttā|

7. 

 sattaupakāra niratā kusalai sahāyā

bhau dul‍labhā bhuvi narā vihatappamādā,

laṃkādhipaṃ guṇadhanaṃ kusalai sahāyaṃ

āgamma saṃcaratha dhammamalaṃ pamādaṃ|

8.

 dhammau tilauka saraṇau paramau rasānaṃ

dhammau mahaggharatanau ratanaisu laukai,

dhammau bhavai tibhavadukkha vināsahaitu

dhammaṃ samācaratha jāgariyānuyuttā|

9.

 niddaṃ vinaudayatha bhāvayathappamaiyyaṃ

dukkhaṃ anic‍campi caiha anattatañ‍ca,

daihai ratiṃ jahatha ja jaj‍jarabhājanābhau

dhammaṃ samācaratha jāgariyānuyuttā|

10. 

 aukāsa maj‍ja mama natthi suvai karissaṃ

dhammaṃ itīhalasatā kusalappayaugai,

nā’laṃ tiyaddhasu tathā bhuvanattayai ca

kāmaṃ na catthi manujau maraṇā pamuttau|

11.

 khittau yathā nabhasi kainacidaiva laiḍḍu

bhūmiṃ samāpatti bhāratayā khaṇaina,

jātattamaiva khalu kāraṇamaikamatra

laukaṃ sadā nanu dhuvaṃ maraṇāya gantuṃ|

12.

 kāmaṃ narassa patatau girimuddhanātau

majjhai na kiñ‍ci bhayanissaraṇāya haitu,

kāmaṃ vajanti maraṇaṃ tibhavaisu sattā

bhaugai ratiṃ pajahathāpi ca jīvitai ca|

13. 

 kāmaṃ patanti mahiyā khalu vassadhārā

vij‍jul‍latā vitatamaigha mukhā pamuttā,

aivaṃ narā maraṇabhīma papātamajjhai

kāmaṃ patanti nahi kauci bhavaisu nic‍cau|

14.

 vailātaṭai paṭutarauru taraṃgamālā

nāsaṃ vajanti satataṃ salilālayassa,

nāsaṃ tathā samupayanti narāmarānaṃ

pāṇāni dāruṇatarai maraṇaudadhimhi|

15.

 ruddhaupi sau rathavarassagajādhipaihi

yaudhaihi cāpi sabalaihi ca sāyudhaihi,

laukaṃ vivaṃciya sadā maraṇūsabhau sau

kāmaṃ nihanti bhuvanattaya sāli daṇḍaṃ|

16. 

 bhau mārutaina mahatā vihatau padīpau

khippaṃ vināsa mukhamaiti mahappabhaupi,

laukai tathā maraṇacaṇḍa samīraṇaina

khippaṃ vinassati narāyumahā padīpau|

17

 rāmaj‍junappabhūti bhūpati puṃgavā ca

sūrā purai raṇamukhai vijitāri saṅghā,

taipīha caṇḍa maraṇaugha nimuggadaihā

nāsaṃ gatā jagati kai maraṇā pamuttā?

18.

 lakkhī ca sāgarapaṭā sadharādharā ca

sampattiyau ca vividhā api rūpasaubhā,

sabbā ca tā api ca mittasutā ca dārā

kai cāpi kaṃ anugatā maraṇaṃ vajantaṃ?

19. 

 brahmāsurāsuragaṇā ca mahānubhāvā

gandhabbakin‍naramahauragarakkhasā ca,

tai cā parai ca maraṇaggisikhāya sabbai

antai patanti salabhā iva khīṇapuñ‍ñā|

20.

 yai sāriputtapamukhā munisāvakā ca

suddhā sadāsavanudā paramiddhipattā,

tai cāpi mac‍cuvaḷabhā mukha san‍nimuggā

dīpānivānalahatā khayataṃ upaitā|

21.

 buddhāpi buddhakamalāmalacārunaittā

battiṃsalakkhaṇa virājita rūpasaubhā,

sabbāsacakkhayakarāpi ca laukanāthā

sammadditā maraṇamattamahāgajaina|

22. 

 raugāturaisu karuṇā na jarāturaisu

khiḍḍāparaisu sukumārakumārakaisu,

laukaṃ sadā hanati mac‍cu mahāgajindau

davānalau vanamivāvarataṃ asaisaṃ|

23.

 āpuṇṇatā na salilai na jalāsayassa

kaṭṭhassa cāpi bahutā na hutāsanassa,

bhutvāna sau tibhūvanampi tathā asaisaṃ

bhau niddayau na khalu pītimupaiti mac‍cu|

24.

 bhau mauha mauhitatayā vivasau adhañ‍ñau

laukau patatyapipi mac‍cumukhai subhīmai,

bhaugai ratiṃ samupayāti nihīnapañ‍ñau

daulā taraṅgacapalai supinaupamaiyyai|

25. 

 aikaupi mac‍curabhihantumalaṃ tilaukaṃ

kiṃ niddayā api jarāmaraṇānuyāyī,

kau vā karaiyya vibhasuvaisu ca jīvitāsaṃ

jātau narau supina saṃgama san‍nibhaisu|

26.

 nic‍cāturaṃ jagadidaṃ sabhayaṃ sasaukaṃ

disvā ca kaudhamadamauhajarābhibhūtaṃ,

ubbaigamattamapi yassa na vij‍jatī cai

sau dāruṇauna maraṇaṃ vata taṃ dhiratthu!

bhau bhau na passatha jarāsidharañhi mac‍cu

māhañ‍ñamānamakhilaṃ satataṃ tilaukaṃ,

kiṃ niddayā nayatha vītabhayā tiyāmaṃ

dhammaṃ sadā’savanudaṃ caratha’ppamattā|

28. 

 bhāvaitha bhau maraṇamāravivaj‍janāya

laukai sadā maraṇa sañ‍ñamimaṃ yatattā,

aivañhi bhāvanaratassa narassa tassa

taṇhā pahīyati sarīragatā asaisā|

29.

 rūpaṃ jarā piyataraṃ malinīkarauti

sabbaṃ balaṃ harati attani ghauraraugau,

nānūpabhauga parirakkhita mattabhāvaṃ

bhau mac‍cu saṃharati kiṃ phalamattabhāvai?

30.

 kammānilāpahataraugataraṃgabhaṃgai

saṃsāra sāgara mukhai vitatai vipan‍nā,

mā māpamādamakarittha karautha maukkhaṃ

dukkhaudayau nanu pamādamayaṃ narānaṃ|

31. 

 bhaugā ca mittasutapaurisa bandhavā ca

nārī ca jīvitasamā api khaittavatthu,

sabbāni tāni paralaukamitau vajantaṃ

nānubbajanti kusalākusalaṃva laukai|

32.

 bhau vij‍jucaṃcalatarai bhavasāgaramhi

khittā purā katamahāpavanaina taina,

kāmaṃ vibhij‍jati khaṇaina sarīranāvā

hatthai karautha paramaṃ guṇahatthasāraṃ|

33.

 nic‍caṃ vibhij‍jatiha āmaka bhājanaṃva

saṃrakkhitaupi bahudhā iha attabhāvau,

dhammaṃ samācaratha saggapatippatiṭṭhaṃ

dhammau suciṇṇamihamaiva phalaṃ dadāti|

34. 

 rantvā sadā piyatarai divi daivaraj‍jai

namhā cavanti vibudhā api khīṇapuñ‍ñā,

sabbaṃ sukhaṃ divi bhuvīha viyauganiṭṭhaṃ

kau pañ‍ñavā bhavasukhaisu ratiṃ karaiyya?

35.

 buddhau sasāvakagaṇau jagadaikanāthau

tārāvalīparivutaupi ca puṇṇacandau,

indaupi daivamakuṭaṃkita pādakañ‍jau

kau phaiṇapiṇḍa-na-samau tibhavaisu jātau?

36.

 līlāvataṃsamapi yaubbana rūpasaubhaṃ

attūpamaṃ piyajanaina ca sampayaugaṃ,

disvāpi vij‍jucapalaṃ kurutai pamādaṃ

bhau mauhamauhitajanau bhavarāgarattau|

37. 

 puttau pitā bhavati mātu patīha puttau

nārī kadāci jananī ca pitā ca puttau,

aivaṃ sadā viparivattati jīvalaukau

cittai sadāticapalai khalu jātiraṅgai|

38.

 rantvā purai vividhaphul‍lalatākulaihi

daivāpi nandanavanai surasundarīhi,

tai vai’kadā vitatakaṇṭakasaṃkaṭaisu

bhau kauṭisimbalivanaisu phusanti dukkhaṃ|

39.

 bhutvā sudhan‍namapi kañ‍canabhājanaisu

saggai purai suravarā paramiddhipattā,

tai cāpi paj‍jalitalauhagulaṃ gilanti

kāmaṃ kadāci narakālaya vāsabhūtā|

40. 

 bhutvā narissaravarā ca mahiṃ asaisaṃ

daivādhipā ca divi dibbasukhaṃ surammaṃ,

vāsaṃ kadāci khurasañ‍citabhūtalaisu

tai vā mahārathagaṇānugatā divīha|

41.

 daivaṅganā lalitabhin‍nataraṅgamālai

raṅgai mahissarajaṭāmakuṭānuyātai,

rantvā purai suravarā pamadāsahāyā

tai cāpi ghaurataravaitaraṇiṃ patanti|

42.

 phul‍lāni pal‍lavalatāphalasaṃkulāni

rammāni nandanavanāni manauramāni,

dibbaccharālalitapuṇṇadarīmukhāni

kailāsamairusikharāni ca yanti nāsaṃ|

43. 

 daulā’nilā’nalataraṃgasamā hi bhaugā

vij‍juppabhāticapalāni ca jīvitāni,

māyāmarīcijalasaumasamaṃ sarīraṃ

kau jīvitai ca vibhavai ca karaiyya rāgaṃ?

44.

 kiṃ dukkhamatthi na bhavaisu ca dāruṇaisu

sattaupi tassa vividhassa na bhājanau kau,

jātau yathā maraṇaraugajarābhibhūtau

kau saj‍janau bhavaratiṃ pihayaiyya’bālau?

45.

 kai vāpi paj‍jalitalauhagulaṃ gilanti

sak‍kā kathañ‍cidapi pāṇitalaina bhīmaṃ,

dukkhaudayaṃ asucinissavanaṃ anantaṃ

kau kāmayaitha khalu daihamimaṃ abālau?

46. 

 laukai na mac‍cusamamatthi bhayaṃ narānaṃ

na vyādhidukkhasamamatthi ca kiṃci dukkhaṃ,

aivaṃ virūpakaraṇaṃ na jarāsamānaṃ

mauhaina bhau ratimupaiti tathāpi daihai|

47.

 nissāratau nalakalīkadalīsamānaṃ

attānamaiva parihañ‍ñati attahaitu,

sampausitaupi kusahāya ivākatañ‍ñū

kāyau na yassa anugacchati kālakairā|

48.

 taṃ phaiṇapiṇḍasadisaṃ visasūlakappaṃ

tauyā’nilā’nalamahīuragādhivāsaṃ,

jiṇṇālayaṃva paridubbalamattabhāvaṃ

disvā narau kathamupaiti ratiṃ sapañ‍ñau?

49. 

 āyukkhayaṃ samupayāti khaṇai khaṇaipi

anvaiti mac‍cu hananāya jarāsipāṇī,

kālaṃ tathā na parivattati taṃ atītaṃ

dukkhaṃ idaṃ nanu bhavaisu acintanīyaṃ?

50.

 appāyukassa maraṇaṃ sulabhaṃ bhavaisu

dīghāyukassa ca jarā vyasanaṃ ca’naikaṃ,

aivaṃ bhavai ubhayataupi ca dukkhamaiva

dhammaṃ samācaratha dukkhavināsanāya|

51.

 dukkhagginā sumahatā paripīḷitaisu

laukattayassa vasatau bhavavārakaisu,

sabbattatā sucaritassa pamādakālau

bhau bhau na hauti paramaṃ kusalaṃ ciṇātha|

52. 

 appaṃ sukhaṃ jalalavaṃ viya bhau tiṇaggai

dukkhantu sāgarajalaṃ viya sabbalaukai,

saṃkappanā tadapi hauti sabhāvatau hi

sabbaṃ tilaukamapi kaivaladukkhamaiva|

53.

 kāyau na yassa anugacchati kāyahaitu

bālau anaikavidhamācaratīha dukkhaṃ,

kāyau sadā kali malākalilañhi laukai

kāyai ratau’navarataṃ vyasanaṃ paraiti

54.

 mīḷhākaraṃ kalimalākaramāmagandhaṃ

sūḷāsisal‍lavisapan‍nagaraugabhūtaṃ,

daihaṃ vipassatha jarāmaraṇādhivāsaṃ

tucchaṃ sadā vigatasāramimaṃ vinindyaṃ|

55. 

 dukkhaṃ anic‍camasubhaṃ vata attabhāvaṃ

mā saṃkilaisaya na vij‍jati jātu nic‍cau,

ambhau na vij‍jati hi appamapīha sāraṃ

sāraṃ samācaratha dhammamalaṃ pamādaṃ|

56.

 māyāmarīcikadalīnalaphaiṇapuñ‍ja-

gaṃgātaraṅgajalabubbulasan‍nibhaisu,

khandhaisu pañ‍casu chaḷāyatanaisu taisu

attā na vij‍jati hi kau na vadaiyya’bālau?

57.

 vañjhāsutau sasavisāṇamayai rathai tu,

dhāvaiyya cai cirataraṃ sadhuraṃ gahaitvā,

dīpac‍cimālamiva taṃ khaṇabhaṅgabhūtaṃ

attāti dubbalatarantu vadaiyya daihaṃ|

58. 

 bālau yathā salilabubbulabhājanaina

ākaṇṭhatau vata pibaiyya marīcitauyaṃ,

attāni sārarahitaṃ kadalīsamānaṃ

mauhā bhaṇaiyya khalu daihamimaṃ anattaṃ|

59.

 yau’dumbarassa kusumaina marīcitauyaṃ

vāsaṃ yadicchati sa khaidamupaiti bālau,

attānamaiva parihañ‍ñati attahaitu

attā na vij‍jati kadācidapīha daihai|

60.

 pausau yathā hi kadalī suvinibbhujantau

sāraṃ tadappampi naupalabhaiyya kāmaṃ,

khandhaisu paṃcasu chaḷāyatanaisu taisu

suñ‍ñaisu kiñ‍cidapi naupalabhaiyya sāraṃ|

61. 

 suttaṃ vinā na paṭabhāvamihatthi kiṃci

daihaṃ vinā na khalu kauci mihatthi sattau,

daihau sabhāvarahitau khaṇabhaṃgayuttau,

kau attahaitu aparau bhuvi vij‍jatīha?

62.

 disvā marīcisalilañhi sudūratau bhau

bālau migau samupadhāvati tauyasañ‍ñī,

aivaṃ sabhāvarahitai viparītasiddhai

daihai paraiti parikappanayā hi rāgaṃ|

63.

 daihai sabhāvarahitai parikappasiddhai

attā na vij‍jati hi vij‍jumivantalikkhai,

bhāvaitha bhāvanaratā vigatappamādā

sabbāsavappahananāya anattasañ‍ñaṃ|

64. 

 lālākarīsarudhirassuvasānulittaṃ

daihaṃ imaṃ kalimalākalilaṃ asāraṃ,

sattā sadā pariharanti jigucchanīyaṃ

nānāsucīhi paripuṇṇaghaṭaṃ yathaiva|

65.

 ṇahātvā jalañhi sakalaṃ catusāgarassa

mairuppamāṇamapi gandhamanuttarañ‍ca,

pappauti naiva manujau hi suciṃ kadāci

kiṃ bhau vipassatha guṇaṃ kimu attabhāvai?

66.

 daihau sa aiva vividhāsucisan‍nidhānau

daihau sa aiva vadhabandhanaraugabhūtau,

daihau sa aiva navadhā paribhin‍nagaṇḍau

daihaṃ vinā bhayakaraṃ na susānamatthi|

67. 

 antaugataṃ yadiva muttakarīsabhāgau

daihā bahiṃ aticaraiyya vinikkhamitvā,

mātā pitā vikaruṇā ca vinaṭṭhapaimā

kāmaṃ bhavaiyyu kimu bandhusutā ca dārā?

68.

 daihaṃ yathā navamukhaṃ kimisaṅghagaihaṃ

maṃsaṭṭhisaidarudhirākalilaṃ vigandhaṃ,

pausainti yai vividhapāpamihācaritvā

tai mauhitā maraṇadhammamahau vataivaṃ!

69.

 gaṇḍūpamai vividharauga nivāsabhūtai

kāyai sadā rudhiramuttakarīsapuṇṇai,

yau aittha nandati narau sasigālabhakkhai

kāmañhi saucati parattha sa bālabuddhi|

70. 

 bhau phaiṇapiṇḍasadisau viya sārahīnau

mīḷhālayau viya sadā paṭikūlagandhau,

āsīvisālayanibhau sabhayau sadukkhau

daihau sadā savati lauṇaghaṭauva bhin‍nau|

71.

 jātaṃ yathā na kamalaṃ bhuvi nindanīyaṃ

paṅkaisu bhau asucitauya samākulaisu,

jātaṃ tathā parahitampi ca daihabhūtaṃ

taṃ nindanīyamiha jātu na hauti laukai|

72.

 dvattiṃsabhāgaparipūratarau visaisau

kāyau yathā hi naranāri gaṇassa laukai,

kāyaisu kiṃ phalamihatthi ca paṇḍitānaṃ

kāmaṃ tadaiva nanu hauti paraupakāraṃ|

73. 

 pausauna paṇḍitataraina tathāpi daihau

sabbattanā cirataraṃ paripālanīyau,

dhammaṃ caraiyya suciraṃ khalu jīvamānau

dhammai havai maṇivarau iva kāmadau bhau

74.

 khīrai yathā suparibhāvitamausadhamhi

snaihaina ausadhabalaṃ paribhāsataiva,

dhammau tathā iha samācaritau hi laukai

chāyāva yāti paralauka mitau vajantaṃ|

75.

 kāyassa bhau viracitassa yathānukūlaṃ

chāyā vibhāti rucirāmaladappaṇai tu,

katvā tathaiva paramaṃ kusalaṃ parattha

sambhūsitā iva bhavanti phalaina taina|

76. 

 daihai tathā vividhadukkha nivāsabhūtai

mauhā pamādavasagā sukhasañ‍ñamūḷhā,

tikkhai yathā khuramukhai madhulaihamānau

bāḷhañ‍ca dukkhamanugacchati hīnapañ‍ñau|

77.

 saṃkapparāgavigatai niratattabhāvai

dukkhaṃ sadā samadhigacchati appapañ‍ñau,

mūḷhassa caiva sukhasañ‍ñamihatthilaukai

kiṃpak‍kamaiva nanu hauti vicāramānai|

78.

 sabbaupabhauga dhanadhañ‍ñavisaisalābhī

rūpaina bhau sa makaraddhajasan‍nibhaupi,

yau yaubbanaipi maraṇaṃ labhatai akāmaṃ

kāmaṃ paratthaparapāṇaharau narau hi|

79.

 sau yācakau bhavati bhin‍nakapālahatthau

muṇḍau dhigakkharasataihi ca taj‍jayantau,

bhikkhaṃ sadāribhavanai sakucailavāsau

daihai paratthi paracittaharau narau yau|

80. 

 itthī namuñ‍cati sadā puna itthibhāvā

nārī sadā bhavati sau purisau parattha,

yau ācaraiyya paradāramalaṅghanīyaṃ

ghaurañ‍ca vindati sadā vyasanañ‍ca naikaṃ|

81.

 dīnau vigandhavadanau ca jaḷau apañ‍ñau

mūgau sadā bhavati appiyadassanau ca,

pappauti dukkhamatulañ‍ca manussabhūtau

vācaṃ musā bhaṇati yau hi apañ‍ñasattau|

82.

 ummattakā vigatalaj‍jaguṇā bhavanti

dīnā sadā vyasanasaukaparāyaṇā ca,

jātā bhavaisu vividhaisu virūpadaihā

pītvā halāhalavisaṃva suraṃ vipañ‍ñā|

83.

 pāpāni yaina iha ācaritāni yāni

yau vassakauṭinahutāni anappakāni,

laddhāna ghauramatulaṃ narakaisu dukkhaṃ

pappauti caittha vividhavyasanañ‍ca naikaṃ|

84. 

 laukattayaisu sakalaisu samaṃ na kiṃci

laukassa santikaraṇaṃ ratanattayaina,

taṃtaijasā sumahatā jitasabbapāpau

sauhaṃ sadādhigatasabbasukhau bhavaiyyaṃ|

85.

 laukattayaisu sakalaisu ca sabbasattā

mittā ca majjharipubandhujanā ca sabbai,

tai sabbadā vigataraugabhayā visaukā

sabbaṃ sukhaṃ adhigatā muditā bhavantu|

86.

 kāyau karīsabharitau viya bhin‍nakumbhau

kāyau sadā kalimalavyasanādhivāsau,

kāyai vihañ‍ñati ca sabbasukhanti laukau

kāyau sadā maraṇaraugajarādhivāsau|

87. 

 sau yaubbanauti thavirauti ca bālakauti

sattai na paikkhati vihañ‍ñatiraiva mac‍cu,

sauhaṃ ṭhitaupi sayitaupi ca pak‍kamantau

gacchāmi mac‍cuvadanaṃ niyataṃ tathā hi|

88.

 aivaṃ yathā vihitadausamidaṃ sarīraṃ

nic‍caṃva taggatamanā hadayai karautha,

maittaṃ parittamasubhaṃ maraṇassatiñ‍ca

bhāvaitha bhāvanaratā satataṃ yatattā|

89.

 dānādi puñ‍ñakiriyāni sukhudrayāni

katvā ca tamphalamasaisa mihappamaiyyaṃ,

daiyyaṃ sadā parahitāya sukhāya caiva

kimbhau tadaiva nanu hatthagatañhi sāraṃ?

90.

 haituṃ vinā na bhavatī hi ca kiṃci laukai

saddauva pāṇitalaghaṭṭanahaitujātau,

aivañ‍ca haituphala bhāvavibhāgabhin‍nau

laukau udaiti ca vinassati tiṭṭhatī ca|

91. 

 kammassa kāraṇāmayañhi yathā avij‍jā

bhau kammanā samadhigacchati jātibhaidaṃ,

jātiṃ paṭic‍ca ca jarāmaraṇādidukkhaṃ

sattā sadā paṭilabhanti anādikālai|

92.

 kammaṃ yathā na bhavatīha ca mauhanāsā

kammakkhayāpi ca na hauti bhavaisu jāti,

jātikkhayā iha jarāmaraṇādidukkhaṃ

sabbakkhayau bhavati dīpaivānilaina|

93.

 yau passatīha satataṃ munidhammakāyaṃ

buddhaṃ sa passati narau iti sau avauca,

buddhañ‍ca dhammamamalañ‍ca tilaukanāthaṃ

sampassituṃ vicinathā’pi ca dhammataṃ bhau|

94. 

 sal‍laṃva bhau sunisitaṃ hadayai nimuggaṃ

dausattayaṃ vividhapāpamalaina littaṃ,

nānāvidhabyasanabhājanamappasan‍naṃ

pañ‍ñāmayaina balisaina nirākarautha|

95.

 nākampayanti sakalāpi ca laukadhammā

cittaṃ sadāpagatapāpakilaisasal‍laṃ,

rūpādayau ca vividhā visayā samaggā

phuṭṭhaṃva mairusikharaṃ mahatānilaina|

96.

 saṃsāradukkhamagaṇaiyya yathā munindau

gambhirapāramita sāgaramuttaritvā,

ñaiyyaṃ abaudhi nipuṇaṃ hatamauhajālau

tasmā sadā parahitaṃ paramaṃ ciṇātha|

97.

 auhāya sau’dhigatamaukkhasukhaṃ paraisaṃ

atthāya saṃcari bhavaisu mahabbhayaisu,

aivaṃ sadā parahitaṃ puratau karitvā

dhammau mayānucaritau jagadatthamaiva|

98. 

 laddhāna dul‍labhatarañ‍ca manussayauniṃ

sabbaṃ papañ‍carahitaṃ khaṇasampadañ‍ca,

ñatvāna āsavanudaikahitañ‍ca dhammaṃ

kau pañ‍ñavā anavaraṃ na bhajaiyya dhammaṃ?

99.

 laddhāna buddhasamayaṃ atidul‍labhaṃca

saddhamma maggamasamaṃ sivadaṃ tathaiva,

kalyāṇamittapavarai matisampadañ‍ca

kau buddhimā anavaraṃ na bhajaiyya dhammaṃ?

100.

 aivampi dul‍labhataraṃ vibhavai suladdhā

macchairadausa viratā ubhayatthakāmā,

saddhādidhammasahitā satatappamattā

bhau! bhau! karautha amatādhigamāya puñ‍ñaṃ|
