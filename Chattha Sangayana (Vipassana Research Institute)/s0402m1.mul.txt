1. paṭhamapaṇṇāsakaṃ


1. kammakaraṇavaggau

1. vaj‍jasuttaṃ

1. aivaṃ  mai sutaṃ – aikaṃ samayaṃ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṃ viharati jaitavanai anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāmai| tatra khau bhagavā bhikkhū āmantaisi – ‘‘bhikkhavau’’ti| ‘‘bhadantai’’ti tai bhikkhū bhagavatau pac‍cassausuṃ| bhagavā aitadavauca –

‘‘dvaimāni, bhikkhavai, vaj‍jāni| katamāni dvai? diṭṭhadhammikañ‍ca vaj‍jaṃ samparāyikañ‍ca vaj‍jaṃ| katamañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, diṭṭhadhammikaṃ vaj‍jaṃ? idha, bhikkhavai, aikac‍cau passati cauraṃ āgucāriṃ rājānau gahaitvā vividhā kammakāraṇā vividhāni kammakaraṇāni (ka॰) kāraintai; kasāhipi tāḷaintai, vaittaihipi tāḷaintai, addhadaṇḍakaihipi tāḷaintai, hatthampi chindantai, pādampi chindantai, hatthapādampi chindantai, kaṇṇampi chindantai, nāsampi chindantai, kaṇṇanāsampi chindantai, bilaṅgathālikampi karauntai, saṅkhamuṇḍikampi karauntai, rāhumukhampi karauntai, jautimālikampi karauntai, hatthapaj‍jautikampi karauntai, airakavattikampi karauntai, cīrakavāsikampi  karauntai, aiṇaiyyakampi karauntai, baḷisamaṃsikampi karauntai, kahāpaṇikampi karauntai, khārāpatacchikampi  khārāpaṭicchakampi (syā॰ kaṃ॰ ka॰) karauntai, palighaparivattikampi karauntai, palālapīṭhakampi palālapiṭṭhikampi (sī॰) karauntai, tattainapi tailaina ausiñ‍cantai, sunakhaihipi khādāpaintai, jīvantampi sūlai uttāsaintai, asināpi sīsaṃ chindantai|

‘‘tassa aivaṃ  hauti – ‘yathārūpānaṃ khau pāpakānaṃ kammānaṃ haitu cauraṃ āgucāriṃ rājānau gahaitvā vividhā kammakāraṇā kārainti; kasāhipi tāḷainti, vaittaihipi tāḷainti, addhadaṇḍakaihipi tāḷainti, hatthampi chindanti, pādampi chindanti, hatthapādampi chindanti, kaṇṇampi chindanti, nāsampi chindanti, kaṇṇanāsampi chindanti, bilaṅgathālikampi karaunti, saṅkhamuṇḍikampi karaunti, rāhumukhampi karaunti, jautimālikampi karaunti, hatthapaj‍jautikampi karaunti, airakavattikampi karaunti, cīrakavāsikampi karaunti, aiṇaiyyakampi karaunti, baḷisamaṃsikampi karaunti, kahāpaṇikampi karaunti, khārāpatacchikampi karaunti, palighaparivattikampi karaunti, palālapīṭhakampi karaunti, tattainapi tailaina ausiñ‍canti, sunakhaihipi khādāpainti, jīvantampi sūlai uttāsainti, asināpi sīsaṃ chindanti| ahañ‍caiva ahañ‍cai (?) khau pana aivarūpaṃ pāpakammaṃ karaiyyaṃ, mampi rājānau gahaitvā aivarūpā vividhā kammakāraṇā kāraiyyuṃ; kasāhipi tāḷaiyyuṃ…pai॰… asināpi sīsaṃ chindaiyyu’nti| sau diṭṭhadhammikassa vaj‍jassa bhītau na paraisaṃ pābhataṃ vilumpantau carati| idaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, diṭṭhadhammikaṃ vaj‍jaṃ|

‘‘katamañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, samparāyikaṃ vaj‍jaṃ? idha, bhikkhavai, aikac‍cau iti paṭisañ‍cikkhati – ‘kāyaduc‍caritassa khau pana pāpakau dukkhau vipākau abhisamparāyaṃ, vacīduc‍caritassa pāpakau dukkhau vipākau abhisamparāyaṃ, manauduc‍caritassa pāpakau dukkhau vipākau abhisamparāyaṃ| ahañ‍caiva khau pana kāyaina duc‍caritaṃ caraiyyaṃ, vācāya duc‍caritaṃ caraiyyaṃ, manasā duc‍caritaṃ caraiyyaṃ| kiñ‍ca taṃ yāhaṃ na kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapaj‍jaiyya’nti| sau  samparāyikassa vaj‍jassa bhītau kāyaduc‍caritaṃ pahāya kāyasucaritaṃ bhāvaiti, vacīduc‍caritaṃ pahāya vacīsucaritaṃ bhāvaiti, manauduc‍caritaṃ pahāya manausucaritaṃ bhāvaiti, suddhaṃ attānaṃ pariharati| idaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, samparāyikaṃ vaj‍jaṃ| ‘‘imāni khau, bhikkhavai, dvai vaj‍jāni| tasmātiha, bhikkhavai, aivaṃ sikkhitabbaṃ – ‘diṭṭhadhammikassa vaj‍jassa bhāyissāma, samparāyikassa vaj‍jassa bhāyissāma, vaj‍jabhīrunau bhavissāma  vaj‍jabhayadassāvinau’ti| aivañhi vau, bhikkhavai, sikkhitabbaṃ| vaj‍jabhīrunau, bhikkhavai, vaj‍jabhayadassāvinau aitaṃ pāṭikaṅkhaṃ yaṃ parimuc‍cissati  sabbavaj‍jaihī’’ti| paṭhamaṃ|

2. padhānasuttaṃ

2. ‘‘dvaimāni, bhikkhavai, padhānāni durabhisambhavāni laukasmiṃ| katamāni dvai? yañ‍ca gihīnaṃ agāraṃ ajjhāvasataṃ cīvarapiṇḍapātasaināsanagilānapac‍cayabhaisaj‍japarikkhārānuppadānatthaṃ padhānaṃ, yañ‍ca agārasmā anagāriyaṃ pabbajitānaṃ sabbūpadhipaṭinissaggatthaṃ padhānaṃ| imāni khau, bhikkhavai, dvai padhānāni durabhisambhavāni laukasmiṃ|

‘‘aitadaggaṃ, bhikkhavai, imaisaṃ dvin‍naṃ padhānānaṃ yadidaṃ sabbūpadhipaṭinissaggatthaṃ padhānaṃ| tasmātiha, bhikkhavai, aivaṃ sikkhitabbaṃ – ‘sabbūpadhipaṭinissaggatthaṃ padhānaṃ padahissāmā’ti| aivañhi vau, bhikkhavai, sikkhitabba’’nti| dutiyaṃ|

3. tapanīyasuttaṃ

3. ‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, dhammā tapanīyā| katamai dvai? idha, bhikkhavai, aikac‍cassa kāyaduc‍caritaṃ kataṃ hauti, akataṃ hauti kāyasucaritaṃ; vacīduc‍caritaṃ kataṃ hauti; akataṃ hauti vacīsucaritaṃ; manauduc‍caritaṃ kataṃ hauti, akataṃ hauti manausucaritaṃ| sau ‘kāyaduc‍caritaṃ mai kata’nti tappati, ‘akataṃ mai kāyasucarita’nti tappati; ‘vacīduc‍caritaṃ mai kata’nti tappati, ‘akataṃ mai vacīsucarita’nti tappati; ‘manauduc‍caritaṃ mai kata’nti tappati, ‘akataṃ mai manausucarita’nti tappati| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai dhammā tapanīyā’’ti| tatiyaṃ|

4. atapanīyasuttaṃ

4. ‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, dhammā atapanīyā| katamai  dvai? idha, bhikkhavai, aikac‍cassa kāyasucaritaṃ kataṃ hauti, akataṃ hauti kāyaduc‍caritaṃ; vacīsucaritaṃ kataṃ hauti, akataṃ hauti vacīduc‍caritaṃ; manausucaritaṃ kataṃ hauti, akataṃ hauti manauduc‍caritaṃ| sau ‘kāyasucaritaṃ mai kata’nti na tappati, ‘akataṃ mai kāyaduc‍carita’nti na tappati; ‘vacīsucaritaṃ mai kata’nti na tappati, ‘akataṃ  mai vacīduc‍carita’nti na tappati; ‘manausucaritaṃ mai kata’nti na tappati, ‘akataṃ mai manauduc‍carita’nti na tappati| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai dhammā atapanīyā’’ti| catutthaṃ|

5. upañ‍ñātasuttaṃ

5. ‘‘dvin‍nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, dhammānaṃ upañ‍ñāsiṃ – yā ca asantuṭṭhitā kusalaisu dhammaisu, yā ca appaṭivānitā padhānasmiṃ| appaṭivānī sudāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, padahāmi – ‘kāmaṃ tacau ca nhāru nahāru (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) ca aṭṭhi ca avasissatu, sarīrai upasussatu maṃsalauhitaṃ, yaṃ taṃ purisathāmaina purisavīriyaina purisaparak‍kamaina pattabbaṃ na taṃ apāpuṇitvā vīriyassa saṇṭhānaṃ bhavissatī’ti| tassa mayhaṃ, bhikkhavai, appamādādhigatā sambaudhi, appamādādhigatau anuttarau yaugakkhaimau| tumhai caipi, bhikkhavai, appaṭivānaṃ padahaiyyātha – ‘kāmaṃ tacau ca nhāru ca aṭṭhi ca avasissatu, sarīrai upasussatu maṃsalauhitaṃ, yaṃ taṃ purisathāmaina purisavīriyaina  purisaparak‍kamaina pattabbaṃ na taṃ apāpuṇitvā vīriyassa saṇṭhānaṃ  bhavissatī’ti, tumhaipi, bhikkhavai, nacirassaiva – yassatthāya kulaputtā sammadaiva agārasmā anagāriyaṃ pabbajanti tadanuttaraṃ – brahmacariyapariyausānaṃ diṭṭhaiva dhammai sayaṃ abhiñ‍ñā sacchikatvā upasampaj‍ja viharissatha| tasmātiha, bhikkhavai, aivaṃ sikkhitabbaṃ – ‘appaṭivānaṃ padahissāma| kāmaṃ tacau ca nhāru ca aṭṭhi ca avasissatu, sarīrai upasussatu maṃsalauhitaṃ, yaṃ taṃ purisathāmaina purisavīriyaina purisaparak‍kamaina pattabbaṃ na taṃ apāpuṇitvā vīriyassa saṇṭhānaṃ bhavissatī’ti| aivañhi vau, bhikkhavai, sikkhitabba’’nti| pañ‍camaṃ|

6. saṃyaujanasuttaṃ

6. ‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, dhammā| katamai dvai? yā ca saṃyaujaniyaisu dhammaisu assādānupassitā, yā ca saṃyaujaniyaisu dhammaisu nibbidānupassitā| saṃyaujaniyaisu, bhikkhavai, dhammaisu assādānupassī viharantau rāgaṃ na  pajahati, dausaṃ na pajahati, mauhaṃ na pajahati| rāgaṃ appahāya, dausaṃ appahāya, mauhaṃ appahāya na parimuc‍cati jātiyā jarāya maraṇaina saukaihi paridaivaihi dukkhaihi daumanassaihi upāyāsaihi| na parimuc‍cati dukkhasmāti vadāmi|

‘‘saṃyaujaniyaisu, bhikkhavai, dhammaisu nibbidānupassī viharantau rāgaṃ pajahati, dausaṃ pajahati, mauhaṃ pajahati| rāgaṃ pahāya, dausaṃ pahāya, mauhaṃ  pahāya, parimuc‍cati jātiyā jarāya maraṇaina saukaihi paridaivaihi dukkhaihi daumanassaihi upāyāsaihi| parimuc‍cati dukkhasmāti vadāmi| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai dhammā’’ti| chaṭṭhaṃ|

7. kaṇhasuttaṃ

7. ‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, dhammā kaṇhā| katamai dvai? ahirikañ‍ca anauttappañ‍ca| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai dhammā kaṇhā’’ti| sattamaṃ|

8. suk‍kasuttaṃ

8. ‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, dhammā suk‍kā| katamai dvai? hirī hiri (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) ca auttappañ‍ca| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai dhammā suk‍kā’’ti| aṭṭhamaṃ|

9. cariyasuttaṃ

9. ‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, dhammā suk‍kā laukaṃ pālainti| katamai dvai? hirī ca auttappañ‍ca| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai suk‍kā dhammā laukaṃ na pālaiyyuṃ, nayidha pañ‍ñāyaitha mātāti vā mātucchāti  vā mātulānīti vā ācariyabhariyāti vā garūnaṃ dārāti vā| sambhaidaṃ laukau agamissa, yathā ajaiḷakā kuk‍kuṭasūkarā sauṇasiṅgālā sauṇasigālā (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰)| yasmā ca khau, bhikkhavai, imai dvai suk‍kā dhammā laukaṃ pālainti tasmā pañ‍ñāyati pañ‍ñāyanti (sī॰) mātāti vā mātucchāti vā mātulānīti vā ācariyabhariyāti vā garūnaṃ dārāti vā’’ti| navamaṃ|

10. vassūpanāyikasuttaṃ

10. ‘‘dvaimā, bhikkhavai, vassūpanāyikā| katamā dvai? purimikā ca pacchimikā ca| imā khau, bhikkhavai, dvai vassūpanāyikā’’ti| dasamaṃ|

saṃyaujanañ‍ca kaṇhañ‍ca, suk‍kaṃ cariyā vassūpanāyikaina vaggau||



2. adhikaraṇavaggau

11. ‘‘dvaimāni, bhikkhavai, balāni| katamāni dvai? paṭisaṅkhānabalañ‍ca bhāvanābalañ‍ca| katamañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, paṭisaṅkhānabalaṃ? idha, bhikkhavai, aikac‍cau iti paṭisañ‍cikkhati – ‘kāyaduc‍caritassa khau pāpakau vipākau diṭṭhai caiva dhammai abhisamparāyañ‍ca, vacīduc‍caritassa pāpakau vipākau diṭṭhai caiva dhammai abhisamparāyañ‍ca, manauduc‍caritassa pāpakau vipākau diṭṭhai caiva dhammai abhisamparāyañ‍cā’ti| sau iti paṭisaṅkhāya kāyaduc‍caritaṃ pahāya kāyasucaritaṃ bhāvaiti, vacīduc‍caritaṃ pahāya vacīsucaritaṃ bhāvaiti, manauduc‍caritaṃ pahāya manausucaritaṃ bhāvaiti, suddhaṃ attānaṃ pariharati| idaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, paṭisaṅkhānabalaṃ|

‘‘katamañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhāvanābalaṃ| tatra, bhikkhavai, yamidaṃ yadidaṃ (sī॰) bhāvanābalaṃ saikhānamaitaṃ saikhamaitaṃ (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰) balaṃ| saikhañhi sau, bhikkhavai, balaṃ āgamma rāgaṃ pajahati, dausaṃ pajahati, mauhaṃ pajahati| rāgaṃ pahāya, dausaṃ pahāya, mauhaṃ pahāya yaṃ akusalaṃ na taṃ karauti, yaṃ pāpaṃ  na taṃ saivati| idaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, bhāvanābalaṃ| imāni khau, bhikkhavai, dvai balānī’’ti|

12. ‘‘dvaimāni, bhikkhavai, balāni| katamāni dvai? paṭisaṅkhānabalañ‍ca bhāvanābalañ‍ca| katamañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, paṭisaṅkhānabalaṃ? idha, bhikkhavai, aikac‍cau iti paṭisañ‍cikkhati – ‘kāyaduc‍caritassa khau pāpakau vipākau diṭṭhai caiva dhammai abhisamparāyañ‍ca, vacīduc‍caritassa pāpakau vipākau diṭṭhai caiva dhammai abhisamparāyañ‍ca, manauduc‍caritassa pāpakau vipākau diṭṭhai caiva dhammai abhisamparāyañ‍cā’ti| sau iti paṭisaṅkhāya kāyaduc‍caritaṃ pahāya kāyasucaritaṃ bhāvaiti, vacīduc‍caritaṃ pahāya vacīsucaritaṃ bhāvaiti, manauduc‍caritaṃ pahāya manausucaritaṃ bhāvaiti, suddhaṃ attānaṃ pariharati| idaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, paṭisaṅkhānabalaṃ|

‘‘katamañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhāvanābalaṃ? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu  satisambaujjhaṅgaṃ bhāvaiti vivaikanissitaṃ  virāganissitaṃ niraudhanissitaṃ vausaggapariṇāmiṃ, dhammavicayasambaujjhaṅgaṃ bhāvaiti… vīriyasambaujjhaṅgaṃ bhāvaiti… pītisambaujjhaṅgaṃ bhāvaiti… passaddhisambaujjhaṅgaṃ bhāvaiti… samādhisambaujjhaṅgaṃ bhāvaiti… upaikkhāsambaujjhaṅgaṃ bhāvaiti vivaikanissitaṃ virāganissitaṃ niraudhanissitaṃ vausaggapariṇāmiṃ| idaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, bhāvanābalaṃ| imāni khau, bhikkhavai, dvai balānī’’ti|

13. ‘‘dvaimāni, bhikkhavai, balāni| katamāni dvai? paṭisaṅkhānabalañ‍ca bhāvanābalañ‍ca| katamañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, paṭisaṅkhānabalaṃ? idha, bhikkhavai, aikac‍cau iti paṭisañ‍cikkhati – ‘kāyaduc‍caritassa khau pāpakau vipākau diṭṭhai caiva dhammai abhisamparāyañ‍ca, vacīduc‍caritassa khau pāpakau vipākau diṭṭhai caiva dhammai abhisamparāyañ‍ca, manauduc‍caritassa khau pāpakau vipākau diṭṭhai caiva dhammai abhisamparāyañ‍cā’ti| sau iti paṭisaṅkhāya kāyaduc‍caritaṃ pahāya kāyasucaritaṃ bhāvaiti, vacīduc‍caritaṃ pahāya vacīsucaritaṃ bhāvaiti, manauduc‍caritaṃ pahāya manausucaritaṃ bhāvaiti, suddhaṃ attānaṃ pariharati| idaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, paṭisaṅkhānabalaṃ|

‘‘katamañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, bhāvanābalaṃ? idha, bhikkhavai, bhikkhu vivic‍caiva kāmaihi, vivic‍ca akusalaihi dhammaihi savitak‍kaṃ savicāraṃ vivaikajaṃ pītisukhaṃ paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ upasampaj‍ja viharati| vitak‍kavicārānaṃ vūpasamā ajjhattaṃ sampasādanaṃ caitasau aikaudibhāvaṃ avitak‍kaṃ avicāraṃ samādhijaṃ pītisukhaṃ dutiyaṃ jhānaṃ upasampaj‍ja viharati| pītiyā ca virāgā upaikkhakau ca viharati satau ca sampajānau, sukhañ‍ca kāyaina paṭisaṃvaidaiti, yaṃ taṃ ariyā ācikkhanti – ‘upaikkhakau satimā sukhavihārī’ti tatiyaṃ jhānaṃ upasampaj‍ja viharati| sukhassa ca pahānā dukkhassa ca pahānā pubbaiva saumanassadaumanassānaṃ atthaṅgamā adukkhamasukhaṃ upaikkhāsatipārisuddhiṃ catutthaṃ jhānaṃ upasampaj‍ja viharati| idaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, bhāvanābalaṃ| imāni khau, bhikkhavai, dvai balānī’’ti|

14. ‘‘dvaimā, bhikkhavai, tathāgatassa dhammadaisanā| katamā dvai? saṃkhittaina ca vitthāraina ca| imā khau, bhikkhavai, dvai tathāgatassa dhammadaisanā’’ti|

15. ‘‘yasmiṃ, bhikkhavai, adhikaraṇai āpan‍nau āpattāpan‍nau (ka॰) ca bhikkhu caudakau ca bhikkhu na sādhukaṃ attanāva attānaṃ pac‍cavaikkhati tasmaitaṃ, bhikkhavai, adhikaraṇai pāṭikaṅkhaṃ dīghattāya  kharattāya vāḷattāya saṃvattissati, bhikkhū  ca na phāsuṃ na phāsu (ka॰) viharissantīti viharissanti (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ ka॰)| yasmiñ‍ca khau, bhikkhavai, adhikaraṇai āpan‍nau ca bhikkhu caudakau ca bhikkhu sādhukaṃ attanāva attānaṃ pac‍cavaikkhati tasmaitaṃ, bhikkhavai, adhikaraṇai pāṭikaṅkhaṃ na dīghattāya kharattāya vāḷattāya saṃvattissati, bhikkhū ca phāsuṃ viharissantīti|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, āpan‍nau bhikkhu sādhukaṃ attanāva attānaṃ pac‍cavaikkhati? idha, bhikkhavai, āpan‍nau bhikkhu iti paṭisañ‍cikkhati – ‘ahaṃ khau akusalaṃ āpan‍nau kañ‍cidaiva kiñ‍cidaiva (ka॰) daisaṃ kāyaina| maṃ sau tasmā maṃ sau (sī॰ syā॰) bhikkhu addasa akusalaṃ āpaj‍jamānaṃ kiñ‍cidaiva daisaṃ kāyaina| nau cai ahaṃ akusalaṃ āpaj‍jaiyyaṃ kiñ‍cidaiva daisaṃ kāyaina, na maṃ sau bhikkhu passaiyya akusalaṃ āpaj‍jamānaṃ kiñ‍cidaiva daisaṃ kāyaina| yasmā ca khau, ahaṃ akusalaṃ āpan‍nau kiñ‍cidaiva daisaṃ kāyaina, tasmā maṃ sau bhikkhu addasa akusalaṃ āpaj‍jamānaṃ kiñ‍cidaiva daisaṃ kāyaina| disvā ca pana maṃ sau bhikkhu akusalaṃ āpaj‍jamānaṃ kiñ‍cidaiva daisaṃ kāyaina anattamanau ahausi| anattamanau samānau anattamanavacanaṃ anattamanavācaṃ (ka॰) maṃ sau bhikkhu avaca| anattamanavacanāhaṃ anattamanavācaṃ nāhaṃ (ka॰) taina bhikkhunā vuttau samānau anattamanau attamanau (ka॰) ahausiṃ| anattamanau samānau paraisaṃ āraucaisiṃ| iti mamaiva tattha ac‍cayau ac‍cagamā  suṅkadāyakaṃva bhaṇḍasminti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, āpan‍nau bhikkhu sādhukaṃ attanāva attānaṃ pac‍cavaikkhati|

‘‘kathañ‍ca, bhikkhavai, caudakau bhikkhu sādhukaṃ attanāva attānaṃ pac‍cavaikkhati? idha, bhikkhavai, caudakau bhikkhu iti paṭisañ‍cikkhati – ‘ayaṃ khau bhikkhu akusalaṃ āpan‍nau kiñ‍cidaiva daisaṃ kāyaina| ahaṃ imaṃ bhikkhuṃ addasaṃ akusalaṃ āpaj‍jamānaṃ kiñ‍cidaiva daisaṃ kāyaina| nau cai ayaṃ bhikkhu akusalaṃ āpaj‍jaiyya kiñ‍cidaiva daisaṃ kāyaina, nāhaṃ imaṃ bhikkhuṃ passaiyyaṃ akusalaṃ āpaj‍jamānaṃ kiñ‍cidaiva daisaṃ  kāyaina| yasmā ca khau, ayaṃ bhikkhu akusalaṃ āpan‍nau kiñ‍cidaiva daisaṃ kāyaina, tasmā ahaṃ imaṃ bhikkhuṃ addasaṃ akusalaṃ āpaj‍jamānaṃ kiñ‍cidaiva daisaṃ kāyaina| disvā ca panāhaṃ imaṃ bhikkhuṃ akusalaṃ āpaj‍jamānaṃ kiñ‍cidaiva daisaṃ kāyaina anattamanau ahausiṃ| anattamanau  samānau anattamanavacanāhaṃ imaṃ bhikkhuṃ avacaṃ| anattamanavacanāyaṃ bhikkhu mayā vuttau samānau anattamanau ahausi| anattamanau  samānau paraisaṃ āraucaisi| iti mamaiva tattha ac‍cayau ac‍cagamā suṅkadāyakaṃva bhaṇḍasminti| aivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, caudakau bhikkhu sādhukaṃ attanāva attānaṃ pac‍cavaikkhati|

‘‘yasmiṃ, bhikkhavai, adhikaraṇai āpan‍nau ca bhikkhu caudakau ca bhikkhu na sādhukaṃ attanāva attānaṃ pac‍cavaikkhati tasmaitaṃ, bhikkhavai, adhikaraṇai pāṭikaṅkhaṃ dīghattāya kharattāya vāḷattāya saṃvattissati, bhikkhū ca na phāsuṃ viharissantīti| yasmiñ‍ca khau, bhikkhavai, adhikaraṇai āpan‍nau  ca bhikkhu caudakau ca bhikkhu sādhukaṃ attanāva attānaṃ pac‍cavaikkhati tasmaitaṃ, bhikkhavai, adhikaraṇai pāṭikaṅkhaṃ na dīghattāya kharattāya vāḷattāya saṃvattissati, bhikkhū ca phāsu viharissantī’’ti|

16. atha khau añ‍ñatarau brāhmaṇau yaina bhagavā tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavatā saddhiṃ sammaudi| sammaudanīyaṃ kathaṃ sāraṇīyaṃ vītisāraitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdi| aikamantaṃ nisin‍nau khau sau brāhmaṇau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca – ‘‘kau nu khau, bhau gautama, haitu kau pac‍cayau yaina midhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapaj‍jantī’’ti? ‘‘adhammacariyāvisamacariyāhaitu khau, brāhmaṇa, aivamidhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapaj‍jantī’’ti|

‘‘kau nu khau, bhau gautama, haitu kau pac‍cayau yaina midhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā sugatiṃ saggaṃ laukaṃ upapaj‍jantī’’ti? ‘‘dhammacariyāsamacariyāhaitu khau, brāhmaṇa, aivamidhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā sugatiṃ saggaṃ laukaṃ upapaj‍jantī’’ti|

‘‘abhik‍kantaṃ, bhau gautama! abhik‍kantaṃ, bhau gautama! saiyyathāpi, bhau gautama, nik‍kuj‍jitaṃ nikuj‍jitaṃ (ka॰) vā uk‍kuj‍jaiyya, paṭicchan‍naṃ vā vivaraiyya, mūḷhassa vā maggaṃ ācikkhaiyya, andhakārai vā tailapaj‍jautaṃ dhāraiyya – ‘cakkhumantau rūpāni dakkhantī’ti, aivamaivaṃ bhautā gautamaina anaikapariyāyaina dhammau pakāsitau| aisāhaṃ bhavantaṃ gautamaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi dhammañ‍ca bhikkhusaṅghañ‍ca| upāsakaṃ  maṃ bhavaṃ gautamau dhāraitu aj‍jataggai pāṇupaitaṃ saraṇaṃ gata’’nti|

17. atha khau jāṇussauṇi jāṇusauṇi (ka॰) brāhmaṇau yaina bhagavā tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavatā saddhiṃ  sammaudi| sammaudanīyaṃ kathaṃ sāraṇīyaṃ vītisāraitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdi| aikamantaṃ nisin‍nau khau jāṇussauṇi brāhmaṇau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca – ‘‘kau nu khau, bhau gautama, haitu kau pac‍cayau yaina midhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapaj‍jantī’’ti? ‘‘katattā ca, brāhmaṇa, akatattā ca| aivamidhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapaj‍jantī’’ti| ‘‘kau pana, bhau gautama, haitu kau pac‍cayau yaina midhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā sugatiṃ saggaṃ laukaṃ upapaj‍jantī’’ti? ‘‘katattā ca, brāhmaṇa, akatattā ca| aivamidhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā sugatiṃ saggaṃ laukaṃ upapaj‍jantī’’ti| ‘‘na khau ahaṃ imassa bhautau gautamassa saṃkhittaina bhāsitassa vitthāraina atthaṃ avibhattassa vitthāraina atthaṃ ājānāmi| sādhu mai bhavaṃ gautamau tathā dhammaṃ daisaitu yathā ahaṃ imassa bhautau gautamassa saṃkhittaina bhāsitassa vitthāraina atthaṃ avibhattassa vitthāraina atthaṃ ājānaiyya’’nti| ‘‘taina hi, brāhmaṇa, suṇāhi, sādhukaṃ manasi karauhi; bhāsissāmī’’ti| ‘‘aivaṃ  bhau’’ti khau jāṇussauṇi brāhmaṇau bhagavatau pac‍cassausi| bhagavā aitadavauca –

‘‘idha, brāhmaṇa, aikac‍cassa kāyaduc‍caritaṃ kataṃ hauti, akataṃ hauti kāyasucaritaṃ; vacīduc‍caritaṃ kataṃ hauti, akataṃ hauti vacīsucaritaṃ; manauduc‍caritaṃ kataṃ hauti, akataṃ hauti manausucaritaṃ| aivaṃ khau, brāhmaṇa, katattā ca akatattā ca aivamidhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapaj‍janti| idha pana, brāhmaṇa, aikac‍cassa kāyasucaritaṃ kataṃ hauti, akataṃ hauti kāyaduc‍caritaṃ; vacīsucaritaṃ kataṃ  hauti, akataṃ hauti vacīduc‍caritaṃ; manausucaritaṃ kataṃ hauti, akataṃ hauti manauduc‍caritaṃ| aivaṃ khau, brāhmaṇa, katattā ca akatattā ca aivamidhaikac‍cai sattā kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā sugatiṃ saggaṃ laukaṃ upapaj‍jantī’’ti|

‘‘abhik‍kantaṃ, bhau gautama…pai॰… upāsakaṃ maṃ bhavaṃ gautamau dhāraitu aj‍jataggai pāṇupaitaṃ saraṇaṃ gata’’nti|

18. atha khau āyasmā ānandau yaina bhagavā tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdi| aikamantaṃ nisin‍naṃ khau āyasmantaṃ ānandaṃ bhagavā aitadavauca  – ‘‘aikaṃsaināhaṃ, ānanda, akaraṇīyaṃ vadāmi kāyaduc‍caritaṃ vacīduc‍caritaṃ manauduc‍carita’’nti| ‘‘yamidaṃ, bhantai, bhagavatā aikaṃsaina akaraṇīyaṃ akkhātaṃ kāyaduc‍caritaṃ vacīduc‍caritaṃ manauduc‍caritaṃ tasmiṃ akaraṇīyai kayiramānai kau ādīnavau pāṭikaṅkhau’’ti? ‘‘yamidaṃ, ānanda, mayā aikaṃsaina akaraṇīyaṃ akkhātaṃ kāyaduc‍caritaṃ vacīduc‍caritaṃ manauduc‍caritaṃ tasmiṃ akaraṇīyai kayiramānai ayaṃ ādīnavau pāṭikaṅkhau – attāpi attānaṃ upavadati, anuvic‍ca viñ‍ñū garahanti, pāpakau kittisaddau abbhuggacchati, sammūḷhau kālaṃ karauti, kāyassa bhaidā  paraṃ maraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapaj‍jati| yamidaṃ, ānanda, mayā aikaṃsaina akaraṇīyaṃ akkhātaṃ kāyaduc‍caritaṃ vacīduc‍caritaṃ manauduc‍caritaṃ tasmiṃ akaraṇīyai kayiramānai ayaṃ ādīnavau pāṭikaṅkhau’’ti|

‘‘aikaṃsaināhaṃ, ānanda, karaṇīyaṃ  vadāmi kāyasucaritaṃ vacīsucaritaṃ manausucarita’’nti| ‘‘yamidaṃ, bhantai, bhagavatā aikaṃsaina karaṇīyaṃ akkhātaṃ kāyasucaritaṃ vacīsucaritaṃ manausucaritaṃ tasmiṃ karaṇīyai kayiramānai kau ānisaṃsau pāṭikaṅkhau’’ti? ‘‘yamidaṃ, ānanda, mayā aikaṃsaina karaṇīyaṃ akkhātaṃ kāyasucaritaṃ vacīsucaritaṃ manausucaritaṃ tasmiṃ karaṇīyai kayiramānai ayaṃ ānisaṃsau pāṭikaṅkhau – attāpi attānaṃ na upavadati, anuvic‍ca viñ‍ñū pasaṃsanti, kalyāṇau kittisaddau abbhuggacchati, asammūḷhau kālaṃ karauti, kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā sugatiṃ saggaṃ laukaṃ upapaj‍jati| yamidaṃ, ānanda, mayā aikaṃsaina karaṇīyaṃ akkhātaṃ kāyasucaritaṃ vacīsucaritaṃ manausucaritaṃ tasmiṃ karaṇīyai kayiramānai ayaṃ ānisaṃsau pāṭikaṅkhau’’ti|

19. ‘‘akusalaṃ, bhikkhavai, pajahatha| sak‍kā, bhikkhavai, akusalaṃ pajahituṃ| nau caidaṃ nau caitaṃ (syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰ ka॰) saṃ॰ ni॰ 3.28 passitabbaṃ, bhikkhavai, sak‍kā abhavissa akusalaṃ pajahituṃ, nāhaṃ aivaṃ vadaiyyaṃ – ‘akusalaṃ, bhikkhavai, pajahathā’ti| yasmā ca khau, bhikkhavai, sak‍kā  akusalaṃ pajahituṃ tasmāhaṃ aivaṃ vadāmi – ‘akusalaṃ, bhikkhavai, pajahathā’ti| akusalañ‍ca hidaṃ, bhikkhavai akusalaṃ bhikkhavai (ka॰), pahīnaṃ ahitāya dukkhāya saṃvattaiyya nāhaṃ aivaṃ vadaiyyaṃ – ‘akusalaṃ, bhikkhavai, pajahathā’ti| yasmā ca khau, bhikkhavai, akusalaṃ pahīnaṃ hitāya sukhāya saṃvattati tasmāhaṃ aivaṃ vadāmi – ‘akusalaṃ, bhikkhavai, pajahathā’’’ti|

‘‘kusalaṃ, bhikkhavai, bhāvaitha| sak‍kā, bhikkhavai, kusalaṃ bhāvaituṃ| nau caidaṃ, bhikkhavai, sak‍kā abhavissa kusalaṃ bhāvaituṃ, nāhaṃ aivaṃ vadaiyyaṃ – ‘kusalaṃ, bhikkhavai, bhāvaithā’ti| yasmā ca khau, bhikkhavai, sak‍kā kusalaṃ bhāvaituṃ tasmāhaṃ aivaṃ vadāmi – ‘kusalaṃ, bhikkhavai, bhāvaithā’ti| kusalañ‍ca hidaṃ, bhikkhavai, bhāvitaṃ ahitāya dukkhāya saṃvattaiyya, nāhaṃ aivaṃ vadaiyyaṃ – ‘kusalaṃ, bhikkhavai, bhāvaithā’ti| yasmā ca khau, bhikkhavai, kusalaṃ bhāvitaṃ hitāya sukhāya saṃvattati tasmāhaṃ aivaṃ vadāmi – ‘kusalaṃ, bhikkhavai, bhāvaithā’’’ti|

20. ‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, dhammā saddhammassa sammausāya antaradhānāya saṃvattanti| katamai dvai? dun‍nikkhittañ‍ca padabyañ‍janaṃ atthau ca dun‍nītau| dun‍nikkhittassa, bhikkhavai, padabyañ‍janassa atthaupi dun‍nayau hauti| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai dhammā saddhammassa sammausāya antaradhānāya saṃvattantī’’ti|

21. ‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, dhammā saddhammassa ṭhitiyā asammausāya anantaradhānāya saṃvattanti| katamai dvai? sunikkhittañ‍ca padabyañ‍janaṃ atthau ca sunītau| sunikkhittassa, bhikkhavai, padabyañ‍janassa atthaupi sunayau hauti| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai dhammā saddhammassa ṭhitiyā asammausāya anantaradhānāya saṃvattantī’’ti|

adhikaraṇavaggau dutiyau|



3. bālavaggau

22. ‘‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, bālā| katamai dvai? yau ca ac‍cayaṃ ac‍cayatau na passati, yau ca ac‍cayaṃ daisaintassa yathādhammaṃ nappaṭiggaṇhāti| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai bālā’ti| ‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, paṇḍitā| katamai dvai? yau ca ac‍cayaṃ ac‍cayatau passati, yau ca ac‍cayaṃ daisaintassa yathādhammaṃ paṭiggaṇhāti| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai paṇḍitā’’’ti|

23. ‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, tathāgataṃ abbhācikkhanti| katamai dvai? duṭṭhau vā dausantarau, saddhau vā duggahitaina duggahītaina (sī॰)| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai tathāgataṃ abbhācikkhantī’’ti|

24. ‘‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, tathāgataṃ abbhācikkhanti| katamai dvai? yau  ca abhāsitaṃ alapitaṃ tathāgataina bhāsitaṃ lapitaṃ tathāgataināti dīpaiti, yau ca bhāsitaṃ lapitaṃ tathāgataina abhāsitaṃ alapitaṃ tathāgataināti dīpaiti| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai tathāgataṃ abbhācikkhantī’ti| ‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, tathāgataṃ nābbhācikkhanti| katamai dvai? yau ca abhāsitaṃ alapitaṃ tathāgataina abhāsitaṃ alapitaṃ tathāgataināti dīpaiti, yau ca bhāsitaṃ lapitaṃ tathāgataina bhāsitaṃ lapitaṃ tathāgataināti dīpaiti| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai tathāgataṃ nābbhācikkhantī’’’ti|

25. ‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, tathāgataṃ abbhācikkhanti| katamai dvai? yau ca naiyyatthaṃ suttantaṃ nītatthau suttantauti dīpaiti, yau ca nītatthaṃ suttantaṃ naiyyatthau suttantauti dīpaiti| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai tathāgataṃ abbhācikkhantī’’ti|

26. ‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, tathāgataṃ nābbhācikkhanti| katamai dvai? yau ca naiyyatthaṃ suttantaṃ naiyyatthau suttantauti dīpaiti, yau ca nītatthaṃ suttantaṃ nītatthau suttantauti dīpaiti| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai tathāgataṃ nābbhācikkhantī’’ti|

27. ‘‘paṭicchan‍nakammantassa, bhikkhavai, dvin‍naṃ gatīnaṃ añ‍ñatarā gati pāṭikaṅkhā – nirayau vā tiracchānayauni vāti| appaṭicchan‍nakammantassa, bhikkhavai, dvin‍naṃ gatīnaṃ añ‍ñatarā gati pāṭikaṅkhā – daivā vā manussā vā’’ti|

28. ‘‘micchādiṭṭhikassa, bhikkhavai, dvin‍naṃ gatīnaṃ añ‍ñatarā gati pāṭikaṅkhā – nirayau vā tiracchānayauni vā’’ti|

29. ‘‘sammādiṭṭhikassa, bhikkhavai, dvin‍naṃ gatīnaṃ añ‍ñatarā gati pāṭikaṅkhā – daivā vā manussā vā’’ti|

30. ‘‘dussīlassa, bhikkhavai, dvai paṭiggāhā – nirayau vā tiracchānayauni vā| sīlavatau, bhikkhavai, dvai paṭiggāhā – daivā vā manussā vā’’ti daivau vā manussau vāti (ka॰)|

31. ‘‘dvāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, atthavasai sampassamānau arañ‍ñavanapatthāni arañ‍ñai pavanapatthāni (sī॰ pī॰) pantāni saināsanāni paṭisaivāmi| katamai dvai? attanau ca diṭṭhadhammasukhavihāraṃ sampassamānau, pacchimañ‍ca  janataṃ anukampamānau| imai khau ahaṃ, bhikkhavai, dvai atthavasai sampassamānau arañ‍ñavanapatthāni pantāni saināsanāni paṭisaivāmī’’ti|

32. ‘‘dvai mai, bhikkhavai, dhammā vij‍jābhāgiyā| katamai dvai? samathau ca vipassanā ca| samathau, bhikkhavai, bhāvitau kamattha kimattha (syā॰ kaṃ॰), katamattha (ka॰) manubhauti? cittaṃ  bhāvīyati| cittaṃ bhāvitaṃ kamatthamanubhauti? yau rāgau sau pahīyati| vipassanā, bhikkhavai, bhāvitā kamatthamanubhauti? pañ‍ñā bhāvīyati| pañ‍ñā bhāvitā kamatthamanubhauti? yā avij‍jā sā pahīyati| rāgupak‍kiliṭṭhaṃ vā, bhikkhavai, cittaṃ na vimuc‍cati, avij‍jupak‍kiliṭṭhā vā pañ‍ñā na bhāvīyati| iti khau, bhikkhavai, rāgavirāgā caitauvimutti, avij‍jāvirāgā pañ‍ñāvimuttī’’ti|

bālavaggau tatiyau|



4. samacittavaggau

33. ‘‘asappurisabhūmiñ‍ca  vau, bhikkhavai, daisaissāmi sappurisabhūmiñ‍ca| taṃ suṇātha, sādhukaṃ manasi karautha| bhāsissāmī’’ti| ‘‘aivaṃ, bhantai’’ti khau tai bhikkhū bhagavatau pac‍cassausuṃ| bhagavā aitadavauca –

‘‘katamā  ca, bhikkhavai, asappurisabhūmi? asappurisau, bhikkhavai, akatañ‍ñū hauti akatavaidī| asabbhi haitaṃ, bhikkhavai, upañ‍ñātaṃ yadidaṃ akatañ‍ñutā akatavaiditā| kaivalā aisā, bhikkhavai, asappurisabhūmi yadidaṃ akatañ‍ñutā akatavaiditā| sappurisau ca khau, bhikkhavai, katañ‍ñū hauti katavaidī| sabbhi haitaṃ, bhikkhavai, upañ‍ñātaṃ yadidaṃ katañ‍ñutā katavaiditā| kaivalā aisā, bhikkhavai, sappurisabhūmi yadidaṃ katañ‍ñutā katavaiditā’’ti|

34. ‘‘dvin‍nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, na suppatikāraṃ vadāmi| katamaisaṃ dvin‍naṃ? mātu ca pitu ca| aikaina, bhikkhavai, aṃsaina mātaraṃ pariharaiyya, aikaina aṃsaina pitaraṃ pariharaiyya vassasatāyukau vassasatajīvī sau ca naisaṃ ucchādanaparimaddananhāpanasambāhanaina| tai ca tatthaiva  muttakarīsaṃ cajaiyyuṃ| na tvaiva, bhikkhavai, mātāpitūnaṃ kataṃ vā hauti paṭikataṃ vā| imissā ca, bhikkhavai, mahāpathaviyā pahūtarattaratanāya pahūtasattaratanāya (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) tikanipātai mahāvaggai dasamasuttaṭīkāyaṃ dassitapāḷiyā samaiti mātāpitarau issarādhipac‍cai raj‍jai patiṭṭhāpaiyya, na tvaiva, bhikkhavai, mātāpitūnaṃ kataṃ vā hauti paṭikataṃ vā| taṃ kissa haitu? bahukārā bahūpakārā (ka॰), bhikkhavai, mātāpitarau puttānaṃ āpādakā pausakā imassa laukassa dassaitārau| yau ca khau, bhikkhavai, mātāpitarau assaddhai saddhāsampadāya samādapaiti nivaisaiti patiṭṭhāpaiti, dussīlai sīlasampadāya samādapaiti nivaisaiti patiṭṭhāpaiti, maccharī cāgasampadāya samādapaiti nivaisaiti patiṭṭhāpaiti, duppañ‍ñai pañ‍ñāsampadāya samādapaiti nivaisaiti patiṭṭhāpaiti, aittāvatā khau, bhikkhavai, mātāpitūnaṃ katañ‍ca hauti paṭikatañ‍cā’’ti paṭikatañ‍ca atikatañ‍cāti (sī॰ pī॰)|

35. atha khau añ‍ñatarau brāhmaṇau yaina bhagavā tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavatā saddhiṃ sammaudi| sammaudanīyaṃ kathaṃ…pai॰… aikamantaṃ nisin‍nau khau sau brāhmaṇau bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca  – ‘‘kiṃvādī bhavaṃ gautamau kimakkhāyī’’ti? ‘‘kiriyavādī cāhaṃ, brāhmaṇa, akiriyavādī cā’’ti| ‘‘yathākathaṃ pana bhavaṃ gautamau kiriyavādī ca akiriyavādī cā’’ti?

‘‘akiriyaṃ khau ahaṃ, brāhmaṇa, vadāmi kāyaduc‍caritassa vacīduc‍caritassa manauduc‍caritassa, anaikavihitānaṃ pāpakānaṃ akusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ akiriyaṃ  vadāmi| kiriyañ‍ca khau ahaṃ, brāhmaṇa, vadāmi kāyasucaritassa vacīsucaritassa manausucaritassa, anaikavihitānaṃ kusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ kiriyaṃ vadāmi| aivaṃ khau ahaṃ, brāhmaṇa, kiriyavādī  ca akiriyavādī cā’’ti|

‘‘abhik‍kantaṃ, bhau gautama…pai॰… upāsakaṃ maṃ bhavaṃ gautamau dhāraitu aj‍jataggai pāṇupaitaṃ saraṇaṃ gata’’nti|

36. atha khau anāthapiṇḍikau gahapati yaina bhagavā tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdi| aikamantaṃ nisin‍nau khau anāthapiṇḍikau gahapati bhagavantaṃ aitadavauca – ‘‘kati nu khau, bhantai, laukai dakkhiṇaiyyā, kattha ca dānaṃ dātabba’’nti? ‘‘dvai khau, gahapati, laukai dakkhiṇaiyyā – saikhau ca asaikhau ca| imai khau, gahapati, dvai laukai dakkhiṇaiyyā, aittha ca dānaṃ dātabba’’nti|

idamavauca bhagavā| idaṃ vatvāna vatvā (sī॰ pī॰) aivamīdisaisu ṭhānaisu sugatau athāparaṃ aitadavauca satthā –

‘‘saikhau asaikhau ca imasmiṃ laukai,

āhunaiyyā yajamānānaṃ haunti|

tai uj‍jubhūtā ujubhūtā (syā॰ kaṃ॰ ka॰) kāyaina, vācāya uda caitasā|

khaittaṃ taṃ yajamānānaṃ, aittha din‍naṃ mahapphala’’nti||

37. aivaṃ mai sutaṃ – aikaṃ samayaṃ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṃ viharati jaitavanai anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāmai| taina khau pana samayaina āyasmā sāriputtau sāvatthiyaṃ viharati pubbārāmai  migāramātupāsādai| tatra khau āyasmā sāriputtau bhikkhū āmantaisi – ‘‘āvusau bhikkhavai’’ti| ‘‘āvusau’’ti khau tai bhikkhū āyasmatau sāriputtassa pac‍cassausuṃ| āyasmā sāriputtau aitadavauca – ‘‘ajjhattasaṃyaujanañ‍ca, āvusau, puggalaṃ daisaissāmi bahiddhāsaṃyaujanañ‍ca| taṃ suṇātha, sādhukaṃ manasi karautha; bhāsissāmī’’ti| ‘‘aivamāvusau’’ti  khau tai bhikkhū āyasmatau sāriputtassa pac‍cassausuṃ| āyasmā sāriputtau aitadavauca –

‘‘katamau cāvusau, ajjhattasaṃyaujanau puggalau? idhāvusau, bhikkhu sīlavā hauti, pātimaukkhasaṃvarasaṃvutau viharati ācāragaucarasampan‍nau, aṇumattaisu vaj‍jaisu bhayadassāvī, samādāya sikkhati sikkhāpadaisu| sau kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā añ‍ñataraṃ daivanikāyaṃ upapaj‍jati| sau tatau cutau āgāmī hauti, āgantā itthattaṃ| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, āvusau, ajjhattasaṃyaujanau puggalau āgāmī hauti, āgantā itthattaṃ|

‘‘katamau  cāvusau, bahiddhāsaṃyaujanau puggalau? idhāvusau, bhikkhu sīlavā hauti, pātimaukkhasaṃvarasaṃvutau viharati ācāragaucarasampan‍nau, aṇumattaisu vaj‍jaisu bhayadassāvī, samādāya sikkhati sikkhāpadaisu| sau añ‍ñataraṃ santaṃ caitauvimuttiṃ upasampaj‍ja viharati| sau kāyassa bhaidā paraṃ maraṇā añ‍ñataraṃ daivanikāyaṃ upapaj‍jati| sau tatau cutau anāgāmī hauti, anāgantā itthattaṃ| ayaṃ vuc‍catāvusau, bahiddhāsaṃyaujanau puggalau anāgāmī hauti, anāgantā itthattaṃ|

‘‘puna caparaṃ, āvusau, bhikkhu sīlavā hauti…pai॰… samādāya sikkhati sikkhāpadaisu| sau kāmānaṃyaiva nibbidāya virāgāya niraudhāya paṭipan‍nau hauti| sau bhavānaṃyaiva nibbidāya virāgāya niraudhāya paṭipan‍nau hauti| sau taṇhākkhayāya paṭipan‍nau hauti| sau laubhakkhayāya paṭipan‍nau hauti| sau kāyassa bhaidā  paraṃ maraṇā añ‍ñataraṃ daivanikāyaṃ upapaj‍jati| sau tatau cutau anāgāmī hauti, anāgantā itthattaṃ| ayaṃ vuc‍catāvusau, bahiddhāsaṃyaujanau puggalau anāgāmī hauti, anāgantā itthatta’’nti|

atha khau sambahulā samacittā daivatā yaina bhagavā tainupasaṅkamiṃsu; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ aṭṭhaṃsu| aikamantaṃ ṭhitā khau tā daivatā bhagavantaṃ aitadavaucuṃ  – ‘‘aisau, bhantai, āyasmā sāriputtau pubbārāmai migāramātupāsādai bhikkhūnaṃ ajjhattasaṃyaujanañ‍ca puggalaṃ daisaiti bahiddhāsaṃyaujanañ‍ca| haṭṭhā, bhantai, parisā| sādhu, bhantai, bhagavā yaināyasmā sāriputtau tainupasaṅkamatu anukampaṃ upādāyā’’ti| adhivāsaisi bhagavā tuṇhībhāvaina| atha khau bhagavā – saiyyathāpi nāma balavā purisau samiñ‍jitaṃ sammiñ‍jitaṃ (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) vā bāhaṃ pasāraiyya, pasāritaṃ vā bāhaṃ samiñ‍jaiyya, aivamaivaṃ – jaitavanai antarahitau pubbārāmai migāramātupāsādai āyasmatau sāriputtassa sammukhai pāturahausi| nisīdi bhagavā pañ‍ñattai āsanai| āyasmāpi khau sāriputtau bhagavantaṃ  abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdi| aikamantaṃ nisin‍naṃ khau āyasmantaṃ sāriputtaṃ bhagavā aitadavauca –

‘‘idha, sāriputta, sambahulā samacittā daivatā yaināhaṃ tainupasaṅkamiṃsu; upasaṅkamitvā maṃ abhivādaitvā aikamantaṃ aṭṭhaṃsu| aikamantaṃ ṭhitā khau, sāriputta, tā daivatā maṃ aitadavaucuṃ – ‘aisau, bhantai, āyasmā sāriputtau pubbārāmai migāramātupāsādai  bhikkhūnaṃ ajjhattasaṃyaujanañ‍ca puggalaṃ daisaiti bahiddhāsaṃyaujanañ‍ca| haṭṭhā, bhantai, parisā| sādhu, bhantai, bhagavā yaina āyasmā sāriputtau tainupasaṅkamatu anukampaṃ upādāyā’ti| tā khau pana, sāriputta, daivatā dasapi hutvā vīsampi hutvā tiṃsampi hutvā cattālīsampi hutvā pañ‍ñāsampi hutvā saṭṭhipi hutvā āraggakauṭinitudanamattaipi tiṭṭhanti, na ca añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ byābādhainti byābādhaintīti (sabbattha)| siyā khau pana pana tai (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰), sāriputta, aivamassa – ‘tattha nūna tāsaṃ daivatānaṃ tathā cittaṃ bhāvitaṃ yaina tā daivatā dasapi hutvā vīsampi hutvā tiṃsampi hutvā cattālīsampi hutvā pañ‍ñāsampi hutvā saṭṭhipi hutvā āraggakauṭinitudanamattaipi tiṭṭhanti na ca añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ byābādhaintī’ti| na khau panaitaṃ, sāriputta, aivaṃ daṭṭhabbaṃ| idhaiva khau, sāriputta, tāsaṃ daivatānaṃ tathā cittaṃ bhāvitaṃ, yaina tā daivatā dasapi hutvā…pai॰… na ca añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ byābādhainti| tasmātiha, sāriputta, aivaṃ sikkhitabbaṃ – ‘santindriyā bhavissāma santamānasā’ti| aivañhi vau, sāriputta, sikkhitabbaṃ| ‘santindriyānañhi vau, sāriputta, santamānasānaṃ santaṃyaiva kāyakammaṃ bhavissati santaṃ vacīkammaṃ santaṃ manaukammaṃ| santaṃyaiva upahāraṃ upaharissāma sabrahmacārīsū’ti| ‘aivañhi vau, sāriputta, sikkhitabbaṃ| anassuṃ khau, sāriputta, añ‍ñatitthiyā paribbājakā yai imaṃ dhammapariyāyaṃ nāssausu’’’nti|

38. aivaṃ mai sutaṃ – aikaṃ  samayaṃ āyasmā mahākac‍cānau varaṇāyaṃ viharati bhaddasāritīrai  kaddamadahatīrai (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰)| atha  khau ārāmadaṇḍau brāhmaṇau yaināyasmā mahākac‍cānau tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā āyasmatā  mahākac‍cānaina saddhiṃ sammaudi| sammaudanīyaṃ kathaṃ sāraṇīyaṃ vītisāraitvā aikamantaṃ nisīdi| aikamantaṃ nisin‍nau khau ārāmadaṇḍau brāhmaṇau āyasmantaṃ mahākac‍cānaṃ aitadavauca – ‘‘kau nu khau, bhau kac‍cāna, haitu kau pac‍cayau yaina khattiyāpi khattiyaihi vivadanti, brāhmaṇāpi brāhmaṇaihi vivadanti, gahapatikāpi gahapatikaihi vivadantī’’ti? ‘‘kāmarāgābhinivaisavinibandha kāmarāgavinivaisavinibaddha (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) paligaidhapariyuṭṭhānajjhausānahaitu khau, brāhmaṇa, khattiyāpi khattiyaihi vivadanti, brāhmaṇāpi brāhmaṇaihi vivadanti, gahapatikāpi gahapatikaihi vivadantī’’ti|

‘‘kau pana, bhau kac‍cāna, haitu kau pac‍cayau yaina samaṇāpi samaṇaihi vivadantī’’ti? ‘‘diṭṭhirāgābhinivaisavinibandhapaligaidhapariyuṭṭhānajjhausānahaitu khau, brāhmaṇa, samaṇāpi samaṇaihi vivadantī’’ti|

‘‘atthi pana, bhau kac‍cāna, kauci laukasmiṃ yau imañ‍caiva kāmarāgābhinivaisavinibandhapaligaidhapariyuṭṭhānajjhausānaṃ samatik‍kantau, imañ‍ca diṭṭhirāgābhinivaisavinibandhapaligaidhapariyuṭṭhānajjhausānaṃ samatik‍kantau’’ti? ‘‘atthi, brāhmaṇa, laukasmiṃ yau imañ‍caiva kāmarāgābhinivaisavinibandhapaligaidhapariyuṭṭhānajjhausānaṃ samatik‍kantau, imañ‍ca diṭṭhirāgābhinivaisavinibandhapaligaidhapariyuṭṭhānajjhausānaṃ samatik‍kantau’’ti|

‘‘kau pana sau, bhau kac‍cāna, laukasmiṃ yau imañ‍caiva kāgarāgābhinivaisavinibandhapaligaidhapariyuṭṭhānajjhausānaṃ  samatik‍kantau, imañ‍ca diṭṭhirāgābhinivaisavinibandhapaligaidhapariyuṭṭhānajjhausānaṃ samatik‍kantau’’ti? ‘‘atthi, brāhmaṇa, puratthimaisu janapadaisu sāvatthī nāma nagaraṃ| tattha sau bhagavā aitarahi viharati arahaṃ sammāsambuddhau| sau hi, brāhmaṇa, bhagavā imañ‍caiva kāmarāgābhinivaisavinibandhapaligaidhapariyuṭṭhānajjhausānaṃ samatik‍kantau, imañ‍ca diṭṭhirāgābhinivaisavinibandhapaligaidhapariyuṭṭhānajjhausānaṃ samatik‍kantau’’ti|

aivaṃ vuttai ārāmadaṇḍau brāhmaṇau uṭṭhāyāsanā aikaṃsaṃ uttarāsaṅgaṃ karitvā dakkhiṇaṃ jāṇumaṇḍalaṃ pathaviyaṃ nihantvā yaina bhagavā tainañ‍jaliṃ paṇāmaitvā tikkhattuṃ udānaṃ udānaisi –

‘‘namau  tassa bhagavatau arahatau sammāsambuddhassa, namau tassa bhagavatau arahatau sammāsambuddhassa, namau tassa bhagavatau arahatau sammāsambuddhassa| yau hi sau bhagavā imañ‍caiva kāmarāgābhinivaisavinibandhapaligaidhapariyuṭṭhānajjhausānaṃ samatik‍kantau, imañ‍ca diṭṭhirāgābhinivaisavinibandhapaligaidhapariyuṭṭhānajjhausānaṃ samatik‍kantau’’ti|

‘‘abhik‍kantaṃ, bhau kac‍cāna, abhik‍kantaṃ, bhau kac‍cāna! saiyyathāpi, bhau kac‍cāna, nik‍kuj‍jitaṃ vā uk‍kuj‍jaiyya, paṭicchan‍naṃ vā vivaraiyya, mūḷhassa vā maggaṃ ācikkhaiyya, andhakārai vā tailapaj‍jautaṃ dhāraiyya – ‘cakkhumantau rūpāni dakkhantī’ti, aivamaivaṃ bhautā kac‍cānaina anaikapariyāyaina dhammau pakāsitau| aisāhaṃ, bhau kac‍cāna, taṃ bhavantaṃ gautamaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi dhammañ‍ca bhikkhusaṅghañ‍ca| upāsakaṃ maṃ bhavaṃ kac‍cānau dhāraitu aj‍jataggai pāṇupaitaṃ saraṇaṃ gata’’nti|

39. aikaṃ  samayaṃ āyasmā mahākac‍cānau madhurāyaṃ viharati gundāvanai| atha khau kandarāyanau kaṇḍarāyanau (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) brāhmaṇau yaināyasmā mahākac‍cānau tainupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā āyasmatā mahākac‍cānaina saddhiṃ…pai॰… aikamantaṃ nisin‍nau khau kandarāyanau brāhmaṇau āyasmantaṃ mahākac‍cānaṃ aitadavauca – ‘‘sutaṃ maitaṃ, bhau kac‍cāna, ‘na samaṇau kac‍cānau brāhmaṇai jiṇṇai vuddhai mahal‍lakai addhagatai vayauanuppattai abhivādaiti vā pac‍cuṭṭhaiti vā āsanaina vā nimantaitī’ti| tayidaṃ, bhau kac‍cāna, tathaiva? na hi bhavaṃ kac‍cānau brāhmaṇai jiṇṇai vuddhai mahal‍lakai addhagatai vayauanuppattai abhivādaiti vā pac‍cuṭṭhaiti vā āsanaina vā nimantaiti| tayidaṃ, bhau kac‍cāna, na sampan‍namaivā’’ti|

‘‘atthi, brāhmaṇa, taina bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhaina  vuddhabhūmi ca akkhātā daharabhūmi ca| vuddhau caipi, brāhmaṇa, hauti āsītikau vā nāvutikau vā vassasatikau vā jātiyā, sau ca kāmai paribhuñ‍jati kāmamajjhāvasati kāmapariḷāhaina pariḍayhati kāmavitak‍kaihi khaj‍jati kāmapariyaisanāya ussukau| atha khau sau bālau na thairautvaiva saṅkhyaṃ gacchati| daharau caipi, brāhmaṇa, hauti yuvā susukāḷakaisau bhadraina yaubbanaina saman‍nāgatau paṭhamaina vayasā| sau ca na kāmai paribhuñ‍jati na kāmamajjhāvasati, na kāmapariḷāhaina  pariḍayhati, na kāmavitak‍kaihi khaj‍jati, na kāmapariyaisanāya ussukau| atha khau sau paṇḍitau thairautvaiva saṅkhyaṃ gacchatī’’ti|

aivaṃ vuttai kandarāyanau brāhmaṇau uṭṭhāyāsanā aikaṃsaṃ  uttarāsaṅgaṃ karitvā daharānaṃ sataṃ sudaṃ (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰) bhikkhūnaṃ pādai sirasā vandati – ‘‘vuddhā bhavantau, vuddhabhūmiyaṃ ṭhitā| daharā mayaṃ, daharabhūmiyaṃ ṭhitā’’ti|

‘‘abhik‍kantaṃ, bhau kac‍cāna…pai॰… upāsakaṃ maṃ bhavaṃ kac‍cānau dhāraitu aj‍jataggai pāṇupaitaṃ saraṇaṃ gata’’nti|

40. ‘‘yasmiṃ, bhikkhavai, samayai caurā balavantau haunti, rājānau tasmiṃ samayai dubbalā haunti| tasmiṃ, bhikkhavai, samayai rañ‍ñau na phāsu hauti atiyātuṃ vā niyyātuṃ vā pac‍cantimai vā janapadai anusañ‍ñātuṃ| brāhmaṇagahapatikānampi tasmiṃ samayai na phāsu hauti atiyātuṃ vā niyyātuṃ vā bāhirāni vā kammantāni paṭivaikkhituṃ| aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, yasmiṃ samayai pāpabhikkhū balavantau haunti, paisalā bhikkhū tasmiṃ samayai dubbalā haunti| tasmiṃ, bhikkhavai, samayai paisalā bhikkhū tuṇhībhūtā tuṇhībhūtāva saṅghamajjhai saṅkasāyanti saṅkamma jhāyanti (ka॰), sañ‍cāyanti (sī॰ aṭṭha॰) pac‍cantimai vā janapadai acchanti bhajanti (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰)| tayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, hauti bahujanāhitāya bahujanāsukhāya, bahunau janassa anatthāya ahitāya dukkhāya daivamanussānaṃ|

‘‘yasmiṃ, bhikkhavai, samayai rājānau balavantau haunti, caurā tasmiṃ samayai dubbalā haunti| tasmiṃ, bhikkhavai, samayai rañ‍ñau phāsu hauti atiyātuṃ vā niyyātuṃ vā pac‍cantimai vā janapadai anusañ‍ñātuṃ| brāhmaṇagahapatikānampi tasmiṃ samayai phāsu hauti atiyātuṃ vā niyyātuṃ vā bāhirāni vā kammantāni paṭivaikkhituṃ| aivamaivaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, yasmiṃ samayai paisalā bhikkhū balavantau haunti, pāpabhikkhū tasmiṃ samayai dubbalā haunti| tasmiṃ, bhikkhavai, samayai pāpabhikkhū  tuṇhībhūtā tuṇhībhūtāva saṅghamajjhai saṅkasāyanti, yaina vā pana taina pak‍kamanti papatanti (sī॰ syā॰ kaṃ॰ pī॰)| tayidaṃ, bhikkhavai, hauti bahujanahitāya bahujanasukhāya, bahunau janassa atthāya hitāya sukhāya daivamanussāna’’nti|

41. ‘‘dvin‍nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, micchāpaṭipattiṃ na vaṇṇaimi, gihissa vā pabbajitassa vā| gihī vā, bhikkhavai, pabbajitau vā micchāpaṭipan‍nau micchāpaṭipattādhikaraṇahaitu na ārādhakau hauti ñāyaṃ dhammaṃ kusalaṃ|

‘‘dvin‍nāhaṃ, bhikkhavai, sammāpaṭipattiṃ vaṇṇaimi, gihissa vā pabbajitassa vā| gihī vā, bhikkhavai, pabbajitau vā sammāpaṭipan‍nau sammāpaṭipattādhikaraṇahaitu ārādhakau hauti ñāyaṃ dhammaṃ kusala’’nti|

42. ‘‘yai tai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhū duggahitaihi suttantaihi byañ‍janappatirūpakaihi atthañ‍ca dhammañ‍ca paṭivāhanti tai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhū bahujanāhitāya paṭipan‍nā bahujanāsukhāya, bahunau janassa anatthāya ahitāya dukkhāya daivamanussānaṃ| bahuñ‍ca tai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhū apuñ‍ñaṃ pasavanti, tai cimaṃ saddhammaṃ antaradhāpainti|

‘‘yai tai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhū suggahitaihi suttantaihi byañ‍janappatirūpakaihi atthañ‍ca dhammañ‍ca anulaumainti tai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhū bahujanahitāya paṭipan‍nā bahujanasukhāya, bahunau janassa atthāya hitāya sukhāya daivamanussānaṃ| bahuñ‍ca tai, bhikkhavai, bhikkhū puñ‍ñaṃ pasavanti, tai cimaṃ saddhammaṃ ṭhapaintī’’ti|

samacittavaggau catutthau|



5. parisavaggau

43. ‘‘dvaimā, bhikkhavai, parisā| katamā dvai? uttānā ca parisā  gambhīrā ca parisā| katamā ca, bhikkhavai, uttānā parisā? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ bhikkhū uddhatā haunti un‍naḷā capalā mukharā vikiṇṇavācā muṭṭhassatī asampajānā asamāhitā vibbhantacittā pākatindriyā| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, uttānā parisā|

‘‘katamā ca, bhikkhavai, gambhīrā parisā? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ bhikkhū anuddhatā haunti anun‍naḷā acapalā amukharā avikiṇṇavācā upaṭṭhitassatī sampajānā samāhitā aikaggacittā saṃvutindriyā| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, gambhīrā parisā| imā khau, bhikkhavai, dvai parisā| aitadaggaṃ, bhikkhavai, imāsaṃ dvin‍naṃ parisānaṃ yadidaṃ gambhīrā parisā’’ti|

44. ‘‘dvaimā, bhikkhavai, parisā| katamā dvai? vaggā ca parisā samaggā ca parisā| katamā ca, bhikkhavai, vaggā parisā? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ bhikkhū bhaṇḍanajātā kalahajātā vivādāpan‍nā añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ mukhasattīhi vitudantā viharanti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, vaggā parisā|

‘‘katamā ca, bhikkhavai, samaggā parisā? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ bhikkhū samaggā sammaudamānā avivadamānā khīraudakībhūtā añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ piyacakkhūhi sampassantā viharanti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, samaggā parisā| imā khau, bhikkhavai, dvai parisā| aitadaggaṃ, bhikkhavai, imāsaṃ dvin‍naṃ parisānaṃ yadidaṃ samaggā parisā’’ti|

45. ‘‘dvaimā, bhikkhavai, parisā| katamā dvai? anaggavatī ca parisā aggavatī ca parisā| katamā  ca, bhikkhavai, anaggavatī parisā? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ thairā bhikkhū bāhulikā bāhul‍likā (syā॰ kaṃ॰ ka॰) ṭīkā aulaukaitabbā haunti sāthalikā, auk‍kamanai pubbaṅgamā, pavivaikai nikkhittadhurā, na vīriyaṃ  ārabhanti appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya| taisaṃ pacchimā janatā diṭṭhānugatiṃ āpaj‍jati| sāpi hauti bāhulikā sāthalikā, auk‍kamanai pubbaṅgamā, pavivaikai nikkhittadhurā, na vīriyaṃ ārabhati appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, anaggavatī parisā|

‘‘katamā ca, bhikkhavai, aggavatī parisā? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ thairā bhikkhū na bāhulikā haunti na sāthalikā, auk‍kamanai nikkhittadhurā, pavivaikai pubbaṅgamā, vīriyaṃ ārabhanti appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya| taisaṃ pacchimā janatā diṭṭhānugatiṃ āpaj‍jati| sāpi hauti na bāhulikā na sāthalikā, auk‍kamanai nikkhittadhurā, pavivaikai pubbaṅgamā, vīriyaṃ ārabhati appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, aggavatī parisā| imā khau, bhikkhavai, dvai  parisā| aitadaggaṃ, bhikkhavai, imāsaṃ dvin‍naṃ parisānaṃ yadidaṃ aggavatī parisā’’ti|

46. ‘‘dvaimā, bhikkhavai, parisā| katamā dvai? anariyā ca parisā ariyā ca parisā| katamā ca, bhikkhavai, anariyā parisā? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ bhikkhū ‘idaṃ dukkha’nti yathābhūtaṃ nappajānanti, ‘ayaṃ dukkhasamudayau’ti yathābhūtaṃ nappajānanti, ‘ayaṃ dukkhaniraudhau’ti yathābhūtaṃ nappajānanti, ‘ayaṃ dukkhaniraudhagāminī paṭipadā’ti yathābhūtaṃ nappajānanti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, anariyā parisā|

‘‘katamā ca, bhikkhavai, ariyā parisā? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ bhikkhū ‘idaṃ dukkha’nti yathābhūtaṃ pajānanti, ‘ayaṃ dukkhasamudayau’ti yathābhūtaṃ pajānanti, ‘ayaṃ dukkhaniraudhau’ti yathābhūtaṃ pajānanti, ‘ayaṃ dukkhaniraudhagāminī paṭipadā’ti yathābhūtaṃ pajānanti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, ariyā parisā| imā khau, bhikkhavai, dvai parisā| aitadaggaṃ, bhikkhavai, imāsaṃ dvin‍naṃ parisānaṃ yadidaṃ ariyā parisā’’ti|

47. ‘‘dvaimā, bhikkhavai, parisā| katamā dvai? parisākasaṭau ca parisāmaṇḍau ca| katamau  ca, bhikkhavai, parisākasaṭau? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ bhikkhū chandāgatiṃ gacchanti, dausāgatiṃ gacchanti, mauhāgatiṃ gacchanti, bhayāgatiṃ gacchanti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, parisākasaṭau|

‘‘katamau ca, bhikkhavai, parisāmaṇḍau? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ bhikkhū na chandāgatiṃ gacchanti, na dausāgatiṃ gacchanti, na mauhāgatiṃ gacchanti, na bhayāgatiṃ gacchanti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, parisāmaṇḍau| imā khau, bhikkhavai, dvai parisā| aitadaggaṃ, bhikkhavai, imāsaṃ dvin‍naṃ parisānaṃ yadidaṃ parisāmaṇḍau’’ti|

48. ‘‘dvaimā, bhikkhavai, parisā| katamā dvai? auk‍kācitavinītā parisā naupaṭipucchāvinītā, paṭipucchāvinītā parisā nauauk‍kācitavinītā| katamā ca, bhikkhavai, auk‍kācitavinītā parisā naupaṭipucchāvinītā? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ bhikkhū yai tai suttantā tathāgatabhāsitā gambhīrā  gambhīratthā  laukuttarā suñ‍ñatāpaṭisaṃyuttā taisu bhañ‍ñamānaisu na sussūsanti na sautaṃ audahanti na añ‍ñā cittaṃ upaṭṭhapainti na ca tai dhammai uggahaitabbaṃ pariyāpuṇitabbaṃ mañ‍ñanti| yai pana tai suttantā kavitā kavikatā (sabbattha) ṭīkā aulaukaitabbā kāvaiyyā cittakkharā cittabyañ‍janā bāhirakā sāvakabhāsitā taisu bhañ‍ñamānaisu sussūsanti sautaṃ audahanti añ‍ñā cittaṃ upaṭṭhapainti, tai dhammai uggahaitabbaṃ pariyāpuṇitabbaṃ mañ‍ñanti, tai ca taṃ dhammaṃ pariyāpuṇitvā na caiva añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ paṭipucchanti na ca paṭivicaranti  – ‘idaṃ kathaṃ, imassa kau atthau’ti? tai avivaṭañ‍caiva na vivaranti, anuttānīkatañ‍ca na uttānīkaraunti, anaikavihitaisu ca kaṅkhāṭhāniyaisu dhammaisu kaṅkhaṃ na paṭivinaudainti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, auk‍kācitavinītā parisā nau paṭipucchāvinītā|

‘‘katamā ca, bhikkhavai, paṭipucchāvinītā parisā nauauk‍kācitavinītā? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ bhikkhū yai tai suttantā kavitā kāvaiyyā cittakkharā cittabyañ‍janā bāhirakā sāvakabhāsitā taisu bhañ‍ñamānaisu na sussūsanti na sautaṃ audahanti na añ‍ñā cittaṃ upaṭṭhapainti, na ca tai dhammai uggahaitabbaṃ pariyāpuṇitabbaṃ mañ‍ñanti| yai pana tai suttantā tathāgatabhāsitā gambhīrā gambhīratthā laukuttarā suñ‍ñatāpaṭisaṃyuttā taisu bhañ‍ñamānaisu sussūsanti sautaṃ audahanti añ‍ñā cittaṃ upaṭṭhapainti, tai ca dhammai uggahaitabbaṃ pariyāpuṇitabbaṃ  mañ‍ñanti| tai taṃ dhammaṃ pariyāpuṇitvā añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ paṭipucchanti paṭivicaranti – ‘idaṃ kathaṃ, imassa kau atthau’ti? tai avivaṭañ‍caiva  vivaranti, anuttānīkatañ‍ca uttānīkaraunti, anaikavihitaisu ca kaṅkhāṭhāniyaisu dhammaisu kaṅkhaṃ paṭivinaudainti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, paṭipucchāvinītā parisā nauauk‍kācitavinītā| imā khau, bhikkhavai, dvai parisā| aitadaggaṃ, bhikkhavai, imāsaṃ dvin‍naṃ parisānaṃ yadidaṃ paṭipucchāvinītā parisā nauauk‍kācitavinītā’’ti|

49. ‘‘dvaimā, bhikkhavai, parisā| katamā dvai? āmisagaru parisā nau saddhammagaru, saddhammagaru parisā nau āmisagaru| katamā ca, bhikkhavai, āmisagaru parisā nau saddhammagaru? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ bhikkhū gihīnaṃ audātavasanānaṃ sammukhā añ‍ñamañ‍ñassa vaṇṇaṃ bhāsanti – ‘asukau bhikkhu ubhataubhāgavimuttau, asukau pañ‍ñāvimuttau, asukau kāyasakkhī, asukau diṭṭhippattau, asukau saddhāvimuttau, asukau dhammānusārī, asukau saddhānusārī, asukau sīlavā kalyāṇadhammau, asukau dussīlau pāpadhammau’ti| tai taina lābhaṃ labhanti| tai taṃ lābhaṃ labhitvā gathitā gadhitā (ka॰) mucchitā ajjhaupan‍nā ajjhausānā (ka॰), anajjhaupan‍nā (sī॰ syā॰ ka॰) tikanipātai kusināravaggai paṭhamasuttaṭīkā aulaukaitabbā anādīnavadassāvinau anissaraṇapañ‍ñā paribhuñ‍janti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, āmisagaru parisā nau saddhammagaru|

‘‘katamā ca, bhikkhavai, saddhammagaru parisā nauāmisagaru? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ bhikkhū gihīnaṃ audātavasanānaṃ sammukhā añ‍ñamañ‍ñassa vaṇṇaṃ na bhāsanti – ‘asukau bhikkhu ubhataubhāgavimuttau, asukau pañ‍ñāvimuttau, asukau kāyasakkhī, asukau diṭṭhippattau, asukau saddhāvimuttau, asukau dhammānussārī, asukau saddhānusārī, asukau sīlavā kalyāṇadhammau, asukau dussīlau pāpadhammau’ti| tai  taina lābhaṃ labhanti| tai taṃ lābhaṃ labhitvā agathitā amucchitā anajjhausan‍nā ādīnavadassāvinau nissaraṇapañ‍ñā paribhuñ‍janti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, saddhammagaru parisā nauāmisagaru| imā khau, bhikkhavai, dvai parisā| aitadaggaṃ, bhikkhavai, imāsaṃ dvin‍naṃ parisānaṃ yadidaṃ saddhammagaru parisā nauāmisagarū’’ti|

50. ‘‘dvaimā, bhikkhavai, parisā| katamā dvai? visamā ca parisā samā ca parisā| katamā  ca, bhikkhavai, visamā parisā? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ adhammakammāni pavattanti dhammakammāni nappavattanti, avinayakammāni pavattanti vinayakammāni nappavattanti, adhammakammāni dippanti dhammakammāni na dippanti, avinayakammāni dippanti vinayakammāni na dippanti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, visamā parisā| ( ) (visamattā bhikkhavai parisāya adhammakammāni pavattanti… vinayakammāni na dippanti|) (sī॰ pī॰)

‘‘katamā ca, bhikkhavai, samā parisā? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ dhammakammāni pavattanti adhammakammāni nappavattanti, vinayakammāni pavattanti  avinayakammāni nappavattanti, dhammakammāni dippanti adhammakammāni na dippanti, vinayakammāni dippanti avinayakammāni na dippanti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, samā  parisā| ( ) (samattā bhikkhavai parisāya dhammakammāni pavattanti… avinayakammāni na dippanti|) (sī॰ pī॰) imā khau, bhikkhavai, dvai parisā| aitadaggaṃ, bhikkhavai, imāsaṃ dvin‍naṃ parisānaṃ yadidaṃ samā parisā’’ti|

51. ‘‘dvaimā, bhikkhavai, parisā| katamā dvai? adhammikā ca parisā dhammikā ca parisā…pai॰… imā khau, bhikkhavai, dvai parisā| aitadaggaṃ, bhikkhavai, imāsaṃ dvin‍naṃ parisānaṃ yadidaṃ dhammikā parisā’’ti|

52. ‘‘dvaimā, bhikkhavai, parisā| katamā dvai? adhammavādinī ca parisā dhammavādinī ca parisā| katamā ca, bhikkhavai, adhammavādinī parisā? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ bhikkhū adhikaraṇaṃ ādiyanti dhammikaṃ vā adhammikaṃ vā| tai taṃ adhikaraṇaṃ ādiyitvā na caiva añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ sañ‍ñāpainti na ca sañ‍ñattiṃ upagacchanti, na ca nijjhāpainti na ca nijjhattiṃ upagacchanti| tai asañ‍ñattibalā anijjhattibalā appaṭinissaggamantinau  tamaiva adhikaraṇaṃ thāmasā parāmāsā parāmassa (sī॰ pī॰) abhinivissa vauharanti – ‘idamaiva sac‍caṃ maughamañ‍ña’nti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, adhammavādinī parisā|

‘‘katamā ca, bhikkhavai, dhammavādinī parisā? idha, bhikkhavai, yassaṃ parisāyaṃ bhikkhū adhikaraṇaṃ ādiyanti dhammikaṃ vā adhammikaṃ vā| tai taṃ adhikaraṇaṃ ādiyitvā añ‍ñamañ‍ñaṃ sañ‍ñāpainti caiva sañ‍ñattiñ‍ca  upagacchanti, nijjhāpainti caiva nijjhattiñ‍ca upagacchanti| tai sañ‍ñattibalā nijjhattibalā paṭinissaggamantinau, na tamaiva adhikaraṇaṃ thāmasā parāmāsā abhinivissa vauharanti – ‘idamaiva sac‍caṃ maughamañ‍ña’nti| ayaṃ vuc‍cati, bhikkhavai, dhammavādinī  parisā| imā khau, bhikkhavai, dvai parisā| aitadaggaṃ, bhikkhavai, imāsaṃ dvin‍naṃ parisānaṃ yadidaṃ dhammavādinī parisā’’ti|

2. dutiyapaṇṇāsakaṃ


(6) 1. puggalavaggau

53. ‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, puggalā laukai uppaj‍jamānā uppaj‍janti bahujanahitāya bahujanasukhāya, bahunau janassa atthāya hitāya sukhāya daivamanussānaṃ| katamai dvai? tathāgatau ca arahaṃ sammāsambuddhau, rājā ca cak‍kavattī| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai puggalā laukai uppaj‍jamānā uppaj‍janti bahujanahitāya bahujanasukhāya, bahunau janassa atthāya hitāya sukhāya daivamanussāna’’nti|

54. ‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, puggalā laukai uppaj‍jamānā uppaj‍janti acchariyamanussā| katamai  dvai? tathāgatau ca arahaṃ sammāsambuddhau, rājā ca cak‍kavattī| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai puggalā laukai uppaj‍jamānā  uppaj‍janti acchariyamanussā’’ti|

55. ‘‘dvin‍naṃ, bhikkhavai, puggalānaṃ kālakiriyā bahunau janassa anutappā hauti| katamaisaṃ dvin‍naṃ? tathāgatassa ca arahatau sammāsambuddhassa, rañ‍ñau ca cak‍kavattissa| imaisaṃ khau, bhikkhavai, dvin‍naṃ puggalānaṃ kālakiriyā bahunau janassa anutappā hautī’’ti|

56. ‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, thūpārahā| katamai dvai? tathāgatau ca arahaṃ sammāsambuddhau, rājā ca cak‍kavattī| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai thūpārahā’’ti|

57. ‘‘dvaimai, bhikkhavai, buddhā| katamai dvai? tathāgatau ca arahaṃ sammāsambuddhau, pac‍caikabuddhau ca| imai khau, bhikkhavai, dvai buddhā’’ti|
